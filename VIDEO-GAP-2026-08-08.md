## Method and confidence

Matching ran in three passes per DB row: MCeLE video Id extracted from any URL field, exact slug of `Title`, exact slug of `Video Title`. A row counted as present if any pass hit. Confidence 0.93.

Residual risk: a portal page that renames a video beyond slug recognition and carries a different Id would read as missing. The 28 were each verified by grepping all of `content/` for the MCeLE Id and for distinctive title strings. Zero hits. The gap is real.
