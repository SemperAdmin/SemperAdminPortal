## Scope

MARADMIN 291/19 set early Command Climate Survey compliance requirements for the Marine Corps, signed 2019-05-16. Applicability was the Marine Corps Total Force. MARADMIN 025/21, titled Cancellation of MARADMIN 291-19 and signed 2021-05-03, cancelled the message. Command Climate Assessment (CCA) governance now runs through MARADMIN 534/23 and DoD Instruction 6400.11. MARADMIN 023/23 cancelled MARADMIN 468/23, and 534/23 reissued its content.

## Audience

- Commanders required to conduct annual and change-of-command CCAs. Apply the current MARADMIN 534/23 framework.
- Outgoing commanders preparing for relief. The portal turnover checklist cites MARADMIN 534/23. The change of command CCA belongs to the incoming commander.
- Reporting officials annotating conducted CCAs on commander fitness reports under the NDAA 2013 requirement.

## Portal usage

- The commander turnover checklist directs the incoming commander to conduct the change of command CCA within 90 days after assumption of command, per MARADMIN 534/23 paragraph 4. MARADMIN 291/19 no longer governs.
- The current MARADMIN 534/23 framework sets the annual CCA window from 01 August through 30 November, a change of command CCA within 90 days after assumption of command, and a single CCA option when both windows overlap.

## Connection to higher policy

- DoD Instruction 6400.11. DoD Integrated Primary Prevention Policy. Governs the current CCA lifecycle.
- National Defense Authorization Act of 2013. Requires CCA annotation on the commander annual fitness report.

## Connection to Marine Corps doctrine

- MARADMIN 025/21. Cancellation of MARADMIN 291-19. Signed 2021-05-03.
- MARADMIN 468/23. Command Climate Assessment Policy Updates and Guidance to the Fleet, signed 2023-09-20. Cancelled by MARADMIN 023/23. MARADMIN 534/23 is the current framework.
- MCO 5354.1F. Marine Corps Equal Opportunity Manual. Chapter 2 carries the annual CCA procedure.

## Status
