## Scope

PAA 03-26 makes the Unit Diary-level Diary Notes capability in UD/MIPS mandatory
for every unit diary requiring certification, effective 1 October 2026. The
advisory answers a Notice of Finding and Recommendation (2025-0001-FIN-GFMC)
from the Independent Public Accountant, received 6 February 2026. The finding
says the Research Diary Report certification statement and signature
implicitly acknowledge review but do not document the procedures performed.
The advisory adds auditable evidence of the review before certification.

The requirement does not apply to Transaction User Notes, Transaction System
Notes, or Diary Feedback Report (DFR) Notes unless otherwise specified. The
advisory changes no MCTFS transaction, code, or table.

## Audience

- Preparers, Reviewers, and Certifiers in every reporting unit using UD/MIPS.
- S-1 and IPAC admin specialists (0102, 0111, 0170) who prepare and submit diaries.
- Certifying officers and commanders who hold accountability for certified diaries.
- Addressees who disseminate the advisory to subordinate commands (paragraph 1).

## Action language

The Notes capability sits at the bottom of each unit diary and is role-specific
(paragraph 4). Users enter notes only for their assigned role. The advisory
lists the following annotations as the minimum. Replace YYYYMMDD with the
date of the action.

| Role | Trigger | Annotation | Paragraph |
|------|---------|------------|-----------|
| Preparer | Submit a diary for review or certification | YYYYMMDD - Diary has been submitted for review/certification. | 5 |
| Preparer | Resubmit after corrective action | YYYYMMDD - Diary returned with corrections for review/certification. | 5 |
| Reviewer | Finish review and forward to the Certifier | YYYYMMDD - Diary reviewed and forwarded for certification. | 6 |
| Reviewer | Return the diary to the Preparer | YYYYMMDD - Diary returned to Preparer for corrections. | 6 |
| Certifier | Return a diary for correction | YYYYMMDD - Returned for corrections. | 7 |
| Certifier | Finish review, before certification | YYYYMMDD - Diary transactions reviewed against supporting documentation and verified as accurate. Diary is ready for certification. | 7 |

The Reviewer role is optional. A reporting unit has no requirement to use it.
If the unit uses the role, the Reviewer annotations apply (paragraph 6).

Paragraph 8 sets the chronological history rule. Until system enhancements
provide an append-only record, users keep the full sequence of annotations in
their role note.

- Append each new annotation to the existing note.
- Never delete, alter, or overwrite a prior annotation.
- Separate annotations with a forward slash (/).
- Apply the rule to every submission, return for correction, corrective action, resubmission, review, and certification.
- Put the date inside every annotation. Editing a note updates the system Last Update date.

Example from paragraph 8: "20261001 - Diary returned for corrections / 20261002 - Diary transactions reviewed against supporting documentation and verified as accurate. Diary is ready for certification."

Paragraph 9 calls the arrangement a procedural control pending the system
enhancement. Commands ensure prior annotations stay intact.

Paragraph 10 points to a tutorial under the UD/MIPS header of the Application
Training and Information Tile on the MISSA and MISSO portal.

## Connection to Marine Corps policy

The three note-entering roles match the UD/MIPS roles in
[MCTFSPRIUM 2-20115](/citations/mctfsprium-20115). Paragraph 3 states the MCTFS
PRIUM will be updated to establish the standardized procedures and describes
the advisory as advance notice and amplification of the forthcoming guidance.
Until the PRIUM update posts, treat the advisory as the controlling text.
