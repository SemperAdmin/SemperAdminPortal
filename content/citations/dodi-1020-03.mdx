## Scope

DODI 1020.03 carries the DoD harassment prevention and response policy for the armed forces. The 2025-12-10 issuance is one of the two DoD references behind MARADMIN 349/26, the realignment of the Marine Corps program terminology to Military Equal Opportunity.

## Audience

- Commanders responding to harassment complaints under the DoD framework.
- Equal Opportunity Advisors and the S-1 staff supporting complaint handling.

## Connection to Marine Corps doctrine and lower policy

- MARADMIN 349/26. The terminology realignment citing this instruction as reference B.
- MCO 5354.1G. The Marine Corps prevention and response order.
- DODI 1350.02. The companion DoD MEO program policy.
