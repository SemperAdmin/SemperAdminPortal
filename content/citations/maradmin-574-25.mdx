## Scope

MARADMIN 574/25 set Marine Corps FLPB eligibility for Fiscal Year 2026, effective 1 October 2025.

## Status

Partly in effect. MARADMIN 480/26 supersedes and rescinds paragraph 4.a.1.a, so the PMOS 0370 and 0372 minimum stays ILR 1. Marines who certified proficiency in FY26 keep FY26 rules until those scores expire, per MARADMIN 480/26 paragraph 6.c. A recertification on or after 1 October 2026 moves the Marine to MARADMIN 480/26.
