## Access

Personnel Administrative Advisory Notices publish through RFF and MISSA channels behind DoD authentication. No public URL exists. The notice carries a controlled distribution, so this entry states the rule in portal words and reproduces no notice text.

## Scope

No CONUS COLA pays for the days authorized for PCS travel between the old and new permanent duty station. The finance office deducts COLA for actual travel days through the date of report. The date of report deducts at the new duty station rate, because MCTFS starts the new rate on the date of report. Other travel days deduct at the old duty station rate or the designated place rate. Delay (leave) days before travel are not travel days.

## Connection to higher policy

- 37 U.S.C. 403b. Bars CONUS COLA for days authorized for PCS travel.
- DoD FMR Volume 7A Chapter 67, Table 67-1. CONUS COLA entitlement rules.
- FPM Volume 1. The notice announces an update to paragraph 070502.D.
