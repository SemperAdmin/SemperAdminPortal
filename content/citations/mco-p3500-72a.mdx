## Scope

MCO P3500.72A establishes the Marine Corps Ground Training and Readiness (T&R) Program. The order defines the T&R framework for ground combat units, setting standards for individual and collective training events, event coding, Combat Readiness Percentage (CRP) calculation, training plan development and submission, after action review (AAR) conduct, and training exception processing. The order applies to all ground combat units and integrates with the Marine Corps Training Information Management System (MCTIMS).

## Audience

Commanders use this order to build unit training plans, assign METL tasks, and manage CRP. Unit Training Managers (UTMs) use it to build MCTIMS entries, manage training calendars, and process exceptions. S-1 admin specialists and 0170 Personnel Officers use it to manage the administrative component of training records and the 01XX T&R events.

## Chapter map

The order contains seven chapters. Chapter 1 covers the Marine Corps Philosophy of Training. Chapter 2 covers the background and development of the Marine Corps Training Program. Chapter 3 covers policy for T&R Manual development and use. Chapter 4 covers T&R program structure. Chapter 5 covers Combat Readiness Percentage (CRP). Chapter 6 covers automated support for training management. Chapter 7 covers evaluation.

## Operative content

- T&R event coding. Individual and collective events use a 4-4-4 alphanumeric system (MOS-FunctionalArea-Level). Individual events range from 1000-level (formal school) through 2200-level (advanced). Collective events range from 3000-level (team/section) through 9000-level (command).
- CRP calculation. Combat Readiness Percentage measures unit training readiness. Unit CRP is the average of the CRP of each Mission Essential Task (MET). Only E-coded events carry a CRP value, and only E-coded events supporting the approved METL count. Deferred and delinquent events reduce CRP per paragraph 4006.
- METL review. Commanders review and update the METL annually, within 45 days of change of command, and within 15 days of a new mission assignment per paragraph 4.b.(6)(e) of MCO 1553.3C cross-reference.
- Training exceptions. Defer (paragraph 4006.1), Waive (paragraph 4006.2), and Delinquent (paragraph 4006.3) are the three exception categories. All exceptions are documented in MCTIMS.
- AAR requirement. After Action Reviews are required for all scheduled T&R events. Informal AARs run after most training. Formal AARs run after MCCRE and formal evaluations. AAR conduct rules sit at paragraph 7000.3.
- Training plan submission. Commanders publish a unit training plan within 90 days of taking command, built on the approved METL.

## Connection to higher policy

Published by the Commandant of the Marine Corps as service-level training policy, sponsored by TECOM PSD, and applicable to the Marine Corps Total Force. The portal registry links no DoD or SECNAV parent directive to this order. The order itself stands as the top of the ground T&R policy chain within the Marine Corps.

## Connection to Marine Corps doctrine and lower policy

- MCO 1553.3C Unit Training Management Program. Establishes UTM roles and MCTIMS documentation requirements executing this framework.
- NAVMC 3500.3E Manpower and Administration Training and Readiness Manual. T-R Manual for MOS 0102, 0111, 0170.
- MCRP 7-20A.1 Training Plan Design, MCRP 7-20A.2 Event and Exercise Design, and MCRP 7-20A.5 Training Data Management. Doctrinal publications whose portal registry entries cross-reference this order.
- MCTIMS. Marine Corps Training Information Management System, the system of record the order's documentation requirements target.
- Portal admin training-sustainment pages, covering T&R event coding, CRP calculation and reporting, training exceptions, AAR conduct, and annual METL review, cite this order.
