## Scope

MARADMIN 474/23 provided advance notification of upcoming changes to Special Leave Accrual (SLA) under MCO 1050.3J, Regulations for Leave, Liberty, and Administrative Absence. The message published on 2023-09-21 and served as the interim SLA notice until Headquarters Marine Corps issued the replacement notification. MARADMIN 188/25 superseded it and restated the 90-day accrued leave ceiling that 474/23 paragraph 1 already set, 60 days of annual leave plus 30 days of SLA carryover from one fiscal year to the next. MARADMIN 545/26 paragraph 4.a now supplies the current SLA procedures in place of the 188/25 procedures.

## Audience

- The Marine Corps Total Force, per paragraph 8 of this message. The current SLA procedure lives in MARADMIN 545/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 545/26" />, which supersedes the MARADMIN 188/25 procedures and rescinds the MARADMIN 341/26 suspension.
- Commanders and S-1 staff. Route SLA requests under MARADMIN 545/26, not this message or MARADMIN 188/25.
- HQMC Manpower Plans and Policy Division (MPO). Owned the policy and issued the replacement.

## Supersession by MARADMIN 188/25

- MARADMIN 188/25, dated 2025-04-11, cancels MARADMIN 474/23 outright.
- The successor authorizes Marines to carry over a maximum of 90 days of accrued leave from one fiscal year to the next, 60 days of annual leave plus 30 days of SLA.
- The portal's Special Leave Accrual page cites MARADMIN 188/25 paragraph 2 for the carryover ceiling and records the cancellation of this message.
- SLA approval authority, qualifying duties, and expiration rules live in the MARADMIN 545/26 entry. The one-time SLA sell-back is MARADMIN 188/25 paragraph 8. MARADMIN 545/26 says only a balance over 90 days is eligible for sell-back.

## Connection to higher policy

- DoD Instruction 1327.06 with Change 5, dated 2023-08-25. Leave and Liberty Procedures for Service Members. Drives the SLA changes the notification series announced.
- Public Law 117-263 Section 632, dated 2022-12-23. Statutory basis for the accrued leave changes.

## Connection to Marine Corps doctrine

- MARADMIN 188/25. Advance Notification of Changes to Special Leave Accrual. Cancels this MARADMIN and sets the current 90-day accrued leave ceiling.
- MCO 1050.3J. Regulations for Leave, Liberty, and Administrative Absence. The order receiving the SLA changes.

## Status

Released 2023-09-21. Originator CMC Washington DC MRA MP. Cancelled by MARADMIN 188/25 dated 2025-04-11. Use this entry for historical context only. For any current SLA question, apply MARADMIN 545/26 and MCO 1050.3J. MARADMIN 545/26 superseded the 188/25 procedures.
