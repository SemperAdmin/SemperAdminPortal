## Scope

SECNAVINST 5210.8F sets Department of the Navy records management policy and assigns responsibilities across the Navy and Marine Corps. It cancels SECNAVINST 5210.8E. SECNAV M-5210.1 carries the procedures and disposition schedules. MCO 5210.11F implements both for the Marine Corps.

## Operative content the portal leans on

- Paragraph 5.l. Train every DON member on records management, with annual refresher training. Training covers the topics in NARA Bulletin 2017-01 and 36 CFR 1220.34(f). IGMC FAC 5210 item 0103 cites it.
- Enclosure 4 paragraph 6.g. Echelon II commanders keep the command Capstone official list accurate against the DON list and report every Capstone official change. IGMC FAC 5210 items 0402 and 0403 cite it.
- Enclosure 5 paragraph 4. Records manager duties, cited alongside enclosure 4 on items 0402 and 0403.
