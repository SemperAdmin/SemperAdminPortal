## What this form is

NAVMC 11512 is the complaint form behind the Military Equal Opportunity informal and formal complaint process. The 2024-06-01 revision still carries the former program title, Prohibited Activities and Conduct Complaint Form. Two pages, fillable, stock number NONSN21012602.

MARADMIN 380/26 names this form, or an MPE approved form, as the instrument for an informal or formal MEO complaint. A confidential report uses no complaint form at all.

## Where it sits in the process

| Lane | Form | What follows |
|------|------|--------------|
| Confidential report | None | Services and documentation with no command notification outside three exceptions |
| Informal complaint | NAVMC 11512 or MPE approved form | Conflict resolution offered where feasible, voluntary for both parties |
| Formal complaint | NAVMC 11512 or MPE approved form | EOA forwards to the commander within 5 calendar days |
| Anonymous allegation | None | No MEO complaint for discrimination or discriminatory harassment |

## Status

Current revision dated 2024-06-01. The form title tracks the former PAC program name until the form itself is revised. Program terminology moved to Military Equal Opportunity under MARADMIN 357/26, and MARADMIN 380/26 added the confidential reporting lane on 2026-08-20. Expect a form revision alongside MCO 5354.1H.
