## Scope

PAA 01-26 moves access to the Marine Corps Total Force System (MCTFS) from the
locally installed Reflection Desktop application to the Multi-Host Internet
Access Portal (MIAP). Paragraph 3 gives the reason as security compliance
requirements. Reflection Desktop is scheduled for disablement and removal from
Marine Corps workstations on or after 31 August 2026. Effective 1 September
2026, personnel use MIAP to access MCTFS.

The advisory changes the access method only. Paragraph 5 leaves existing MCTFS
permissions, roles, and authorized personnel administrative procedures in place.

## Audience

- Commands responsible for personnel who need MCTFS access.
- S-1 and IPAC admin specialists (0102, 0111, 0170) who work in MCTFS daily.
- Every individual user with MCTFS access.
- Addressees who disseminate the advisory to subordinate commands (paragraph 1).

## Dates

- On or after 31 August 2026. Reflection Desktop is scheduled for disablement and removal.
- Before 1 September 2026. Commands confirm every MCTFS user has established and validated MIAP access.
- 1 September 2026. Personnel use MIAP to access MCTFS.

## Action language

- Paragraph 1 directs addressees to disseminate the advisory to subordinate commands.
- Paragraph 4.a requires commands to ensure all personnel who need MCTFS access have established and validated access to MIAP before 1 September 2026.
- Paragraph 4.b requires personnel to validate their ability to access MCTFS through MIAP before the local application is removed.
- Paragraph 4.c directs commands to begin transitioning personnel immediately and resolve access issues before 1 September 2026 to prevent interruption to personnel administrative operations.
- Paragraph 4.d sets 1 September 2026 as the date personnel use MIAP to access MCTFS.
- Paragraph 5 keeps MCTFS permissions, roles, and authorized procedures unchanged.
- Paragraph 6 reserves any change to the timeline or additional guidance for communication through appropriate channels.
- Paragraph 7 lists the DISA help line, 1-844-347-2457 (1-844-DISA-HLP), as the point of contact.

## Connection to Marine Corps policy

[PAA 06-10](/citations/paa-06-10) records the 2010 move of Mainframe 3270 access
to MIAP. PAA 01-26 is the current advisory on MIAP as the MCTFS access path.
The portal's MIAP (3270) entry on the [links](/links) page points at the DISA
portal.
