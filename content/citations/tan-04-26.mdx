## Scope

Travel Advisory Notice 04-26, DTG R 041700Z SEP 26. It tells the Marine Corps disbursing community about a change to PCS per diem for Marines executing a PCS to a new Permanent Duty Station (PDS). The change amends Joint Travel Regulations (JTR) Table 5-5, PCS Per Diem when Traveling by Airplane, Ship or Bus.

The notice states two things. First, effective 7 July 2026, lodging is authorized on the last day of travel to the new PDS, not to exceed the applicable locality rate, with 75 percent of the locality Meals and Incidental Expenses (M&IE) rate. The rule also covers accession members on PCS orders. Second, Temporary Lodging Expense (TLE) is the primary allowance for temporary lodging at the new PDS. Travel-item lodging is authorized only when too little time elapses to establish a standard TLE claim on arrival. When enough time elapses to cover the arrival day, the traveler files for TLE instead.

TAN 04-26 cancels TAN 01-26.
