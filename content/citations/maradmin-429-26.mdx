## Scope

MARADMIN 429/26, R 181625Z SEP 26, is Change 2 to MARADMIN 023/26 and MARADMIN 208/26. It extends FY27 SRB eligibility to PMOS 0315 Scout. Not applicable to the Marine Corps Reserve.

## Key paragraph map

- Para 3. E5 and below Marines with PMOS 0311, 0315, 0331, 0341, or 0352 who graduated the Basic Scout Course (M030J14 or M100J15, school code 0J1), or who hold AMOS 0317 Scout Sniper, convert to PMOS 0315 with no added MOS training.
- Para 3.a. PMOS 0315 bonus amounts. Zone A at $7,500 (E3), $8,000 (E4), $10,000 (E5). Zone B at $18,630 (E5).
- Para 4. PMOS 0315 is added to the 24-month and 36-month FMF Infantry kickers. All other kicker requirements stay.
- Para 6. HQMC suspends or terminates the program before 30 September 2027 when funds obligate faster than planned.

## Status

Active. Signed 22 September 2026. Release authorized by Lieutenant General William J. Bowers, Deputy Commandant for Manpower and Reserve Affairs.
