## Scope

MARADMIN 467/26 (R 292034Z SEP 26) lists the data management and artificial intelligence (AI) training resources open to Marines and government civilians. The end state is a baseline of basic AI awareness for all Marines, with advanced and specialized training for those who want deeper expertise. TECOM CDAO publishes this catalog update on a quarterly basis. Release authorized by Lieutenant General T. B. Savage, Deputy Commandant for Training and Education.

MARADMIN 472/26 amends paragraph 3e. The WarU address printed in 3e duplicated the JKO address. The correct WarU address is https://www.waru.edu/.

## Training catalog, paragraph 3

- 3a. MCeLE at https://www.mcele.usmc.mil. Basic AI Course (CDETBAIC01), mandatory for Active Duty and Reserve Marines NLT 31 December 2026 per ref (c). II MEF Basic Data Literacy Course (DATA0BAS01).
- 3b. IT Modernization Centers of Excellence, 2024 AI Community of Practice training series. 21 recorded sessions across technical, acquisitions, and leadership and policy tracks.
- 3c. Digital University, Workera resources. Request an account at https://digitalu.af.mil/app.
- 3d. Joint Knowledge Online (JKO) AI training page with an interactive dashboard.
- 3e. Warfighting Acquisition University (WarU). AI training tailored to the DoW workforce, basic concepts through testing applications. Address corrected by MARADMIN 472/26.
- 3f. GenAI.mil live virtual training events hosted by Google at https://genai.mil/events.
- 3g. Google Skills, asynchronous learning made available through GenAI.mil, at https://www.skills.google.
- 3h. Enterprise Information Services training on Power Platform, SharePoint, and Azure.
- 3i. War Data Platform (WDP) training, beginner to intermediate, self-paced by role plus a monthly virtual series.
- 3j. Microsoft Organizational Skilling at https://esi.microsoft.com. Requires linking a personal Microsoft account to the .mil account.
- 3k. Palantir training inside the Maven Smart System (MSS). Get MSS access per ref (d) first. The USMC MSS Training Hub sits on SIPR.
- 3l. Waypoints, the Department of the Navy learning and talent management system for the civilian workforce.
- 3m. Local command data and AI training programs.

## Academic institutions, paragraph 4

- 4a. US Navy Community College (USNCC). Certificates and associate degrees in AI and machine learning, data analytics, and cybersecurity.
- 4b. Naval Postgraduate School (NPS) distance learning. Degrees and certificates in modeling, visualization, and simulation, data science and decision making, and AI for military use.
