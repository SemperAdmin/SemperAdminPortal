## Scope

MARADMIN 459/26 modifies MCO 1754.4C. It removes the separate EFMP assignment review for OCONUS orders and for the designated remote CONUS locations.

## Key rules

- The separate EFMP OCONUS assignment review is eliminated, including the designated remote CONUS locations, Hawaii and Alaska. Paragraphs 1 and 2.a.
- EFMP-enrolled Marines start Overseas Suitability Screening on the standard timeline on receipt of orders. Paragraph 2.a.
- BUMED stays the determining authority for medical, dental and educational suitability. Paragraph 2.b.
- The HQMC Family Member Travel Screening Cell is the primary liaison with BUMED. Paragraph 3.
- The message cancels once incorporated into MCO 1754.4C. Paragraph 5.
