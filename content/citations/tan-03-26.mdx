## Scope

TAN 03-26 is a Travel Advisory Notice from HQMC Programs and Resources (RFF),
published 14 August 2026 to the disbursing community. Effective 11 July 2026,
it revokes the authority to reimburse Individual Mobilization Augmentee (IMA)
Inactive Duty Training (IDT) lodging. Lodging before 11 July 2026
stays authorized for payment and processes in the Defense Travel System (DTS).

## Status

The message carries a "DoW COMMUNITY ONLY" distribution and has no public
link. The portal summarizes paragraphs 2 and 3 only. Verify the current standing
with your IMA Operational Sponsor before you act.
