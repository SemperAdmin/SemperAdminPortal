## Scope

MARADMIN 472/26 (011515Z OCT 26) amends paragraph 3e of MARADMIN 467/26. Paragraph 3e as released printed the Joint Knowledge Online address for Warfighting Acquisition University (WarU). The corrected WarU address is https://www.waru.edu/.

WarU provides training on AI-related topics tailored to the DoW workforce, from basic concepts to testing applications.

All other instructions, details, and authorities in MARADMIN 467/26 and its references remain in effect. Release authorized by Lieutenant General T. B. Savage, Deputy Commandant for Training and Education.

## Status

Active. Read together with MARADMIN 467/26.
