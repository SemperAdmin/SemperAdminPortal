## Scope

MCO 5810.8 is titled "CRIMINAL JUSTICE INFORMATION (CJI) REPORTING REQUIREMENTS AND GUIDANCE." Paragraph 1.a states the purpose. CJI collected during investigations, and the resulting dispositions, reach the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI), which maintains databases including the National Instant Criminal Background Check System (NICS) used in the firearm purchase process. Paragraph 3 states the mission. All Marine Corps commands, law enforcement agencies (LEAs), and correctional facilities collect and report CJI to prevent the unlawful purchase or possession of firearms and ammunition. Paragraph 1.b.(3) adds a duty for individual Marines to report domestic violence convictions and qualifying restraining orders. Paragraph 6.a.(3) applies the order to the Marine Corps Total Force. The order carries the text "Department of War (DoW)" in paragraph 1.b.(2) and Appendix A, as written in the source.

- Paragraph 2. Cancellation. Prints "MCBul 5810 dtd May 2025, MARADMIN 209/04, and MARADMIN 186/03."
- Paragraph 3. Mission.
- Paragraph 4. Execution. Commander's intent and concept of operations (4.a). Tasks for ADC I&L (LF), CG TECOM, CG MCSC, COMMARFORRES, and battalion-level and above COs (4.b).
- Paragraph 5. Administration and logistics. Records management (5.a), Privacy Act (5.b), forms DD Form 2760 and DD Form 2873 (5.c), proponent ADC I&L (LF), MCICOM G-3/5 Law Enforcement Branch (5.e).
- Paragraph 6. Command and signal. UCMJ exposure for violations of Enclosure (5) (6.a.(1)). Effective the date signed (6.b).
- Appendix A. Glossary of acronyms. Appendix B. Glossary of terms, including Military Protection Order (B-12), Misdemeanor Crime of Domestic Violence (B-13), Restraining Order (B-15), and Unlawful User of or Addicted to Any Controlled Substance (B-16).

## Enclosure (2) - Guidance for Commanding Officers

- Paragraph 1.a. Seven categories of persons the Gun Control Act and implementing regulations prohibit from possessing a firearm or ammunition.
- Paragraph 1.b. Referral to a GCM. Temporary prohibition on receiving, shipping, or transporting. The service member keeps firearms already in possession until conviction.
- Paragraph 2. Reporting requirements. COs provide supporting documentation and direct the service member to report to the servicing LEA. 2.a domestic violence and restraining orders. 2.b wrongful drug use under Article 112a (12-month prohibition from adjudication). 2.c court-martial triggers. 2.d return from deserter status.
- Paragraph 3. Timing and method. 3.a written notice to the servicing LEA and/or correctional facility within five working days of disposition. 3.b units not aboard an installation with a servicing LEA email Marine Corps CID at usmccid@ncis.navy.mil with the subject "REQUEST FOR NICS ENTRY" within five working days.
- Paragraph 4. Deployed conduct. Direct the service member to the servicing LEA as soon as possible after redeployment.
- Paragraph 5. Weapons access. 5.a no access to privately owned firearms in unit armories. 5.b government use exception, which excludes domestic violence convictions.
- Paragraph 6. Disposition of personally owned firearms and ammunition. Written counseling after consulting the servicing SJA (6.a), temporary storage option (6.a.(2)), PMO escort (6.b).
- Paragraph 7. Mandatory self-reporting. DD Form 2760 for domestic violence convictions (7.a). Restraining order copy (7.b).
- Paragraph 8. Consult the SJA before disciplinary action against a service member who self-reported.
