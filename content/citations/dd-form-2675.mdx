## What this form is

DD Form 2675 is the claim form for adoption expense reimbursement under 10 U.S.C. 1052 and DOWI 1341.09. Complete one form for each adopted child.

## How the Marine Corps routes it

Under MARADMIN 501/26, the Marine submits the form and supporting documents through the chain of command to the servicing Personnel Administrative Office. The PAO verifies eligibility and forwards the package with a NAVMC 11116 to the disbursing or finance office in DTMS.
