## Scope

MARADMIN 518/26 changes the NMOS 6053 CH-53K Maintenance Specialist entry in the FY 2027 MOS Manual, NAVMC 1200.1M. The change folds into the FY 2028 manual.

## Key rules

- OJT no longer counts as a pathway to NMOS 6053. Paragraph 1.
- Award follows the requisite transition course, the final course of a CH-53K initial accession pipeline, or other training approved by DCA and DC T&E. Paragraphs 2 and 3.a.
- Transition and pipeline course IDs by prerequisite PMOS sit in paragraph 3.c.
