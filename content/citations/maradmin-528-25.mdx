## Scope

MARADMIN 528/25 establishes the Fiscal Year 2026 Judge Advocate Continuation Pay program, the retention pay for Marine judge advocates. The message carries the program's eligibility requirements, payment provisions, and application procedures for the fiscal year.

## Audience

- Judge advocates eligible for FY26 continuation pay.
- MMOA monitors and S-1 personnel routing applications.
- HQMC Judge Advocate Division and MPP-30 program managers.

## Action items

- The application window originally set by this message extends through 2026-09-30 under MARADMIN 323/26. Apply against the extended deadline.
- Eligibility requirements and all other provisions remain in effect as published here.

## Connection to Marine Corps policy

- MARADMIN 323/26. Extends this program's application window through 2026-09-30, all other provisions unchanged.
- MCO 5800.16 LSAM. The legal community structure the program retains officers inside.
- DoD 7000.14-R DoDFMR Volume 7A Chapter 3. Officer special pay framework continuation pays ride under.
