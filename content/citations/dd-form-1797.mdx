## What this form is

Form 1797, Personal Property Counseling Checklist. Fillable PDF on file from the 2026-04-29 forms archive snapshot. The form is the counseling checklist for household goods (HHG), unaccompanied baggage (UB), non-temporary storage (NTS), weapons, privately owned vehicles (POV), boats, and liability and claims.

## Purpose printed on the form

> To ensure the member, dependent, and government employee has been briefed properly on the movement of their personal property within the Defense Transportation System (DTS). Information collected may also be used in determining validity of claims, improper shipments and any third party responsibility.

Quoted from the form's Privacy Act statement.

## Who prepares, signs, and files

The Personal Property Shipping Office (PPSO) counsels the Marine during the session this form records. The counselor and the customer both sign the form to acknowledge the property counseling. S-1 supports the documentation alongside the DD Form 1299 shipment application.

## When the form triggers

The form applies to personal property moves under PCS orders. Counseling follows the form parts. Part I covers HHG, Part II UB, Part III NTS, Part IV weapons, Part V POV, Part VI boats, Part VII liability, claims, and protection, and Part VIII general instructions.

## Connection to Marine Corps policy

The portal anchors PPM and household goods guidance to JTR Chapter 5 paragraph 0570, DTR 4500.9-R Volume 4, and MCO 4050.6, the Marine Corps Personal Property Movement Manual, with the DD Form 1299 and DD Form 1797 pair as the documentation framework. Move.mil now fronts most HHG transactions, and the counseling record documents the briefing the Marine received before the move. The portal covers the workflow on the Household Goods and PPM page and the PCS Orders and Overview page.
