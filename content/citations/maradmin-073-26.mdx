## Scope

MARADMIN 073/26, R 271330Z FEB 26, is Change 2 to MARADMIN 613/25. It rewrites the first two sentences of MARADMIN 066/26 paragraph 6.

## Key paragraph map

- Para 1. The Marine Corps WHtR standard is less than or equal to 0.52, regardless of sex.
- Para 2. All other instructions, details, and authorities in MARADMIN 066/26 remain in effect.

## Effect

A Marine with a waist-to-height ratio of exactly 0.52 meets the standard. Under the MARADMIN 066/26 wording, the same Marine exceeded it and moved to a body fat evaluation.

Active. Signed 27 February 2026. Release authorized by Lieutenant General Benjamin T. Watson, Deputy Commandant for Training and Education.
