## Scope

MARADMIN 225/26, R 151350Z MAY 26, affirms the annual Command Climate Assessment requirement and gives 2026 DEOCS administration guidance. It applies to the Marine Corps Total Force.

## Key paragraph map

- Para 1. All O-5 and O-6 slated commanders, Commanding Generals, and Deputy Commandants with 50 or more personnel conduct the annual DEOCS between 1 August and 30 November. Registration for the 2026 DEOCS opens 31 July 2026. Administration starts no later than 31 October 2026.
- Para 2. Commanders give all military and civilian personnel dedicated time during working hours to take the survey.
- Para 3. Commanders designate a survey administrator who completes JKO course Prevention 004, How to Conduct a Command Climate Assessment and Administer the DEOCS (5 hours), before administering. Integrated Primary Prevention Workforce personnel at levels 3 to 5 do not serve as administrators.
- Para 4. Survey resources at the Defense Climate Portal Survey Resource Center on prevention.mil.

## Status

Active for the 2026 survey cycle. Signed 18 May 2026. Release authorized by Brigadier General Lauren S. Edwards, Director, Manpower Plans and Policy Division, Manpower and Reserve Affairs. MARADMIN 534/23 remains the standing CCA guidance.
