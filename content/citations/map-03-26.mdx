## Scope

The Update to Document (UTD) for Military Advisory Panel item 03-26(R), dated 7 July 2026. The Chair of the Per Diem, Travel, and Transportation Allowance Committee approved the item for publication in the Joint Travel Regulations (JTR). The changes appear in the JTR dated 1 August 2026.

The item adds row 7 to JTR Table 5-5. When a Service member travels to the new or first PDS and lodging is required the day travel ends, per diem is the lesser of the actual lodging cost or the applicable lodging rate, plus 75 percent of the applicable M&IE rate. The item aligns Service member PCS allowances with civilian PCS allowances.

## Effective date

The memo states the revision is effective 6 July 2026, the day the Chair approved it. This entry carries the memo date. TAN 04-26, the Marine Corps disbursing notice, states 7 July 2026, and the portal pages quote the TAN date. The JTR change carries the memo date. For travel ending on 6 July, confirm the entitlement with the finance office. The memo titles Table 5-5 with airplane, ship, bus, or train. The TAN title omits train. The portal follows the memo.
