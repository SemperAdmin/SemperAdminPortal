## Scope

MARADMIN 228/06 announced the Marine Corps Active Duty Career Retention and Permanent Limited Duty (PLD) Policy for combat wounded Marines. MARADMIN 457/16 cancelled the 2006 policy, paragraph 2. MARADMIN 457/16 outlines the requirements for retention on Expanded Permanent Limited Duty (EPLD) status.

## Audience

- Combat wounded Marines. Were eligible to request PLD status to remain on active duty. Read the EPLD requirements in MARADMIN 457/16 for current rules.
- Wounded Warrior Regiment. Coordinated PLD applications and case management under the 2006 policy.
- Medical Evaluation Boards (MEBs). Adjudicate medical fitness for continued service.
- HQMC Manpower Management Separations and Retirements Branch. Adjudicated PLD applications under the 2006 policy. MARADMIN 457/16 now sets the EPLD requirements.

## Connection to Marine Corps doctrine

- SECNAVINST 1850.4. DON Disability Evaluation Manual.
- MCO 1740.18. Wounded Warrior Regiment Order.

## Status

Released 2006-05. Originator CMC Washington DC MRA. Applicability was combat wounded Marines on active duty. Cancelled by MARADMIN 457/16.
