## Scope

CNAF M-3710.7 prescribes general flight and operating instructions and procedures for the operation of all naval aircraft and related activities. The manual complements the platform NATOPS flight manuals. Marine aviation operates under it alongside Navy aviation. Special instructions and waivers issue from COMNAVAIRFOR or CMC as applicable.

## Audience

- Commanders of Marine aviation reporting custodians. Own NATOPS program execution in the unit.
- NATOPS officers and aviation operations staff running evaluations, qualifications, and flight records.
- Aircrew and enlisted crewmembers subject to flight and qualification requirements.
- IGMC inspectors working Functional Area 3710.7.

## Program assignments

Paragraph 2.2.1 of the manual assigns the NATOPS program roles.

- CNO N98. Overall NATOPS program sponsor.
- COMNAVAIRFOR. Overall management of the NATOPS program. COMNAVAIRFOR N455 serves as program administrator for daily administration of NATOPS policy.
- COMNAVAIRSYSCOM. Administration and maintenance of NATOPS publications through its Airworthiness and CYBERSAFE Office.

## IGMC functional area

The IGMC NATOPS checklist, FA 3710.7, revised 8 June 2026, applies to aviation reporting custodians as defined by COMNAVAIRFOR Manual 3710.7. The checklist cites this manual 26 times across its 38 questions, beside MCO 3710.8, MCO 5100.29C Volume 4, NAVMC 3500.14F, and the platform NATOPS flight manuals.
