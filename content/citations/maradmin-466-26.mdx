## Scope

MARADMIN 466/26 carries immediate implementation guidance for Military Equal Opportunity program reform, pending revision of MCO 5354.1G. It replaces MARADMIN 380/26, which CANX-014/26 cancelled. It sets the confidential complaint option, stops MEO processing of anonymous allegations, directs conflict resolution before a complaint, gives commanders eight dismissal grounds, sets five deadlines, opens an appeal to the next higher commander holding General Court-Martial Convening Authority, and updates the definitions of harassment, bullying, and hazing. Applies to Marines and Sailors.

## Audience

- Marines and Sailors weighing a confidential complaint against an informal or formal complaint.
- Commanders holding the dismissal decision and the favorable personnel action restart.
- General Court-Martial Convening Authorities receiving dismissal appeals.
- Equal Opportunity Advisors processing confidential complaints and forwarding formal complaints.
- Staff judge advocates consulted before any dismissal.
- S-1 staff tracking complaint packages, written notifications, and delayed favorable actions.

## Three reporting lanes

- Confidential complaint. Paragraph 2. The Marine or Sailor receives services and discloses personally identifiable information to an EOA without opening an informal or formal complaint on NAVMC 11512 or an MPE approved form. EOAs are the only personnel authorized to process a confidential complaint, paragraph 2.b.1. The complaint stays open up to 60 calendar days, converts to an informal or formal complaint at any time in the window, and closes on day 61 if not converted, paragraphs 2.b.2 and 2.b.3.
- Confidential complaint exceptions. Paragraph 2.c. No command notification, inquiry, or investigation follows unless the complainant waives confidentiality in writing, the complaint reveals a clear and present risk to the health or safety of any person, the complaint reveals intent to commit a future crime or fraud, federal law, state law, or Department of War policy requires disclosure, or a military judge or federal court orders disclosure.
- Anonymous allegation. Paragraph 3. Anonymous allegations of prohibited discrimination or discriminatory harassment produce no MEO complaint. Commanders still take appropriate action on the information. Anonymous complaints of harassment, hazing, bullying, and sexual harassment follow MEO policy under ALNAV 048/26.
- Conflict resolution. Paragraph 4. Offered before an informal or formal complaint where feasible, voluntary for both parties. Opting out or a failed resolution starts or resumes the MEO complaint process before any command inquiry or investigation completes.

## The five deadlines

| Action | Clock | Owner | Paragraph |
|--------|-------|-------|-----------|
| Forward a formal complaint to the commander | 5 calendar days from receipt | EOA | 5.b |
| Determine whether to dismiss | 30 calendar days from receipt | Commander | 5.c |
| Notify the complainant in writing of a dismissal | 7 calendar days from the determination | Commander | 5.d |
| Appeal a dismissal to the next GCMCA | 5 calendar days from the written determination | Complainant | 6.a |
| Resume or complete a delayed favorable personnel action | 7 calendar days from dismissal or an unsubstantiated finding | Commander | 7 |

## Eight dismissal grounds, paragraph 5.a

The commander consults the command Equal Opportunity Advisor and the staff judge advocate before dismissing a formal complaint without investigation.

1. Untimely filing. More than 60 calendar days after the most recent incident, 120 calendar days for a Reserve member.
2. The allegation falls outside the scope of MEO policy.
3. The complaint lacks sufficient or actionable information.
4. Conflict resolution or other informal means resolved the matter.
5. The complainant requests withdrawal.
6. The complainant fails to respond to requests for information.
7. The complaint duplicates another.
8. The complaint constitutes a false official statement under Article 107, UCMJ.

## Changes from MARADMIN 380/26

- The confidential option now carries a 60-day window and a day 61 closure.
- EOAs are the only authorized processors, not interim recipients.
- The disclosure exceptions add written waiver, state law, and a court order, and replace the imminent serious bodily harm test with a clear and present risk to health or safety.
- Anonymous harassment complaints join hazing, bullying, and sexual harassment under ALNAV 048/26 routing.
