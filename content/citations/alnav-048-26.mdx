## Scope

ALNAV 048/26 carries the Department of the Navy implementation guidance for Military Equal Opportunity reform, released 2026-08-07. MARADMIN 380/26 implements it for the Marine Corps and cites it as reference F. Provisions cover confidential reporting, anonymous complaints, conflict resolution, dismissal of formal complaints, and the definitions of harassment, bullying, and hazing.

This is the first ALNAV record in the portal citation registry.

## Audience

- Commanders and Equal Opportunity Advisors across the Department of the Navy.
- Marine Corps commands reading MARADMIN 380/26 against its parent message.
- S-1 staff tracing a Marine Corps requirement back to the Navy source.

## Operative content

- Confidential reporting. A service member discloses identifying information and allegations to authorized personnel without filing a complaint. Exceptions cover a specific and imminent threat of serious bodily harm or death and disclosed intent to commit a future crime or fraud.
- Anonymous allegations of discrimination and discriminatory harassment stop being processed as MEO complaints. Commanders still act on the information. Anonymous complaints of sexual harassment, hazing, and bullying stay under existing routing pending further review.
- Conflict resolution offered where feasible before escalation. The MEO complaint process resumes when the complainant opts out or the resolution fails.
- Dismissal grounds for formal complaints, including filing more than 60 calendar days after the most recent incident, 120 calendar days for a Reserve member.
- Harassment, bullying, and hazing definitions align to the Secretary of War memoranda of 30 September 2025.
- Implementation status reporting begins 30 September 2026 and repeats every 60 days.

## Reading it against MARADMIN 380/26

Section numbering differs between the two messages. The ALNAV covers the same provisions under its own section numbers. Cite MARADMIN 380/26 for Marine Corps action and this message for the Department of the Navy frame. Verify a section number against the PDF before quoting it.

Paragraph 3.c of MARADMIN 380/26 routes anonymous sexual harassment, hazing, and bullying complaints under this message. This one line makes the ALNAV operative for Marine Corps practice rather than background.
