## Scope

MARADMIN 541/26 directs the mandatory transition from the manual DD Form 2875 System Authorization Access Request (SAAR) to the Naval Identity Services (NIS) automated SAAR workflow. It aligns the Marine Corps with ALNAV 037/26 and the DoW CIO memo of 19 December 2025 on modernizing SAAR and account provisioning through ICAM workflows.

## Audience

- Marine Corps Functional Area Managers, Program Managers, and System Owners for NIPRNET systems. The message binds them, paragraph 3.
- S-1 and IPAC staff, who keep using the access path each system owner publishes until the system moves.
- Commanders and supervisors approving access requests.

## Action items

- Transition to the NIS automated SAAR workflow and stop using manual DD Form 2875 processes by 30 September 2027, the DON enterprise deadline in ALNAV 037/26. Paragraph 3.a.
- Complete NIS integration for ICAM provisioning on the NIS Program Office milestones scheduled for each system. Paragraph 3.b.

## Background

- Reference A, a CMC DC I IC4 AMHS message of 15 May 2026, directs all Marine Corps Cyberspace Environment IT systems to converge on the DON ICAM solution. Paragraph 2.
- ALNAV 037/26 directs DON activities to automated access authorization through NIS and retirement of manual DD Form 2875 processes by 30 September 2027. Paragraph 2.
- The DoW CIO memo makes programs responsible for transition milestones and downstream provisioning integration in their own systems. Paragraph 2.

## What it does not change

- No end-user steps. The message sets no new submission path for a Marine or a clerk.
- No change to TASO appointment or duties.
- No system-by-system dates. Each system moves on its own NIS milestone.

## Connection to higher policy

- ALNAV 037/26. DON mandate for NIS automated SAAR.
- DoW CIO memo, Modernizing SAAR and Account Provisioning Through ICAM Workflows, 19 December 2025.
- DD Form 2875. The manual SAAR being retired.

## Connection to Marine Corps doctrine

- PAA 02-26. Moved MCTFS end-user access from AutoSAAR to DoD E-ICAM on 21 September 2026.
- MCO 5239.2B. Marine Corps Cybersecurity Program.
