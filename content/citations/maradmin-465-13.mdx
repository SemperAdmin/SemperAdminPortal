## Scope

MARADMIN 465/13 directs the mandatory use of myPay to create an alternate Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) account for Marines. The alternate EFT account covers travel payments only. It lets a Marine deposit travel payments to an account different from the regular military pay EFT account. Marines set it in myPay only, and disbursing officers no longer accept paper alternate EFT forms for travel payments. Applicability is the Marine Corps Total Force.

## Audience

- Marines on Active Duty. Create the alternate travel EFT account through myPay.
- Disbursing officers and pay administrators. Track compliance through MCTFS.

## Action items

1. Log into myPay at https://mypay.dfas.mil.
2. Establish an alternate EFT account.
3. Update the routing and account information as needed.
