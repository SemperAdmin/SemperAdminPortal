## Scope

DoDI 1340.27, reissued 17 August 2022, sets DoD policy and procedures for the Foreign Language Proficiency Bonus and the Senior Reserve Officers' Training Corps skill proficiency bonus under 37 U.S.C. 353(b). Each Service implements it through its own order. The Marine Corps order is MCO 7220.52G.

## Marine Corps use

MARADMIN 480/26 cites this instruction for the FY27 program, the $12,000 annual cap, the Category 5 lump-sum bonus, and the exception permitting dual-modality OPI scores for PMOS 0370 and 0372.
