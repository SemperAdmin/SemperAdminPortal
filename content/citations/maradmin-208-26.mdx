## Scope

MARADMIN 208/26, 051200Z MAY 26, is Change 1 to MARADMIN 023/26. It revises PMOS bonus amounts and lateral move eligibility for Marines with an ECC from 1 October 2026 to 30 September 2027. All other FY27 SRB authorities in MARADMIN 023/26 stay in effect. Not applicable to the Marine Corps Reserve.

## Key paragraph map

- Para 3.a. Revised PMOS bonus amounts on reenlistment.
  - Zone A. 2141 at $12,000 (E3), $15,000 (E4), $15,750 (E5 and above). 1721 at $53,500, $55,000, $57,750. 6114 at $21,500, $23,000, $23,500.
  - Zone B. 0871 at $45,000 (E6 and above). 1721 at $51,000 (E5), $53,000 (E6 and above). 2141 at $15,000, $17,000. 2629 at $53,000 (E6 and above). 2631 at $15,000 (E5). 2862 at $36,000 (E6 and above).
  - Zone C. 0871 at $27,500 (E6), $30,000 (E7 and above). 1721 at $51,300, $53,000. 2141 at $15,000, $17,000. 2629 at $51,300, $53,000. 2862 at $37,450, $40,200. 3044 at $24,300, $37,000. 5821 at $28,750, $30,250.
- Para 3.b. Marines reenlisting for a lateral move into 1721 no longer rate the 84-month LM kicker. The Zone A PMOS bonus for a lateral move into 1721 is discontinued. All other LM bonuses stay.
- Para 5. HQMC suspends or terminates the program before 30 September 2027 when funds obligate faster than planned.

## Status

Active. Signed 5 May 2026. Release authorized by Lieutenant General William J. Bowers, Deputy Commandant for Manpower and Reserve Affairs. Further changed by MARADMIN 429/26.
