## Scope

MARADMIN 189/26 directs the identification, processing, and involuntary separation of Marines who have a current diagnosis or history of, or exhibit symptoms consistent with, gender dysphoria and who did not request voluntary discharge under ALNAV 038/25. It implements ALNAV 011/26 and the SECNAV and OUSW(P&R) memoranda it cites. Applies to the Total Force.

## Audience

- Marines subject to the policy, who hold board, counsel, waiver, and retirement rights.
- Commanders, who identify, refer, place Marines on administrative absence, and report.
- S-1 and IPAC staff, who run the MOL, MCTFS, unit diary, and pay actions.
- MPO, the CDA, and MMSR-2, who route and decide.

## Key provisions

- Policy. Marines adhere to standards of their biological sex, DEERS and every system of record reflect sex accurately, and every exception to policy allowing a different standard is revoked. Paragraph 1.a.
- Identification. Through the Periodic Health Assessment, or commander's awareness limited to four listed sources, with any other facts going to the first GO for a case-by-case decision. Paragraph 2.
- Medical verification. Commanders contact the BUMED Deployability Action Cell and notify MPO within 24 hours. Paragraph 2.c.
- Referral. Naval letter with name, command, EDIPI, and special pay or bonus status with a recoupment recommendation, titled with initials and the last five digits of the EDIPI, saved as PDF, routed to MPO then the CDA. Paragraph 3.
- Administrative absence. Duty limitation code E, ADMIN, NON-DEPLOY. Full pay and benefits, no bonus, special, or incentive pays. SMCR drills excused without drill pay or points. Paragraph 4.
- Authorities. Commander, Navy Installations Command is the CDA. SECNAV is the separation authority. Paragraph 5.a.
- Rights. Notification within 30 days of referral, a board regardless of time in service, 10 calendar days to elect, a minimum 30 days from notification to board. Paragraph 5.b and 5.e.
- SPD codes. JDK2 for officers, JFF8 for enlisted. Paragraph 5.c.
- Characterization. Honorable when gender dysphoria is the only basis. Paragraph 5.f.2.
- Retention waiver. 15 calendar days to submit to MPO, three eligibility criteria, first GO endorsement on compelling government interest, SECNAV sole approval. Paragraph 6.
- Separation terms. RE-3G, service obligation waived, involuntary separation pay eligibility, bonus termination and possible recoupment, transition benefits except SkillBridge. Paragraph 7.
- Retirement. FMCR or retirement request within 30 days of identification, TERA for 18 to 20 years, TERA exception requests for 15 to 18 years. Paragraph 8.
- DES and other separations. DES runs concurrently and takes precedence. Other administrative separation processing takes precedence, with this processing held in abeyance. Paragraph 9.
- Reporting. MCTFS strength category X, duty status C. Twice-monthly report to MPO through the MSC. Paragraphs 10 and 12.c.
- Commander duties. Dignity and respect, PHI privacy, pre-separation checklist. Paragraph 12.

## Changes

- MARADMIN 539/26 removes paragraph 11, Eligibility for Extension, effective 2026-10-28. No extension requests for separation processing. A Marine reaching EAS before proceedings complete separates on EAS.
