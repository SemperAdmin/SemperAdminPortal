## Scope

MARADMIN 502/26, R 161529Z OCT 26, ends the monthly Active-Duty Corporal and Sergeant promotion authority MARADMIN. It sets annual FY27 guidance for promotions to Cpl and Sgt in the Active Component.

## Key paragraph map

- Para 1. No monthly NCO promotion authority MARADMIN will be released. This message supersedes the monthly publication. Commands reference MCO P1400.32D W/CH 2 when creating promotion warrants.
- Para 2. MCO P1400.32D W/CH 2 is the promotion authority for all Active-Duty LCpls and Cpls promoting to Cpl and Sgt, effective with 1 October 2026 promotions.
- Para 3. Cutting scores post in MCTFS after each month's select grades post. Commands keep each month's cutting scores for future reference. Scores also post on the Enlisted Promotions website.
- Para 4. Viewing cutoff scores in MCTFS, time in grade and time in service, PME, commander responsibilities, and the junior remedial process sit on the Enlisted Promotions website under Sergeants and Below Promotions.
- Para 5.a. FY27 promotion effective dates and record cutoff dates.
- Para 5.b. Promotions take effect on the first day of the month, with date of rank equal to the effective date. The JEPES score for consideration comes from the record cutoff date.
- Para 6. Enlisted promotion contacts.
