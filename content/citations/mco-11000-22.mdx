## Scope

MCO 11000.22, MARINE CORPS BACHELOR AND FAMILY HOUSING MANAGEMENT, establishes Marine Corps policy and prescribes requirements governing the management and administration of the Marine Corps Bachelor and Family Housing enterprise. The portal files it under Unaccompanied Housing because commander and admin surfaces reach it for UH duties. The "MCO 11000.1" alias resolves to this entry so historical references in commander and admin pages continue to render a chip.

> This Order establishes Marine Corps policy and prescribes requirements governing the management and administration of the Marine Corps Bachelor and Family Housing enterprise. Recent investments in Family and Bachelor Housing infrastructure have significantly improved the quality of life for Marines and their families. [...]

Quoted from the document's opening section.

## Audience

- Commanders who establish unaccompanied housing management policies. The Installation Commander approves BAH without dependents. CNA numbers come from installation transient housing for TAD travelers per Chapter 11 paragraph 6.
- Installation housing offices under MCICOM (GF), the sponsor of both housing functional areas.
- S-1 and PAC personnel processing BAH without dependents transactions.
- IGMC inspectors working the Unaccompanied Housing and Family Housing functional area checklists.

## Content scope

Repo evidence names no full chapter map for the order. The BAH without dependents rule sits in Enclosure 2 Chapter 10 paragraphs 7c to 7e. Older inspection cites to paragraphs 2600.2 and 2600.3 of MCO P11000.22 W CH 1-6, the Marine Corps Housing Management Manual sharing the same number, point to a cancelled manual. MCO 11000.22 cancelled it.

## Operative content

- Commanders establish UH management policies, per the commander oversight matrix summarizing the IGMC functional area checklist. The Installation Commander approves BAH without dependents. Installation transient housing issues CNA numbers for TAD travelers per Chapter 11 paragraph 6.
- The Installation Commander approves BAH without dependents for non-availability only at 95 percent installation occupancy, or for a paragraph 7d or 7e exception. E-6 and above electing off base need no approval. See Enclosure 2 Chapter 10 paragraphs 7c to 7e, with DoD FMR Volume 7A Chapter 26.

## Connection to higher policy

- DoD 7000.14-R Financial Management Regulation Volume 7A, Chapter 26. The BAH entitlement rules behind the approval requirement.

## Connection to Marine Corps doctrine and lower policy

- MCO 11000.1. The unaccompanied housing order named in the IGMC registry as functional area 11000.1, Unaccompanied Housing, CoRE, sponsor MCICOM (GF), effective 2024-03-06.
- Family Housing functional area 11000.22. Non-CoRE, sponsor MCICOM (GF), effective 2024-03-19.
- MCO P11000.22 Marine Corps Housing Management Manual. The older manual with the same number, CH 4 announced by MARADMIN 302/04 on 2004-07-09. MCO 11000.22 paragraph 2 cancelled it. MCAAT milpay items still cite it.
- MARADMINs 429/11 and 015/20. Cited beside the BAH without dependents approval items.
- MCTFSPRIUM Chapter 8 Section 2 and Chapter 13 Appendix C. The reporting side of BAH transactions.
