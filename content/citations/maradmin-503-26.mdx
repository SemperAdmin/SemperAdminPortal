## Scope

MARADMIN 503/26, R 161530Z OCT 26, ends the Reserve Component Corporal and Sergeant promotion authority MARADMIN. It sets annual FY27 guidance for promotions to Cpl and Sgt in the Marine Corps Reserve, covering the Active Reserve (AR), the Selected Marine Corps Reserve (SMCR), and the Individual Ready Reserve (IRR). MARADMIN 502/26 is the Active Component companion.

## Key paragraph map

- Para 1. The NCO promotion authority MARADMIN will no longer be released. This message supersedes it. Commands use MCO P1400.32D W/CH 2 as the reference when creating promotion warrants.
- Para 2. MCO P1400.32D W/CH 2 is the promotion authority for all USMCR LCpls and Cpls, AR, SMCR, and IRR, effective with 1 October 2026 promotions.
- Para 3. Cutting scores post in MCTFS after each month's select grades post. Commands keep each month's cutting scores for future reference. Scores also post on the Enlisted Promotions website.
- Para 4. Viewing cutoff scores in MCTFS, time in grade and time in service, PME, commander responsibilities, and the junior remedial process sit on the Enlisted Promotions website under Sergeants and Below Promotions.
- Para 5.a. FY27 quarterly promotion effective dates and record cutoff dates.
- Para 5.b. USMCR promotions take effect quarterly on the first day of October, January, April, and July. Date of rank equals the effective date. The JEPES score for consideration comes from the record cutoff date.
- Para 6. Enlisted promotion contacts.
