## Scope

ALNAV 056/26 sets the Secretary of the Navy Special Leave Accrual designations for Fiscal Year 2026. DTG R 141700Z SEP 26. MARADMIN 545/26 implements it for the Marine Corps and cites it as reference B.

## Audience

- Marine Corps commands reading MARADMIN 545/26 against its parent message.
- S-1 staff tracing an SLA requirement back to the Department of the Navy source.

## Operative content

- Designates Operation EPIC FURY as an operational mission for SLA. Eligibility follows DoWI 1327.06 paragraph 3.8.d.
- Designates the deployable ships and mobile units qualifying for FY26 SLA, under DoWI 1327.06 paragraph 3.8.c.
- Delegates to CMC the authority to define and designate deployable ships and mobile units for SLA. MARADMIN 545/26 paragraph 2.b records the further delegation to Dir, MP, M&RA, in effect until further notice.

## Connection to higher policy

- DoWI 1327.06 incorporating Change 1 of 30 June 2026, paragraph 3.8.
- Title 37 USC 501.

## Connection to Marine Corps doctrine

- MARADMIN 545/26. The Marine Corps implementation.
- MARADMIN 341/26. The suspension pending these designations.
