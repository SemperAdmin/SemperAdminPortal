## Scope

10 U.S.C. 7837 gives the Department of the Navy authority to remit or cancel a debt a military member owes. The authority covers debts from overpayments of active duty pay. It does not cover overpayments of military retired pay.

## Operative content

- The authority reaches any debt incurred on or after 7 October 2001.
- The Service processes the request. DFAS does not process remission requests.
- DoD civilian employees are not eligible.

## Implementation

DoD FMR Volume 16 Chapter 4 paragraphs 9.1 and 9.2 describe the authority and the request route.
