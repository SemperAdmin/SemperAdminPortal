## Scope

15 U.S.C. 9401 holds the definitions for the National Artificial Intelligence Initiative. Paragraph (3) defines artificial intelligence. Executive Order 14434 and MARADMIN 521/26 tie the term Super Intelligence (SI) to the technologies inside this statutory definition.

## Status

Active. The statutory text keeps its published wording. MARADMIN 521/26 paragraph 5.b requires preserving statutory wording when quoting it.
