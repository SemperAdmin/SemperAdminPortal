## Scope

DODI 1350.02 carries the DoD Military Equal Opportunity Program policy. The 2026-03-11 issuance is the DoD-level frame the Marine Corps MEO program aligns to under MARADMIN 357/26, which realigned the service program's terminology from Prohibited Activities and Conduct to Military Equal Opportunity.

## Audience

- Commanders owning the command MEO program and climate.
- Equal Opportunity Advisors and Coordinators executing it.

## Connection to Marine Corps doctrine and lower policy

- MARADMIN 357/26. The terminology realignment aligning the Marine Corps program to this instruction.
- MCO 5354.1G. The Marine Corps prevention and response order carrying the program.
- DODI 1020.03. The companion DoD harassment prevention and response policy.
