## Scope

MARADMIN 539/26 removes paragraph 11, Eligibility for Extension, from MARADMIN 189/26 and rescinds the authority to grant service extensions to Marines undergoing involuntary administrative separation processing under MARADMIN 189/26. Applies to the Marine Corps Total Force.

## Action items

- Effective immediately, command elements do not submit, endorse, or approve extension of service requests, including the NAVMC 10274, to allow separation processing under MARADMIN 189/26. Paragraph 3.a.
- Marines in MARADMIN 189/26 processing who reach EAS before proceedings complete separate on their scheduled EAS. Paragraph 3.b.
- Commanders keep advising affected Marines of mental health, financial, and separation resources and keep positive engagement. Paragraph 4.
- Military OneSource stays available 24/7 at 1-800-342-9647. Paragraph 5.

## Limits

- The message covers only separation processing under MARADMIN 189/26. Extension rules for other separation bases under MCO 1900.16 are unchanged.
- The message does not state how the separation basis, SPD code, or involuntary separation pay apply when EAS arrives before proceedings complete. Direct those questions to MPO.

## Connection to Marine Corps doctrine

- MARADMIN 189/26. The policy this message changes.
