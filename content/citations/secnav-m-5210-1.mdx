## Scope

SECNAV M-5210.1, the Department of the Navy Records Management Manual, sets policy and procedure for the life cycle of every DON record, creation, maintenance, use, and disposition. The manual implements SECNAVINST 5210.8D and operates under SECNAVINST 5430.7N. Part III points to the Department of the Navy Directives and Records Management Division (DRMD) portal, which holds the approved DON retention schedules. When a Marine Corps page asks how long a record lives and what happens to it afterward, the answer traces here.

## Audience

- Records officers and records-management leads at every DON activity.
- S-1 and IPAC personnel building file plans and executing disposition.
- Commanders accountable for command records through inspection cycles.
- Legal clerks applying court-martial and NJP retention rules.

## Operative content

- Retention standards carry the schedule numbers NARA approved and sit in 13 subject chapters, Part II paragraph 1.f. Each schedule sets a disposition action, destruction or transfer to NARA. Use the Schedules Cross Walked by Chapter map to go from an SSIC to its disposition.
- Electronic records carry the same retention and disposition rules as paper. Command-sponsored electronic reports systems register in DITPR-DON under SECNAV Note 5210 so the records inside stay managed.
- Part 1 paragraph 16.b.(e) carries the Capstone email management rules for senior officials, an IGMC checklist item under functional area 5210.
- Retention periods sit in the approved schedules, not in the manual body. Part III paragraph 2 sends the reader to the DRMD portal. Look up court-martial, administrative discharge, OMPF, DD-214, and award record retention there. DD Form 577 appointment records stay at the command a minimum of 10 years per MCTFSPRIUM 20104. Unit Punishment Book, three full years.

## Part map

- Part I. Program policy and responsibilities, including electronic records and email management.
- Part III. A pointer to the DRMD portal, where the approved DON retention schedules live.

## Connection to higher policy

- SECNAVINST 5210.8D. The DON records management policy the manual implements.
