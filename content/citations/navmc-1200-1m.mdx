## Scope

NAVMC 1200.1M is the Military Occupational Specialties Manual. It lists every Marine Corps MOS with its prerequisites, requirements, and related standards. Career planners, monitors, and S-1 staff use it to confirm PMOS, AMOS, and lateral move eligibility.

## Status

Current on the MCPEL. Published 2 March 2026. Supersedes NAVMC 1200.1L. MARADMINs released before March 2026 cite NAVMC 1200.1K or 1200.1L. Apply 1200.1M for current MOS requirements.
