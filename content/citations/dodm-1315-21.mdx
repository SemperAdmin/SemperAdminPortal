## Scope

DoDM 1315.21 Volume 2 sets Department procedures for military personnel assignments outside the continental United States, including OCONUS tour lengths, dependent travel, and in-place consecutive overseas tours (IPCOT). The issuance carries the DOWM designation following the 2026 Department of War renaming. Earlier references to the same volume use the DoDM form. Both forms resolve to this entry.

## How the portal uses it

- MARADMIN 450/26 cites this volume as reference (a), dated 21 May 2026, and points to it for IPCOT, which the message keeps separate and distinct from the Overseas Tour Extension Incentive Program.
- The Joint Travel Regulations supplement Tour Lengths and Tours of Duty OCONUS sets the initial tour lengths an OTEIP extension builds on.
