## Scope

Cancels MARADMIN 349/26, the PAC to MEO terminology realignment, due to an administrative error. Released 2026-08-07, DTG R 071300Z AUG 26. The updated message published the same day as MARADMIN 357/26 with identical policy effect. Release authorized by Lieutenant General William J. Bowers, Deputy Commandant, Manpower and Reserve Affairs.

## Status

Executed. The cancellation runs in the MARADMIN cancellation number series, distinct from the regular MARADMIN series. Cite MARADMIN 357/26 for the realignment.
