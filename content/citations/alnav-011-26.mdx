## Scope

ALNAV 011/26, DTG R 061945Z MAR 26, carries the Department of the Navy involuntary separation guidance under the policy on prioritizing military excellence and readiness. MARADMIN 189/26 implements it for the Marine Corps and cites it as reference A.

## Operative content

- Terminates bonus, incentive, and special pay on placement in administrative absence until separation proceedings resolve, per MARADMIN 189/26 paragraph 7.d.1.
- Authorizes retirement in lieu of involuntary separation for eligible Marines and TERA exception requests for 15 to 18 years of service, per MARADMIN 189/26 paragraph 8.a.
- Supplies the administrative absence comment text entered in MOL, per MARADMIN 189/26 paragraph 4.a.3.

## Connection to Marine Corps doctrine

- MARADMIN 189/26. The Marine Corps implementation.
