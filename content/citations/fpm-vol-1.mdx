## Scope

Volume 1 of the Marine Corps Finance Policy Manual, titled Pay Policies. The volume carries military pay entitlements and governing policies inside MCTFS, basic pay, allowances, special and incentive pays, deductions, debts, and pay-event reporting through the unit diary. The most-cited FPM volume across admin pages and MCAAT checklists. Snapshot on file is the September 2026 edition, 487 pages across 19 chapters. The July 2026 edition it replaces is archived by the publisher.

## Audience

- IPAC and unit pay clerks validating entitlement starts, stops, and corrections.
- Finance offices running the MCAAT-inspected pay processes.
- Auditors working the internal audit chapters against unit records.

## September 2026 changes

Each chapter opens with a summary of major changes. Five entries carry a
September 2026 date. Changed text is marked in blue font in the source.

- Chapter 1, paragraph 010104 E. Certifying Officer responsibility for DTMS. Appointment runs through the Resources, Fiscal and Finance branch head after Certifying Officer Legislation Training. A digital signature on the DD Form 577 carries pecuniary liability, and the officer records an attestation statement in the DTMS Remarks naming the documents reviewed.
- Chapter 9, paragraphs 090102, 090103, and 090105. Replaced the Involuntary Notification Letter with the Debt Notification Letter (DNL).
- Chapter 12, paragraph 120202 B. Updated In-Service DNL requirements.
- Chapter 20, paragraph 200101 D. Updated In-Service DNL requirements for reserve delayed checkage.
- Chapter 21, paragraph 210105 A.2. Replaced the Involuntary Notification Letter with the Out-of-Service DNL.

The portal never carried the Involuntary Notification Letter term, so the rename
required no correction. Portal pages cite this volume at chapter level, and none
of the five changed paragraphs anchored existing content.

## Chapter map

Loci the portal's MCAAT checklists cite.

- Chapter 1 with Appendices F and G. Pay policy foundations, cited across finance administrative management and pay processes.
- Chapters 2, 3, 5 through 9, 11 through 13, and 17. Entitlement-family chapters riding separations and pay processes checklists, paired with DoDFMR Volume 7A.
- Chapter 16. Internal audit, the backbone of the finance internal audit processes checklist, paired with FPM Volume 2 paragraph 080202 and Volume 3 Chapter 18.
- Chapter 21. Cited through separations and pay processes.
- Paragraphs 060102, 090101.d, 090106, and 090108. Milpay and outbound management checklist anchors, 170104.C(5)(c) alongside.
