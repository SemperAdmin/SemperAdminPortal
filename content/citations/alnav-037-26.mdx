## Scope

ALNAV 037/26, DTG 071428Z JUL 26, directs the mandatory transition of Department of the Navy activities to Naval Identity Services (NIS) for automated System Authorization Access Request workflows and the retirement of the manual DD Form 2875. MARADMIN 541/26 implements it for the Marine Corps and cites it as reference B.

## Audience

- Marine Corps commands reading MARADMIN 541/26 against its parent message.
- System owners and program managers tracing the 30 September 2027 deadline to its source.

## Operative content

- DON activities transition to automated access authorization workflows through NIS.
- Manual DD Form 2875 processes retire by 30 September 2027.

## Connection to Marine Corps doctrine

- MARADMIN 541/26. The Marine Corps implementation.
