## What this form is

DD Form 2875 is the manual System Authorization Access Request. The requestor, supervisor, security manager, and information owner sign it to justify and approve access to a DoD information system.

## How the Marine Corps is retiring it

- MCTFS end-user access left the form on 21 September 2026. PAA 02-26 moved create, modify, and revoke to DoD E-ICAM, which builds the SAAR record from its online form.
- UD-MIPS and DTMS access runs through Naval Identity Services (NIS) since 17 March 2026.
- Per MARADMIN 541/26 and ALNAV 037/26, every Marine Corps NIPRNET system retires manual DD Form 2875 processes by 30 September 2027. Each system moves on its own NIS Program Office milestone.

Until a system owner announces its move, the access path the system publishes stands.
