## Scope

MCO 5354.1 is the Marine Corps prevention and response order series behind the Military Equal Opportunity program. MARADMIN 357/26 realigned the program designation from Prohibited Activities and Conduct, PAC, to MEO, reissuing cancelled MARADMIN 349/26 of 2026-07-30 with identical policy effect on 2026-08-07, the order keeps its PAC title until the next revision. The order sets command responsibilities for prevention and response, equal opportunity and anti-harassment policy statements, command climate assessment, and annual program training. MARADMIN 357/26 reference A dates the current revision, MCO 5354.1G, at 2025-04-11, and the MCPEL lists Admin Ch 1 published 2024-05-31. The source PDF on file from the 2026-04 archive snapshot carries a Marine Corps Directives Compliance with Executive Order 14168 cover.

## Audience

- Commanders. Own the PAC program, the policy statements, the appointments, and the climate assessment.
- Equal Opportunity Advisors (EOA) and Equal Opportunity Coordinators (EOC) appointed in writing.
- Equal Opportunity Representatives (EOR) and Command Resilience Team members.
- S-1 staff processing Page 11 entries tied to EO and PAC matters.

## Chapter map

The portal does not index the remaining chapters.

## Operative content

- The commander appoints the EOA and EOC in writing and ensures 85 percent completion of annual PAC training in MCTIMS, per the commander oversight matrix for the PAC functional area.
- An incoming commander re-issues the Equal Opportunity and PAC Prevention and Response Policy Statement and the Anti-Harassment and Human Relations Policy Statement under personal signature within 30 days of assuming command, per the commander turnover checklist citing MCO 5354.1F.
- The commander appoints EORs and Command Resilience Team members, per the turnover checklist citing MCO 5354.1F and MARADMIN 468/23.
- A new commander conducts a CCA with DEOCS within 90 days of assuming command, documents the corrective action POA&M, and annotates completion in the annual FITREP, per MARADMIN 468/23 paragraph 4 and MCO 5354.1F.
- The annual CCA runs under MCO 5354.1F Chapter 2 within the window of 01 August to 30 November and begins no later than 31 October, per MARADMIN 468/23.

## Connection to Marine Corps doctrine and lower policy

- MARADMIN 357/26. Realigns the program terminology to Military Equal Opportunity, effective 2026-08-07, administrative only, in effect until the next revision of this order. Reissues MARADMIN 349/26, cancelled for an administrative error.
- MARADMIN 468/23. Sets the annual CCA window and the 90-day new-commander CCA requirement.
- MCO 1752.5C, the SAPR order. Lists MCO 5354.1E with Admin CH as a companion reference for discrimination and harassment prevention.
- IGMC Functional Area 5354.1, Prohibited Activities and Conduct Prevention and Response Program (PAC). CoRE category, sponsored by M&RA (MPE), effective 2025-05-15.
- Revision line in the portal record. MCO 5354.1E dated 2018-03-26 with a later Change 1, MCO 5354.1F dated 2021-04-20 announced with a 90-day training inspection moratorium, and MCO 5354.1G with Admin Ch 1 published 2024-05-31.

## Status

The current revision is MCO 5354.1G, dated 2025-04-11 per MARADMIN 357/26 reference A, with Admin Ch 1 on the MCPEL published 2024-05-31. The program the order carries now designates Military Equal Opportunity per MARADMIN 357/26, the order title updates at its next revision.
