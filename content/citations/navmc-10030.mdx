## What this form is

NAVMC 10030 is the commander's signed acknowledgement of the records management responsibilities the commander owns. MCBul 5210 paragraph 3.b.(1)(a) Note 1 sets the clock. Sign within 90 days of assuming command. The signed form goes to the Command Designated Records Manager (CDRM), who uploads it to the command's CROSS page under the Documents tab, Miscellaneous folder.

## Who touches it

- Commander or civilian leader. Signs within 90 days of assuming command.
- CDRM. Initiates the form at change of command, receives it signed, and uploads it to CROSS.
- IGMC inspector. Checks the form under FAC 5210 items 0102 and 0403.

## What the inspector checks

- The form carries the current commander's signature, not a predecessor's.
- The signature date falls inside 90 days of the change of command.
- The signed form sits in CROSS, Documents tab, Miscellaneous folder.

## Where to get it

Order the form from the DLA Document Services Navy Forms Online site. The CROSS page and the HQMC ARD Records SharePoint site carry the current version on the mil network.

## Status

Current. MCBul 5210 dated 29 August 2025 introduced the 90-day rule. The IGMC Records Management checklist effective 23 September 2025 inspects against it under items 0102 and 0403. The form carries no expiration. A new commander signs a new form.
