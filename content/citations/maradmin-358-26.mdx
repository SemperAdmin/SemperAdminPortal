## Scope

MARADMIN 358/26 resumes the recording of body composition data in the Marine Corps Training Information Management System, MCTIMS. Every body composition evaluation conducted since 26 February 2026 gets recorded and certified in MCTIMS through the Unit Training module. MARADMIN 066/26 Change 1 and MCO 6110.3A W ADMIN CH-4 stay in effect except for the changes in this message.

## Audience

- Unit training and body composition personnel entering and certifying scoresheets.
- Commanders certifying backdated evaluations and endorsing extension requests.
- Admin staff tracking accountability for missed semi-annual evaluations.

## Action items

- Record body composition evaluations for every Marine who conducted the requirement during the first semi-annual period while assigned to the unit, even after the Marine leaves. Paragraph 2.
- Certify backdated scoresheets no later than 4 September 2026. Route extension requests with O-6 endorsement through ETMS2 to USMC TECOM. Paragraph 2.
- Enter waist and height in inches, rounded down to the nearest half inch, as XX.X. MCTIMS computes the waist-to-height ratio and flags any value above 0.52000. Paragraphs 2.a.1 through 2.a.3.
- Keep measuring and entering weight. Enter 75 for backdated evaluations where the unit did not record weight. Paragraph 2.a.4.
- Enter at least one body fat value when the waist-to-height ratio is out of standards. Enter BIA results exactly as printed. Paragraphs 2.a.5 through 2.a.7.
- No body composition data flows from MCTIMS into MOL or MCTFS yet. Paragraph 2.c.

## Accountability and exemptions

- A missed semi-annual evaluation is adjudicated under MCO 1900.16 and MCO P1070.12K and draws an adverse evaluation under MCO 1610.7B. Paragraph 3.
- Pregnancy, post-partum, and pregnancies not carried to full term are the only exemptions. The semi-annual requirement is non-waivable. Paragraphs 4 and 5.

## Connection to Marine Corps doctrine

- MCO 6110.3A W ADMIN CH-4. The Body Composition and Military Appearance Program order.
- MCBUL 6110 W ADMIN CH-1. Bioelectrical impedance analysis implementation.
- MARADMIN 066/26 Change 1. Advance notification of changes to the PFT and body composition evaluation process.
