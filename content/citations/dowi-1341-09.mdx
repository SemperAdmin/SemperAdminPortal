## Scope

DOWI 1341.09 implements 10 U.S.C. 1052 for the Department. It sets eligibility, reimbursable and non-reimbursable expenses, the payment caps, and the claim rules for adoption expense reimbursement. Married members who both serve file one claim per child and share one $5,000 calendar year cap.

The issuance carries the DOWI designation following the 2026 Department of War renaming. Earlier references to the same document use the DoDI form. Both forms resolve to this entry.

## Marine Corps implementation

MARADMIN 501/26 implements the instruction for the Marine Corps. It cancelled MARADMIN 076/18.
