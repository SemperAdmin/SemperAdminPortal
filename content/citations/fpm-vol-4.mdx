## Section 0208, the Role Control Agent

FPM Volume 1 paragraph 010104 D points here for DTMS user access, so the two
volumes have to be read together. Chapter 2 carries a June 2026 date.

The Role Control Agent is the DTMS equivalent of the ELSIG Control Officer. The
role assigns users within the disbursing or finance office DTMS scope as
reviewers, preparers, certifiers, online transaction certifiers, and internal
control permissions. It sometimes sits with the same person appointed as the
ELSIG Control Officer.

- Appointment is in writing, on the template held on the RFF SharePoint.
- Grade floor is commissioned officer, warrant officer, civilian equivalent at GS-9 or above, or gunnery sergeant and above.
- Anyone holding these permissions comes off active certifying roles such as internal controls and operations, which is the separation of duties the MCAAT checklists inspect.
- Deviation from the grade and position requirements needs RFF Policy approval.

The scope is the disbursing or finance office, not the S-1. This resolves against
rather than contradicts the Naval Identity Services path the portal documents for
Marine Online applications. See the account provisioning page.
