## Scope

DD Form 577 documents the appointment and termination of authorized signature authority for personnel filling fiscal, certifying, accountable, departmental accountable, disbursing, deputy disbursing, agent, cashier, paying agent, change fund custodian, imprest fund cashier, collection agent, and similar accountable positions. Prescribed under E.O. 9397, 31 USC sections 3325 and 3528, and DoDFMR 7000.14-R Vol. 5. Updated 29 November 2024.

## Audience

- Certifying officials for UDMIPS, DTMS, DMM, and other Marine Corps pay systems.
- Disbursing officers, deputy disbursing officers, agents, cashiers, paying agents.
- Accountable officials at every echelon of command.
- Marine Corps Comptroller (RFA) and Comptroller staff.
- Marine Corps Pay and Allowances offices (PAOs).
- IPAC certifying officials.

## Key fields

- Section I Appointee. Items 1 to 8. Name, DoD ID, title, component or organization, address, the position appointed to (Item 6), the responsibilities (Item 7), and the publications to follow (Item 8).
- Section II Appointing Authority. Items 9 to 13. Name, title, component or organization, date, and signature. The form carries no monetary limit or period of appointment field.
- Section III Appointee Acknowledgement. Items 14 to 16. Printed name, date, and the appointee signature, digital or manual.
- Section IV Appointment Termination. Items 17 to 21. Termination date, appointee initials, and the name, title, and signature of the appointing authority. The form carries no reason field.

## Status

Active. MCTFSPRIUM 20104 paragraph 1.b requires the command to retain all DD Form 577s, appointments and terminations, for a minimum of 10 years. MCO 4650.39A governs DTS appointments. MARADMIN 015/20 (MAA 1-20) set catch-up document upload dates for the OMPF and set no per-appointment filing deadline.
