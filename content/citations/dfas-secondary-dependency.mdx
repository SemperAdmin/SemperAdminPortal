## Scope

The DFAS Secondary Dependency Claims page sets the criteria DFAS applies to secondary dependency determinations. It covers eligible categories, the more than 50 percent support test, the DD Form 137 series, and the recertification cadence. Read on the live site 16 November 2026, page updated 5 November 2026.

## Recertification rules on the page

- A secondary dependent drawing BAH recertifies annually.
- A secondary dependent holding a USIP card recertifies every four years.
- DFAS completes no recertification determination more than 90 days before the current one expires.
- DFAS completes no determination for a child approaching age 21 more than 90 days before the 21st birthday.
- Failure to recertify suspends the benefits. No back payment of BAH covers a period of lapse.

## Marine Corps path

DFAS serves as a criteria reference for Marines. The Commandant determines USMC secondary dependency through CMC (MFP-1), the DEERS and Dependency Support Section, under MCO 1751.3 chapter 1 and MARADMIN 311/25. CMC (MFP-1) initiates the annual recertification. The 90-day limit caps how early a determination completes. It does not open a submission window and does not replace the CMC (MFP-1) suspense.
