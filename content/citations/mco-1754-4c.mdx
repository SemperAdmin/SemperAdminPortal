## Scope

MCO 1754.4C sets Marine Corps policy and responsibilities for the Exceptional Family Member Program (EFMP). The program identifies families with a special medical or educational need and supports assignment of the sponsor to a duty station where services exist. Enrollment does not prejudice assignment or promotion, paragraph 3.

## Audience

- Sponsors with a family member who has a qualifying medical or educational need.
- Installation EFMP offices and HQMC EFMP (M&RA, MF, Family Care Branch).
- MOS monitors generating PCS orders for enrolled sponsors.
- Commanders and S-1 staff supporting enrolled families through PCS.

## Key requirements

- Enroll promptly once you know of a qualifying need, paragraph 4.a.(2)(a).
- Enroll through the installation EFMP office or directly with HQMC EFMP. File a DD Form 2792 for each family member, plus a DD Form 2792-1 for a child with a current IFSP or IEP, paragraph 4.a.(2)(b).
- PCS orders for enrolled sponsors route to HQMC EFMP, paragraph 4.a.(2)(c).
- Paperwork counts as current when HQMC EFMP receives it within 90 days of the provider and school official signatures. Update every three years, or sooner when the need changes, enclosure (2).
- Nurse Case Managers determine priority housing, housing modifications, and Level of Need for respite care reimbursement. Level of Need is used for respite care only and does not equal the Navy enrollment categories, enclosure (2).
- A sponsor requests reconsideration of an enrollment determination through the enrollment resolution procedure, enclosure (2).
