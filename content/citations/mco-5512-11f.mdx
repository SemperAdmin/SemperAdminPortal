## Scope

MCO 5512.11F is the Marine Corps designation for the joint Service identification card publications. RAPIDS sites and S-1 ID card operators run on it.

- Volume 1 W/CH-1. Identification Cards for Members of the Uniformed Services, Their Eligible Family Members, and Other Eligible Personnel. Joint publication with DAFI 36-3026 Volume 1. Change 1 is the DAF Guidance Memorandum dated 16 January 2024.
- Volume 2. Common Access Card (CAC). Joint publication with DAFI 36-3026 Volume 2, dated 24 January 2023. MCPEL page https://www.marines.mil/News/Publications/MCPEL/Electronic-Library-Display/Article/3458860/mco-551211f-volume-2/.

## Status

Both volumes Current on the MCPEL. The Volume 2 cover page still lists the Marine Corps designation as MCO 5512.11E. The MCPEL carries it as MCO 5512.11F Volume 2.
