## What this form is

NAVMC 11116 authorizes a one-time special payment outside routine MCTFS pay processing. The Personnel Administrative Office prepares it and forwards it to the disbursing officer or finance office with the substantiating documents.

## Where the portal uses it

- Adoption expense reimbursement. MARADMIN 501/26 paragraph 7.d has the PAO forward the verified package with a NAVMC 11116 to the DO/FO in DTMS.
