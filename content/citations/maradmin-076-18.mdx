## Scope

MARADMIN 076/18, R 052201Z FEB 18, set the Marine Corps procedure for reimbursing adoption expenses. It required DD Form 2675 for each child, a claim while on active duty, and submission no later than two years after the adoption became final or the child's certificate of U.S. citizenship was issued. It capped payment at $2,000 per child and $5,000 per calendar year.

## Status

Cancelled. MARADMIN 501/26 cancelled this message on 15 October 2026 and sets the current process. Cite MARADMIN 501/26 for any adoption reimbursement claim.
