## Scope

MARADMIN 514/26 gives advance notice of a change to MCO 1001R.1L. The Reserve Affairs BIC assignment and management policy memo dated 17 October 2026 sets interim procedures until the order changes. Applies to the Marine Corps Reserve.

## Key rules

- Commands no longer transfer a Marine to the IRR solely to vacate a BIC. Commands forward a request for assignment to an applicable excess billet indicator (EBI) to the HQMC authority, and no IRR transfer happens while the request is pending. Paragraphs 2 and 3.
- SMCR Marines in non-resident PME request EX011 for up to two years, approved by HQMC (RAM). Paragraph 4.a.
- SMCR Marines accepting ADOS orders request EX098, approved by HQMC (RAP-2). Paragraph 4.b.
- HQMC (RAP-3) assigns IMA Marines to EX098 when no valid IMA BIC exists. The assignment is temporary and authorizes no duty with the IMA unit. Paragraph 5.
- Commands still correct BIC discrepancies and return Marines to a valid BIC. Paragraph 6.
