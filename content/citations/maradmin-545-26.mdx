## Scope

MARADMIN 545/26 implements the SECNAV Special Leave Accrual designations and delegation of authority in ALNAV 056/26. It rescinds the SLA suspension in MARADMIN 341/26 and replaces the processes and procedures in MARADMIN 188/25. Applies to the Total Force.

## Audience

- Marines who lost leave on 1 October 2026 or hold a returned SLA package.
- Unit commanders, who endorse packages and restore approved days in Marine Online (MOL).
- The first Marine general officer in the administrative chain, the sole approval authority.
- S-1 and IPAC personnel, who review held packages and retain approved ones.

## Qualifying categories

Paragraph 4.b authorizes FY26 SLA under three categories.

- Operation EPIC FURY, under DoWI 1327.06 paragraph 3.8.d. Paragraph 4.b.1. FY26 only per paragraph 4.d.
- Designated deployable ships and mobile units deployed or operating away from homeport or PDS for more than 60 consecutive days in support of operations in defense of national security. Paragraph 4.b.2. The list sits on the MPO page of the M&RA website. Paragraph 5.b.
- At least 120 consecutive days in a location designated for Hostile Fire Pay or Imminent Danger Pay. Paragraph 4.b.3.

SECNAV has designated no "other qualifying duty." Approvers will not approve SLA under it, and commands return those requests without action before they reach HQMC. Paragraph 4.c.

Every item in paragraph 4 except EPIC FURY stays in effect for future fiscal years. Paragraph 4.d.

## Definitions

- Deployable ship or mobile unit. A commissioned naval vessel, established operational FMF unit, or task-organized operational formation whose mission requires it to deploy, maneuver, or operate away from its PDS or homeport for sustained expeditionary, combat, or contingency operations. Paragraph 3.a.
- Task-organized and provisional formations count. MEUs and ARG Marine elements, SPMAGTFs, Crisis Response Forces, JTF enablers, and provisional battalions, squadrons, task forces, or composite detachments formed under an approved execute or deployment order. Paragraph 3.b.
- Coverage. Marines joined permanently, or attached on TAD under formal deployment orders, during the designated window. Eligibility runs for the verified attachment while deployed or away from homeport or PDS. Paragraph 3.c.

## Approval

- The first Marine general officer in the administrative chain approves. No further delegation and no by direction signature. Paragraph 6.a.
- The GO approves only where national security operations, natural disaster, or catastrophe prevented the Marine from taking accrued leave. SLA does not salvage leave lost to poor leave management. Paragraph 6.b.
- Maximum 30 days beyond the 60-day limit, 90 days total. Excess over 90 days is eligible for sell-back per DoWI 1327.06. Paragraph 6.c.
- SLA expires at the end of the second FY for which it is approved. FY26 SLA restored in FY27 stays available until 1 October 2028. Paragraph 6.c.1 and 6.c.2.

## Action items

1. Commanders and S-1 sections review all pending, held, and returned SLA packages against the designated units list. Paragraph 7.a.
2. Forward qualified packages through the administrative chain to the first Marine GO. Paragraph 7.b.
3. Each package carries an Administrative Action Form requesting SLA, verification of assignment or TAD attachment to a designated unit during the qualifying period (web orders, deployment manifest, or operational travel claims), and the CO's written endorsement. Paragraph 7.c.
4. After GO approval, the documents go to the unit S-1, and the unit CO restores qualifying days in MOL with a comment naming the approving GO and the approval date. Paragraph 7.d and 7.d.1.
5. The restoring unit S-1 maintains the package under the Marine Corps Records Management Program. Paragraph 7.d.2.
6. Requests to add or reconsider a ship or unit go to Dir, MP with GO-endorsed justification through the MPO SharePoint site. Paragraph 8.a.

## Coordinating instructions

- Future FY designated units lists publish on the MPO website 60 days before FY closeout. Paragraph 8.b.
- Leave charges last in, first out. Carried-over and SLA leave charge only after current FY leave runs out. Paragraph 8.c.
- Marines check SLA on the LES and in MOL under Leave/Liberty, Leave Overview, SLA History. Paragraph 8.d.
- The LES carries the forecast of lost leave. Paragraph 8.e.
- Process questions go to the unit admin section or PAC. Policy questions go to MPO. Paragraph 9.
