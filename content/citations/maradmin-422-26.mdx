## Scope

MARADMIN 422/26 clarifies 1169 Utilities Chief PMOS attainment and the promotion rule tied to it, under the MOS Manual (NAVMC 1200.1M) and MCO P1400.32D. Applies to the Active and Reserve Components.

## Key rules

- Utilities Gunnery Sergeants complete the Utilities Chief Course, course ID M0311E2, to earn the 1169 PMOS. Utilities Staff Sergeants are eligible and highly encouraged to attend. Paragraph 2.
- An in-zone Gunnery Sergeant without the 1169 PMOS is not eligible for promotion consideration and stays out of the board population. Paragraphs 1 and 3.
- Career counselors and unit leaders counsel affected Gunnery Sergeants and eligible Staff Sergeants. Commanders give Gunnery Sergeants priority for course seats. Paragraph 5.
