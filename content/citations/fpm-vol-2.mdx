Volume 2 of the [Marine Corps Finance Policy Manual](/citations/fpm),
titled Travel Policies. Snapshot pulled 2026-09-14, 203 pages across eight
chapters and ten appendices.

## Scope

Travel entitlements and settlement policy. TAD and PCS travel claims, DTS roles, advances, and liquidation. Pairs with the JTR and DODFMR Volume 9 on most cited items.

## Dating, read this before citing a paragraph

This volume is a rolling compilation, not a single-dated edition. Each chapter
and appendix carries its own revision date, and the spread is wide. Citing "the
current volume" tells a reader nothing about whether the paragraph they need
moved. Name the section and its date.

## September 2026 change

One paragraph moved in the 14 September 2026 pull. Appendix G paragraph G-0206,
Utility Module privileges for the Integrated Automated Travel System (IATS). The
appendix it sits in replaces a December 2022 version.

The rule limits entry to the Utility Module for reviewing the Electronic Funds
Transfer Change Report to a small number of people outside the direct travel
production process, and fixes their privilege set at four items. One of the four,
viewing locked user reports, is assigned by the system and cannot be removed.

This is a disbursing and finance office control. The MCAAT finance administrative
management checklist item 10 asks whether the office maintains required accesses
and separation of duties for IATS, and cites this appendix alongside DoDFMR
Volume 5 Chapter 1 and FPM Volume 1 Chapter 16. The item names the
disbursing officer and finance officer as its audience, not S-1.

## Access notes

The Finance Policy Manual is published by the HQMC Finance Branch (RFF) with
distribution marked DoD community only. No public marines.mil URL exists.
Pull the working copy through the HQMC RFF portal or the servicing finance
office. This entry intentionally carries no externalUrl so citation chips
route here instead of hitting an authentication wall.
