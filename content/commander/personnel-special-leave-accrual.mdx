## Your Authority

You do not approve SLA. Per MARADMIN 545/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 545/26" /> paragraph 6.a, the first Marine general officer in the requestor's administrative chain holds the authority. It is not further delegated, and by direction signature is not authorized.

You carry three obligations.

1. Review every pending, held, and returned SLA package against the designated units list. Paragraph 7.a.
2. Endorse qualifying packages in writing. Paragraph 7.c.3.
3. Restore the approved days in MOL after the GO decides. Paragraph 7.d.

The first one is the obligation commands miss. Packages returned without action under the MARADMIN 341/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 341/26" /> suspension sit in S-1 files. MARADMIN 545/26 rescinds the suspension, and those Marines lost leave on 1 October 2026.

## What Qualifies

Per paragraph 4.b, three categories qualify for FY26.

- Operation EPIC FURY, designated by ALNAV 056/26<Citation source="ALNAV 056/26" /> under DOWI 1327.06<Citation source="DOWI 1327.06" /> paragraph 3.8.d. FY26 only.
- A designated deployable ship or mobile unit away from homeport or PDS for more than 60 consecutive days in support of operations in defense of national security. The unit must be on the MPO designated units list.
- At least 120 consecutive days in an HFP or IDP area.

Other qualifying duty is closed. SECNAV has designated none. Per paragraph 4.c.3, your command returns those requests without action before they reach HQMC.

## Your Endorsement

Your endorsement certifies the Marine met a paragraph 4.b category and lost leave to operations, not to poor leave management. Per paragraph 6.b, the GO approves only leave lost to national security operations, natural disaster, or catastrophe.

Endorse when the record shows the Marine had no real opportunity to take leave. Decline when the Marine had leave windows and did not use them. Paragraph 6.b closes SLA to leave lost through poor management.

The package your S-1 routes holds the NAVMC 10274<Citation source="NAVMC 10274" /> requesting SLA, proof of assignment or TAD attachment (web orders, deployment manifest, or operational travel claim), and your written endorsement. Paragraph 7.c.

## Your Signature in MOL

After the GO approves, the documents come to your S-1. Per paragraph 7.d, you restore the qualifying days in MOL.

- Per paragraph 7.d.1, your MOL comment states which GO approved and the approval date.
- Per paragraph 7.d.2, your S-1 keeps the package under MCO 5210.11F<Citation source="MCO 5210.11F" />.

An uncommented restoration leaves no trail back to the approval. Inspectors read the comment first.

## The Exception Path

### A qualifying unit missing from the list

Per paragraph 8.a, submit formal justification, endorsed by a general officer, to Dir, MP through the MPO SharePoint site. When Dir, MP decides, your unit is notified and the list updates. Hold the affected packages until then.

### A question about GO authority

Paragraph 6.b frames GO approval around designated ships and mobile units. The EPIC FURY and HFP or IDP categories sit beside them in paragraph 4.b. When your chain questions whether the GO approves a request in those categories, your S-1 takes the policy question to MPO per paragraph 9.

## Limits You Brief

- 30 days of SLA beyond the 60-day limit, 90 days total. Paragraph 6.c.
- SLA expires at the end of the second FY for which it is approved. FY26 SLA restored in FY27 stays available until 1 October 2028. Paragraph 6.c.2.
- No new SLA while the Marine holds 30 days or more. MARADMIN 188/25<Citation source="MARADMIN 188/25" /> paragraph 7.b.
- Leave charges last in, first out. SLA is charged only after current FY leave runs out. Paragraph 8.c.

## Next Fiscal Year

Per paragraph 4.d, the ship and unit category and the HFP or IDP category carry into future fiscal years. Per paragraph 8.b, each future designated units list publishes on the MPO website 60 days before the FY closes. Check it then, and route requests before the new FY starts.
