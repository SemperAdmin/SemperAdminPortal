## Your Authority

You do not separate a Marine under this policy, and you do not initiate the proceedings. Per MARADMIN 189/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 189/26" /> paragraph 5.a, the Commander, Navy Installations Command is the Consolidated Disposition Authority (CDA) and holds exclusive authority to initiate. The Secretary of the Navy is the separation authority for every officer and enlisted case.

Your obligations run before, around, and after the board.

1. Identify within the limits of paragraph 2.
2. Refer through MPO per paragraph 3.
3. Place the Marine on administrative absence per paragraph 4.
4. Report per paragraphs 10 and 12.c.
5. Treat the Marine with dignity and respect, and protect health information, per paragraphs 12.a and 12.b.

## No Extensions

Per MARADMIN 539/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 539/26" />, you do not submit, endorse, or approve an extension of service, NAVMC 10274 included, to allow processing under MARADMIN 189/26. A Marine reaching EAS before proceedings finish separates on the scheduled EAS. Paragraph 11 of MARADMIN 189/26 no longer exists.

The rule covers this policy only. Extension rules for other separation bases are unchanged.

## Identification Has Hard Limits

Per paragraph 2.b, your awareness counts only when it comes from one of four sources.

1. A prior exception to policy request on sex-based standards.
2. A prior request to change the DEERS sex marker.
3. A medical treatment plan, restricted duty status, or deployment waiver tied to a documented diagnosis.
4. The Marine's written self-identification to the chain of command.

Anything else goes to the first general officer in the Marine's chain. The general officer decides case by case with a judge advocate and the BUMED Deployability Action Cell, based on medical record review. Paragraph 2.b.5. If the general officer makes the determination, the general officer assumes the referral responsibilities. Paragraph 3.b.6.

Per paragraph 2.a, you do not direct a Periodic Health Assessment outside its normal periodicity.

## Medical Verification

Per paragraph 2.c, contact the BUMED Deployability Action Cell at usn.ncr.bumedfchva.mbx.bumed-deployability-action-cell@health.mil to start an individualized medical record review. Notify MPO within 24 hours of the request. No review is needed when medical verification was already completed, for example in support of a prior exception to policy request.

## Your Referral Letter

Per paragraph 3.b, sign it in naval letter format with the Marine's name, command, and EDIPI. State whether the Marine receives special pay or a bonus, and if so give a recommendation with justification on debt recoupment. Your S-1 titles the file with the Marine's initials and the last five digits of the EDIPI, as a PDF, and routes it to MPO. MPO verifies the package and routes it to the CDA. Paragraph 3.c.

## Administrative Absence

Per paragraph 4, once MPO confirms medical verification, place the Marine on administrative absence. Pre-separation requirements start immediately, including the Transition Assistance Program, with maximum flexibility to complete them remotely or in civilian attire.

- Active and Active Reserve. Full pay and benefits, no bonus, special, or incentive pays.
- SMCR. Excuse the Marine from drill for the process. Excused drills earn no drill pay or retirement points.
- You remain fully responsible for the Marine while in administrative absence. Paragraph 4.c.

If you elect not to place the Marine on administrative absence, or not to excuse an SMCR Marine from drill, notify MPO within 24 hours.

## The Board

- Per paragraph 5.b.4, make sure the Marine understands the right to a board and the 10-day election timeline. Missing it waives the board.
- Per paragraph 5.e.1, the Marine gets at least 30 days from notification to board. If you anticipate any delay, report it to MPO within 24 hours.
- Per paragraph 5.e.2, your command funds board travel and related expenses.
- Per paragraph 5.f.2, characterization is Honorable when gender dysphoria is the only basis.

## Pre-Separation Checklist

Per paragraph 12.d, drive these to completion before the board convenes.

1. Final physical, page 3 of DD 2808 or DD 2807, stating "Medically Qualified for Separation/Retirement."
2. DD Form 2648, Pre-Separation/Transition Counseling Checklist.
3. Commanding Officer's Interview, required for every enlisted Marine.
4. DD Form 2963, Service Treatment Record Transfer and Transmittal.
5. Unit check-out sheet.

## Retention Waiver Endorsement

Per paragraph 6.e.2, the Marine gives the chain the complete waiver package while also submitting it to MPO. Route it to the first general officer for an endorsement assessing compelling government interest. The complete package reaches DC M&RA, via MPO, no later than 30 calendar days from the Marine's initial waiver due date.

Per paragraph 6.e.3, compelling government interest covers special experience, training, and advanced education in a highly technical career field designated by SECNAV as mission critical and hard to fill, tied directly to warfighting operational needs. SECNAV alone approves. Paragraph 6.f.

## Retirement in Lieu of Separation

Per paragraph 8.b, a retirement request reaches MMSR and MPO within 30 calendar days of identification, with a requested date no more than 6 months after identification. It carries a NAVMC 10274 per Appendix J and routes via the first general officer and DC M&RA. Place Marines pending retirement on administrative absence per paragraph 4. Paragraph 8.d.

## 24-Hour Notifications to MPO

- Medical verification requested. Paragraph 2.c.
- Administrative absence not used, or SMCR drill not excused. Paragraph 4.
- Board delay anticipated. Paragraph 5.e.1.
- Marine referred to the DES. Paragraph 9.a.3.
- Marine becomes subject to administrative separation for another reason. Paragraph 9.b.2.

## Reporting

- MCTFS. On identification, report the Marine non-deployable with strength category code X and duty status code C until the process concludes. Paragraph 10.
- MPO. Submit a report twice a month through your major subordinate command. Request the template from MPO. Paragraph 12.c.

## Other Processes

- DES runs concurrently and takes precedence. SECNAV holds the final separation determination until the DES concludes. Paragraph 9.a.2.
- Another administrative separation takes precedence. Processing under this policy holds in abeyance until the other board concludes. Paragraph 9.b.1.

## Dignity, Privacy, and Support

- Use every command authority available to treat affected Marines with dignity and respect. Paragraph 12.a.
- Protect health information like any other protected health information, restricted to personnel with a specific need to know. Paragraph 12.b.
- Advise the Marine of mental health, financial, and separation resources, and check in regularly. Paragraph 12.f and MARADMIN 539/26 paragraph 4.
- Military OneSource is available 24/7 at 1-800-342-9647. Paragraph 13.

Questions on command authorities or tools go to MPO. Paragraph 12.e.

## Where to Go Next

- Administrative Separation covers involuntary separation on other bases.
- The S-1 procedural page carries the MOL, MCTFS, unit diary, and pay actions.
