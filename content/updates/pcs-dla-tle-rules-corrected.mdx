The DLA page listed retirement PCS as eligible, paid a reduced rate to new
accessions, and limited DLA to one payment per PCS. The TLE page used 75 and 50
percent dependent rates and said a PCS to OCONUS earns no TLE. The separation
page said a separating Marine receives DLA and TLE and travels to a home of
selection within one year.

JTR 050509 bars DLA on the move from your last PDS to home or the PLEAD, and
pays DLA to the first PDS only when a dependent relocates. JTR 050501-B limits
DLA to one payment per fiscal year with listed exceptions. JTR Table 5-17 sets
TLE at 65 percent for you alone, 100 percent with one dependent, plus 35
percent for each added dependent 12 or older and 25 percent for each child
under 12. Table 5-13 pays up to 7 days of TLE at the CONUS PDS before an OCONUS
PCS. Separation travel runs to the home of record or PLEAD and must begin
within 180 days. Retirement travel to a home of selection must begin within 3
years.
