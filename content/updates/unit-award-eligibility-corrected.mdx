The unit award pages said every Marine on the rolls or assigned during the
qualifying period wears the unit ribbon. They called the 30-day TAD rule
conventional and said formal attachment waives it. The Marine unit awards page
said the President approves the PUC.

SECNAV M-1650.1 Chapter 3 paragraph 3.1.b.(2)(a) requires physical presence and
participation in the action cited. The Marine must be permanently assigned, or
attached for 30 days or more. Reserve members need 14 days on orders. Under 30
days needs a commanding officer certification of significant contribution.
Paragraph 3.3.b has SECNAV take final action on the PUC, awarded in the name
of the President.
