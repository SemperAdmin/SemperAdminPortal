The SDAP pages put drill instructors at the highest level, Level 6, and listed
MSG, MCSF, Embassy Security Group and formal school instructors as SDAP
billets. They also said discipline ends SDAP and units audit it monthly.

MCO 7220.12R paragraph 4.d sets drill instructors at SD-5 and recruiters at
SD-6. MARADMIN 628/20 paragraph 5 limits SDAP to SDA billets, and MARADMIN
181/26 names Recruiter, Drill Instructor, Combat Instructor and MSG Detachment
Commander. Other billets draw AIP. SDAP ends on relief and removal, loss of
grade or confinement, and an O-5 or above certifies qualification each year.
