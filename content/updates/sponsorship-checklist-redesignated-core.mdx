The Marine Corps Sponsorship Program checklist (1320) now sits in the CoRE
bucket. CoRE functional areas are inspected during every Independent
Command Inspection and Comprehensive Command Inspection. Sponsor of record
on the IGMC listing is M&RA (MF).

The checklist document itself still shows a revision date of 24 March 2025
and carries the same six items. The change is scope, not content. IGMC
applies a 30 to 90 day informal evaluation window to newly designated
areas, so expect observations before findings during the first inspections
under the new designation.

Walk the six items against the unit program before the next inspection
cycle. A program with no written directive or no assigned sponsors for
inbound Marines now draws a CoRE finding.
