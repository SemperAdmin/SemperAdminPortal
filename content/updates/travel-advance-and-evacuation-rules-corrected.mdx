The S-1 travel pages described travel advances as general up-front support
under Vol 7A Chapter 32 and applied a flat rate per diem past 30 days. They
listed Service members as evacuees, cited JTR Chapter 7 for evacuation, and
routed evacuation claims through DTS on a DD Form 1351-2. They also waived
lodging receipts for flat rate cases.

DoDFMR Vol 9 Chapter 5 allows advances only for travelers with a GTCC
exemption, paid by EFT and capped near 80 percent of estimated allowances.
Vol 9 Chapter 7 says uniformed members are not evacuated and DTS does not
support evacuation travel. JTR Chapter 6 holds evacuation, and JTR 020312 defines
long-term TDY as 31 days or more. Vol 9 Chapter 8 paragraph 4.5.1 requires all
lodging receipts on every claim.
