The standby categories page put members with long-term medical conditions on
the ISL and said the ASL needs basic readiness standards. It listed civilian
employment as an ASL reason, called for annual readiness updates, and said an
ISL member needs screening to return.

MCO 1001R.1L Chapter 9 Section 1 paragraph 7.a lists the ISL criteria. They
are non-participating affiliation, mobilization potential, 20 years of service
with a disability under 30 percent, board action, and HIV-positive status. ASL
and ISL members give MCIRSA a current primary residence. An ISL member asks in
writing through MCIRSA, and DC M&RA decides if a requirement exists.
