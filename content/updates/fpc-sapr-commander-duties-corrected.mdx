The commander pages listed the monthly FPC chair as non-delegable and said an
XO chair is a finding. The SAPR playbook made SARC notification a CO action
due within 24 hours of an Unrestricted Report.

MCO 1500.60A paragraph 4.b requires FPCs monthly for the active component and
at least semi-annually for the reserve component. It holds no non-delegable
chair rule. When CIRRAS is down, paragraph 4.b.(3)(l) requires a memorandum for
the record for each FPC. MCO 1752.5C Chapter 3 paragraph 4 has the SARC inform
the Installation Commander within 24 hours. Contact the SARC when you learn of
the report.
