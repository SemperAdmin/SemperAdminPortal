The flight pay page named the program Aviation Career Incentive Pay under 37
USC 301a and showed a peak of $750 for years 6 to 14. It paid $585 from year 14
and shifted the late-career steps one tier early. It also put enlisted aircrew
on the aviator rates. The sea pay page tied Career Sea Pay to 30 days
embarked, called temporary sea duty a different pay, and used three generic
tiers.

DoD FMR Volume 7A Chapter 22 names the program Aviation Incentive Pay under 37
USC 334. Table 22-11 peaks at $1,000 over 10 years of aviation service, drops
to $840 over 17 years, and reaches $585 only over 22 years. Enlisted aircrew
draw Hazardous Duty Incentive Pay under Table 22-6. Chapter 18 paragraph 2.2.3
starts Career Sea Pay on qualifying sea duty with no 30-day threshold, counts
temporary ship assignment under paragraph 2.2.3.2, and sets rates by pay grade
in Table 18-3. Recheck any aviator or sea pay rate you set from the old pages.
