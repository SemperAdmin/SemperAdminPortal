The portal had no coverage of MARADMIN 189/26, released 23 April 2026.
Three pages now carry it, each written for its reader.

- Marines. Rights, the 10-day board election, the 15-day waiver window,
  pay during administrative absence, separation terms, and retirement.
- Commanders. Identification limits, referral, administrative absence, the
  24-hour notifications to MPO, waiver endorsement, and reporting.
- S-1. The referral PDF, the MOL and unit diary entries, MCTFS strength
  category X and duty status C, SPD codes JDK2 and JFF8, and RE-3G.

All three reflect MARADMIN 539/26, which removed extension eligibility.
