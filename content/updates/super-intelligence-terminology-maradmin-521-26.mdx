MARADMIN 521/26 implements Executive Order 14434. The Marine Corps now says
Super Intelligence (SI) in place of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in
correspondence, websites, reports, briefings, and training materials. SI
covers the same technology the law defines as AI, so nothing about what you
use or how you use it changes. Security, privacy, acquisition, and
responsible use rules all still apply.

Official titles keep their published wording. The Basic AI Course, GenAI.mil,
MCO 5231.4, and older MARADMIN titles still read AI. The DC I Chief Data and
Super Intelligence Office (CDSO) remains the Service lead.

The portal's SI pages, navigation, and link descriptions now use SI. Page
addresses stay the same, so your bookmarks still work.
