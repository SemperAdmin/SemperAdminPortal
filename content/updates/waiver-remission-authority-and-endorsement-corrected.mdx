The waiver and remission pages cited DoDFMR Vol 7A Chapter 50 as the waiver
policy and Chapter 52 for debt validation and package indexing. They said a
package without endorsement at every command level gets rejected. They said the S-1
cuts the debt closeout transaction and a Page 11 entry records the
outcome.

Vol 7A Chapter 50 covers Bankruptcy and Chapter 52 covers deduction priority.
DoDFMR Vol 16 Chapter 4 governs waiver, remission, and debt validation. DD Form
2789 item 20 makes a commander endorsement optional for Marines. Under PAA
02-24 the Administrative community no longer reports the liquidation TTC 559/000.
Disbursing and Finance reports it. MCO P1070.12K paragraph 4006 prescribes no
Page 11 entry for the outcome.
