The UPB preparation page mapped the form to blocks the
current NAVMC 10132 does not carry. It put member identification in Block 1, charges in Block 2,
and the election in Block 5 with a witness signature. It said a reduction posts
next pay period, a one-time forfeiture takes a single deduction, correctional
custody changes pay status, and a Page 11 entry records the NJP. The pay
processing page also sent adverse pay actions to Page 11 and tied court-martial
forfeiture to sentence finality.

The REV 08-2023 form holds offenses in Item 1, the accused election in Item 2,
and accused identity in Items 18 to 20. An unsuspended reduction or forfeiture
takes effect on the imposition date, and DoDFMR Vol 7A Chapter 49 limits NJP
pay effects to forfeiture and reduction. Correctional custody has no direct pay
impact. MCO P1070.12K paragraph 4006 prescribes no Page 11 entry for these
actions. Court-martial forfeitures begin 14 days after the sentence is
announced under Chapter 48 paragraph 5.2.1.
