The awards overview told the S-1 to cut each approved award as a UD-MIPS
transaction in MCTFS. It also set the nomination in a NAVMC 11533 packet from
the chain and sent the Page 11 under a 30-day OMPF filing rule.

MCTFSPRIUM 50101 paragraphs 1 and 2 bar local commands from reporting personal
awards, the Combat Action Ribbon, the Humanitarian Service Medal, and foreign
awards. The Commandant reports them. Units submit service awards, unit awards,
and other decorations through the Personnel Administration Center and iAPS.
MARADMIN 093/25 paragraph 4 originates every DoN personal military decoration
in iAPS, and the citation upload there drives OMPF inclusion.
