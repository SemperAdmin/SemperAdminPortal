The 5110.1 source lists Subsections 1 through 4 in one header cell above
codes 0101 to 0410. The import joined the four titles into one label and
filed 92 items under it. Each code carries its subsection number in its
first two digits, so the split is in the source.

- Subsection 1, Administration. 15 items, 0101 to 0115.
- Subsection 2, Finance. 16 items, 0201 to 0216.
- Subsection 3, Operations. 51 items, 0301 to 0351.
- Subsection 4, Quality Management. 10 items, 0401 to 0410.
- Subsection 5, Official Mail Program. 20 items, 0501 to 0520.

Item codes, questions, and references did not change. The 23 September sweep
listed 5110.1 as matching source on subsection. It did not.

The grading rubric on the IGMC page now states the MCO 5040.6K Chapter 4
rule excluding assist visits from the command tier.
