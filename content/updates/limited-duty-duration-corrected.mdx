The limited duty pages capped LIMDU at 8 months, built from a 6 month period
plus a 2 month medical extension. They had the MTF commander sign the LIMDU
and extension determinations. They also set light duty at 30 days or less.

MCO 1900.16 paragraphs 8003.24 and 8102 set TLD at up to two 6 month periods,
12 months cumulative for enlisted at the MTF. The paragraph 8111 medical
extension extends the contract, not the duty period. It covers the TLD period
up to 6 months, DES or PEB cases up to 12 months, and short-term care up to 2
months. The MTF recommends TLD. The commanding officer approves the first
extension and CMC (MMSR-4) approves later ones. Light duty runs up to two 30
day periods and never exceeds 90 days.
