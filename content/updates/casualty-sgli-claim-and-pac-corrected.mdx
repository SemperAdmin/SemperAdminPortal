The casualty affairs overview listed the SGLV 8286 SGLI Election as the
insurance benefit form and cited DoDFMR Chapter 36 for it. It also had the S-1
cut the casualty status code transaction in UD-MIPS at PCR submission. It told
the DD Form 93 audit to run annually.

MCO 3040.4 chapter 7 section 3 paragraph 2.c and DoDFMR Vol 7A Chapter 47
paragraph 13.0 make the SGLV 8283 the death claim, filed by the beneficiary
through MFPC. Chapter 36 covers the death gratuity only. MCO 3040.4 has the
unit coordinate with the PAC, which runs the deceased transaction in MCTFS. The
DD Form 93 audit runs at check-in, TAD over 30 days, before deployment, before
PCS departure and triennially.
