The PLD page said the PEB recommends PLD for a Marine found fit for retention
and described PLD as a career-long restriction. The EPLD page described it as
broader PLD for fit Marines. Both pages put retention authority with the
Secretary of the Navy and HQMC M&RA and set an annual review.

MCO 1900.16 paragraph 8108 retains a Marine the PEB found unfit, on CMC
(MMSR-4) approval, until a specified date. Paragraph 8109 limits EPLD to
combat wounded or injured Marines found unfit, with a combat-related injury
after 11 September 2001 and meeting Purple Heart criteria. DC M&RA waives the
Purple Heart criteria case by case. A Marine found fit returns to duty. PLD
over 12 months gets reevaluation under paragraph 8108.8.
