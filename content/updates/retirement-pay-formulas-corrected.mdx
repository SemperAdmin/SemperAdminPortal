The Reserve pages gave one formula, P/360 x 0.025 x B, and said to return pay
forms 30 days before the 60th birthday. The disability pages used the rating
times basic pay, rounded up to the next 10 percent, or years times 2.5 percent
of basic pay.

MCO 1900.16 paragraph 3013.2 uses 0.025 for Marines who entered before 1
January 2018 and did not opt into BRS, and 0.020 under BRS. Paragraph 3017.3
sets the signed DD Form 108 return at least three months before pay
eligibility, with DD Form 2656 to DFAS. DoDFMR Volume 7B Chapter 1 computes
disability pay on the retired pay base. It uses years times 2.5 percent
(legacy) or 2.0 percent (BRS), or the disability percentage capped at 75
percent, and pays the higher.
