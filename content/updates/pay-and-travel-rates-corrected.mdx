A currency audit found pay and travel figures behind current tables.

- Family Separation Allowance. Two admin pages said $250 a month. FSA is
  $300 for every FSA type from 18 December 2025 per MARADMIN 150/26.
- PCS mileage. Four pages said MALT was about $0.18 a mile. MALT is $0.235
  for travel on or after 1 July 2026, and $0.205 from 1 January to 30 June
  2026, per DTMO UTD/CTD 46-26.
- TDY mileage. The TDY page used $0.18. The TDY car rate is $0.76 a mile
  from 1 July 2026. Motorcycles pay $0.74.
- Dislocation allowance. The page gave rough ranges and said the
  with-dependent rate ran 2.5 times the single rate. The page now carries the
  CY2026 DTMO table. An E-4 to E-6 draws $2,389.42 single or $3,548.02 with
  dependents.
- Hardship Duty Pay. The admin page showed an HDP-L tier up to $300. FMR
  Volume 7A Chapter 17 pays $50, $100, or $150. HDP-M is a flat $150.
- CONUS M&IE. Two pages said $59 to $79 a day. GSA tiers run $68 to $92.

Check any travel voucher or FSA start computed from the old figures.
