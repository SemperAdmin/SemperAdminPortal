MARADMIN 480/26 sets Foreign Language Proficiency Bonus policy for FY27,
effective 1 October 2026. Marines qualify through one of five categories.
MOS requirements, host nation languages, ad hoc missions, strategic language
oral fluency at ILR 3 speaking, and an annual lump sum open to every Marine
except for Spanish. Each tested skill pays $50 to $400 a month by ILR level,
capped at $1,000 a month and $12,000 a year. The message rescinds MARADMIN
574/25 paragraph 4.a.1.a, so 0370 and 0372 stay at ILR 1.

Rules lock to the fiscal year a Marine certifies. FY25 and FY26 payments
continue until those scores expire. Recertifying early ends them, and the
policy in effect on the new test date governs.

This entry also corrects the portal. The FLPB pages described the DoD
framework, not the Marine Corps program. They cited 37 U.S.C. 305a, the
Career Sea Pay statute, in place of 37 U.S.C. 353. They set rates by
strategic-language tier under DoDI 5160.70, limited FLPB to strategic
languages, used the retired FLPP name, and limited eligibility to active
duty. The Marine page, the S-1 procedural page, and the IPAC page now follow
MCO 7220.52G and MARADMIN 480/26.
