The birth and adoption page required a final adoption decree and said an
unmarried Marine without a voluntary acknowledgment or court order cannot claim
a child. The divorce pages said only a final decree counts and ignored interim
rulings.

MCO 1751.3 chapter 1 paragraph 1.b lists pre-adopted children as eligible
dependents, so a child placed in the home counts before the final decree.
Chapter 1 paragraph 5.a(3) accepts a birth certificate naming the Marine, a
court order, or a signed statement of parentage. Chapter 2 paragraph 10 ends
spouse BAH at the interlocutory decree date when the decree provides no spouse
support, unless the Marine proves support. The birth and adoption page also
placed birth parent convalescent leave inside the 84 days. DoDI 1327.06
paragraph 3.11.c.(3)(a) places it ahead of the 84 days.
