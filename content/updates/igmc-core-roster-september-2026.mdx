The IGMC checklist listing dated 8 September 2026 changes the CoRE roster.

New functional areas.

- 5351.1 Operational Stress Control and Readiness, CoRE, sponsor M&RA (MF),
  effective 21 July 2026.
- 5700.45 Communication Strategy and Operations, CoRE+, sponsor HQMC (CD),
  effective 8 September 2026.

Redesignated.

- 1320 Marine Corps Sponsorship, Non-CoRE to CoRE, effective
  8 September 2026.
- 1700.36 Single Marine Program, Non-CoRE to CoRE, effective
  8 September 2026.
- 1553.2 Formal School Management, Non-CoRE to CoRE+, redesignation
  effective 28 May 2026, checklist revised 5 June 2026.

The CoRE bucket now holds 43 functional areas. The portal carries
checklists for the admin-facing subset only. The two new areas and Single
Marine Program have no portal checklist page yet.

Also reissued since the last sweep with no portal page: 3070 Operations
Security, 5370 Assistance and Hotline, 1500.60 Force Preservation Council,
5239 Cyber Security Management, 5580 Law Enforcement Operations, 1640
Military Correctional Facilities, and 6110 Body Composition and Military
Appearance.
