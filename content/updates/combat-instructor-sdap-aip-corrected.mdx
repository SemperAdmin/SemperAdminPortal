The SDAP and AIP pages told Combat Instructors they receive AIP only, at $150
per month or a $4,900 lump sum, with no SDAP. The pages treated the billet as
a T1 screenable billet.

MARADMIN 181/26 paragraph 7 says Marines who earn the additional MOS and fill
the billet receive SDAP and AIP. Paragraph 7.d lists the Combat Instructor
total at $300 per month. Combat Instructors do not receive VSI. Check your LES
for both lines.
