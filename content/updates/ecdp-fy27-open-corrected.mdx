The career reenlistments page told E-8 and E-9 Marines the ECDP was not
available for FY27 and listed an ECC cutoff of 30 September 2026. A Marine
reading it skipped a program the Marine qualified for.

MARADMIN 190/26 paragraph 1 makes the FY27 ECDP effective on release, 23 April
2026. Paragraph 2 sets the eligibility. The ECC falls before 30 September 2027,
and the Marine is an E-8 select with 15 years of service, or a current E-8 or
E-9 with at least two years time in grade or at least 15 years of service.
The SRB obligation does not run concurrent with the initial ECDP obligation.
