The parental leave page said a second qualifying event forfeits unused days
from the first and starts a new 84 days, and called the 1-year window firm.
The convalescent leave page said only the medical authority grants or extends
convalescent leave and set no cap.

DoDI 1327.06 paragraph 3.11.c.(6) carries unused days into the second event,
which adds only enough days to restore 84. Paragraph 3.11.c.(11) extends the
1-year window for deployments, PME, TDY, and hospital stays of 90 or more
days. Paragraph 3.11.a caps convalescent leave at 30 days per condition
outside childbirth unless the Secretary approves more, and the unit commander
or MTF director grants it on the provider's recommendation.
