The sponsorship pages told S-1 sections to document sponsor completion on a
Page 11 and to run an eAOC touchpoint in MOL. They set sponsor designation at 30
to 60 days before arrival and described a monthly unit metrics cycle with an
annual HQMC roll-up.

MCO 1320.11H paragraph 4.b.(7)(e) requires sponsor training to be recorded in
MCTIMS under event code US. Paragraph 4.b.(6)(f) triggers the welcome aboard
letter on receipt of the NAVMC 11799. Paragraph 4.b.(6)(g) requires sponsor
assignment no later than 60 days before transfer. Paragraph 4.b.(7)(i) has the
SC report utilization metrics quarterly, by the end of the first month of the
next quarter, and the IR&R Manager rolls them up annually.
