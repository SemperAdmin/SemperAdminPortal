The court-martial page said a special court-martial seats three or more
officers and a general court-martial seats five or more members.

R.C.M. 501(a)(2) of the Manual for Courts-Martial provides a military judge and
four members at a special court-martial, or a military judge alone. R.C.M.
501(a)(1) provides a military judge and eight members at a non-capital general
court-martial, twelve in capital cases, or a military judge alone.
