The breast milk shipment page listed storage bags, insulated containers,
cold packs, and refrigeration as reimbursable. MARADMIN 603/24 removed them
from MARADMIN 574/24 in December 2024 to match JTR Chapter 2 Table 2-24 Item
20. Packaging and packing material are not authorized.

Reimbursable expenses are commercial shipping fees, excess baggage fees, and
dry ice or regular ice, up to $1,000 per PCS. New expense types qualify only
after the JTR publishes them.
