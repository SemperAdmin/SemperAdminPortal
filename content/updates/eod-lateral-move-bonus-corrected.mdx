The lateral moves page said the EOD (2336) bonus reached $137,750 and was the
highest among targeted MOSs. It listed 0211 and 1751 up to $105,000. A Marine
planning a move around those figures expected more than the message pays.

MARADMIN 023/26 paragraphs 4.c and 5.a pay a Zone A E-5 on an 84 month lateral
move with the $50,000 kicker $107,750 for 2336, $116,500 for 0321, $115,000
for 1751, $110,000 for 0372 and $101,000 for 0211. Zone PMOS bonuses require
at least 48 months of additional obligated service.
