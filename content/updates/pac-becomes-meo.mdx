The Prohibited Activities and Conduct program now carries the Military Equal
Opportunity name. MCO 5354.1 remains the governing order and DODI 1350.02
remains the higher authority. Nothing about how you receive, route, or track
a complaint changes.

Update local directives, turnover binders, and briefing decks on the next
revision cycle. Watch the acronym on the way through. PAC also names the
Personnel Administration Center and Pay and Allowance Continuation across
the portal, and neither one moved.
