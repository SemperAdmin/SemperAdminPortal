The old rule on the page read one travel day per 350 miles, rounding up.
Rounding up is wrong. The ACTSMAN adds a day only when the remainder runs
over 51 miles. A 2,500-mile move breaks into seven full increments plus a
50-mile remainder, so it allows seven days, not eight.

Two related rules landed with the fix. Any commercial leg adds one day
regardless of distance, and a move under 401 miles allows one day whatever
the mode. The TDY page picked up proceed time in the same pass, four days
maximum, not charged to leave, and authorized on PCS moves only.
