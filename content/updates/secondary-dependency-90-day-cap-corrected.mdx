The secondary dependency and BAH error catch pages said a 90-day
submission window opens before a certification expires, and the section
tracker keyed on the date. The DFAS Secondary Dependency Claims page says
the opposite. DFAS completes no determination more than 90 days before the
current one expires. The limit caps how early a determination completes.

CMC (MFP-1) initiates the annual recertification under MCO 1751.3. Track
the CMC (MFP-1) suspense, not a 90-day mark. The pages now also carry two
DoD FMR Volume 7A Chapter 26 rules. A Marine recertifies on arrival at a
new PDS, paragraph 3.2.3.1, and a Reserve Component Marine recertifies at
least every third year, paragraph 3.2.3.4.
