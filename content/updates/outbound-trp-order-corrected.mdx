The separation and retirement handoff page cited MCO 1700.31 and the Transition
Assistance Program in most of its steps. It listed three TAP tracks:
employment, education and entrepreneur.

MCO 1700.31A of 2 March 2026 cancelled MCO 1700.31 and renamed the program the
Transition Readiness Program. Marines elect one of four 2-Day Tracks:
Employment, Education, Vocational or Entrepreneurship. Tier level I, II or III
sets the requirements.
