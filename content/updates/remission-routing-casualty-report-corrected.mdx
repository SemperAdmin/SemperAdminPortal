The waiver and remission pages sent both package types to DFAS on DD Form 2789
and cited 10 USC 6161 for remission. The casualty pay page set the Personnel
Casualty Report deadline at 8 hours.

DoD FMR Volume 16 Chapter 4 paragraph 9.2 sends remission requests to the
Service on its designated form and states DFAS does not process them. Paragraph
9.1 cites 10 USC 7837 for Department of the Navy remission. DD Form 2789 and
DFAS stay in place for waivers under 10 USC 2774. MCO 3040.4 requires the
report immediately on knowledge and verification of the incident. The 8-hour
figure is the notification goal.
