The BAH pages said BAH-Diff applies to a Marine whose dependents live at a separate location. They sent a barracks Marine with a spouse overseas to BAH-Diff and listed BAH-Diff for dependents left behind during a PCS or unaccompanied tour. The PCS page also ended old-station BAH at departure.

DoDFMR Vol 7A Chapter 26 paragraph 5.6.1 limits BAH-Diff to a member in single-type Government quarters who pays child support at or above the BAH-Diff rate. Paragraph 10.2.1 pays a member with a dependent BAH at the with-dependent rate. Paragraph 2.2.1 starts new-station BAH on the reporting day.
