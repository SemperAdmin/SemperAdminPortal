The appellate leave page said the S-1 officer signs the authorization, and
the convalescent leave page said the medical authority sets the duration.

MCO 1050.16A paragraphs 4 and 7 make the officer exercising general
court-martial jurisdiction the approval authority for voluntary appellate
leave and the ordering authority for involuntary appellate leave. DoDI 1327.06
Change 1 paragraph 3.11.a has the provider recommend convalescent leave and
the unit commander or MTF director grant it, capped at 30 days outside
childbirth.
