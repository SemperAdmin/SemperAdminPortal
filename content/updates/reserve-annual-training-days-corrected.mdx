The I&I admin pages gave annual training as 12 to 14 consecutive days and used
a 12 day example for an SMCR Marine.

MCO 1001R.1L Chapter 7 Section 2 paragraph 10.a(1) requires SMCR unit members
to perform a minimum of 14 days of annual training, 15 days including travel,
each fiscal year. IMAs perform a minimum of 12 days. Plan SMCR training
schedules and AT entries on 14 days.
