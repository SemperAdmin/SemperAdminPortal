The casualty affairs pages told S-1 sections to cut the PCR within 8 hours
through the DCIPS-Forward reporting chain. They also had the S-1 prepare DD
Form 1300, named SGLV 8283 as the death gratuity claim, and made equal or
higher CACO grade mandatory.

MARADMIN 186/25 requires a voice report to MFPC within 30 minutes and the
DCIPS-PCR spreadsheet emailed to casualty.section@usmc.mil within one hour.
MCO 3040.4 chapter 4 sets 8 hours as the notification goal from learning of the
casualty. MFPC alone produces DD Form 1300. DD Form 397 is the death gratuity
claim. A CACO must be an SNCO, warrant officer or officer, with equal or higher
grade as a preference.
