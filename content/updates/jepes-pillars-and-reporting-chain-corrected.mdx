The JEPES leader page said the category list lives in a MARADMIN, said an NCO
writes for Lance Corporals and below, and tied JEPES to a 30-day Lance Corporal
counseling rhythm. It also said weak results block promotion regardless of
rifle score.

MCO 1616.1 lists four pillars at 25 percent each: Warfighting, Physical
Toughness, Mental Agility, and Command Input. The reporting chain runs First
Line Supervisor, Evaluator, Reviewer, and Approver, with no required rank or
billet. Every active Marine follows the semi-annual cycle. The order requires
one initial written counseling within 30 days.
