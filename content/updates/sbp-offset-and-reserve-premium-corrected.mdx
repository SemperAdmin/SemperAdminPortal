The Survivor Benefit Plan page said the annuity is subject to a Social Security
offset and said SBP and VA Dependency Indemnity Compensation might interact. It
also priced every Reserve retiree premium at a flat 6.5 percent of the base
amount.

DoDFMR Volume 7B Chapter 42 ended the Social Security offset on 1 April 2008 and
phased out the DIC offset between 1 January 2021 and 31 December 2022. Chapter
45 paragraph 3.2.1 gives a Reserve retiree the more favorable of 6.5 percent of
the base amount or 2.5 percent of the threshold amount plus 10 percent of the
base above it.
