The SMP and PLC page said a commissioned PLC graduate arrives at I&I ready to
drill in an SMCR unit. It tied SMP members to NROTC unit enrollment and listed
disenrollment or an enlistment contract after a PLC course failure.

MCO 1001R.1L Chapter 2 Section 3 paragraph 4 limits SMP to PLC members drilling
in SMCR units. Commissioned PLC members transfer to the IRR and stay there until
TBS completes. A Marine who does not complete the PLC course returns to the
parent SMCR unit.
