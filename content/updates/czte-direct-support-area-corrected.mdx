The CZTE and federal tax withholding pages said service in a Direct Support
Area qualifies for CZTE. The Bahrain answer said CZTE applies the same as
direct combat zone service.

DoD FMR Volume 7A Chapter 44 paragraph 2.3.2.5 adds a condition. DSA service
qualifies only in a month you also draw hostile fire pay or imminent danger pay
tied to the combat zone. Without one of those pays, your pay stays fully taxed
for the month.
