The R&R page said Marines deployed about 9 months qualify and a Marine
elects SR&R when signing the OCONUS extension agreement. The admin R&R page
said the deployed commander selects between R&R and Special R&R.

DoDI 1327.06 Change 1 paragraph 3.10.i.(2)(b) grants R&R leave only after at
least 12 months at a designated R&R duty location. MARADMIN 450/26 paragraph
7.d has the Marine select the incentive when executing it. Special R&R is an
overseas tour extension incentive the Marine elects.
