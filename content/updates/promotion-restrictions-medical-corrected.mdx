The promotion pages listed limited duty, MEB, pending PEB, and adverse FITREPs as promotion restrictions. One page said a return from limited duty restores eligibility and NJP restriction time follows the forfeiture and reduction periods.

MCO P1400.32D paragraph 1203.4a states the enlisted promotion system has no medical prerequisites. Hospitalization and temporary limited duty do not remove eligibility or selection outside the listed exceptions. Paragraph 1204.4j sets NJP at 3 months from the date awarded. A punitive reduction adds the full time in grade for the lower grade.
