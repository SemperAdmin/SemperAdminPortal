MARADMIN 341/26 suspends paragraphs 6.b, 6.c, and 6.d of MARADMIN 188/25.
Marines no longer qualify for Special Leave Accrual through deployable ship
duty, other designated duty, or direct support to a contingency operation
until the Secretary of the Navy publishes the underlying designations.

Requests already in the system under a suspended paragraph return without
action. Resubmit under paragraph 6.a when the Marine served in a hostile
fire or imminent danger pay area, since 6.a survives the suspension intact.
Anyone counting on a suspended category to protect leave above the 60-day
ceiling needs a new plan before the fiscal year closes.
