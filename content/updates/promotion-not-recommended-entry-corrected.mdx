The PFC and LCpl promotion page said a chain-of-command Page 11 suspends the
automatic promotion. A unit cutting only the Page 11 saw the Marine
promote anyway.

MARADMIN 559/23 paragraph 2 puts the stop in the NOT RECOMMENDED entry. Report
it in the MCTFS promotion module, or by unit diary, before the 1st of the
month. Every NOT RECOMMENDED entry also needs a Page 11 entry, but the Page 11
alone does not stop the promotion.
