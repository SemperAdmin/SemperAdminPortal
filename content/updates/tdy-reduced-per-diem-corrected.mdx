The TDY pages said per diem drops to 75 percent after 30 days and to a flat 55
percent after 180 days. They also said TDY over 180 days converts to PCS and
AEA tops out near 150 percent of the lodging cap.

JTR 020308 makes reduced per diem a discretionary action the AO requests before
travel. No tier applies on its own. JTR 010206-B stops per diem on the 181st
day of TDY unless the Table 1-7 authority approved it in advance, and MMIB-3 or
the Secretary approves for Marines. TDY over 180 days does not change to PCS
automatically. JTR 020307 allows AEA up to 300 percent of the locality per diem
rate. Check any authorization built on the old tiers.
