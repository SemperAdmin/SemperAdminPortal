The life events pages told Marines to submit NAVMC 10922 within 10 working
days of a marriage, birth, adoption, divorce, death, or custody change. They
also said a late filing loses past entitlement and BAH starts on the
submission date. Leader pages cited MCO 5000.14D as the source of the deadline.

MCO 1751.3 chapter 1 paragraph 1.f requires substantiating documentation
within 30 days of the life event. No source sets a 10 working day window or a
forfeiture. Late filing delays payment. It does not erase entitlement back to
the event date. The pages now state the 30-day rule. Brief Marines on the
longer window and still push for filing early. Leaders and admin should also
list the Marine as claimant and an authorized admin attesting officer on the
form. No spouse signature or witness applies.
