The administrative investigations page told S-1 staff a confidential MEO
report triggers no command inquiry and a commander dismisses a formal MEO
complaint on eight grounds, both under MARADMIN 380/26.

MARADMIN CANX-014/26 cancelled MARADMIN 380/26 to add definitions and
refinements. MARADMIN 466/26, signed 1 October 2026, replaced it. Follow
MARADMIN 466/26 and MCO 5354.1G, and confirm the lane before you open an
investigation file.
