The GTCC pages had the unit commander sign the 61, 91, and 121 day memoranda
and the misuse memorandum, approve exemptions, and designate Mission Critical
status. They had the APC cancel the IBA on separation after the DMM cleared
debt, and had the losing APC transfer the account on PCS. They also implied
contractors held a non-standard card, tied limits to rank, and required a signed TTRA statement.

The DoD GTCC Regulations annexes show the APC signs the memoranda and the
commander acknowledges receipt. MCO 4600.40C sends exemption requests to the
CPM via the HL3 APC. Appendix B sets Closed Temporary Block on separation with
the commander ensuring payment. The gaining APC pulls the account at check-in,
the vendor charges off at 211 days past billing, and contractors hold no GTCC.
