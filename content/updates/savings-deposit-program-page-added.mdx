The Savings Deposit Program pays 10 percent a year, compounded quarterly, on up
to 10,000 dollars set aside while deployed. A Marine qualifies while drawing the
Combat Zone Tax Exclusion, after 30 consecutive days in an eligible area or one
day in each of three consecutive months.

The new page covers eligibility, which pay is depositable and which is not, the
two contribution methods, and how the money comes back. Three points carry the
most weight for a Marine.

- The account closes and every dollar returns by direct deposit 120 days after leaving the combat zone, with no action required.
- Withdrawing earlier runs through myPay, and any allotment has to stop first.
- Interest accrues from the day the disbursing office takes the money, so starting in month one of a deployment beats starting in month seven.

Until now the portal named the program twice as a bare bullet on the financial
management page and explained none of it. The combat zone tax exclusion page,
which covers the entitlement gating eligibility, never mentioned it at all. Both
pages now link through.

Sourced from Finance Policy Manual Volume 3 Chapter 8 paragraph 080409, dated
September 2026, with allotment mechanics from Volume 1 Chapter 18. The September
update did not change the program rules themselves.
