The TDY per diem page told Marines their M&IE drops 25 percent for breakfast,
30 percent for lunch, and 45 percent for dinner whenever government meals are
available. It also listed laundry on long TDY, meal transportation, and phone
calls as incidentals.

JTR Table 2-17 sets a proportional meal rate built by averaging the standard
Government meal rate and the meals portion of the locality rate. It applies
only when you lodge in Government quarters on a U.S. installation with meals
directed, or when you receive deductible meals at no cost and are not
traveling. JTR 020102 lists the incidentals. Laundry counts only OCONUS, and
CONUS laundry is a personal expense.
