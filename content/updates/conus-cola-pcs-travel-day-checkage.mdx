PAAN 03-26, published 3 September 2026, tells finance offices how to deduct
CONUS COLA for PCS travel days. No COLA pays for the days authorized for
travel between the old and new duty stations. The deduction runs from the
first travel day through the day you report. Leave taken before travel is not
deducted. New duty station COLA shows from the day after you report.

This entry also corrects the portal. The Marine CONUS COLA page said COLA
ran until the day before you report to the new duty station, with no mention
of the travel-day bar. The page now carries the rule and a worked example.
