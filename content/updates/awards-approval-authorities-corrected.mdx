The awards overview listed NAM approval at O-4 commanders and NC approval at
O-5 commanders. It put NX, DSM, SS, DFC, NM, and BSM all at SECNAV. The foreign
awards page sent every personal award for foreign military personnel to SECNAV.
The Purple Heart page sent every mTBI reclama to CMC.

MARADMIN 077/25 delegates NAM to O-5 commanders and NC to O-6 commanders. A
Recruiting Station commanding officer holds NAM at O-4. DFC for non-combat
service sits at O-9, and BSM sits with component commanders who sub-delegate
to O-8. Component commanders approve the CAR and sub-delegate to O-7. CMC
approves the NC at O-6 equivalent and below and the NAM at O-4 equivalent and
below for foreign military personnel. CMC retains only mTBI reclamas for
wounds before 15 April 2011. Later reclamas through 30 August 2021 go to
MARCENT or MARFORSOC.
