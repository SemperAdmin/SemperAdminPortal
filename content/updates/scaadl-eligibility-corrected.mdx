The illness and injury pay page listed the SCAADL tiers as 1-12, 13-24, and
25-36. It said other federal in-home services are considered in the
calculation, and it said a Marine receives the higher of SCAADL and VA Aid and
Attendance.

DoD FMR Volume 7A Chapter 13 paragraph 3.4.2 sets the tiers at 1 to 12, 13 to
20, and 21 or higher. Paragraph 3.3.1.1 bars SCAADL when another federal agency
provides outpatient or in-home services. Paragraph 3.3.1.2 stops SCAADL once the
veteran receives VA special monthly compensation under 38 U.S.C. 1114(r)(2). No
higher-of-two election exists.
