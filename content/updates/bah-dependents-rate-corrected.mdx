The PAC page for BAH with dependents told admin to verify the family address
matches the duty station zip code area before paying the standard rate. It
also limited BAH-DIFF to members whose dependents live apart, and it treated a
full-time student under 23 as an ordinary child dependent.

MCO 7220.56A enclosure 1 chapter 1 paragraph 1.a sets the rate by grade,
dependency status, and PDS, wherever the dependents live. The step now verifies
dependency status. MCO 1751.3 chapter 1 paragraph 5.a(3) ties BAH-DIFF to
dependents in another person's custody and members in single-type quarters. A
student aged 21 to 22 is a secondary dependent who needs a DD Form 137, over 50
percent support, and annual recertification. Recheck any case denied on address
alone.
