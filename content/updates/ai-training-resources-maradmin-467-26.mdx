MARADMIN 467/26 is TECOM's quarterly catalog of data management and AI
training for Marines and government civilians. It adds Warfighting
Acquisition University, GenAI.mil live events, Google Skills, War Data
Platform training, Enterprise Information Services training, Microsoft
Organizational Skilling, and Maven Smart System training on SIPR, alongside
MCeLE, Digital University, JKO, USNCC, and NPS.

MARADMIN 472/26 corrects paragraph 3e. The WarU address in the original
message pointed at JKO. The correct address is waru.edu.

Nothing new is required of you. The Basic AI Course stays mandatory by
31 December 2026 under MARADMIN 214/26. The AI and Data Management Training
page now follows MARADMIN 467/26, and nine new entries sit on the links
portal.
