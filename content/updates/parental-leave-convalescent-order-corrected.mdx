The parental leave and convalescent leave pages placed a six-week birth
parent convalescent period inside the 84 days of parental leave. Both points
were wrong.

DoDI 1327.06 Change 1 paragraph 3.11.c.(3)(a) authorizes 12 weeks of
parental leave following convalescence, separate from it and in addition to
it. MARADMIN 051/23 paragraph 3.c removed the standardized six-week Maternity
Convalescent Leave. A health care provider now recommends convalescent leave
in writing for a diagnosed condition, and the commander approves it, with no
fixed length.

A birth parent whose leave was planned as 84 days total has convalescent
leave plus the full 84 days. Check any approved leave schedule built on the
old figure. Reserve Component maternity leave under MARADMIN 191/26 keeps its
six-week convalescent period and is unchanged.
