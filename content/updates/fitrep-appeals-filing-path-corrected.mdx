The appeal pages sent the petition up the chain of command with commander and
general officer endorsements. They had the S-1 route substantive corrections
through MMSB and listed a vague filing window. They also called the PERB
decision final.

MCO 1610.7B Chapter 10 paragraph 3a lets only the Marine and appointed counsel
petition. The Marine submits a DD 149 straight to CMC (MMRP-13) by email or
mail, and the S-1 assists only. Paragraph 3c(2) sets three years from finding
the error. Paragraph 4d has the PERB grant or disapprove. A disapproval
forwards to the BCNR automatically.
