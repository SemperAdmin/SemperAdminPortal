The aviation commander pages named an Aviation Commander's Course at MAWTS-1
as mandatory before assumption. They also listed an M&RA waiver for a late
arrival. The pages cited MCO 5100.29C Enclosure 1 for both points.

MCO 5100.29C Volume 1 Chapter 5 paragraph 050302.A.2 requires commanders of
aviation organizations who are aircraft reporting custodians to complete the
School of Aviation Safety Aviation Safety Commanders course within two years
before assumption of command. MARADMIN 270/05 paragraph 4.b sets no waiver
path. MMOA coordinates orders to meet the six-month timeline to the maximum
extent possible.
