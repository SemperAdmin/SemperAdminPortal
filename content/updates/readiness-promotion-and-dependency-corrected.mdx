The readiness pages called C the Equipment On-Hand rating, built the P-rating
from three calculations, and swapped R and T. The officer promotion page had
S-1 route a presidential warrant and cut the MCTFS transaction. The PAC page
set a 10 working day clock and cited MARADMIN 661/19 for annual revalidation.

MCO 3000.13B defines C as the lowest of P, S, R, and T. P uses the lower of
personnel strength and critical MOS, R is equipment condition, and T is
training. MCO 1400.31D paragraphs 6002 and 6007 make the promotion MARADMIN
the authority and have CMC (MMPR-1) make the MCTFS entry. MCO 1751.3 sets no
PAC clock and limits annual re-determination to secondary dependents.
