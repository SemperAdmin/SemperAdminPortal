The command investigation pages set a preponderance standard for line of duty
and misconduct findings. They also let a litigation-report investigation cover
a death, had the legal advisor release litigation reports, named SecNav as the
action authority for courts of inquiry, and told the IO to collect signed
witness statements under oath.

JAGINST 5800.7G section 0214 requires clear and convincing evidence to rebut
the line of duty presumption, the mental responsibility presumption, and the
short unauthorized absence presumption. It also applies to a finding of
intentional harm by a deceased member. Section 0210 bars litigation-report
investigations for any active duty death. Under section 0210.d the IO takes no
signed or recorded witness statements, and under section 0210.h OJAG Code 15
alone releases the report. Under section 0211.j the CA endorses a court or
board report or returns it. Under section 0207.c military witnesses are subject
to an oath requirement and civilians are asked, not required.
