The permissive TAD page said transition PTAD prepares any Marine for
separation or retirement and listed spiritual retreat PTAD of 3 to 5 days as
an authorized category.

MCO 1050.3J Chapter 5 paragraph 1c(11) authorizes transition PTAD only for
Marines being involuntarily separated, in seven listed categories. CONUS
Marines receive up to 20 days. A voluntary EAS does not qualify. Chapter 2
paragraph 3.f treats a retreat as a reason to grant leave, not PTAD.
