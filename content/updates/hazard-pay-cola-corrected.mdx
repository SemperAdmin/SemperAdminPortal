The pay pages treated Hardship Duty Pay-Location and Hostile Fire or Imminent
Danger Pay as independent, both paying in full. They limited Hostile Fire Pay
to areas outside the imminent danger list. The COLA page called all COLA
taxable and placed Hawaii and Alaska under CONUS COLA.

DoD FMR Volume 7A Chapter 17 paragraph 3.1 caps HDP-L at $100 in any month you
draw HFP or IDP. Chapter 10 paragraph 2.2 example 4 pays HFP for the month of a
certified hostile fire event inside an IDP area. Paying both for one month is
the error. Chapter 68 paragraph 1.1 makes OCONUS COLA, including Hawaii and
Alaska, non-taxable. CONUS COLA stays taxable. HFP rose to $450 on 1 October
2026 under MARADMIN 433/26, which exceeds the $325 combined cap. Route an Ask
MPO request.
