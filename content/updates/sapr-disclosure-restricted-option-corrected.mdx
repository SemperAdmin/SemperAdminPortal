The sexual assault reporting page said a report becomes Unrestricted when the
victim tells a non-confidential person. It listed the chain of command, NCIS,
and MP or MAA as people who receive an Unrestricted Report.

MCO 1752.5C chapter 1 paragraph 2.b(1) says telling others, except chaplains,
VLCs, and healthcare personnel, risks removing the Restricted option. It does not
file a report. Paragraph 3 limits report intake to a SARC or SAPR VA. A victim
who told the chain of command contacts a SARC to learn what status remains.
