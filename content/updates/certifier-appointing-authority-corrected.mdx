The certifier page had the S-1 officer sign every authorization and set a
Staff Sergeant with sufficient date of rank as the threshold. It listed MCTFS
and UD-MIPS certifier training and a security manager countersignature.

MCTFSPRIUM 2-20104 paragraph 1a has the CO, OIC, I-I or PAC Director delegate
the authority on a DD Form 577. The candidate holds pay grade E-6 or above with
PMOS 0111, or is a GS-7 or above. The COL Foundations course is annual within
365 days and reports with codes ZL and ZM. Termination uses the DD Form 577.
