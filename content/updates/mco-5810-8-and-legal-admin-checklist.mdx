MCO 5810.8, Criminal Justice Information (CJI) Reporting Requirements and
Guidance, dated 10 August 2026, cancels MCBul 5810 of 22 May 2024. The CJI
reporting pages now cite the order.

The IGMC Legal Administration checklist revised 21 September 2026 follows
the order. It grows from 45 to 47 items.

- New item 0502. Page 11 counseling for a rehabilitation period carries the
  required deficiency, corrective action, and consequence language.
- New item 0603. A member convicted of a domestic violence offense is
  restricted from government weapons and ammunition.
- Items 0115 to 0117 are reworded. Every item after each insertion moved
  down one code. Six CJI items now cite MCO 5810.8. MCO 1900.16 references
  move to Change 3.

The inspector guide for this checklist is off until a guide rebuilt against
the 21 September 2026 revision passes review.
