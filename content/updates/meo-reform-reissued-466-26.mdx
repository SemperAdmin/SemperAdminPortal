MARADMIN CANX-014/26 cancelled MARADMIN 380/26 on 29 September 2026, and
the portal told readers to follow MCO 5354.1 until a replacement published.
MARADMIN 466/26, signed 1 October 2026, is the replacement. It restores the
confidential complaint option, the anonymous allegation rule, conflict
resolution, the eight dismissal grounds, the five deadlines, and the GCMCA
appeal.

What changed from 380/26. A confidential complaint stays open up to 60
calendar days and closes on day 61 if not converted. EOAs are the only
personnel authorized to process one. Confidentiality breaks on written
waiver, a clear and present risk to health or safety, intent to commit a
future crime or fraud, a disclosure duty under federal law, state law, or
Department of War policy, or a court order. Cite MARADMIN 466/26 in place of
380/26 in local directives and complaint packages.
