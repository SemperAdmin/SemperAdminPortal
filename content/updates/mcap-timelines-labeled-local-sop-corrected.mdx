Several S-1 pages cited MCO 5000.14D or DoDI 8500.01 for hard windows. They
claimed a 24-hour account closure finding, a 5 working day MISSA escalation,
a 30-day open case finding, an immediate finding for a missed monthly audit,
and fixed PAC processing windows.

None of those windows appear in the sources. DoDI 8500.01 has no 24-hour rule.
PAA 02-26 paragraph 5 requires access termination on transfer or separation.
MCO 5000.14D enclosure 6 has the MISSO submit trouble calls beyond its
capability to MISSA, and enclosure 5 paragraph 4.f requires the join and second
stage audits. The pages now treat the other windows as local SOP.
