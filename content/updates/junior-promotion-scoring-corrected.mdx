The junior enlisted promotions page said Sergeant promotion requires reenlistment and called the requirement unchanged. The leader board prep page said E-2 through E-5 promote on composite scores built from conduct and proficiency marks.

MARADMIN 222/26 paragraph 3.a cancels the Small-Unit Leader Initiative and lists no reenlistment requirement for Sergeant. Promotion to Sergeant rests on JEPES, 30 months TIS, 12 months TIG, and PME. MCO 1616.1 replaces the legacy proficiency and conduct system with JEPES. PFC promotion is time based. LCpl through Sgt run on JEPES scores against monthly cutting scores.
