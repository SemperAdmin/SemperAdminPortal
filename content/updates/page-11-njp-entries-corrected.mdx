The Page 11 pages listed NJP results, court-martial outcomes, routine training, and policy acknowledgments as Page 11 entries. The OMPF receipt and handover pages also called for a Page 11 on every record receipt or handover.

MCO P1070.12 paragraph 4007.1 records NJP on the Unit Punishment Book, and paragraph 4007.2 does not authorize court-martial entries. Paragraph 4006 bars using Page 11 as a local training record or for acknowledgment of local regulations. Receipt and handover of records are not Page 11 matters.
