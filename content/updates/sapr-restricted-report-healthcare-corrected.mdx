The sexual assault reporting page and the MCO 1752.5C citation entry listed
healthcare personnel, and in the citation entry chaplains, as people who
receive a Restricted Report. The citation entry also said a Restricted Report
reaches no command and named the commander as chair of the Case Management
Group.

MCO 1752.5C Enclosure 1 chapter 1 paragraph 3 says only a SARC or SAPR VA
receives a Restricted or Unrestricted Report. Healthcare personnel and
chaplains help the victim contact a SARC. The installation command receives a
notice with no personally identifiable information. The installation
commander, or the deputy, chairs the monthly Case Management Group.
