The leader pages placed Sergeants in JEPES and said FITREPs began at Staff
Sergeant and ran to O-10. They also said JEPES results fed promotion for every
grade through E-5.

MCO 1616.1 covers the grades Private through Corporal. MCO 1610.7B covers
Sergeant through Major General, and Generals and Lieutenant Generals receive no
evaluation. JEPES scores drive the monthly competition for promotion to
Corporal and Sergeant. Promotion to PFC and LCpl does not use JEPES.
