The Survivor Benefit Plan page started the 90 day election clock at the Notice
of Eligibility for Retirement at age 60. It also said an Option B survivor
receives a reduced benefit if the member dies before age 60.

MCO 1001R.1L Chapter 12 Section 9 paragraph 3.c(4) starts the 90 days at the
20 year eligibility notice, elected on DD Form 2656-5. A married member who
misses the window receives full spouse coverage by default. Paragraph 4.a(2)
starts Option B on the member's 60th birthday date at 55 percent of retired
pay, or a lesser elected base. Recheck any election counseling built on the
age 60 clock.
