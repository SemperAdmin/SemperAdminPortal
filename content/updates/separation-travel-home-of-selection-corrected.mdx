The separation pay page gave home of selection travel to retirees only and sent
every other separating Marine to the home of record. It also said DFAS
typically pays final pay within 30 days of separation.

JTR paragraph 051003 pays travel to a home of selection after retirement, or
after separation with severance or separation pay following 8 or more years
of continuous active duty with no single break over 90 days. Other separations
travel to the home of record. No archive paragraph sets a 30 day final pay
window, so the page now points Marines to the final LES.
