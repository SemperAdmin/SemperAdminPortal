The commissary and exchange page said IRR members and their families hold only
limited access, and it grouped Standby members with the IRR.

MCO 1001R.1L Chapter 12 Section 3 paragraph 2.a gives members of the Ready
Reserve, IRR included, unlimited exchange and commissary privileges on the same
basis as active duty. Paragraph 2.b limits Standby Reserve members to the
entitlement to buy necessary uniform clothing, accessories, and equipment.
Stop counseling IRR members to expect restricted access.
