MARADMIN Cancellation 009/26 pulled MARADMIN 349/26 on 2026-08-07 due to an
administrative error. MARADMIN 357/26, released the same day, reissues the
Prohibited Activities and Conduct to Military Equal Opportunity realignment
with identical policy effect. Nothing about the program change itself moved.
Definitions, reporting paths, timelines, and command authorities hold.

One action. Anywhere a local directive, brief, or turnover binder cites
MARADMIN 349/26 for the MEO realignment, swap the citation to MARADMIN
357/26. The portal registry now routes the realignment to the new message
and marks 349/26 cancelled.
