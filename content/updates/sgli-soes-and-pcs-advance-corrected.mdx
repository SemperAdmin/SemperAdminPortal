The pay pages named the paper SGLV 8286 as the SGLI election record. They also
said a Marine elects the PCS advance amount up to a grade-based cap, with up to
three months for E-4 and above on commander approval.

DoDFMR Vol 7A Chapter 47 requires members to manage SGLI coverage,
beneficiaries, and election changes in SOES. Members with full-time coverage no
longer complete the paper SGLV 8286. Chapter 32 Table 32-1 note 7 grants one
month of basic pay on request. Only the Secretary concerned approves two or
three months, and only on a hardship showing.
