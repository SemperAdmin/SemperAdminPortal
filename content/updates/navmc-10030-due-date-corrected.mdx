The Day 0-7 turnover page said the NAVMC 10030 was due within 90 days of
assuming command.

The form instructions say all commanders at the O-5 level and above complete
and sign the NAVMC 10030 within 30 days of assuming command. Confirm the CROSS
CDRM appointment in the first week so the 30-day deadline holds.
