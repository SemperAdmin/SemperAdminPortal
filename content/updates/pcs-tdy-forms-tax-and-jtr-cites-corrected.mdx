The DLA page said RITA and WTA offset the tax on DLA. The PCS overview named
DD Form 1614 as the Marine PCS order. The TDY page cited JTR Chapter 4 for air,
rental car and local transportation.

JTR paragraph 053611 limits the Relocation Income Tax allowance to civilian
employees, so a Marine's DLA stays taxable with no offset. DD Form 1614 is the
civilian travel authorization. The TDY rules sit in JTR Chapter 2 paragraphs
020206 and 020209 to 020213. Chapter 4 covers government-funded leave.
