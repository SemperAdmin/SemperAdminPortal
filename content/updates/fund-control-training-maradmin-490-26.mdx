MARADMIN 468/26 canceled MARADMIN 350/11 on 30 September 2026. MARADMIN
490/26 canceled 468/26 on 13 October 2026. The tier system is gone.
Personnel are either Funds Control Personnel (FCP) or Non-Comptroller
Personnel. Every FCP holds a written Delegation of Authority.

Commanders complete the Funds Control for Commanding Officers course within
two weeks of assuming command, then Appropriations Law refresher training
every three years while in command. Comptrollers, Deputy Comptrollers, and
Budget Officers add annual ADA training and the DON Budget Execution Course
once in their DON tenure. The semiannual report is now due 31 March and 30
September. An FCP out of compliance loses authority to approve obligating
documents.

The commander turnover and battle rhythm pages, and the commander
obligations dashboard, carried the canceled tier model and the old 7 April
and 7 October dates until this change.
