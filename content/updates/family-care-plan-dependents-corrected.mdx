The Family Care Plan page listed four groups who must keep an FCP. It called
a plan voluntary for a Marine with a civilian spouse and children. It defined
short-term care as 30 days or less and let a single service member serve as
caregiver.

MCO 1740.13D paragraph 4.a.(2)(b) requires every Service Member with a
dependent to have a validated FCP. Only dual-military couples with no
dependents are exempt. Paragraph 4.b.(5)(c)1 sets short-term care at a
24-hour response and up to 14 days. Paragraph 4.a.(2)(d) bars any Service
Member from serving as a caregiver. A Marine who skipped an FCP because a
civilian spouse is home needs one now.
