The utilization categories and Ready Reserve pages called pay group B SMCR with
reduced participation and put IMAs in pay group F. The Ready Reserve table also
gave the IRR pay group P. The utilization page set a 4 hour minimum for funeral
honors duty.

MCO 1001R.1L Chapter 3 Section 1 paragraph 3.b sets pay group A for SMCR unit
personnel, B for IMAs, F for SelRes personnel attending IADT, and Q for the
Active Reserve. Chapter 3 Section 2 paragraph 4.c sets a 2 hour minimum for
funeral honors duty, including travel. Check every pay group entry built on the
old letters.
