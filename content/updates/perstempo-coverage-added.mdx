MARADMIN 436/26 summarizes DOWI 1336.07 for the Total Force and cancels ALMAR
026/01, ALMAR 052/01, MARADMIN 481/03, and MARADMIN 484/04. A unit SOP citing
any of the four is out of date.

Three new pages carry the framework. The S-1 page holds the reporting mechanics,
the MCTFS 112 monitoring workflow, the 730-day look-back, and the waiver package.
The Marine page covers the MOL self-service report and how to contest a count.
The commander page covers the 120-day identification obligation and the decision
between requesting a waiver and resourcing the mission differently.

The rule driving most disputes is the day away rule. A creditable event spans
more than one calendar day. The departure day counts as a full day away. The
return day does not count.

One correction rides along. The High-Deployment Allowance page had listed five
PERSTEMPO categories, two of them without a source paragraph, including a
hostile fire category appearing in no authority. That block now carries the
MARADMIN 436/26 taxonomy, plus a table separating the 401-day HDA threshold from
the 400-day PERSTEMPO waiver threshold.
