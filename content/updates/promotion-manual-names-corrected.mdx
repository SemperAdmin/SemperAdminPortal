Two registry entries listed the promotion manuals under the wrong names.

- MCO 6110.3A, body composition. MCO P1400.32D read Officer Assignment
  Manual and MCO P1400.31C read Enlisted Assignment Manual. P1400.32D is
  the Promotion Manual, Volume 2, Enlisted Promotions. P1400.31C is
  Volume 1, Officer Promotions, now MCO 1400.31D.
- MCO 1070.1, records. MCO P1400.31C read Enlisted Promotion Manual
  Volume 1. It is Volume 1, Officer Promotions, now MCO 1400.31D.

The MCO 1400.31D entry no longer says S-1 pages cite the C revision. Those
pages moved to the D revision on 2026-10-20. See the superseded order
letters entry.

Check any local SOP or turnover binder using the old names.
