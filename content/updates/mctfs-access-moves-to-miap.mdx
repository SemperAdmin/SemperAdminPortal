PAA 01-26 took effect 1 September 2026. The portal reflects it on 20 October,
so read this as a catch-up notice.

- Reflection Desktop, the locally installed application, is scheduled for disablement and removal from Marine Corps workstations on or after 31 August 2026 for security compliance.
- Commands owed a check before 1 September. Every person needing MCTFS holds established and validated MIAP access.
- Effective 1 September 2026, personnel use MIAP to access MCTFS.
- The change covers the access method only. MCTFS permissions, roles, and authorized procedures stay as they were.

Any user who has not validated MIAP access needs to fix it now. The advisory
lists the DISA help line, 1-844-347-2457, as the point of contact. The advisory
also reserves later timeline changes and added guidance for appropriate channels.
