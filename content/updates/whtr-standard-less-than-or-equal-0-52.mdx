The body composition page stated the waist-to-height ratio standard as less
than 0.52, the wording in MARADMIN 066/26. MARADMIN 073/26, 27 February
2026, rewrote the sentence. The standard is less than or equal to 0.52,
regardless of sex.

A Marine measuring exactly 0.52 meets the standard and takes no body fat
evaluation. Reporting seniors select directed comment 1 for the Marine.
