The appellate leave page said back pay follows when a discharge is set aside on
appeal. It gave no reduction and no exception.

MCO 1050.16A paragraph 9.c pays a Marine whose punitive discharge or dismissal
is set aside or disapproved for the involuntary excess leave period. The pay is
reduced by gross wages, personal service income, unemployment compensation and
public assistance received during the excess leave period. A rehearing adjudging
a punitive discharge or dismissal again bars the pay.
