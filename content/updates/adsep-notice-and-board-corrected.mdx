The legal administration overview listed admin separation notification letters
among the documents the S-1 officer signs by direction. The Marine page said a
board right applied to most OTH cases and certain other cases, and described a
board of three members led by a judge or appointed officer.

MCO 1900.16 paragraph 6302.3 requires the commanding officer to sign
notification letters and command recommendations personally, or the officer
succeeding to command signs Acting. By direction signatures are not authorized.
Paragraph 6303.3a(8) gives a board right in every case with a possible OTH
characterization and in any case at 6 or more years of total service.
Paragraph 6315.1.a requires at least three members, E-7 or above for enlisted
members, one member at O-4 or above, and an officer majority.
