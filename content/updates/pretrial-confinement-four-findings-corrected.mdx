The pretrial confinement page listed three findings for the confinement order,
offense, person, and inadequacy of lesser restraint. It also cited Article 13
UCMJ for sentence credit and said an IRO cannot sit in the accused's chain of
command.

R.C.M. 305(i)(2)(B) requires four findings. An offense was committed, the
confinee committed it, confinement is necessary because the confinee will not
appear at trial or will engage in serious criminal misconduct, and less severe
restraint is inadequate. Article 13 prohibits punishment before trial. Credit
for confinement served follows the sentencing rules and R.C.M. 305(k) and
305(l). R.C.M. 305(j)(2) requires a neutral and detached officer and states no
chain of command bar.
