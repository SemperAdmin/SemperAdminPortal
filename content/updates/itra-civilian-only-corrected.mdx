The long-term TDY page said the Income Tax Reimbursement Allowance applies to a
Marine on TDY exceeding one year and reimburses the federal tax impact. It
compared ITRA to the RITA and WTA adjustments used for PCS moves.

JTR 020313 lists ITRA as an allowance for civilians. A RIT allowance
reimburses a civilian employee, not a Service member. The page no longer tells
Marines to plan on ITRA or to coordinate it with the AO and S-1.
