IGMC republished the Defense Travel System functional area checklist
effective 20 July 2026. Item count holds at 21 across the LDTA and ODTA
subsections. Fourteen items carry new wording.

What changed for the inspected unit.

- Training evidence. Items 0105, 0106, 0205, and 0206 now accept an
  exported TRAX training record in place of individual certificates.
  Training remains effective for one year.
- Waivers. Items 0104 and 0204 add two hard rules. LDTAs and ODTAs never
  sit in a routing list as an AO. An AO holds no other DTS role. Item 0204
  now requires the first O-6 or GS-15 in the supervisory chain to sign,
  matching 0104.
- Check-in and check-out. Item 0207 adds appointment termination at
  checkout and moves the outstanding-debt check ahead of transfer or
  separation.
- Approved Status Report. Items 0110 and 0209 reframe the FDTA task as
  correcting rejects every two weeks. The 0209 reference set narrows to
  MCO 4650.39A chapter 6, paragraph 4.a.
- Unsubmitted Voucher Report. Item 0208 now covers returned vouchers under
  the same five-day process.
- Complete Traveler Information List. Item 0201 adds a check for Mandatory
  EFT Payment set to yes.

Update local desktop procedures and self-inspection binders before the
next command inspection.
