TAN 03-26, published 14 August 2026, revokes the authority to reimburse
Individual Mobilization Augmentee (IMA) Inactive Duty Training (IDT) lodging.
The revocation took effect 11 July 2026. The portal reflected it on 20 October
2026.

Lodging before 11 July 2026 stays authorized and pays through the
Defense Travel System (DTS). If you are an IMA Marine with an IDT lodging claim,
contact your IMA Operational Sponsor. The portal has not read the memoranda
behind the TAN, so it does not define the program the TAN revokes.
