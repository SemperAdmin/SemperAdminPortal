The TDY transportation page said POV travel is authorized when total POV cost
matches or beats the alternative, TDY mileage pays actual round-trip miles, and
a 350 miles per day limit applies with AO authorization above it. All three
were wrong.

JTR paragraph 020210 Table 2-10 row 1 treats a private automobile or
motorcycle as advantageous at 400 miles or less one way, with no cost
comparison. Above 400 miles one way the AO decides. Mileage pays the DTOD
official distance between official points. JTR paragraph 020302-A, applied in
DTMO UTD/CTD 46-26, allows one travel day for every 400 official miles.
