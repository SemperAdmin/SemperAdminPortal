The BAH page said a Marine on transitional PTAD without dependents draws BAH
without dependents at the transitional PTAD location rate, not the prior PDS
rate.

PAA 10-13 paragraphs 4 and 5 start BAH without dependents on departure, at the
rate JFTR Table U10E-12 sets for the PDS circumstance. The PAA examples use the
PDS zip code. Confirm the start date with your IPAC pay clerk before you
execute transitional PTAD.
