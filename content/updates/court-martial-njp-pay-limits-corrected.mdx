The court-martial pay page tied forfeitures and reductions to the convening
authority action and appellate timeline. It keyed NJP forfeiture limits to the
Marine's grade, gave officers a company-grade forfeiture, and said automatic
forfeiture always leaves two-thirds pay.

The Manual for Courts-Martial RCM 1102(b) sets both effects 14 days after the
sentence is announced. A summary court-martial effect starts on convening
authority approval if earlier. Under Part V paragraph 5.b, a company-grade
imposing officer limits forfeiture to 7 days pay and a field-grade officer to
half of one month for 2 months, for any enlisted grade. An officer faces NJP
forfeiture only from a general court-martial convening authority or a general
or flag officer in command. A general court-martial with a punitive discharge
and confinement forfeits all pay and allowances. A special court-martial with a
bad conduct discharge and confinement forfeits two-thirds pay.
