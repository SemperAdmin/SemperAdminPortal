The POV page said a second POV driven by another family member earns no
additional MALT. It also said an unaccompanied OCONUS tour earns Government POV
storage.

JTR 050203 Table 5-1 pays reimbursement for each vehicle when a Service member
and a dependent relocate on a PCS move using two POVs, including car ferry
fees for each POV. Passengers in the same vehicle draw no separate MALT. JTR
0532-A1 allows storage for one POV only in listed cases, such as an OCONUS PDS
where POV shipment is prohibited. An unaccompanied tour does not qualify on
its own.
