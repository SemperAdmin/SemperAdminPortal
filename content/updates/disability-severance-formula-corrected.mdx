The disability severance page used 12 months of basic pay per year of service. Its worked example showed $432,000 for 8 years at $4,500 a month. It also set a 6-year minimum and called the pay taxable unless exempt under CRSC rules.

DoDFMR Vol 7A Chapter 35 paragraph 5.3.1 multiplies 2 months of basic pay by the years of service, not over 19. The same case yields $72,000. Paragraph 5.3.2 sets a 3-year minimum, or 6 years for a combat-related disability. Paragraph 5.4.1 makes the pay tax-free for a combat-related injury or when the VA awards or proposes a disability rating.
