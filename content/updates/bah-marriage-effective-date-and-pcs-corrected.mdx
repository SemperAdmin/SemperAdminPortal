The marriage page said BAH back-dates only when filed on time and gave very late
filers BAH from the submission date. The PAC BAH overview
stopped old station BAH at PCS departure and placed a member with dependents
elsewhere in the without-dependent category.

DoDFMR Vol 7A Chapter 26 section 10 pays with-dependent BAH from the date the
dependent is acquired. Late filing delays payment only. Paragraph 10.2.1 pays a
member with a dependent the with-dependent rate, normally at the PDS rate.
Tables 26-38 and 26-20 continue old PDS BAH through PCS travel, leave and TDY
en route, until the member reports to the new PDS.
