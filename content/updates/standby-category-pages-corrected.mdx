These pages said Standby members have completed their obligated service and
earn limited membership points. They placed key civilian employees and
hardship cases on the ISL, and they showed no break in service for an ISL
transfer.

MCO 1001R.1L Chapter 9 Section 1 paragraph 2 places Marines who have not
completed their MSO on the ASL. Chapter 1 Section 1 paragraph 4.a(2) puts key
federal employees and temporary hardship cases on the ASL. Paragraph 8.a(2)
bars ISL members from earning points. ASL members earn points through unpaid
training. Chapter 4 Section 7 paragraph 3.c(1) makes an ISL transfer a break in
service, with a new anniversary year on return.
