The medical and dental readiness page told leaders to track Medical Readiness
Class 1 to 4 and defined Class 4 as status unknown. It defined dental Class 4
the same way.

DoDI 6025.19 Change 1 paragraph 3.1.a places every Service member in an IMR
category: Fully Medically Ready, Partially Medically Ready, or Not Medically
Ready. Paragraph 3.5.b defines DRC 4 as overdue for the annual dental
readiness assessment, which makes the Marine PMR. DRC 3 is NMR.
