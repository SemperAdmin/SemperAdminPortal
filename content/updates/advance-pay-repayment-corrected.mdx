The advance pay pages gave every Marine up to 3 months of basic pay repaid over
24 months. They described local pay as 80 percent of net pay for a missed
paycheck. They listed AWOL, court-martial, and medical hold Marines under
partial pay, and let the Marine elect any schedule up to the maximum.

DoD FMR Volume 7A Chapter 32 Table 32-1 pays E-3 and below 1 month of basic
pay and E-4 and above up to 3 months with approval. Table 32-2 repays over 12
months. The commander approves 24 months only on a severe hardship
justification. Paragraph 3.1 limits local and partial pay to overseas areas
without on-base banking, up to accrued entitlement. Paragraph 3.2 covers
emergency partial pay for missed pay inside the United States.
