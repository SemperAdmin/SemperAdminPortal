The cookies, privacy, and terms pages said the site stores only your role,
recent pages, and two acknowledgment flags. They also named a DOCX Counseling
Builder and a PFT calculator as tools with local text input.

The site also stores inspector guide checklist progress in localStorage and a
session audit log in sessionStorage. The log holds role changes and PDF or DOCX
exports, clears when you close the tab, and never leaves your browser. The
hosted input tools are the PDF Letter Builder and the Naval Letter Builder.
