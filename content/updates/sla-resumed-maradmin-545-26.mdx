MARADMIN 545/26 rescinds the MARADMIN 341/26 suspension and replaces the
MARADMIN 188/25 procedures. FY26 SLA runs under three categories.

- Operation EPIC FURY, FY26 only.
- A ship or mobile unit on the MPO designated units list, away from homeport
  or PDS more than 60 consecutive days.
- At least 120 consecutive days in a hostile fire or imminent danger pay
  area.

Other qualifying duty stays closed, and contingency operations no longer
stands as its own category.

The first Marine general officer approves, with no delegation and no by
direction signature. The package holds the NAVMC 10274, proof of assignment
or TAD attachment, and the CO's endorsement. After approval, the CO restores
the days in MOL with a comment naming the GO and the approval date, and the
S-1 keeps the package. The OMPF upload in MARADMIN 188/25 no longer applies.

Marines who lost leave on 1 October 2026 and fit a category, ask your S-1
now. Commands, pull every pending, held, and returned package. The S-1
procedural page was rewritten in full. It had carried the pre-2023 model of
a 120-day cap and commander approval by direction.
