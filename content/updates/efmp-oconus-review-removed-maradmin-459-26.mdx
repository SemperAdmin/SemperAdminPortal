The separate EFMP assignment review for OCONUS orders is gone. The change
covers the designated remote CONUS locations, Hawaii and Alaska.

EFMP-enrolled Marines start Overseas Suitability Screening on the standard
timeline when orders arrive. BUMED stays the determining authority for
medical, dental and educational suitability. The HQMC Family Member Travel
Screening Cell works with BUMED.
