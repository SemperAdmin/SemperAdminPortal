Fifty eight video pages pointed at marinenet.marines.mil addresses built
from the page slug rather than the MCeLE media id. Those links resolved to
nothing. Each one now carries the real media id taken from the video
production database.

The portal previously ran three URL conventions side by side across
marinenet.marines.mil, www.mcele.usmc.mil/mvs, and the MCeLE portal host.
As of 8 August 2026, 605 video pages use a single form:
portal.mcele.usmc.mil/content/mcele-portal/en/media/detail.html?Id=
Six pages still carried marinenet.marines.mil addresses. Their videos are
retired, and the pages came out of the portal on 15 November 2026, after the
8 August 2026 repoint.

Nine duplicate pages rendering the same video under two slugs came out at
the same time. Five hand-authored pages still carry example.com placeholder
links: fitrep-self-input, navmc-11000-walkthrough, pft-grading-overview,
promotion-board-records, and separation-packet-flow. They have no working
video until each one gets a real media id.
