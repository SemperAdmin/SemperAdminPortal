Fifty eight video pages pointed at marinenet.marines.mil addresses built
from the page slug rather than the MCeLE media id. Those links resolved to
nothing. Each one now carries the real media id taken from the video
production database.

The portal previously ran three URL conventions side by side across
marinenet.marines.mil, www.mcele.usmc.mil/mvs, and the MCeLE portal host.
All 363 video pages now use a single form:
portal.mcele.usmc.mil/content/mcele-portal/en/media/detail.html?Id=

Nine duplicate pages rendering the same video under two slugs came out at
the same time. Seven pages remain without a working link because the
production database marks the underlying video outdated or has no record of
it. Those seven stay flagged for a re-record decision rather than shipping a
guess.
