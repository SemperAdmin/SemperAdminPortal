The TASO Mainframe link page said the TASO creates the User ACID at check-in
and deletes it at check-out.

PAA 02-26 paragraph 5 lists the TASO duties. The TASO supports users in the
assigned scope, helps validate account status and access, supports suspension,
reactivation, revocation, or deletion as required, reports misuse and account
discrepancies, and ends access when people transfer, separate, or retire. The
PAA lists no ACID creation at check-in. End-user access requests now run
through DoD E-ICAM.
