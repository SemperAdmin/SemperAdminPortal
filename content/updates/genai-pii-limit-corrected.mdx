The AI field manual listed pay records and medical information as CUI content
for a DoD-authorized secured LLM. It told users to replace every placeholder
with a real value before sending a prompt.

MARADMIN 018/26 paragraph 3.a approves GenAI.mil for CUI. It does not approve
the platform for PII, PHI, or classified information. Keep pay records and
medical information out of prompts. Leave PII as placeholders and fill them in
a secure document after the output leaves the platform.
