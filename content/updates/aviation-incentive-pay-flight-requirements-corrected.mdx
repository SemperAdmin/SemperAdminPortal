The flight pay page set an annual minimum flight hour test and told the PAC to
stop AvIP when a member was medically grounded or removed from flying duty. It
also treated Table 22-11 as a rate set updated each year.

DoDFMR Vol 7A Chapter 22 paragraph 3.2.5 requires 4 hours in one calendar
month or 24 hours in six consecutive months. A certified simulator counts.
Paragraph 3.3.2 keeps a temporarily incapacitated member qualified until 365
days pass. AvIP stops on permanent disqualification under 3.3.3 or unauthorized
absence under 3.3.4. Table 22-11 carries a March 1, 2018 date, and DFAS.MIL
holds the current rates.
