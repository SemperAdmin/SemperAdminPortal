Each FA link in the commander oversight matrix was checked against the
references in the live IGMC Functional Area Checklist.

- FA 1050 Leave and Liberty linked MCO 1050.16A, the appellate leave order.
  The checklist cites DoDI 1327.06 and MCO 1050.3J. The row now links
  MCO 1050.3J.
- FA 3710.7 Aviation Operations linked MCO 3710.7, a Marine aviation
  readiness order the checklist never cites. The checklist cites
  CNAF M-3710.7, the NATOPS General Flight and Operating Instructions
  Manual, 26 times. The row now links CNAF M-3710.7.
- FA 1720.1 Suicide Prevention linked NAVMC 1720.1C, the procedures
  manual. The checklist cites MCO 1720.2A in 15 of 17 items, including
  the written appointment and signed program policy items. The row now
  links MCO 1720.2A.

Five more rows moved from legacy redirect entries to the current revision
the checklist cites. FA 1500.60, 5300.17, 1700.31, 1754.9, and 5210.
The commander actions in each row did not change.
