Commands no longer move a Reserve Marine to the IRR solely to vacate a BIC.
The command forwards a request for an excess billet indicator to the HQMC
authority, and the Marine stays put while the request is pending.

SMCR Marines in non-resident PME request EX011, approved by HQMC (RAM).
SMCR Marines on ADOS orders request EX098, approved by HQMC (RAP-2). The
Reserve Affairs memo of 17 October 2026 governs until MCO 1001R.1L changes.
