The pages said Standby Reserve members have completed obligated service and
earn limited membership points. They placed key employees and hardship cases on
the ISL. The point system page capped paid IDT at 130 points and used a 2.5
percent retired pay multiplier for every member.

MCO 1001R.1L Chapter 9 Section 1 and Chapter 1 Section 1 paragraph 4 place
Marines outside the Ready Reserve in the Standby Reserve, many before the MSO
ends. Key federal employees and temporary hardship cases belong on the ASL. ASL
members earn retirement points by training without pay. ISL members earn none.
Chapter 3 Section 2 paragraph 2.b(4)(h) stops paid IDT and paid EDPs at 48 per
fiscal year, 60 for general officers. The 130 cap covers all inactive duty
points. DoD FMR Volume 7B Chapter 1 sets the multiplier at 2.0 percent under
the Blended Retirement System and 2.5 percent for legacy members.
