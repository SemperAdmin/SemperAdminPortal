The S-1 SDAP procedural page said higher headquarters (HQMC RFM) sets SDAP
eligibility and levels. It said turnover and leave up to 90 days do not stop
pay. It cited DoDI 1304.27 and 37 U.S.C. 307 and 307a as the DoD and
statutory base.

MCO 7220.12R paragraph 4.a.(2)(a)2 gives this authority to DC M&RA, and
PDUSD(P&R) sets the rate for each level. Paragraph 5.f caps temporary duty at
90 days. Authorized leave has no cap, and pay ends the day before terminal
leave begins. DoDI 1340.26 Change 1 cancelled DoDI 1304.27 and relies on
37 U.S.C. 352.
