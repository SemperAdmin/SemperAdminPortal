The naval correspondence pages required a DD Form 577 for by direction
signing and said a signature without it voids. They cited SECNAV M-5216.5
chapters which do not match the manual, placed signing at Chapter 8 and naval
messages at Chapter 10, and said unit commanders release MARADMIN messages.

SECNAV M-5216.5 Chapter 2 paragraph 5b(1) requires every delegation of
signature authority in writing, signed by the person delegating. The manual
puts the naval letter at Chapter 7, multiple-address letters at 8,
endorsements at 9, memorandums at 10, business letters at 11, and email at
Chapter 4. It has no naval message chapter. MCO 5216.20B limits MARADMIN
release to the billets it lists.
