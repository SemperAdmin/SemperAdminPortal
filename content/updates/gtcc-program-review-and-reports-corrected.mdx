The GTCC pages described a quarterly program review, an annual root cause
analysis, and annual CBA reports. They also had APCs submit Reports IV through
IX up the chain and had the CPM run an annual review. APC refresher training
was annual.

MCO 4600.40C Enclosure 2 Chapter 1 paragraph 2 sets program reviews every two
years. A Red Marine Corps metric sends the root cause analysis and improvement
plan to the CPM by the close of the next billing cycle. Appendix B paragraph 4a
and Appendix C paragraph 21 have APCs pull, reconcile, and retain reports each
billing cycle. Commanders own the review under DoD GTCC Regulations section
041004, and APC refresher training runs every three years.
