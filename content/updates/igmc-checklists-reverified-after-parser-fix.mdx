The 11 September sweep compared pages against a parse missing the tail of
any References line the source wrapped onto a second line. Two pages it
called unchanged, 3040 Casualty Affairs and 5800.16 Legal Administration,
carried errors. Both are corrected and carry their own entry.

Every tracked checklist was pulled again on 23 September 2026 and compared
item by item on code, question, references, subsection, sponsor, effective
date, and applicability. These 17 match source with no content change:
1040 Career Planning, 1320 Sponsorship, 1610 Performance Evaluation System,
1650 Military Awards, 1700.23 Request Mast, 1700.31 Transition Readiness,
1700.37 Personal Financial Management, 1742.1 Voting Assistance, 1900.16
Separation, Retirement and Limited Duty, 4600.40 Government Travel Charge
Card, 4650.39 Defense Travel System, 5110.1 Postal Affairs Military Postal
Activity, 5110.2 Postal Affairs Organization, 5214 Reports Management, 5215
Directives Management, 5512 Identification Cards, and 5800.16 Victim and
Witness Assistance.
