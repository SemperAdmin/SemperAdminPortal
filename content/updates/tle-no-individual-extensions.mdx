The Temporary Lodging Expense page said a Marine could extend TLE beyond 14
days by routing a request through the chain of command to MMEA or MMOA. Both
claims were wrong. MPO confirmed no individual TLE extension exists, and
MMEA and MMOA hold no authority over TLE.

B4 covers restricted shipments only. MPO is the Marine Corps member of the
MAP. Stop any extension package in progress and plan the move inside the
14-day cap.
