The waiver pages cited DoDFMR Vol 7A Chapter 50, routed hearings to DOHA, and cited 10 USC 6161 as hardship-only remission for enlisted Marines. They sent remission packages to DFAS Indianapolis and sent larger waivers through HQMC.

DoDFMR Vol 16 Chapter 4 covers waiver in section 8.0 and remission in section 9.0. DCMO hearing officials hold hearings. DFAS DCMO decides military waivers up to $10,000 and DOHA decides above. A waiver application must be filed within 5 years and concedes the debt. 10 USC 7837 authorizes remission for members of any grade, and DFAS does not process remission requests.
