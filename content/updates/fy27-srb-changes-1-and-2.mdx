The portal published the FY27 SRB program from MARADMIN 023/26 alone. Two
changes followed.

MARADMIN 208/26, 5 May 2026, removes 1721 from the 84-month lateral move
kicker and ends the Zone A PMOS bonus for a lateral move into 1721. It sets
new amounts for 2141, 1721, and 6114 in Zone A, six PMOSs in Zone B, and
seven in Zone C.

MARADMIN 429/26, 22 September 2026, adds PMOS 0315 Scout. Zone A pays
$7,500 to $10,000 by grade. Zone B pays $18,630 for E5. 0315 joins the 24
and 36 month FMF Infantry kickers. Marines with an ECC from 1 October 2026
to 30 September 2027 check both changes before they reenlist.
