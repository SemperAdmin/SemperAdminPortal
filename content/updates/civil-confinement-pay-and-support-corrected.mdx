The off-base arrest playbook said full pay generally continues when a Marine is
arrested while on leave, on liberty, or on duty. The family law page named
MCO 1740.13 as the source of family support amounts and said a Family Care Plan
applies to single parents and dual-military couples.

DoDFMR Volume 7A Chapter 1 Table 1-12 stops pay and allowances during civil
confinement unless the absence is excused as unavoidable under Table 1-13. Only
the period covered by approved leave, liberty, or pass stays paid. MCO 1740.13D
covers Family Care Plans, and every Marine with dependents validates one.
MCO 5800.16 Volume 9 sets interim support at a pro-rata share of BAH or OHA for
each family member, capped at one third of gross military pay.
