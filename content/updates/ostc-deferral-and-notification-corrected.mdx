The OSTC pages said a commander never disposes of a covered offense once OSTC
exercises authority, called NJP invalid without an OSTC declination, routed
notification by the location of the offense, and named the unit CO as chair of
the CMG. They also credited MARADMIN 633/24 with a bar on transfer, retirement,
separation, and re-enlistment, and gave a 24-hour notification clock.

MARADMIN 633/24 paragraph 2 bars disposal only until a special trial counsel
defers the offense. After deferral the commander takes any action except
referral to special or general court-martial, NJP included. Paragraph 3.a
routes notification by where the accused is stationed, and the message sets
prompt reporting with no hour count. MCO 1752.5C chapter 6 makes the
installation commander or deputy the non-delegable CMG chair.
