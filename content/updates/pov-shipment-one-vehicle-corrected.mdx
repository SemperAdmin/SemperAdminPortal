The POV page said additional POVs ship at Government expense with approval
through the chain of command to MMEA or MMOA. It listed operational need and
Service Secretary approval as routes. It also cited JTR 053302 and 050805 for
shipment and MALT, and listed air shipment.

JTR 053001 authorizes one POV on a PCS to or from an OCONUS duty station. Only
two Service members married to each other ship two. No approval path exists for
a second vehicle, and Table 5-64 does not authorize air transportation. JTR
050203 governs MALT.
