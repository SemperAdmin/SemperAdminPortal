The BAS special categories page said field rations come at no cost to the
member. It also tied separate rations to BAH eligibility, and it showed
Enlisted BAS II with no conditions on the sibling overview page.

MCO 10110.47A Enclosure 1 paragraph 2 charges members for all meals or
rations the government makes available, by payroll checkage at the daily meal
rate. BAS still continues. Separate rations only ends the meal card deduction.
Check field and deployment pay records for a missing meal rate charge.
