MARADMIN 450/26 resets the Overseas Tour Extension Incentive Program and
cancels MARADMIN 202/09 and MARADMIN 259/99. A Marine of any MOS or pay grade
who extends an OCONUS assignment 12 months or more at an eligible location
picks one incentive. $2,000 in 12 installments of $166.66, 30 days of
nonchargeable SR&R leave, or 15 days of SR&R leave with a round trip to the
nearest CONUS port of entry, 20 days for an extension of 13 months or more.

The Marine picks the incentive when using it, not at the extension request.
The incentive runs only during the extension period, and a missed financial
incentive stays claimable for three years after the extension ends. From
1 November 2026, every OCONUS location qualifies except Hawaii and the Marine
Corps Forces Europe area. OTEIPs approved before 1 November 2026 keep their
incentives.

This entry also corrects the portal. Earlier pages described the Department
benefit menu instead of the Marine Corps program. They listed an $80 monthly
special pay option, a $2,000 annual bonus, a designated-specialty requirement,
election at the extension agreement, and recoupment of unearned payments. None
of those match MARADMIN 450/26. The Marine page, the SR&R leave page, and the
Renewal Agreement Travel page now match the message, and a new S-1 procedural
page covers routing, the RTD diary entry, the travel funding request, and OMPF
filing.
