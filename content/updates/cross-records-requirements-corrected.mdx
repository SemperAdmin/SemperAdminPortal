The CROSS link page said MCBul 5210 drives the records management
requirements and set a 90 day window to sign the NAVMC 10030.

MCBul 5210 carries a cancellation date of August 2026. Treat it as cancelled
until ARD names a successor. MCO 5210.11F drives the requirements. The printed
NAVMC 10030 instruction sets 30 days for commanders at O-5 and above, so
confirm the clock with ARD.
