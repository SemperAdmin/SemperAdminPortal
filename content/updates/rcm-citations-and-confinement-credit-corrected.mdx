The clemency page cited R.C.M. 1109 for matters submitted to the convening
authority and R.C.M. 1110 for vacation of a suspended sentence. It gave no day
count for the matters window. The confinement page said a missed 48-hour
review made pretrial confinement unlawful and required release.

The Manual for Courts-Martial 2024 puts matters under R.C.M. 1106 and
vacation under R.C.M. 1108. The accused submits matters within 10 days for a
GCM or SPCM and 7 days for a SCM. R.C.M. 305 sets administrative credit
against the sentence as the remedy for a missed review, not release.
