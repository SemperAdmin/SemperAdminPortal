The dependent death page said bereavement is covered by convalescent leave or
permissive TAD at the commander's discretion. It listed no bereavement leave
entitlement.

MARADMIN 220/23 paragraph 3.c establishes bereavement leave as non-chargeable
paid leave of up to 14 consecutive days on the death of a spouse or child. A
Marine with fewer than 30 days of accrued annual leave qualifies. A Marine
holding 30 or more days uses chargeable leave first until the balance drops
below 30. The commander approves it. Marines who used convalescent or annual
leave for this purpose should ask the commander to review the request.
