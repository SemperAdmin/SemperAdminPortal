A cross reference against the video production database found 90 videos
published to MCeLE with no portal page. All 90 now render under /videos.

Cognos Analytics is the largest block. Fifty seven videos run from getting
access and picking a package through list reports, filters, prompts, SQL,
joins, and date math, to rosters for leave, duty limitations, gains and
losses, and the reenlistment window, ending in a unit strength dashboard.

Command Profile gains five videos on getting in, demographics, deployment
tempo, staffing, and training. Promotions gains 20, covering SULI,
not recs and Page 11 entries, remedials, warrants in MOL, the select grade
roster, and meritorious authority references.

Pay and Entitlements adds Dislocation Allowance and spouse relicensing
reimbursement. The travel card series adds authorized expenses and
non-mandatory use. Large Language Models adds capabilities and limitations.

Twenty of these videos left the portal on 15 October while their MCeLE
publish date was missing from the database. The dates are restored, and the
pages are back.
