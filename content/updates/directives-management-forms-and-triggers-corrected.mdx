The directives pages listed draft cover sheets and coordination memoranda as
required forms. They treated command relocation as a trigger for a full audit
and said a bulletin without a cancellation marker stays in force. They also
required joint signature on every joint directive.

MCO 5215.1K names none of those forms. Chapter 1 paragraph 12 bars republishing
only to change an address or title. Assumption of command triggers a review
within one year. Chapter 3 paragraph 15g cancels a bulletin at 12 months. The
primary principal signs a joint directive by default.
