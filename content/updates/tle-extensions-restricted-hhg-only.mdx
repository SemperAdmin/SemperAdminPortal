The Temporary Lodging Expense page told Marines to extend TLE beyond the
cap for a housing shortage or other hardship by routing a request through the
chain of command to MMEA or MMOA. Both claims were wrong. MPO confirmed MMEA
and MMOA hold no authority over TLE, and no individual extension exists for a
housing delay.

JTR paragraph 050604-B4 is the only individual path. It covers restricted
household goods shipments only, up to 60 days, after the transportation
office validates the restriction. The request routes to MPO, the Marine Corps
MAP member, then to PDTATAC. Stop any extension package built on a housing
delay and plan the move inside the cap.
