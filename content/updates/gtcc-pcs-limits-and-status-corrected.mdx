The GTCC PCS page said credit limits stay at the Standard or Restricted
default during PCS, split PCS and Mission Critical into two statuses, listed
baggage replacement, meals, and ground transport as authorized uses, and
told Marines to claim lost baggage as MEA with receipt.

MCO 4600.40C Appendix D paragraph 4.c sets PCS credit limits at $25,000 with
dependents and $7,500 without. It uses one PCS-Mission Critical status. The status
stops aging, extends to 120 days, and lifts 15 days after the report date.
Accession pipeline and separation orders are excluded. MARADMIN 478/24
paragraph 3.C.4 limits the card to unanticipated official travel expenses
only. The JTR sends lost or delayed baggage claims to the carrier and the
Government Claims Office, and MEA is not part of the PCS voucher.
