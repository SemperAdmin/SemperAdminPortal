The S-1 leave overview listed NAVMC 10274 as the SLA request. Per MARADMIN
545/26 paragraph 7.c, the package holds an Administrative Action Form,
proof of assignment or TAD to a designated unit, and written CO
endorsement.

The DoDI 1327.06 entry said the Secretary designates other qualifying duty.
Per paragraph 4.c, the Secretary has designated none, so those requests
return without action.

SLA carries up to 30 days beyond the 60-day limit, a 90-day balance, and
expires at the end of the second fiscal year approved. The annual leave
pages now state the limits.
