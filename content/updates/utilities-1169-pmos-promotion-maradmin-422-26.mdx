An in-zone Utilities Gunnery Sergeant without the 1169 Utilities Chief PMOS
is not eligible for promotion consideration and stays out of the board
population. The rule covers the Active and Reserve Components.

The 1169 comes from the Utilities Chief Course, course ID M0311E2. Utilities
Staff Sergeants are eligible for the course and encouraged to attend.
Commanders give Gunnery Sergeants priority for seats.
