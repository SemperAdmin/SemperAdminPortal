The MAGTF and MEU page credited MCO P3500.72A with a defined MEU T&R event
roadmap, the MEU work-up schedule, and the MEU certification document. It also
said the MEU commander signs the certification documents.

MCO P3500.72A is the Ground T&R Program order and says nothing about MEU
certification. MCO 3120.12A is the global force management order and names no
certification signature authority. The page now states only what those orders
support.
