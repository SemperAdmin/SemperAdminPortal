The pet page listed health certificates, vaccinations, and crates. The childcare
page listed provider en route lodging. The HHG page understated several weight
allowances. The PCS per diem page paid actual lodging to every Marine and gave
commercial air 1 to 2 days. The TDY en route page called EWS TDY. The breast
milk page started the voucher clock at shipment completion.

JTR 050107 lists microchipping, quarantine, boarding, hotel service charges,
licensing, titer testing, and shipping, with $550 CONUS and $2,000 OCONUS
caps. JTR 050108-B limits provider reimbursement to commercial transportation.
JTR Table 5-37 sets O-1 to O-3 without dependents at 10,000 to 13,000 pounds
and E-6 to E-9 with dependents at 11,000 to 15,000. POV travel pays MALT Plus
with no lodging receipts, and commercial air allows one travel day. A course
of 140 or more days is a PCS, so EWS is a PCS to the school. MARADMIN 574/24
starts the 5 working days at completion of PCS travel.
