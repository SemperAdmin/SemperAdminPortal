The custody, birth, and divorce pages said BAH-DIFF requires court-ordered
child support, and voluntary payments never qualify. The custody page also
named MCO 7220.24 as the BAH-DIFF order. MCO 7220.24 covers retention bonuses.

MCO 1751.3 chapter 2 paragraph 7 says when no court order or legal
separation agreement sets the support, the member must provide an amount not
less than the BAH-DIFF rate. Voluntary support at or above the rate qualifies,
proven with non-cash records. A Marine turned away for lacking a court order
should resubmit with those records.
