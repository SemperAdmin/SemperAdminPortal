The pretrial confinement page gave the commander 48 hours to review
confinement and listed three findings for the order. The court-martial pages
had members decide the sentence, put six members on a special court-martial,
and had the staff judge advocate write a post-trial recommendation. The victim
program pages named COMMARFORCOM as an RVWLO appointing authority.

The Manual for Courts-Martial sets the commander decision at 72 hours under
RCM 305(i)(2)(A). A neutral and detached officer makes the probable cause
determination within 48 hours and reviews at 7 days under RCM 305(j). The order
needs four findings, including a foreseeable flight or serious criminal
misconduct finding. A special court-martial with members has four members, and
the military judge decides the sentence in noncapital cases under RCM 1002(b).
RCM 1106, 1106A, and 1109 govern post-trial matters and convening authority
action. MCO 5800.16 Volume 16 paragraph 040902.A names COMMCICOM, not
COMMARFORCOM.
