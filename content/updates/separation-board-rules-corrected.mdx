The pages called the administrative separation board mandatory at six years
of service or with an OTH characterization. The commander page gave the member
7 or 30 days to respond and described a three member board of O-3 and above.

MCO 1900.16 paragraph 6303.3.a(8) gives a Marine with six or more years of
service, or facing a possible OTH, the right to request a board. The Marine
requests it or waives it. Paragraph 6303.3.c gives a reasonable response
period of at least two working days. Paragraph 6315.1.a requires at least
three officer or SNCO members, enlisted members at E-7 or above and senior to
the respondent, at least one O-4 or above, and a majority commissioned or
warrant officers.
