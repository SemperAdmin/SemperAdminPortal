A cross reference against the video production database found 28 recorded
videos published to MCeLE with no portal page. All 28 now render under
/videos.

The largest block is Manpower. Twenty three videos recorded between April
and June 2026 cover what readiness means and who owns it, the C, A, P, S,
R, and T resource levels, unit level and billet level T/O validation, gap
analysis against on hand strength, out year column reading, the T/O review
battle rhythm, TPFDD basics and report types, Global Force Management and
JOPES, force synchronization, sourcing phases, the JIA process, and service
augments.

Promotions gains the three part Page 11 series on entry requirements,
writing the promotion recommendation entry, and writing the not
recommended entry. Semper Admin gains the July 2026 Meeting of the Minds
morning and afternoon sessions.

One recorded video, MCPP and FDP&E When You Get Called, has no MCeLE upload
and stays out of the portal until the upload lands.
