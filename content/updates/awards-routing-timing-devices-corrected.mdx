The routing page said a non-concurrence returns the package to the originator.
The foreign awards text sent acceptance through DoD and OSD. The timing page
set one 60-day limit and a yearly PCS season MARADMIN. The citation page ended
the meritorious period on the last duty day. The CAR page used a bronze star
and the CE&S table used 5/16-inch stars. The joint awards page added a
concurrence step.

SECNAV M-1650.1 paragraph 2.1.f has a non-concurring endorser record a lower
award or disapproval and still forward. Chapter 7 paragraph 7.2.a has CMC
(MMMA) approve foreign award acceptance. MARADMIN 093/25 is enduring guidance,
ends the meritorious period about two weeks before retirement or terminal
leave, and sets 90 days in iAPS for SECNAV awards. Appendix 2C sets a gold
5/16-inch CAR star for a different operation. CE&S awards use 3/16-inch stars.
MCO 1650.19J enclosure (1) paragraph 3.b drops Marine Corps concurrence for
defense awards.
