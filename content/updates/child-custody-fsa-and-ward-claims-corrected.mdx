The child custody page said FSA applies after 30 or more days of separation
under orders. It sent an unmarried Marine to the DD Form 137 to claim a child
established by court order.

DoDFMR Vol 7A Chapter 27 paragraphs 2.3.1.2 and 2.3.1.3 pay FSA-S and FSA-T
for more than 30 continuous days away from the homeport or duty station.
FSA-R turns on dependents not authorized to travel, with no day count. MCO
1751.3 chapter 1 paragraph 5 makes a child born out of wedlock a primary
dependent. Prove parentage on the NAVMC 10922. Use the DD Form 137 only for a
court ward.
