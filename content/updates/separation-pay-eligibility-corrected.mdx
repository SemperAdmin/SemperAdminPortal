The separation pay pages listed a Marine who requests release as eligible for
half pay. They showed a scenario paying full pay to a Marine separated for
unsatisfactory performance. Two pages
accepted a general characterization for full pay. They wrote the formulas without the 12 months of basic pay
and wrote disability severance as years times basic pay times 0.10.

MCO 1900.16 paragraph 1309 bars separation pay for a Marine separated at his or
her own request, and for misconduct or unsatisfactory performance. Paragraph
1307 states 10 U.S.C. 1174 and DoDI 1332.29 govern separation pay. DoDI
1332.29 paragraph 3.1.a.(2) and DoD FMR Volume 7A Chapter 35 paragraph 3.1.1.2
limit full pay to an honorable characterization. Paragraph 3.1.3.2 accepts
honorable or general for half pay. Paragraph 1306.1 sets full pay at 0.10 times years of service times 12 times
monthly basic pay. Half pay follows the paragraph 1308 conditions. Disability
severance pay is 2 times monthly basic pay times years of service, capped at 19
years, per DoD FMR Volume 7A Chapter 35.
