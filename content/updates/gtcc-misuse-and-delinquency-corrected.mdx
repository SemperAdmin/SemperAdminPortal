The GTCC pages told readers a Page 11 entry follows every suspension and
cancellation, cancellation comes at 120 to 180 days, and unpaid balances go to
DFAS garnishment. They listed 30, 60, 90, and 120 day stages,
said a Restricted card upgrades after 12 months of on-time payment, and listed
training course fees as a GTCC use. They also cited Article 132 for false
statements.

MCO 4600.40C names counseling through administrative separation as the
corrective actions and sets no mandatory Page 11 step. Suspension starts at 61
days past billing, the salary offset warning at 91, the commander review at
121, and charge-off at 210. An active Reduced Payment Plan in good standing
blocks charge-off. An upgrade needs a new soft pull and the minimum credit
score. The GTCC is never used for tuition or training fees. Article 107 and
Article 124 cover false statements and fraud.
