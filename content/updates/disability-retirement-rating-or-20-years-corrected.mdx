The retirement types page said disability retirement requires a rating of 30
percent or higher. It said any rating below 30 percent earns severance pay
only.

DoDI 1332.18 paragraph 7.2.b grants disability retirement at a rating of at
least 30 percent, or at any rating with at least 20 years of service. MCO
1900.16 paragraph 8104.6.b limits severance pay to a rating below 30 percent
with less than 20 years of active service.
