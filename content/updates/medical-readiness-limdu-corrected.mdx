The medical standards page listed four readiness categories, including an
Indeterminate Category 4. The LIMDU page gave an 8 month initial period, an 8
month extension, and a 16 month disposition point.

DoDI 6025.19 Change 1 paragraph 3.1.a places every Service member in one of
three categories, FMR, PMR, or NMR. MCO 1900.16 paragraphs 8102 and 8003.24
set temporary limited duty at up to two 6 month periods, 12 months cumulative.
Track disposition at 12 months, not 16.
