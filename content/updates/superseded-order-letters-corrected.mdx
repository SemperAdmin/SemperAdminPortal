A currency audit against the MCPEL found role pages naming superseded
revisions.

- Officer promotions. MCO 1400.31C is now MCO 1400.31D.
- SAPR on the incident reporting pages. MCO 1752.5A is now MCO 1752.5C.
- Family Care Plans. The leader pages cited MCO 1754.9A, the former family
  readiness order. Family Care Plans sit in MCO 1740.13D.
- ID cards and CAC. MCO 5512.11D is now MCO 5512.11F, Volume 1 W/CH-1 and
  Volume 2.
- Records. MCO P1070.12J is now MCO P1070.12K W/CH 1.
- MOS Manual. NAVMC 1200.1K and 1200.1L are now NAVMC 1200.1M, 2 March 2026.

This entry corrects the citations. The page procedures were not re-checked
line by line against each new revision. Check any local SOP or turnover
binder citing the old revisions.
