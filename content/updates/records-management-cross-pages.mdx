Two new pages cover the electronic records program. CROSS for the CDRM lists
what to upload for each of the 15 IGMC Records Management checklist items,
where it goes in CROSS, and what fails the item. The Staff Section Records
Manager page gives section leaders their duties, from the CROSS appointment
to the SharePoint Online migration.

The S-1 Records Management page described a paper program. It now follows
CROSS, the NAVMC 10030 and NAVMC 10030/1, the essential records inventory,
the Capstone cycle, and command chronology deadlines.

MCBul 5210 passed its one-year cancellation mark on 29 August 2026. Confirm
its status on MCPEL. The IGMC checklist still inspects against its standards.
