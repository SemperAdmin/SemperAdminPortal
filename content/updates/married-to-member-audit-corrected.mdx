The member-to-member audit page described a quarterly peer audit where Marines check each other's records. Related pages listed an annual audit cycle, a ten-chapter IRAM, and an annual full ESR audit per member.

MCTFSPRIUM 120103 defines the member married-to-member audit for dual-military couples. The unit completes it NLT 60 days from the join date, then at least every 12 months. The record audit occasions are gains, mobilization, pre-deployment, TAD, and post-deployment. IRAM paragraph 5000.5 adds a triennial audit. The IRAM has seven chapters.
