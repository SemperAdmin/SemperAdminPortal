The AIP page told Marines to compare a $9,800 discounted lump sum with $10,800
in monthly AIP. Its rate tables showed lump sum columns with no date limit, and
a PCS example recouped a discounted lump sum as if a new Marine took one.

MARADMIN 349/23 paragraphs 2 and 3 discontinued the discounted lump sum for all
newly assigned Marines on 1 July 2023. New assignments are paid monthly. The
lump sum columns apply only to agreements signed before 1 July 2023. A single lump
sum election remains under MARADMIN 181/26 paragraph 7.a.
