The TDY page said training over 180 days at one location triggers a PCS. The
DTS pages gave the unit AO a 5 working day approval target under MCO 4650.39A,
said the Reviewing Official reviews orders, said local commanders no longer
hold pecuniary liability, and named TECOM as the only voucher exception. The
lodging page said the AO issues the certificate of non-availability.

JTR Table 1-6 treats training scheduled for 140 days or more as a permanent duty
assignment. MARADMIN 291/12 limits the Reviewing Official role to vouchers. MCO
4650.39A requires approval within established guidelines and sets no day count.
The unit AO keeps pecuniary liability for authorizations with advances. The
voucher exceptions are TECOM Formal School Training Support and Marines not
attached to a Marine Corps DTS organization. The traveler obtains the
certificate of non-availability number through the lodging registration process.
