The BAH special categories page said barracks Marines draw no BAH and called BAH-Partial uncommon. It also said sea-duty Marines keep home port BAH and reservists on active duty draw standard BAH. The secondary dependency page sent packages to DFAS.

DoDFMR Vol 7A Chapter 26 paragraph 5.5.1 pays BAH-Partial to every member without dependents in single-type government quarters. Paragraph 10.1.5 sets the home port rule by dependents and grade. Paragraph 10.5.3 pays BAH-RC on reserve orders of 30 days or less and BAH or OHA on orders of 31 days or more. A Marine E-5 and below living off base by choice stays on BAH-Partial unless the Installation Commander approves full BAH under MCO 11000.22 Chapter 10.

MCO 1751.3 sends the DD Form 137 package through the S-1 or IPAC to CMC (MFP-1). The Commandant determines USMC secondary dependency.
