The obligated service page listed IRR and Active Reserve as DAP options, said selection survives separation only through DAP, and called refusal of PCS or UDP service a failure of selection. The failure of selection page showed the Reserve twice-passed waiver as current.

MCO P1400.32D paragraph 3102.b lets a selectee carry the selection into the reserve by reenlisting in the IRR, SMCR, or AR within 24 hours of separation. MARADMIN 590/25 limits DAP to SMCR and IMA. Paragraph 1204.4gg makes refusal to obligate service for PCS or UDP orders a curable promotion restriction. MARADMIN 477/25 ended the Reserve waiver on 1 October 2025, so twice failed for selection applies again to SMCR and IRR Marines.
