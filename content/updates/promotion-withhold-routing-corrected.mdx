The commander withhold page described a unit-level withhold with a rebuttal, 30 day review, and backdating when appropriate. The remedial page routed requests to MMEA and promised back pay for the full gap. The reductions page placed administrative reduction authority at the battalion.

MCO P1400.32D paragraph 5200 has the commander notify CMC, and CMC removes the select grade and withholds the authority to promote. Paragraph 1204.2 backdates only a delay ending in full exoneration. Paragraph 2600 sends remedial requests to CMC (MMPB-11) and allows back pay only within 6 months. MARADMIN 559/23 automates junior remedial promotions. Paragraph 6001.1d names the GCMCA as reduction authority for SSgt and above.
