Eligible judge advocates now submit FY26 JACP applications through
2026-09-30. Nothing else about the program moves. Eligibility gates,
obligation lengths, and payment tiers stay as MARADMIN 528/25 wrote them.

Both messages carry full portal entries with the tier tables and the POC
list. Route questions to HQMC JAD (JPI), MPP-30, MMOA-1, or MMOA-3. After
30 September the program reverts to the next fiscal year message cycle.
