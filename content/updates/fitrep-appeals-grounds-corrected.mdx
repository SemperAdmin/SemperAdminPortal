The FITREP appeal page listed inconsistency with the RS profile or unit pattern
as a ground for appeal. It placed the comparative assessment in Section G,
sent substantive items to MMPB-21, and described PERB members as senior
officers selected by CMC.

MCO 1610.7B Chapter 10 paragraph 3b(5) says a report is not unjust solely
because its comparative assessment mark is lower than other reports. Paragraph
1c bars relief based on perceived competitiveness. Paragraph 3b(2) requires
evidence of probable material error, substantive inaccuracy, or injustice. The
comparative assessment is Section K item 3, and Section G is Intellect and
Wisdom. Paragraph 4b(1) draws PERB members from HQMC field grade officers,
CWO4 and CWO5, E-9 SNCOs, and senior civilians.
