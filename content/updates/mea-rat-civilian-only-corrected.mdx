The MEA page gave Marines a flat rate and an itemized cap under a JTR section
0571. The Renewal Agreement Travel page gave Marines a Government-funded round
trip to CONUS when they extend an overseas tour. The PCS overview listed RAT as
a PCS type.

The JTR has no section 0571. MEA sits in section 0541 and applies to civilian
employees. Renewal Agreement Travel sits in JTR Part H section 0550 and also
applies to civilian employees. A Marine on PCS draws DLA under section 0505 and
pet costs under paragraph 050107. A Marine who extends overseas uses OTEIP
under MARADMIN 450/26, and COT leave travel under JTR 050812 applies when the
Marine moves to a second OCONUS PDS.
