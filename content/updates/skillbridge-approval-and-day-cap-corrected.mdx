The career services page listed rank-based SkillBridge day caps of 120 and 90
days, named NCMIS as the system of record, and put approval at LtCol or above,
with a General Officer for the top category. It also reported SkillBridge
Marines as PTAD (Other).

NAVMC 1700.2B paragraph 7 limits participation to 180 days from the retirement
or EAS date. Paragraph 4.a(1) gives approval to the first commanding officer or
OIC with UCMJ authority, delegable to a field grade CO or OIC with NJP
authority. Paragraph 4.a(3) reports these Marines as S-PTAD. A Marine told to
seek a LtCol or General Officer signature was routed to the wrong approver.
