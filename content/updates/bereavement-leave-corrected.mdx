The emergency leave page said Marines have no separate bereavement leave
entitlement beyond emergency leave.

DoDI 1327.06 Change 1 paragraph 3.11.j gives a Marine whose spouse or child
dies up to 14 consecutive days of non-chargeable bereavement leave. The unit
commander grants it. It combines with emergency and annual leave. A Marine
told no such leave exists should ask the commander to review the request.
