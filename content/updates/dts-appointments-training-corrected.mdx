The DTS pages said DD Form 577 is retained for 10 years, appointees train
within two weeks, travelers and NDEAs refresh every three years, and vouchers
route in 3 business days. They also said a first O-6 or GS-15 signs the grade
waiver, and the leader page put voucher liability on the unit AO.

MCO 4650.39A paragraph 4.b(6)(b) sets DD Form 577 retention at six years and
three months from release or cancellation. Chapter 5 paragraph 1.a requires
training within three months of appointment and repeats it annually. Chapter 1
paragraph 5.a sets a 2 business day command review stamp, then 3 business days
for the certifying AO. The order cites a paragraph 4.c it does not contain, so
the waiver signer is unconfirmed. Per MARADMIN 291/12, voucher liability sits
with the DO/FO approving official. Chapter 9 gives travelers 30 days to repay
before payroll collection.
