The funeral honors page listed two funerals in one day as a common error and
told admins to process them as two separate FHD entries.

MCO 1001R.1L Chapter 3 Section 2 paragraph 4.b states no more than one FHD
period is performed in a day. Submit one FHD entry for the day. A second entry
for the same day pays a period the order does not allow.
