The pages mapped the FITREP as Sections A through K with an RS comparative
assessment in Section G scored from MUE to MFE. They called B and C marks
adverse and said every Marine signs and cannot post without it. The admin pages
also had the RO sign before the MRO and used an FD report for temporary duty.

MCO 1610.7B Chapter 4 runs Sections A through L. The RO makes the comparative
assessment in Section K item 3. Chapter 5 paragraph 3e makes only an A mark in
Sections D to H adverse. The MRO signs Section J item 2 on adverse reports only
and a refusal does not stop the report. The RS signs and informs the MRO before
the RO. The parent command submits a TD report at 31 days of temporary duty.
