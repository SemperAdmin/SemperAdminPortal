The SOU pages applied the SOU to relocation and performance cases and allowed
the Admin Chief to witness it. The unsatisfactory participation page listed
return to good standing, transfer to the IRR, and involuntary orders under 10
USC 12303 as the three outcomes.

MCO 1001R.1L Chapter 4 Section 2 paragraph 5 makes the SOU a contract with a
non-mandatory participant, signed on reporting to a formal school. It runs 1, 2,
or 3 years by school length. An officer witnesses it and DC M&RA (RA) approves
it. Chapter 4 Section 3 paragraph 2 gives the commander three actions: retain
with EDPs, extend the MDPSD, or separate through CLA.
