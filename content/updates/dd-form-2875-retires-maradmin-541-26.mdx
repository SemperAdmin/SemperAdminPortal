MARADMIN 541/26 directs Functional Area Managers, Program Managers, and
System Owners of Marine Corps NIPRNET systems to retire the manual DD Form
2875 SAAR and move to the Naval Identity Services automated SAAR workflow.
The Department of the Navy deadline in ALNAV 037/26 is 30 September 2027.
Each system moves on its own NIS Program Office milestone.

The message binds system owners, not S-1 sections. Nothing changes at the
unit today. MCTFS access stays in DoD E-ICAM under PAA 02-26, and UD-MIPS and
DTMS stay in NIS. Systems still taking a paper or emailed DD Form 2875 keep
taking it until their owner publishes the new path. Watch for those
announcements and brief your section when one lands.
