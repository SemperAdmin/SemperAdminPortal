The OMPF submission page credited MARADMIN 015/20 with its metadata, scan,
routing, and timeline rules. It required the SSN, a 300 DPI scan, and a
30-day stale rule. MARADMIN 015/20 does not contain those rules.

MCO 1070.1 Chapter 4 requires the full name and DoD ID on every page and
treats the SSN as optional, encrypted when present. Scanner settings are 200
DPI, gray scale, TIFF. Marines and non-IPAC submitters normally submit within
5 working days of completed action. IPACs submit within 10 working days.
