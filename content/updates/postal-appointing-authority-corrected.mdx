The postal pages said the unit commander appoints the Postal Officer and the
unit mail clerk. They said postal training finishes before a Marine assumes
duties and an annual recertification applies.

MCO 5110.4B Enclosure 2 paragraph 1.b.(6)(a) has the Installation Commander
appoint the Marine Corps Postal Officer or MPO Supervisor in writing. The unit
commander appoints the Unit Mail Officer and assistants under paragraph
1.b.(2)(j)(1), and the Unit Mail Officer appoints mail clerks. Paragraph
1.b.(2)(j)(3) sets the Mail Clerk Indoctrination Course window at 90 days from
appointment, with 30 days for clerks. The order holds no annual recertification.
