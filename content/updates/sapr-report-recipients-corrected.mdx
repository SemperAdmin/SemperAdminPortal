The sexual assault reporting page listed a chaplain and a Special Victims'
Counsel as people who receive a Restricted Report. It also said a victim
requests expedited transfer after any report, and an SVC is available only
after a report. A victim who told a chaplain believed a report existed.

MCO 1752.5C chapter 1 says a chaplain and a VLC cannot accept a restricted or
unrestricted report. Either one helps the victim reach a SARC or SAPR VA.
Chapter 3 paragraph 7.a limits expedited transfer to victims who filed an
Unrestricted Report. A victim consults an SVC before or without filing.
