The death payments page listed the default order as spouse, children, parents,
siblings, children again, and other relatives. Siblings do not hold a place in
the order.

The DD Form 93 instructions and 10 U.S.C. 1477 set the order as the surviving
spouse, children and descendants, parents, the executor or administrator of the
estate, then other next of kin. Update your DD Form 93 if you want a different
split.
