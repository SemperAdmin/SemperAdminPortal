The audit posture and inspection pages marked each FA Commendatory,
Satisfactory, or Unsatisfactory and put the CoRE cycle at one year. They told
admin shops to pull FACs from this portal, tied CoRE+ to IG announcements, and
said the CIG inspects CoRE+ only at commander direction.

MCO 5040.6K Enclosure 1 Chapter 4 grades each FA Effective or Ineffective. Tier
I, II, and III grade the whole command. IGMC inspects every 36 months. Chapter
3 names the IGMC website the sole FAC source. CoRE+ marks CoRE FAs tied to a
capability, and the CIG inspects every applicable one. Corrective actions
open for every Finding and Ineffective FA.
