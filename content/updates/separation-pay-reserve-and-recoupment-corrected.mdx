The PAC separation pay page said a Reserve member involuntarily separated from
drilling status qualifies. It said a member who rejoins service before a set
period repays separation pay from future military pay.

DoD FMR Volume 7A Chapter 35 paragraph 3.1.1.1.1 requires 6 or more
continuous years of active duty or full-time National Guard duty immediately
before an involuntary separation from active duty. Paragraph 3.5 recoups the
gross taxable separation pay from later retired pay, retainer pay, or VA
disability compensation. It sets no rejoin repayment rule.
