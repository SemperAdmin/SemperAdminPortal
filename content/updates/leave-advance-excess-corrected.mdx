The advance and excess leave page said the chain of command sets the advance
leave cap and approves more than 30 days under local policy. It also said
excess leave earns no service credit toward retirement.

DoDI 1327.06 Change 1 paragraph 3.10.b limits advance leave to the lesser of
30 days or the leave earned through remaining service. Advance leave over 30
days needs Secretary of the Navy authorization. DoD FMR Volume 7A Chapter 1
Table 1-2 rule 1 counts excess leave as creditable service, even though it is
a no-pay status.
