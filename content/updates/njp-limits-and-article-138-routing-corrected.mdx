The NJP routing page set the company commander forfeiture at $200, limited field
grade reduction to one grade for every enlisted Marine, and told commanders to
test evidence against beyond reasonable doubt. The Article 138 page said the
next superior officer examines and redresses the complaint, and it let the CO
respond within a reasonable time.

MCM Part V paragraph 5.b limits company grade forfeiture to 7 days' pay and
lets field grade reduce E-4 and below to any lower grade, with E-5 and above
reduced one grade. NJP facts are proved by a preponderance of the evidence.
JAGMAN 0302a has the superior officer forward the complaint to the GCMCA over the
respondent, and JAGMAN 0305a gives the CO 30 calendar days to act on redress.
