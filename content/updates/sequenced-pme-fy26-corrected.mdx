The PME page told Marines not to rely on sequenced PME for the FY26 boards and cited MARADMIN 630/24.

MARADMIN 222/26 paragraph 3.b states Marines selected via Sequenced PME to Staff Sergeant on the FY26 board have 270 days to complete Sergeant PME. The selection is revoked if they miss the deadline.
