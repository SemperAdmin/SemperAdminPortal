Effective 7 July 2026, a Marine on PCS by commercial carrier (airplane, ship, or bus) draws lodging on the last day of travel to the new duty station. The rule pays the lesser of actual lodging cost or the locality lodging rate, plus 75 percent of the locality Meals and Incidental Expenses rate. It covers accession members on PCS orders. TAN 04-26 cancels TAN 01-26.

Temporary Lodging Expense (TLE) comes first. Travel-item lodging stands only when too little time passes to set up a standard TLE claim on arrival. When enough time covers the arrival day, the Marine files for TLE. The sources set no time threshold. The S-1 checks vouchers settled since 7 July 2026 with the finance office.
