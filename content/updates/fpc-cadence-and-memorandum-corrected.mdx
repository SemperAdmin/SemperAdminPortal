The force preservation pages said the CO chairs the monthly FPC and cannot
delegate it. They required a Memorandum for the Record whenever the CO did not
chair, limited CIRRAS profile access to uniformed users, and told leaders to
refer a Marine only after two or more indicators.

MCO 1500.60A paragraph 4.b sets FPCs at least monthly for the active component
and semi-annually for the reserve component, and names no chair. Paragraph
4.b.(3)(l) requires the MFR for an FPC held while CIRRAS is unavailable.
Paragraph 4.b.(3)(c)1 adds civilian command leadership appointed in writing to
profile access. Paragraph 4.b.(5) tells every leader to report any stressor to
the chain, with no two-indicator threshold.
