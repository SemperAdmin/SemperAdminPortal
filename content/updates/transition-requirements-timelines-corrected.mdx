The transition requirements page placed Capstone about 90 days before
separation and TRS about 12 months out. It listed SkillBridge caps of 120 days
for Category I and 90 days for Categories II and III, approval at LtCol and
above or a General Officer, and a 31 August 2024 date split.

MCO 1700.31A Enclosure 2 Chapter 5 paragraph 3 requires counseling no later
than 365 days before EAS, TRS workshops by 180 days, Capstone by 120 days, and
Commander Verification by 90 days. NAVMC 1700.2B of 4 April 2025 limits
SkillBridge to 180 days from retirement or EAS, approves at the first CO or OIC
with UCMJ authority, and bars command-added rank limits. A Marine who planned
Capstone at 90 days finished 30 days late.
