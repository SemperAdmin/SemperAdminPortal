The turnover pages told incoming commanders to attend Cornerstone before
assuming command, sign NAVMC 10030 within 90 days, and accept the supply
account inside a 60-day window. They sent aviation commanders to MAWTS-1 and
required an M&RA waiver to arrive less than six months before the change of
command.

MARADMIN 388/25 paragraph 1 makes Cornerstone mandatory before or shortly
after arrival. NAVMC 10030 sets 30 days from assuming command for O-5 and
above. MCO 4400.201 Volume 3 makes the CO endorsement the formal acceptance
and sets no 60-day window. MARADMIN 270/05 paragraph 3 names three courses:
flight refresher training, the Aviation Commanders Safety Course, and the
MAG/Squadron Commanding Officers Course. It sets six months at the MAW for
flying squadron commanders only.
