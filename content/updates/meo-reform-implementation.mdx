MARADMIN 380/26 is the first Military Equal Opportunity message with operational
substance behind it. MARADMIN 357/26 in early August moved the program name from
Prohibited Activities and Conduct to MEO and changed nothing else. This one
changes how a report enters the system and what the commander owes back.

Three items drive command action.

A confidential report now exists as its own lane. A Marine or Sailor discloses
to an Equal Opportunity Advisor, receives services, and triggers no command
notification, inquiry, or investigation. Three exceptions release it: an
imminent threat of serious bodily harm or death, disclosed intent to commit a
future crime or fraud, and a disclosure required by law or competent legal
authority. Conversion to an informal or formal complaint stays open at any time.

Commanders hold a dismissal authority with eight grounds and a required
consultation with both the command EOA and the staff judge advocate. Five
deadlines attach. The EOA forwards a formal complaint in 5 calendar days. The
commander decides in 30. Written notice follows in 7. The complainant appeals to
the next General Court-Martial Convening Authority in 5. Delayed favorable
personnel actions restart in 7.

Anonymous allegations of discrimination and discriminatory harassment stop
producing MEO complaints. Commanders still act on the information through other
authorities. Anonymous sexual harassment, hazing, and bullying complaints keep
their routing under ALNAV 048/26.

Two clock defects sit in the message itself. Paragraph 5.c starts the 30-day
decision clock at receipt without naming whose receipt. Paragraph 6.a runs a
5-day appeal window from the written determination while paragraph 5.d allows 7
calendar days to deliver it. Count from EOA receipt and deliver notice inside 48
hours, and both problems resolve locally.

The guidance holds until incorporated into MCO 5354.1H. Expect a re-verification
sweep on every page above when the H revision publishes.
