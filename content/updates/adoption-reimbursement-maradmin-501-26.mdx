MARADMIN 501/26 cancels MARADMIN 076/18 and resets the adoption expense
reimbursement process. Submit DD Form 2675, one per child, through your chain
of command to your servicing Personnel Administrative Office. Enroll the child
in DEERS before you file. The PAO verifies eligibility and sends the package
with a NAVMC 11116 to the disbursing or finance office in DTMS, which approves
and pays.

The caps and the window hold. Up to $2,000 per child and $5,000 per calendar
year, filed within 2 years of the final adoption. Separating Marines file
before separation. Reserve Marines qualify on active duty orders of 180 or
more consecutive days. Travel, clothing, bedding, toys, and books do not
qualify.

This entry also corrects the portal. The Birth and Adoption page sent claims
to the S-1 with agency receipts alone and gave secondary caregivers 3 weeks of
parental leave. Claims route to the PAO with the full package, and every
parent receives up to 84 days under MCO 1050.3J.
