MARADMIN 503/26 ends the Reserve Component Corporal and Sergeant promotion
authority MARADMIN. MCO P1400.32D W/CH 2 is the promotion authority for
Active Reserve, SMCR, and IRR promotions effective 1 October 2026 and later.
Cite the order on every warrant. Cutting scores post in MCTFS after select
grades post and on the Enlisted Promotions website.

Reserve promotions stay quarterly. FY27 promotions take effect 1 October,
1 January, 1 April, and 1 July. The JEPES score locks on the record cutoff
dates of 31 August, 30 November, 28 February, and 31 May.

This entry also corrects the portal. The I&I Reserve Promotion Policy page
ran reserve promotions to Cpl and Sgt on a composite score and said HQMC
announced cutting scores by MARADMIN. Reserve Cpl and Sgt promotions run on
JEPES, and cutting scores post in MCTFS. The page cited the enlisted manual
for officer promotions and used Will Not Promote in place of the PromRec
not recommended entry and its MARADMIN 150/16 Page 11. The Reserve Incentive
Programs page described a composite score incentive replaced by the JEPES
Score Points Incentive in MARADMIN 695/21.
