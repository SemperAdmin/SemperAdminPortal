The Records Management checklist wraps each long References line across two
or three lines. The import script kept the first line and dropped the rest, or
pasted the overflow onto the end of the question. Nine of 15 items on the
portal page carried a cut-off reference list, and several questions ended in
stray citation text.

Item 0501 is the visible case. The source document lists MCO 5750.1H
enclosure 1 chapter 5, Annex 5 of Appendix A, and MARADMIN 200/25. The
11 September correction read the split line as a source change and removed
MARADMIN 200/25. The source never dropped it. MARADMIN 200/25 is back.

Restored reference tails.

- 0101. MCBul 5210 dated 29 August 2025, paragraph 3b(1)(a)1.
- 0103. SECNAVINST 5210.8F paragraph 5.1 and enclosure 4, MCO 5210.11F
  chapter 8, MCBul 5210 paragraph 3b(1)(a)5.
- 0105. ARD RM SOP section 3.2.4, SECNAV M-5210.1 Part III paragraph 2b.
- 0201. MCO 5210.11F Figure 3-3.
- 0301. MCO 5210.11F chapter 7, ARD RM SOP section 5.1.
- 0401. MCBul 5210 paragraph 3b(1)(a)3.a.
- 0402. SECNAVINST 5210.8F enclosure 4 paragraph 6.g and enclosure 5,
  SECNAV M-5210.1 Part 1, MCO 5210.11F chapter 1.
- 0403. SECNAVINST 5210.8F enclosure 4 paragraph 6.g and enclosure 5,
  SECNAV M-5210.1 Part 1, MCBul 5210 paragraph 3b(1)(a).
- 0501. MARADMIN 200/25.

Question wording did not change beyond removing the stray citation text.
Checked item by item against the source document pulled 23 September 2026.
The import script now keeps every line of a References block.
