The Exceptional Family Member Program page and the DD Form 2792 citation entry
named MCO 1754.5 as the Marine Corps EFMP order. No order carries this number.

MCO 1754.4C is the Marine Corps EFMP order. Paragraph 4 makes Marine Corps EFMP
a mandatory enrollment program. Both pages now cite MCO 1754.4C.
