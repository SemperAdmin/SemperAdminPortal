The MOVSM, awards overview, and campaign and service medals pages said a
Marine nominates himself for the MOVSM and called it an exception to the
no-role rule.

SECNAV M-1650.1 Chapter 1 paragraph 1.2.b.(6) gives awardees no official role
in nomination and approval. Appendix 4A TAB 18 paragraph h.(1) lets any
commissioned officer or DoD civilian at GS-11 or above, senior in grade,
originate the nomination. A Marine gathers the volunteer service proof and
asks a senior to originate.
