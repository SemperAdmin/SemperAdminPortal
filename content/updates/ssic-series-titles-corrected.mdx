The SSIC page titled 1100-1199 as personnel administration, 1200-1299 as pay and
allowances, and 1600 as medical examinations. It placed the SSIC after the
originator code on a letter and listed 1751.3 as an SSIC.

SECNAV M-5210.2 Part III titles 1100 Recruiting, 1200 Classification and
Designation, and 1600 Performance and Discipline. Military pay sits at 7220.
SSIC 1751 is Dependents Allowances, and 1751.3 is only the MCO number. SECNAV
M-5216.5 paragraph 7-2a puts the SSIC top right with the originator code
directly under it.
