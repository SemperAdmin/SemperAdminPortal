MARADMIN 573/24 announced the move from 14 to 21 days of Temporary Lodging
Expense for Service members moving to a new PDS in CONUS. The JTR now
carries 21 days as the standard. The portal still showed the 14-day cap
with 21 days marked forthcoming.

Plan temporary lodging against 21 days. The 21 days split between the old
PDS and the new PDS as you need them.
