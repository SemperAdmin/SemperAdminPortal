The Reserve ID card page gave drilling SMCR Marines a tan DD Form 2 (Reserve)
card and limited dependent access on drill weekends. It sent members to NAVMC
10922 to enroll a dependent in DEERS.

MCO 1001R.1L Chapter 12 Section 1 paragraph 1.c makes the CAC the standard ID
card for SelRes. Section 3 paragraph 2.a gives Ready Reserve members unlimited
exchange and commissary privileges on the same basis as active duty. Section 4
paragraph 1.b has the unit commander ensure a DD Form 1172 is completed for
DEERS enrollment. NAVMC 10922 covers dependency determinations only.
