The voluntary separations page had the unit S-1 start EAS processing 6 to 12
months out. Other pages set Capstone about 90 days out and tied pre-separation
counseling to a Marine reaching 90 days from EAS.

MCO 1700.31A requires Initial Counseling and the Pre-separation Counseling
Brief no later than 365 days before EAS. Capstone must finish no later than 120
days before EAS. The 90 day mark is the Commander Verification gate. MCO
1900.16 paragraph 1006.3 separately limits early separation to 90 days before
EAS without CMC (MMSR-2) approval.
