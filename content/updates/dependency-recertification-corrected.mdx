The recertification pages credited MARADMIN 661/19 with an annual validation
cycle for all Marines with dependents. They gave a 30 to 60 day submission
deadline and said failure causes automatic DEERS removal and entitlement loss.

MCO 1751.3 chapter 1 paragraph 8 requires annual re-determination only for a
member who claims a housing allowance for a secondary dependent. MARADMIN 661/19
covers audit compensating controls on the NAVMC 10922. Failure to recertify
suspends benefits through CMC. For a dependent over 21 the deadline is 15
working days after the servicing administrative office gives notice, and the
MFP-1 notice date governs.
