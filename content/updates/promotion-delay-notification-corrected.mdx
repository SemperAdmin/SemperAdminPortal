The promotion delay page said a commander notifies CMC by standard naval letter
and requires a Commanding General endorsement. It also listed an investigation
as a restriction category and pointed to paragraph 1204.3 for the list.

MCO P1400.32D paragraph 5200 has the commander notify CMC by naval message
before the promotion effective date. Paragraph 5200.4 requires every
endorsement in the chain to carry a specific recommendation and a signature,
with no by-direction signatures. The restriction list sits in paragraph 1204.4,
and it names a pending court-martial or nonjudicial punishment, not an
investigation.
