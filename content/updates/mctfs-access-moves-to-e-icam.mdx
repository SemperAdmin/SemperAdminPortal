Department of Defense Enterprise Identity, Credential, and Access Management
replaces AutoSAAR as the authoritative method to request, modify, and revoke
MCTFS end-user access. Four dates land inside two weeks.

- 17 September 2026. Last day for AutoSAAR, legacy email, and locally routed DD Form 2875 submissions. A request not approved by then requires resubmission.
- 21 September 2026. E-ICAM becomes the required path for every create, modify, and revoke.
- 25 September 2026. Requestors verify official email, phone, and supervisor in E-ICAM. Supervisors validate assigned role and Information Assurance training date.
- 30 September 2026. Accounts not validated are subject to immediate disablement and a fresh request.

Validation is the part with teeth. Every Marine already holding MCTFS access
signs in, opens the three-dot menu, then Manage Identity and Edit Identity, and
confirms email, phone, supervisor, and assigned roles. Supervisors repeat the
pass for each Marine they carry and set the Information Assurance training date.
The provisioning page carries both walkthroughs step by step.

Two traps sit in the detail. A supervisor has to be registered in E-ICAM before
a user names them as Manager or Supervisor, so an unregistered supervisor stalls
the request. Attachments upload only at submission, so a missed Cyber Awareness
Challenge certificate means cancelling the request and starting over.

The change is method only. Commands still appoint and maintain a primary and
alternate qualified TASO, and every TASO cybersecurity, account management,
oversight, and incident reporting duty stands. Appointment of privileged TASO,
Limited Security Control Account, Zone Control Accessor ID, Division Control
Accessor ID, and Department Control Accessor ID accounts stays under Marine
Corps Cyberspace Operations Group procedures.

Six admin pages carry the new path, the validation walkthrough, the E-ICAM
approval chain, the recertification clocks, and the 35-day inactivity disable.
The links index gains DoD E-ICAM with the MCTFS instance address and the DISA
Global Service Desk, and marks AutoSAAR retired.
