Flight status is a command decision before it reaches Headquarters. You
suspend, you convene, you endorse. The new page lays out each step against
the order and holds the line the ACTSMAN draws twice. An FFPB is not a
disciplinary body, and any discipline arising from the same events runs on
a completely separate track.

Two clocks catch commands out. Temporary medical incapacitation disqualifies
aeronautically designated personnel after 180 days. Permanent incapacitation
terminates entitlement after 365. Terminating the pay is your responsibility,
and it dates to your suspension letter.
