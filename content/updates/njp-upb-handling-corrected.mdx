The NJP pages said a refusal to sign Item 2 only checks the refusal box and the
CO signs instead. They said an appeal not acted on within 5 days stays the
punishment automatically, pen changes to the UPB are not authorized, and the
UPB stays at the unit with only Page 11 routed to the OMPF.

The NAVMC 10132 instructions treat a refusal to sign as a refusal of NJP when
the vessel exception does not apply, and the UPB goes to the officer exercising
court-martial jurisdiction. Part V paragraph 7d of the Manual for Courts-Martial
stays unexecuted restraint or extra duty only on the Marine request and only
after 5 days without action on the appeal. MARADMIN 427/23 keeps pen
corrections on manually prepared forms. MCO 5800.16 Volume 14 paragraph 011109
sends a UPB copy to IPAC for OMPF and ESR scanning, and the Marine gets a copy.
