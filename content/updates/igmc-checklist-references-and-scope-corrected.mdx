All 22 checklists were pulled from igmc.marines.mil on 23 September 2026 and
compared item by item after the import script fix. Four pages moved.

Reference lists. The source wraps long References lines across paragraphs.
The old import kept the first line and pushed the rest into the question.

- 3040 item 0121. MCO 3040.4 chapter 7 section 2 paragraph 24 and PAA 1-17
  restored.
- 3040 item 0122. JAGINST 5800.7F restored to the reference list.
- 5040 item 0305. SECNAV M-5210.1 Part III paragraph 2.b restored.
- 1050 item 0108. MARADMIN 051/23 now stands as its own reference and links
  to its registry entry.

Scope. The 5800.16 Legal Administration page said the checklist applies to
all Marine Corps activities. The source limits it to commanders and
commanding generals at the Air, Ground, Logistics, and Installation level,
and excludes legal support functions inside the Office of the Staff Judge
Advocate. The page now carries the source wording.

Question wording did not change beyond removing stray citation text.
Records Management 5210 carries its own entry.
