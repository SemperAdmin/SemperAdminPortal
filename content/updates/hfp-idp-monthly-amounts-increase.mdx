MARADMIN 433/26 sets both monthly amounts effective 1 October 2026. HFP pays
$450 per month with no proration. IDP pays up to a $275 monthly ceiling,
prorated at $9.16 per day against actual time in a designated area.

Portal pages had treated HFP and IDP as one rate. They now differ by $175. Determine HFP or IDP before you determine the amount.
A start cut at the prior rate underpays a deployed Marine for the length of the
deployment.

Two follow-on effects. The officer Combat Zone Tax Exclusion ceiling equals the
highest enlisted basic pay rate plus the HFP or IDP the officer draws, so the
ceiling rises by a different amount depending on which pay applies. Separately,
the PAC HFP page had described IDP as paying a full month for one qualifying
day. IDP has been prorated daily since 31 December 2011, and that page now
matches MCTFSPRIUM section 90701.

Every other provision of DoDFMR Volume 7A Chapter 10 stays in effect. The
message changes the amounts only.
