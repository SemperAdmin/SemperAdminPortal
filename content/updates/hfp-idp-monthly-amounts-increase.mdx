MARADMIN 433/26 raises both monthly amounts effective 01 October 2026. HFP moves
from 225 to 450 dollars per month with no proration. IDP moves from a 225 to a
275 dollar monthly ceiling, prorated at 9.16 dollars per day against actual time
in a designated area.

Both pays sat at 225 dollars for years, and portal pages treated them as one
rate. They diverge by 175 dollars from 01 October. Determine HFP or IDP before
you determine the amount. A start cut in October at the September rate underpays
a deployed Marine for the length of the deployment.

Two follow-on effects. The officer Combat Zone Tax Exclusion ceiling equals the
highest enlisted basic pay rate plus the HFP or IDP the officer draws, so the
ceiling rises by a different amount depending on which pay applies. Separately,
the PAC HFP page had described IDP as paying a full month for one qualifying
day. IDP has been prorated daily since 31 December 2011, and that page now
matches MCTFSPRIUM section 90701.

Every other provision of DoDFMR Volume 7A Chapter 10 stays in effect. The
message changes the amounts only.
