The separation pay pages allowed a General characterization for full pay,
listed Other Than Honorable among the characterizations paying full or half pay, and left the
Ready Reserve agreement off the eligibility list. The savings page put the
Continuation Pay window at 8 to 12 years of service.

DoD FMR Volume 7A Chapter 35 paragraph 3.1.1.2 requires an Honorable
characterization for full separation pay. Paragraph 3.1.3.2 allows Honorable
or General for half pay. Other Than Honorable earns none. Paragraph 3.1.1.4
requires a written agreement to serve 3 years in the Ready Reserve. Chapter 66
paragraph 2.2.1.2 sets the Continuation Pay window at 7 to 12 years of service.
