The awards pages said the S-1 officer signs by direction on routine awards.
They sent nominations to an HQMC APS 1650 form after APS registration, set SOA
length by award tier, and let a unit commander originate the unit's own award.
They also told Marines on terminal leave to use a DD Form 215 for corrections.

MARADMIN 077/25 paragraph 3.d.3 bars signing awards by direction. Only an
authorized approval authority signs Block 24 under MCO 1650.19J Enclosure 5.
MARADMIN 093/25 requires iAPS origination, a two-page SOA for every PMD, and
60 days for CMC awards or 90 days for SECNAV awards. Unit awards originate at
the next higher echelon under MCO 1650.19J Enclosure 3, and CMC adjudicates the
MUC. A Marine on terminal leave requests a DD-214 correction on SF 180.
