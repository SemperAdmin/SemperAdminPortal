The meritorious promotions page set the cap at Master Sergeant, tied MSgt to both Drill Instructor and Recruiter of the Year, and applied the 5 percent school rule to PFC, LCpl, and Cpl.

MCO P1400.32D paragraph 4100.1b bars meritorious promotion above Gunnery Sergeant. Drill Instructor of the Year promotes SSgt through GySgt. Recruiter of the Year promotes Sgt through MSgt. Paragraph 4201.1c makes students in the grade of LCpl and above ineligible for school meritorious promotion.
