The MAGTF and MEU operations page said an 8-hour personnel casualty report
window applies to casualty actions during operations.

MARADMIN 186/25 paragraph 6 requires the unit to give MFPC a voice report
within 30 minutes of learning of a reportable garrison casualty. The unit
submits the DCIPS-PCR template to casualty.section@usmc.mil within one hour.
Reviews and approvals outside the immediate command do not excuse a late
report.
