The emergency leave page capped standard approval at 30 days, required Red
Cross verification for funded travel, limited funded travel to overseas
Marines going to CONUS, and listed grandparents as immediate family.

MCO 1050.3J Chapter 2 paragraph 12 lets the leave granting authority approve
more than 60 days without CMC approval when advance leave stays at 30 days or
less. Red Cross verification is not required, even for funded leave. Chapter 4
paragraph 5 funds travel overseas to CONUS, CONUS to overseas, and between
overseas areas. Grandparents qualify only when they stood in loco parentis.
