The retirement pages told Marines to submit requests 6 to 18 months out, or 12
to 24 months out on the commander page. The commander page also routed the
request by Naval Letter. The Marine page used a time in grade table with
officer and warrant officer figures the order does not support.

MCO 1900.16 paragraphs 7005.1 and 2004.1 set the window at 14 months to 4
months before the requested date. Reporting units submit the request through
the unit diary with a signed Appendix J. MCTFS rejects requests outside the
window, and those go to CMC (MMSR-2) by separate correspondence. Time in grade
follows paragraph 2003 for officers and paragraph 7004.5 for GySgt and above.
A request without the required time in grade is not approved unless DC M&RA
waives it.
