The court-martial pay pages started forfeitures on convening authority action
or promulgating order signature. The UA page continued BAH with dependents
during unauthorized absence. The BAH page told leaders every Marine
recertifies yearly.

DoD FMR Volume 7A Chapter 48 paragraph 5.2.1 starts forfeitures 14 days after
the sentence is announced. Summary court-martial forfeitures begin when the
convening authority approves the forfeiture portion of the sentence. Chapter 26 paragraph 1
denies housing allowances during unexcused absence without leave. Chapter 26
paragraph 3.2.3 requires primary dependents to recertify on a change of duty
station or dependent status. Annual certification applies to secondary
dependents.
