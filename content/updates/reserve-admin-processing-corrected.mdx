The pages set the IDT mileage threshold at 100 miles with an annual cap, routed
retirement requests through MARFORRES to HQMC, and gave unit commanders ATP
authority. They used composite scores for E-2 through E-5 promotion, treated
the SMP as a ROTC program, and described ROCP as one commissioning path. The
retirement pay examples counted mobilization at the wrong point total, and the
disciplinary page cited MCRAMM for a member self-report duty.

JTR 032304 sets 150 miles one way and a $750 cap for each round trip. A member
submits a retirement request to the reporting unit commander per MCO 1900.16
Figure 3-5, entered in MCTFS 4 to 14 months before transfer. COMMARFORRES and
OpSponsors authorize ATPs within DC M&RA (RA) allocations. MCO 1616.1 and
MARADMIN 503/26 score Cpl and Sgt promotions with JEPES. MCO 1001R.1L Chapter 2
Section 3 makes ROCP three programs, MCP-R, RECP, and OCC-R, and the SMP seats
PLC candidates in SMCR units. Active duty earns one point a day.
