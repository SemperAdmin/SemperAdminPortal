The absentee and deserter pages said desertion runs on a fixed 30-day clock.
They routed the DD Form 553 through the unit security officer to civilian
police, and they stopped pay at hour 1 of an unauthorized absence. They also
set a 24-hour DIP notice on return and a 5-working-day Page 11 deadline.

DoDI 1325.02 paragraph 3.2.a classifies an absentee as a deserter on any of
three triggers: facts showing desertion at any length, 30 consecutive days of
UA, or a foreign asylum request. Paragraph 3.4.b sends the DD 553 to the DIP,
which enters or verifies the NCIC record within 24 hours of the drop. DoDFMR
Volume 7A Chapter 1 paragraph 4.2.2.2 forfeits pay only for an absence not
excused as unavoidable. The 5 working days in MCO P1070.12K paragraph 4006 is
the Marine rebuttal window on an adverse entry, not a processing deadline.
