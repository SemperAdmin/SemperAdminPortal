MARADMIN 502/26 ends the monthly Active-Duty Corporal and Sergeant promotion
authority MARADMIN. MCO P1400.32D W/CH 2 is the promotion authority for
promotions effective 1 October 2026 and later. Cite the order on every
warrant. Cutting scores post in MCTFS after each month's select grades post
and on the Enlisted Promotions website. Commands keep each month's scores on
file.

The message sets fixed FY27 record cutoff dates. A Marine's JEPES score for a
promotion month locks on the last day of the month two months before the
promotion. For 1 October 2026, the cutoff was 31 August 2026. Date of rank
equals the effective date, the first of the month.

This entry also corrects the portal. The S-1 Corporal and Sergeant page
described composite score worksheets, cutting scores published by MMEA, and
warrants issued by MMEA. Cpl and Sgt promotions run on JEPES. Cutting scores
post in MCTFS, and the command writes the warrant. The S-1 Promotions
Overview and Eligibility pages carried the same errors. The Marine promotions
page said a monthly MARADMIN authorized each promotion.

The message covers the Active Component only. Reserve promotion guidance is
unchanged on the portal.
