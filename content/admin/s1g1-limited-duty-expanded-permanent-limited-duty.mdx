## Role and responsibility

CMC approves or disapproves EPLD retention case by case for combat wounded or injured Marines the Physical Evaluation Board (PEB) found unfit, per MCO 1900.16<Citation source="MCO 1900.16" /> Chapter 8 paragraph 8109 (Expanded Permanent Limited Duty). The unit commander applies the expanded duty restrictions per the PEB findings. The MTF medical officer issues the medical scope. The S-1 chief tracks EPLD personnel against the CMC retention decision per paragraph 8109. The IPAC outbound counselor cuts the EPLD duty status transaction in MCTFS through UD-MIPS per MCTFSPRIUM<Citation source="MCTFSPRIUM" />. The S-1 confirms the gaining billet supports the expanded restrictions.

Source documents flow from the Marine's medical board package, the PEB findings supporting EPLD retention, the expanded medical scope, the expanded duty restrictions, the unit commander approval, the Page 11 entry per MCO P1070.12K<Citation source="MCO P1070.12K" /> Chapter 6, the CMC retention decision, and the billet confirmation record. EPLD outputs include the approved EPLD duty status, the PEB findings on file, the OMPF disability tab entry, the CMC retention decision, the billet confirmation, the MCC coding update, and the duty restriction record.

Per Chapter 8 paragraph 8109 (Expanded Permanent Limited Duty), EPLD is for combat wounded or injured Marines found unfit by the PEB. The injury or disability must be combat-related, have occurred after 11 September 2001, and meet Purple Heart criteria. The DC M&RA waives the Purple Heart criteria case by case for unique cases. EPLD differs from standard PLD per paragraph 8108 in these eligibility criteria and in the relaxed fitness and deployability requirements.

## Trigger events

- PEB finds a combat wounded or injured Marine unfit and the Marine requests EPLD retention.
- Standard PLD review surfaces broader restrictions warranting EPLD consideration.
- CMC issues a retention decision or changes the draw case code.
- Marine on EPLD requires PCS to a billet supporting the expanded restriction.
- Disability rating change triggers EPLD re-evaluation.
- Marine on EPLD requests separation or retirement.

## Processing workflow

1. Receive the PEB recommendation. The PEB issues findings supporting EPLD retention with the expanded duty restrictions documented.
2. Validate eligibility. Per MCO 1900.16<Citation source="MCO 1900.16" /> Chapter 8 paragraph 8109, the PEB findings and the expanded restriction scope drive the determination.
3. Route the medical board package to the PEB. Per paragraph 8104 and Section 2, the medical board package routes to the PEB. The PEB issues findings and CMC approves or disapproves retention.
4. Apply expanded duty restrictions. The unit commander applies the duty restrictions per the PEB findings. The S-1 documents the restrictions on Page 11.
5. Cut EPLD duty status transaction. Per Appendix E and MCTFSPRIUM, the IPAC cuts the EPLD duty status transaction in UD-MIPS.
6. Document on Page 11. Per MCO P1070.12K<Citation source="MCO P1070.12K" /> Chapter 6, cut a Page 11 documenting the EPLD status, the expanded duty restrictions, and the PEB findings.
7. Track the CMC decision. Per paragraph 8109, CMC approves or disapproves retention case by case and assigns draw case code CW with duty limitation code 3 or 4.
8. Confirm the billet. EPLD personnel require a billet supporting the expanded restriction. The S-1 confirms the gaining billet supports it.
9. Update MCC coding. The MCC coding reflects the EPLD status for accountability and billet matching.
10. Update OMPF disability tab. Per MCO P1070.12K Chapter 1, document the PEB findings and the EPLD status in the OMPF disability tab.

## Systems of record and forms

### Systems

- Marine Corps Total Force System (MCTFS). Holds EPLD duty status and MCC coding.
- UD-MIPS. Cuts EPLD transactions per Appendix E.
- Disability Evaluation System (IDES) Workflow. Per Chapter 8 Section 2.
- OMPF. Holds the disability tab with PEB findings.

### Forms

- PEB Findings Supporting EPLD Retention. Per DODI 1332.18.
- CMC EPLD Retention Decision. Per paragraph 8109.
- Unit Commander Restrictions Acknowledgment. Per paragraph 8109.
- NAVMC 118 (11) Administrative Remarks. Page 11 documenting EPLD per MCO P1070.12K paragraph 4006.

## Common pitfalls

- EPLD applied without expanded PEB findings. Per Chapter 8 paragraph 8109, the PEB findings supporting the expanded scope drive EPLD eligibility.
- Standard PLD applied where EPLD warranted. Per paragraphs 8108 and 8109, the breadth of the restrictions drives the PLD versus EPLD classification.
- Billet support skipped. Without confirmation, the Marine arrives at an unsupportable billet.
- CMC decision not tracked. Per paragraph 8109, CMC approves or disapproves each case and assigns the draw case code and duty limitation code.
- MCC coding not updated. EPLD personnel accountability depends on the MCC coding.
- OMPF disability tab missing the PEB findings. Per MCO P1070.12K Chapter 1, document the PEB findings.

## Decision points

- PLD versus EPLD. Per paragraphs 8108 and 8109, the breadth of the medical restrictions and the billet matching scope drive the classification.
- EPLD retention versus disability separation. Per Chapter 8 Section 2 and the PEB findings, the disposition flows from the medical scope and the disability rating.
- Billet selection for EPLD. The S-1 weighs the medical restrictions against the available billets.
- Retention decision. CMC approves or disapproves each EPLD request case by case.
