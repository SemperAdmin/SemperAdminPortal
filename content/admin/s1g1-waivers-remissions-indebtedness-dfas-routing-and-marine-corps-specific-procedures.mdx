## Role and responsibility

S-1 admin specialists, IPAC pay clerks, and Marine Corps Comptroller staff build and route Marine waiver and remission packages. Waivers go through DFAS Indianapolis. Remission requests go to the Service, not DFAS. The S-1 chief drafts the DD Form 2789 application for waivers, the legal authority memorandum, and the Marine statement. The IPAC pay clerk submits the package to DFAS through the assigned pay control point. The S-1 officer signs by direction on the package. The Marine Corps Comptroller routes the appeal on a DFAS denial.

Source documents flow from the DD Form 2789 application, the Marine statement, the supporting hardship documentation (for remission cases), the optional command endorsement memorandum, the DFAS receipt acknowledgment, and the DFAS adjudication letter. Outputs include the DFAS case number, the liquidation TTC/SEQ 559/000 reported by Disbursing and Finance on approval, the collection start on denial, and the OMPF Field Folder case packet.

DoDFMR Vol 16 Chapter 4 is the DoD-wide waiver and remission framework for Marine pay debt. DoDFMR Vol 7A Chapter 50 covers Bankruptcy, not waivers.

## Trigger events

- Marine receives the demand letter for a pay debt and elects to apply for waiver or remission.
- DFAS denies an initial waiver remission application and the Marine appeals.
- Audit identifies an overpayment caused by Marine Corps administrative error and the unit applies for a waiver on the Marine behalf.
- Marine separates with an active waiver or remission package and the unit routes the appeal.
- Marine Corps Comptroller identifies a unit-wide pay error and routes a class waiver request.

## Processing workflow

1. Build the DD Form 2789 application for a waiver under 10 USC 2774. Build a remission request on the Service-designated form under 10 USC 7837. Capture the Marine biographics, the debt details (amount, source, date), the legal authority basis, and the request per DoDFMR Vol 16 Chapter 4.
2. Build the legal authority memorandum. The S-1 chief drafts the memorandum identifying the statutory authority, the basis for the application, and the supporting facts per DoDFMR Vol 16<Citation source="DoDFMR Vol 16" /> Chapter 4.
3. Build the Marine statement. The Marine signs a statement of facts, including the debt circumstances and any hardship factors (for remission cases) per DoDFMR Vol 16<Citation source="DoDFMR Vol 16" /> Chapter 4 paragraph 040401.
4. Capture supporting hardship documentation. For remission cases, include the financial statement, dependents data, debt obligation list, and any other hardship factors per 10 USC 7837 and DoDFMR Vol 16<Citation source="DoDFMR Vol 16" /> Chapter 4.
5. Route through the chain of command. A commander endorsement is optional for Marine applications per DD Form 2789 item 20. Route the package whether or not an endorsement is attached.
6. Submit the package. For a waiver, the IPAC pay clerk submits the certified package to DFAS Indianapolis through the assigned pay control point per MCO 7300.21B<Citation source="MCO 7300.21B" /> Marine Corps Financial Management SOP. For a remission, forward the request to the Service for processing. Per DoDFMR Vol 16<Citation source="DoDFMR Vol 16" /> Chapter 4 paragraph 9.2, DFAS does not process remission or cancellation requests for any Service.
7. Track the case. For a waiver, the S-1 chief pulls the DFAS case status on a fixed cycle. DFAS adjudicates the waiver application and issues a written decision per DoDFMR Vol 16<Citation source="DoDFMR Vol 16" /> Chapter 4.
8. Process the approval. On DFAS approval, Disbursing and Finance reports the liquidation TTC/SEQ 559/000 per PAA 02-24<Citation source="PAA 02-24" />. The S-1 does not cut it. Confirm the salary offset stops.
9. Process the denial. On DFAS denial, the IPAC pay clerk routes the debt to active collection per DoDFMR Vol 16<Citation source="DoDFMR Vol 16" /> Chapter 3. The S-1 chief counsels the Marine on the appeal option.
10. Process the appeal. The Marine appeals the DFAS denial within the appeal window per DoDFMR Vol 16<Citation source="DoDFMR Vol 16" /> Chapter 4. The Marine Corps Comptroller staff routes the appeal through DFAS.

## Systems of record and forms

### Systems

- DFAS Indianapolis. Adjudicates Marine waiver applications. DFAS does not process remission requests.
- MCTFS. Holds the debt entry and the liquidation reported by Disbursing and Finance.
- DFAS Case Tracking System. Tracks the DFAS case status.
- Marine Corps Comptroller Office. Routes appeals and class waiver requests.
- IPAC pay control points. The submission channel from the unit to DFAS.

### Forms

- DD Form 2789 Waiver or Remission of Indebtedness Application. The standard application for waivers under 10 USC 2774.
- Legal Authority Memorandum. Internal staff memorandum identifying the statutory basis.
- Marine Statement of Facts. Marine-signed statement covering the debt circumstances.
- Hardship Documentation Package. Financial statement, dependents data, debt obligation list.
- Command Endorsement Memorandum. Optional for Marine applications per DD Form 2789 item 20.

## Common pitfalls

- Wrong legal authority on the package. Waiver routed under 10 USC 7837 or remission under 10 USC 2774. The request is denied. Determine authority at build per DoDFMR Vol 16<Citation source="DoDFMR Vol 16" /> Chapter 4.
- Marine separated before the package adjudicates. Remission under 10 USC 7837 requires active duty enlisted status. The Marine loses eligibility on separation. Submit before separation.
- Package held for a command endorsement. A commander endorsement is optional for Marine waiver and remission applications per DD Form 2789 item 20. Do not hold or return the package for a missing endorsement.
- Hardship documentation thin. The remission is denied for lack of supporting financial data. Include the financial statement, dependents data, and debt obligation list at submission.
- Liquidation not reported on approval. The salary offset continues. Confirm Disbursing and Finance reports TTC/SEQ 559/000 after DFAS approval.

## Decision points

- Waiver versus remission. Waiver applies on government error and equity grounds under 10 USC 2774. Remission applies on hardship grounds for enlisted Marines on active duty under 10 USC 7837. Remission goes to the Service, not DFAS. The S-1 chief routes per the right authority.
- DFAS denial appeal versus collection. The Marine appeals the denial through DFAS or accepts the denial and routes to collection. The S-1 officer counsels the Marine on the appeal window and the cost of additional interest accrual.
- Class waiver request. The Marine Corps Comptroller identifies a unit-wide pay error and routes a class waiver request through DFAS per DoDFMR Vol 16<Citation source="DoDFMR Vol 16" /> Chapter 4.
