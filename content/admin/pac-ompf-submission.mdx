## What MCO 1070.1 Chapter 4 Requires

MCO 1070.1<Citation source="MCO 1070.1" /> Chapter 4 sets how documents reach the OMPF. Key requirements.

- The Marine's full name and DoD ID on every document and every page
- Scanner settings and file format for scanned documents
- Submission timelines for Marines, non-IPAC submitters, and IPACs
- Internal control procedures that validate successful submission
- Documents limited to those authorized for the OMPF

PAC must comply with MCO 1070.1<Citation source="MCO 1070.1" /> Chapter 4 on every OMPF submission. Audits surface non-compliance.

## Required Metadata for All Submissions

MCO 1070.1<Citation source="MCO 1070.1" /> Chapter 4 paragraph 5 requires the Marine's full name and DoD ID on every document. The rest of this list is local PAC practice.

### Member Identification

- Full name (last, first, middle initial) matching MCTFS
- DoD ID (ten digits). Treat the SSN as optional. If the SSN appears on any page, encrypt the email
- Rank and pay grade at time of document
- MOS at time of document
- Unit at time of document

### Document Identification

- Document type per MCO P1070.12<Citation source="MCO P1070.12" /> categories
- Document subject (specific topic)
- Document date (date signed, not date submitted)
- Originating authority (who signed it)
- Effective date if different from document date

### Submission Tracking

- Submitting unit
- Submitter name and contact
- Date submitted to PAC
- Tracking number for follow-up

Without complete metadata, the submission gets rejected.

## Document Quality Standards

Per MCO 1070.1<Citation source="MCO 1070.1" /> Chapter 4 paragraphs 2 and 7, documents must be legible and scanned correctly for OMPF inclusion. The lists under Completeness and Format are local PAC practice.

### Legibility

- All text clearly readable
- All signatures visible and identifiable
- All dates clearly written or printed
- Scanner settings of 200 DPI, gray scale, TIFF image
- No handwritten content that obscures printed text

### Completeness

- All required signature blocks signed
- All required date blocks dated
- All attachments included
- All applicable sections completed
- Member acknowledgment where required

### Format

- Standard 8.5 x 11 page size
- Single-sided when possible
- Clean copies (no marginalia or stray marks)
- Original or certified copy when required
- Digital format when submitted electronically

## Routing Requirements

MCO 1070.1<Citation source="MCO 1070.1" /> Chapter 4 does not set routing by document type. The routing matrix below is local practice. Confirm it with your S-1 and command.

### Direct PAC Submission

- Routine pay actions
- BAH and BAS changes
- Travel claims
- Administrative updates

### Through S-1 to PAC

- Performance documents (Page 11, fitness reports)
- Awards
- Promotion warrants
- NJP results
- School certificates

### Through Command Chain

- Administrative separations
- Letters of Reprimand
- Special qualification certifications

### Direct from Issuing Authority

- HQMC promotion warrants
- Awards from external commands
- Foreign award documents

Knowing the routing prevents misdirected submissions.

## Performance Steps

### Step 1 - Receive Submission

PAC receives the document.

- Log receipt with date and time
- Identify source (S-1, member, external)
- Identify document type
- Verify routing was correct

### Step 2 - Validate Per MCO 1070.1 Chapter 4

Check the document against requirements.

- Metadata complete?
- Quality standards met?
- Required signatures present?
- Originating authority appropriate?
- Member identification verified?

If any item fails, reject and request corrected version.

### Step 3 - Apply OMPF Categorization

Per MCO P1070.12<Citation source="MCO P1070.12" />.

- Major category identified
- Sub-category identified
- Document type code applied
- Indexing tags prepared

### Step 4 - Upload Through O-RMA

Per MARADMIN 412/23<Citation source="MARADMIN 412/23" />, submit through the OMPF Records Management Application (O-RMA), the OMPF system of record since 5 September 2023. MMRP reviews and approves all documents per the IRAM.

- Scan document if hard copy (200 DPI, gray scale)
- Apply member identification and document type in the upload interface
- Submit for MMRP review
- Verify submission completion

### Step 5 - Verify Indexing

After upload.

- Confirm document appears in member's OMPF
- Verify indexing tags applied correctly
- Test retrieval from OMPF view
- Document any indexing issues

### Step 6 - Notify and Track

Close the loop.

- Notify submitting S-1 or member
- Update tracking system with completion
- File working copy per local PAC policy

## Common Rejection Reasons

These get documents rejected back to S-1 or member.

1. **Missing or wrong DoD ID.** Every page needs the full name and DoD ID. Fix before resubmission.
2. **Photocopy when original required.** Marriage certificates, birth certificates, divorce decrees require original or certified copy. Photocopies not accepted.
3. **Missing signature.** Document signature block empty or signature not legible. Return to originator.
4. **Wrong routing.** Document submitted directly to PAC when should have routed through command. Redirect.
5. **Late document.** Per MCO 1070.1<Citation source="MCO 1070.1" /> Chapter 4 paragraph 5.c, Marines and non-IPAC submitters normally submit within 5 working days of completed action. IPACs submit within 10 working days.
6. **Unclear scan quality.** Scanned document too low resolution or too dark to read. Re-scan at higher quality.

## Tracking and Audit

Per MCO 1070.1<Citation source="MCO 1070.1" /> Chapter 4 paragraph 8, administrative units implement internal controls that validate successful submission. PAC tracks submissions for audit.

- Submission log with all required metadata
- Processing time per submission
- Rejection log with reasons
- Re-submission tracking

Audit findings surface from inadequate tracking.

## Submission SLAs

Per MCO 1070.1<Citation source="MCO 1070.1" /> Chapter 4 paragraph 5.c, submission and processing timelines are as follows.

- Marines and non-IPAC submitters: normally within 5 working days of completed action
- IPACs: within 10 working days of completed action
- IPAC scan batches: quality control and release within 5 working days of the scan date (paragraph 7.g)
- Documents submitted through the Input Module: usually placed in the OMPF within 3 to 5 working days of IPAC release
- Documents sent to the MMRP organizational mailbox: usually placed in the OMPF within 10 working days of receipt

PAC tracks adherence and reports to MARFORRES or installation command.

## Common Submission Errors at S-1 Level

These prevent successful submission.

1. **S-1 forwards incomplete package.** Missing supporting documents. PAC rejects, S-1 must rework.
2. **Wrong document type identified.** S-1 calls a Page 11 a fitness report. Reroute and reprocess.
3. **Member DoD ID wrong on S-1 cover sheet.** PAC must verify against MCTFS. Discrepancies cause delays.
4. **Original requested but copy submitted.** S-1 sends copy when original required. PAC rejects, requires original.
5. **Routing skipped command.** S-1 sends directly when document needed command endorsement. Reroute through command.

## Quality Control Before Submission

S-1 should verify before forwarding to PAC.

- All metadata complete
- All signatures present
- Document quality acceptable
- Original or certified copy as required
- Routing through correct chain

This pre-flight check prevents PAC rejections and rework.

## Related Roles

OMPF submission is everyone's problem in the chain.

- **Marines** submit life event documents and personal records updates.
- **Leaders** at S-1 process and route documents to PAC with proper metadata.
- **Commanders** sign documents flowing to OMPF and approve routing per MCO 1070.1<Citation source="MCO 1070.1" /> Chapter 4.
