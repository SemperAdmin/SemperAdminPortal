## Overseas Housing Allowance (OHA)

Per DOD FMR Volume 7A<Citation source="DOD FMR Volume 7A" /> Chapter 26 section 6.0, Marines stationed overseas receive OHA instead of standard CONUS BAH.

### How OHA Differs From BAH

- OHA based on actual rental costs (member submits lease)
- OHA includes a utility and recurring maintenance allowance. It is a fixed locality amount, not receipt-based reimbursement. A utility point score reduces it when rent includes some utilities, per Chapter 26 paragraphs 6.3.1 and 6.3.2
- OHA includes MIHA for move-in costs only. MIHA pays for one dwelling per tour at a PDS unless a government-funded local move occurs. No allowance covers move-out costs, per Chapter 26 paragraphs 6.4.1 and 6.4.3.3
- BAH is fixed by zip code. The OHA rental allowance pays actual rent up to the location cap

### OHA Components

OHA has three parts.

| Component | Coverage |
|-----------|----------|
| Rental Allowance | Actual rent up to maximum for location |
| Utility/Recurring Maintenance Allowance | Fixed locality amount covering utility costs for 80 percent of members in the area. A member without dependents who is not a sharer receives 75 percent of the with-dependent amount. Sharers receive a prorated amount. |
| Move-In Housing Allowance (MIHA) | Move-in costs only, one dwelling per tour at a PDS |

### OHA Eligibility

- Active duty Marines on overseas tours
- Marines not in government quarters at overseas station
- Specific accompanied vs unaccompanied rules apply

### OHA Documentation Requirements

Per DOD FMR Volume 7A Chapter 26, member submits.

- Signed lease agreement
- DD 2367 showing which utilities the rent includes, so the utility point score is correct
- Move-in receipts for MIHA claims
- New DD 2367 and lease documents whenever rent or the dwelling changes, so OHA is recomputed. Chapter 26 paragraph 6.2.3 sets no annual lease renewal requirement

### Performance Steps for OHA

When member arrives at overseas station.

1. Member receives PCS orders to overseas location
2. Member secures off-base housing
3. Member submits lease and OHA application
4. The in-country senior officer or the designated housing office approves the DD 2367. PAC reports OHA after approval
5. PAC processes OHA via UD/MIPS at correct rate
6. MIHA processed for one-time setup costs
7. Recompute OHA on any rent change

## Partial BAH

Per DoDFMR Vol 7A<Citation source="DOD FMR Volume 7A" /> Chapter 26 paragraph 5.5, BAH-Partial is the standing entitlement of every member without dependents assigned to single-type government quarters and not authorized BAH or OHA.

### When Partial BAH Applies

- Every member without dependents in single-type government quarters, barracks Marines included. BAH-Partial posts automatically at accession
- Paragraph 5.5.3 adds the confinement, arrest, hospital, between-PDS and dual-military cases

### Partial BAH Rate

- Smaller amount than standard BAH
- Set per DOD FMR Volume 7A<Citation source="DOD FMR Volume 7A" />
- Different transaction code than standard BAH

A barracks Marine paying child support at or above the BAH-Diff rate draws BAH-Diff instead, per Chapter 26 paragraph 5.6.1.

## BAH for Single SNCO and Senior Officers

Per DoDFMR Vol 7A<Citation source="DOD FMR Volume 7A" /> Chapter 26 paragraph 10.1.3 and MCO 11000.22<Citation source="MCO 11000.22" /> Chapter 10, single SNCO (E-7+) and senior officers have specific BAH provisions.

### Standard Off-Base BAH

- Without-dependents rate at duty station
- Standard BAH processing
- No special category

### Government Quarters Eligibility

- Chapter 26 gives E-7 and above the election at any time. MCO 11000.22 Chapter 10 extends the CONUS election to E-6 without Installation Commander approval, subject to military necessity
- SNCO quarters may apply at some installations
- Specific senior officer quarters at some locations

The category is standard BAH. The "special" aspect is that single SNCOs and senior officers have more housing options than junior enlisted.

## BAH for Single Junior Enlisted

E-1 through E-3 generally live in barracks (government quarters). A Marine without dependents in single-type quarters draws BAH-Partial. A barracks Marine paying child support at or above the BAH-Diff rate draws BAH-Diff instead. Only BAH at the with or without-dependent locality rate is absent. See DoDFMR Vol 7A<Citation source="DOD FMR Volume 7A" /> Chapter 26 paragraphs 5.5.1 and 5.6.1 and MCTFSPRIUM 80201.2.

### Exceptions

- Non-availability of adequate quarters. The Installation Commander approves BAH without dependents at 95 percent occupancy per MCO 11000.22<Citation source="MCO 11000.22" /> Enclosure 2 Chapter 10 paragraph 7c
- Allowable exceptions under paragraph 7d
- Hardship exceptions under paragraph 7e

When approved, member draws standard without-dependents BAH or specific category per circumstance.

## BAH and Government Quarters

In adequate government quarters no BAH pays at the locality rate. A member without dependents in single-type quarters draws BAH-Partial, and a member paying qualifying child support draws BAH-Diff, per Chapter 26 paragraph 5.0.

### Types of Government Quarters

- Barracks (junior enlisted)
- BEQ (Bachelor Enlisted Quarters) for senior enlisted
- BOQ (Bachelor Officer Quarters)
- Family housing (for accompanied tours)
- Senior officer quarters

### BAH Rules with Government Quarters

- Member in adequate government quarters: no BAH at the locality rate. BAH-Partial or BAH-Diff still pays when the member qualifies
- Member temporarily without quarters during transition: BAH may apply for transition period

## BAH for Sea Duty

Marines on sea duty may have specific BAH situations.

### Sea Duty BAH

- A member with dependents draws the home port with-dependent rate on a CO statement of dependent residence, per Chapter 26 paragraph 10.1.5.1
- A member without dependents in E-6 and above elects BAH or OHA
- A member without dependents below E-6 occupies shipboard quarters and draws BAH-Partial unless the Secretary of the Navy authorizes BAH or OHA under paragraph 10.1.5.2
- Specific sea duty allowances may layer on top

### BAH When Underway

- BAH does not change when ship is underway
- Entitlement does not change underway. A member with dependents keeps the home port with-dependent rate
- Combat zone or hostile fire pays may apply during deployment

## BAH for Senior Reserve Members

Per DoDFMR Vol 7A<Citation source="DOD FMR Volume 7A" /> Chapter 26 paragraph 10.5, a reservist on orders of 30 or fewer days, not for a contingency, draws BAH-RC. Orders of 31 or more days, or contingency orders of any length, pay BAH or OHA at the primary residence.

### Reserve Member on Active Duty Over 30 Days

- BAH or OHA applies at the primary residence rate
- Use the primary residence zip code
- Standard BAH processing

### Reserve Member on Drilling Status

- No BAH for inactive duty (drill weekends)
- Drill pay only
- AT and other orders of 30 or fewer days pay BAH-RC. BAH or OHA starts on orders of 31 or more days or on contingency orders of any length

## Performance Steps for Special Categories

### Step 1 - Identify Applicable Category

Determine which special BAH category applies.

- Overseas: OHA processing
- Sea duty: Home port rate by dependent status and grade, per the Sea Duty BAH rules
- Government quarters with allowance: Partial BAH
- Reserve on active duty: BAH-RC on orders of 30 or fewer days, BAH or OHA on 31 or more days or contingency orders

### Step 2 - Apply Specific Calculation

Each category has different calculation rules.

- OHA: Actual rent up to the cap plus the fixed utility allowance, with documentation
- Partial BAH: Fixed reduced rate
- BAH-Diff: Fixed differential rate
- Standard BAH: Zip code, rank, dependent status

### Step 3 - Process via Correct UD/MIPS Code

Each category has its own transaction code per MCTFSCODESMAN.

- Standard BAH: Generic BAH codes
- OHA: OHA-specific transaction codes
- BAH-Diff: BAH-Diff transaction codes
- Partial BAH: Partial BAH codes

### Step 4 - Document the Basis

PAC retains documentation supporting the special category.

- OHA: Lease agreements, approved DD 2367
- Partial BAH: Justification for category
- Hardship-based BAH: Approved hardship documentation

## Common Special Category Errors

These hit during PAC processing.

1. **Member overseas drawing standard BAH instead of OHA.** OHA rate not applied at overseas station. Fix by processing OHA with effective date.
2. **OHA continued after member returns CONUS.** Pay OHA through the day before departure. Pay BAH-Transit from departure through the day before reporting, TDY en route included. Start BAH at the new PDS on the reporting day. Fix by stopping OHA, starting BAH-Transit, then starting BAH.
3. **Partial BAH on an off-base member.** Check entitlement before upgrading. A Marine E-5 and below without dependents who is offered adequate quarters and lives off base at own expense stays on BAH-Partial. Full BAH needs Installation Commander approval for non-availability or an approved exception. E-6 and above elect off base without approval under MCO 11000.22 Chapter 10, unless military necessity applies.
4. **Reserve drilling member drawing BAH.** Drilling reservist not on active duty orders should not draw BAH. Stop and document.
5. **Sea duty BAH location wrong.** Member home port BAH at wrong zip code. Fix by updating home port zip code.

## OHA Review

Per DOD FMR Volume 7A<Citation source="DOD FMR Volume 7A" /> Chapter 26 paragraph 6.2.3, recompute OHA whenever rent changes. Chapter 26 sets no annual review. Any periodic review follows local OHA audit procedures.

- Verify lease still active
- Verify the utility point score matches the utilities the rent includes
- Verify OHA rate current
- Member confirms continued accuracy

## Coordination Touchpoints

Special BAH categories touch.

- **DTMO**: For OHA rates and BAH information
- **Local installation housing office**: For government quarters status
- **Overseas housing officials**: For OHA-specific assistance
- **Member's chain of command**: For hardship and special situation documentation

## Related Roles

Special BAH categories affect specific groups of Marines.

- **Marines** in special situations submit appropriate documentation and verify pay.
- **Leaders** counsel members on applicable category and route documentation.
- **Commanders** support hardship cases and unique situation processing.
