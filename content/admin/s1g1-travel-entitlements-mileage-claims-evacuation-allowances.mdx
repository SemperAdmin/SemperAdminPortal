## Role and responsibility

The IPAC pay clerk processes evacuation allowances per DoDFMR Vol 9<Citation source="DODFMR Vol 9" /> Chapter 7. The S-1 officer signs by direction on routing. The unit commander supports the evacuation execution. The State Department coordinates the evacuation order through the Department of State Foreign Affairs Manual when civilian evacuees are involved. The MARFOR command coordinates family member and civilian evacuation per the JTR<Citation source="JTR" /> Chapter 6. Marines travel on TDY orders.

Source documents flow from the evacuation order (natural disaster, hostile action, voluntary, ordered), the JTR Chapter 6 entitlement framework, the safe haven designation, the safe haven allowance rates for the safe haven location, the supporting receipts, and the prior pay record. Outputs include the executed evacuation travel, the documented safe haven designation, the safe haven allowance payment, the travel allowance for evacuation movement, the evacuation advance and payment record, the return travel processing, and the audit trail.

Per DoDFMR Vol 9 Chapter 7, evacuation allowances cover authorized evacuees (civilian employees and family members) during evacuation events. Per Vol 9 Chapter 7 paragraph 2.2.1, uniformed members are not evacuated. They travel on TDY orders. Per JTR Chapter 6 sections 0601 to 0603<Citation source="JTR" />, the operational implementation sets safe haven allowances and travel allowances. The State Department Foreign Affairs Manual covers civilian evacuee coordination at overseas locations.

## Trigger events

- Evacuation order issued (natural disaster, hostile action).
- Voluntary evacuation authorization.
- Safe haven designation update.
- Per diem rate update for safe haven location.
- Return travel authorization after evacuation.
- Evacuation termination order.

## Processing workflow

1. Receive the evacuation order. The IPAC pay clerk receives from the chain of command, MARFOR, or the State Department.
2. Identify the evacuation category. Per DoDFMR Vol 9<Citation source="DODFMR Vol 9" /> Chapter 7:
   - Natural disaster evacuation.
   - Hostile action evacuation.
   - Ordered departure.
   - Voluntary departure.
3. Apply evacuation entitlements. Per Vol 9 Chapter 7 and JTR<Citation source="JTR" /> Chapter 6, the entitlement framework covers civilian employees and authorized family members. Service members travel on TDY orders.
4. Process safe haven designation. The State Department or DoD designates the safe haven location.
5. Apply safe haven allowances. Per JTR Chapter 6 paragraph 060205, safe haven allowances apply while the evacuee is at the safe haven.
6. Process standard allowances for evacuees. Per JTR Chapter 6 paragraph 060206 and DTMO rates, standard allowances apply while at the designated place.
7. Process travel allowances. Per Vol 9 Chapter 7, travel allowances cover the movement from the evacuated location to the safe haven and the return.
8. Process evacuation advances and payment. Per Vol 9 Chapter 7 paragraph 2.1, the Defense Travel System does not support evacuation travel. Evacuees use departure orders for advances and payment through finance.
9. Coordinate with disbursing. Per DoDFMR Vol 5, the disbursing technician processes evacuation claims.
10. Process return travel and entitlement termination. When the evacuation terminates, the IPAC processes return travel and ends evacuation entitlements.

## Systems of record and forms

### Systems

- Defense Travel System (DTS). Does not support evacuation travel. Per Vol 9 Chapter 7 paragraph 2.1.
- DTMO Per Diem Rate Tables for safe haven location. Per JTR.
- State Department Coordination Records. For overseas civilian evacuees.
- DFAS Pay Processing. Per Vol 9 Chapter 8.

### Forms

- Evacuation Order. Per DoD and State Department.
- Evacuation departure orders. Per Vol 9 Chapter 7.
- Safe Haven Designation Memorandum. Per Vol 9 Chapter 7.
- Safe haven allowance documentation. Per JTR Chapter 6.

## Common pitfalls

- Evacuation entitlement not applied per category. Per DoDFMR Vol 9<Citation source="DODFMR Vol 9" /> Chapter 7, the category drives the entitlement.
- Safe haven per diem rate misaligned. Per DTMO and the JTR<Citation source="JTR" />, the safe haven location and date drive the rate.
- Safe haven allowances not processed. Per JTR Chapter 6 paragraph 060205, safe haven allowances cover the evacuee at the safe haven.
- Return travel not processed at evacuation termination. Per Vol 9 Chapter 7, the return travel processes when the evacuation ends.
- Family member evacuation without authorization. Per Vol 9 Chapter 7 and JTR Chapter 6, family member evacuation requires authorization.
- Civilian employee evacuation coordination skipped. The State Department coordinates civilian evacuee actions.

## Decision points

- Evacuation category. Per Vol 9 Chapter 7, the category (natural disaster, hostile action, ordered, voluntary) drives the entitlement.
- Safe haven location. The State Department and DoD designate the safe haven.
- Safe haven versus standard allowances. Per JTR Chapter 6 paragraphs 060205 and 060206, safe haven allowances apply at the safe haven. Standard allowances apply at the designated place.
- Return travel timing. When the evacuation terminates, the return travel processes.
