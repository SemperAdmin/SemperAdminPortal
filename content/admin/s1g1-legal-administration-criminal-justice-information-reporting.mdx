## Role and responsibility

MCO 5810.8<Citation source="MCO 5810.8" /> enclosure 2 places the reporting duty on the commanding officer (CO). The CO provides supporting documentation (for example the report of investigation or the Unit Punishment Book) and directs the member to report to the servicing law enforcement agency (LEA), such as NCIS or the installation Provost Marshal's Office (PMO), per enclosure 2 paragraph 2. The CO gives written notice of the disposition to the servicing LEA and/or correctional facility within five working days per enclosure 2 paragraph 3.a. The order names no S-1 role. Local SOP assigns the S-1 staff to stage the package for CO signature and to track the five-working-day window. The CO consults the servicing Staff Judge Advocate (SJA) before determining a member is a prohibited person per enclosure 2 paragraph 6.

Source documents flow from the disposition action. Enclosure 2 paragraph 2 lists them: a civilian court restraining order, a SPCM, GCM, or civilian court conviction for a crime of domestic violence, an Article 112a finding at NJP, administrative separation proceeding, SCM, or SPCM, a GCM referral, a GCM conviction of an offense punishable by more than one year, a finding of incompetence to stand trial or not guilty by reason of lack of mental responsibility, UA or deserter status following preferral, and a return from deserter status. Outputs are the written notice to the servicing LEA, the supporting documentation package, and the written counseling of the prohibited person. The CJIS Systems Officer (CSO) at MCICOM G-3/5 LE, or a written designee, alone enters or removes offender information in the NICS Indices per enclosure 3 paragraph 3.a. The unit makes no NICS entry.

Per enclosure 2 paragraph 3.a, the five-working-day clock runs from the disposition. The order carries no separate notification trigger.

## Trigger events

- Civilian court issues a restraining order against a unit member (enclosure 2 paragraph 2.a). The member owes the CO notice within 24 hours of service per enclosure 5 paragraph 2.b.
- Civilian court convicts a unit member of a crime of domestic violence (enclosure 2 paragraph 2.a). The member owes the CO notice within 24 hours per enclosure 5 paragraph 2.a.
- SPCM or GCM convicts a member of a crime of domestic violence (enclosure 2 paragraph 2.a).
- NJP, administrative separation proceeding, SCM, or SPCM finds wrongful use of a controlled substance under Article 112a (enclosure 2 paragraph 2.b). The finding carries a 12-month prohibition on weapons possession from the date of adjudication.
- Charges referred to a GCM when at least one charge is punishable by more than one year of confinement (enclosure 2 paragraphs 1.b and 2.c.(1)).
- GCM convicts a member of any offense punishable by more than one year of confinement, regardless of confinement awarded (enclosure 2 paragraph 2.c.(2)).
- SPCM or GCM finds a member incompetent to stand trial or not guilty by reason of lack of mental responsibility (enclosure 2 paragraph 2.c.(3)).
- A member enters an unauthorized absence or deserter status following preferral of charges (enclosure 2 paragraph 2.c.(4)).
- A member returns from deserter status (enclosure 2 paragraph 2.d).
- A member self-reports a domestic violence conviction or a civilian restraining order (enclosure 2 paragraph 7).
- IGMC Legal Administration FA inspection covers Criminal Justice Information Reporting.

## Processing workflow

1. Identify the reporting trigger. Per MCO 5810.8 enclosure 2 paragraph 2, match the disposition to the category (2.a domestic violence, 2.b wrongful drug use, 2.c court-martial, 2.d return from deserter status).
2. Document the disposition with supporting evidence. Stage the disposition record (court-martial results, civilian court order, NJP UPB entry, administrative separation finding) and the supporting documentation named in enclosure 2 paragraph 2.
3. Direct the member to report to the servicing LEA. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 2, the CO directs the member under the CO's authority to report to the servicing LEA (NCIS or the installation PMO). At a PMO or Marine Corps Police Department, the Desk Sergeant is the primary coordination point per enclosure 3 paragraph 6.a.
4. Provide supporting documentation. The CO forwards the package to the servicing LEA. The S-1 retains a copy per local SOP.
5. Give written notice within five working days of disposition. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 3.a, units aboard a Marine Corps installation with a servicing LEA notify the LEA and/or correctional facility in writing. Per paragraph 3.b, units not aboard such an installation email Marine Corps CID at usmccid@ncis.navy.mil with the subject "REQUEST FOR NICS ENTRY" within five working days of the disposition.
6. Route deployed cases. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 4, when conduct in a deployed environment triggers paragraph 2, the CO directs the member to the servicing LEA as soon as possible after redeployment.
7. Counsel prohibited persons. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 6.a, after consulting the servicing SJA and determining the member is a prohibited person, the CO counsels the member in writing to abide by federal law on restricted access to personally owned firearms and ammunition and to arrange disposal. For a permanent prohibition, the CO advises lawful disposal (paragraph 6.a.(1)). For a temporary prohibition (restraining order, wrongful drug use), the CO has discretion to offer temporary storage without access in the unit armory and is not required to (paragraph 6.a.(2)).
8. Keep prohibited persons out of unit armory stocks. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 5.a, the CO does not permit a prohibited person access to privately owned firearms stored in unit armories. Per paragraph 6.b, coordinate with the PMO for an escort when the member moves personally owned firearms and ammunition. For items outside Marine Corps jurisdiction, coordinate with civilian LEAs through the PMO and the servicing SJA.
9. Apply the government weapons rule. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 5.b, the CO is authorized to issue government weapons and ammunition for government use (for example rifle qualification) to a member under the paragraph 1.a and 1.b prohibitions. The exception excludes members convicted of a domestic violence offense. Do not issue to them.
10. Manage the records. Per MCO 5810.8 paragraph 5.a, records created under the order follow the NARA-approved dispositions in SECNAV M-5210.1 and MCO 5210.11F. Handle PII per the Privacy Act under paragraph 5.b.

## Systems of record and forms

### Systems

- Servicing Law Enforcement Agency (NCIS, installation PMO, Marine Corps CID). Receives the notice and the supporting documentation. Marine Corps LEAs submit fingerprints and DNA per enclosure 3 paragraphs 2 and 4.
- National Instant Criminal Background Check System (NICS). The CSO enters and removes offender information in the NICS Indices per enclosure 3 paragraph 3.
- National Crime Information Center (NCIC). The CO forwards all Military Protection Orders to the servicing LEA for inclusion in NCIC per Appendix B paragraph 12.
- Unit Legal File. Holds the retained copies per local SOP and the disposition schedule.

### Forms

- DD Form 2760, "Qualification to Possess Firearms or Ammunition." The member completes it to self-report a domestic violence conviction. The CO also directs completion when the CO reasonably believes a conviction exists, then forwards the completed form to the servicing LEA. Per paragraph 5.c.(1) and enclosure 2 paragraph 7.a.
- DD Form 2873, "Military Protection Order." The CO issues an MPO on this form. An MPO is not a restraining order for the Gun Control Act prohibition. Per paragraph 5.c.(2) and Appendix B paragraph 12.
- Written notice to the servicing LEA, with supporting documentation. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 3.a. The order prescribes no form number.
- Prohibited person counseling in writing. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 6.a. The order prescribes no form number.

## Common pitfalls

- Notice past five working days. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 3, the notice runs within five working days of the disposition.
- Wrong channel for a unit away from an installation LEA. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 3.b, email CID at usmccid@ncis.navy.mil with the subject "REQUEST FOR NICS ENTRY."
- Member not directed to report to the servicing LEA. The CO's direction is required per enclosure 2 paragraph 2.
- Expecting fingerprints and a case number on administrative dispositions. Per enclosure 3 paragraphs 2.d and 6.c, the LEA does not collect fingerprints or open a case for NJP, administrative separation, or SCM dispositions, or for civilian restraining orders, unless an independent requirement exists. The CO still reports them for the NICS entry.
- Treating a GCM referral as a bar on current possession. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 1.b, the member keeps firearms already in possession until conviction. The member does not receive, ship, or transport firearms or ammunition.
- Prohibited person counseling skipped. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 6.a, the counseling is in writing after SJA consultation.
- Prohibited person with access to privately owned firearms in the armory. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 5.a, the CO does not permit it.
- Government weapons issued to a member convicted of domestic violence. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 5.b, the government use exception does not apply.
- Disciplinary action against a self-reporting member without SJA input. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 8, consult the SJA first.
- MPO left out of the LEA package. Per Appendix B paragraph 12, forward all MPOs to the servicing LEA for NCIC.

## Decision points

- Reporting trigger classification. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 2, the disposition type maps to the category. Article 112a findings route under 2.b. GCM referrals and convictions route under 2.c. Domestic violence and restraining orders route under 2.a.
- Servicing channel. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 3, units aboard an installation with a servicing LEA notify the servicing LEA in writing. Other units email CID.
- Permanent versus temporary prohibition. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 6.a, a permanent prohibition leads to lawful disposal advice. A temporary prohibition (restraining order, wrongful drug use) allows the optional temporary storage without access.
- Self-report handling. Per enclosure 2 paragraph 7, a domestic violence self-report goes on DD Form 2760 to the LEA. For a restraining order self-report, the CO directs the member to provide a copy of the order, in consultation with the SJA, and forwards it to the LEA.
- Individual Marine duty. Per enclosure 5 paragraph 2, the member notifies the CO within 24 hours of a civilian domestic violence conviction or service of a civilian restraining order. Violations are punishable under the UCMJ per paragraph 6.a.(1).
