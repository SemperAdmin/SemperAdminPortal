## Role and responsibility

S-1 admin specialists own casualty processing at the unit level. The S-1 chief gives MFPC a voice report within 30 minutes and emails the Personnel Casualty Report (PCR) on the DCIPS-PCR spreadsheet within one hour, per MARADMIN 186/25. The reporting unit assigns the Casualty Assistance Calls Officer (CACO) after coordination with MFPC, and the commander selects the CACO. MFPC tasks a closer activity when needed. The CACO runs primary next-of-kin (PNOK) notification per MCO 3040.4<Citation source="MCO 3040.4" />.

Source documents flow from incident reports, NCIS or law enforcement notifications, hospital reports, and unit chain of command notifications. The S-1 emails PCRs to casualty.section@usmc.mil, the HQMC Casualty Section (MFPC). Outputs include the certified PCR, the PNOK notification record, the SGLI claim packet, the funeral honors coordination, and the casualty case file.

MCAAT and the IGMC Casualty Affairs FA inspectors check the 30-minute voice report and the one-hour PCR template per MARADMIN 186/25, PNOK notification procedures, SGLI claim processing, and case file completeness. Late PCR or improper PNOK notification triggers immediate findings under MCO 3040.4<Citation source="MCO 3040.4" />.

## Trigger events

- Death in service (hostile, non-hostile, training-related, or off-duty).
- Serious injury or illness requiring CACO support.
- Missing or duty status whereabouts unknown (DUSTWUN).
- Captured or interned status.
- Wounded in action requiring family notification.
- Suicide or attempted suicide.
- Mass casualty event involving multiple Marines.

## Processing workflow

1. Receive casualty notification. Pull from chain, NCIS, hospital, or law enforcement. The S-1 chief runs receipt.
2. Verify Marine identity and status. Cross-check EDIPI, current MCTFS status, and dependency state.
3. Report to MFPC. Give a voice report within 30 minutes of learning of a reportable garrison casualty. Submit the PCR on the DCIPS-PCR spreadsheet, emailed to casualty.section@usmc.mil, within one hour. Per MARADMIN 186/25 paragraphs 2 and 6, the spreadsheet replaced DCIPS-8.0 (DCIPS-Forward) as the sole authorized method. Chapter 3 of MCO 3040.4<Citation source="MCO 3040.4" /> still governs report content.
4. Coordinate PNOK notification. Per MCO 3040.4<Citation source="MCO 3040.4" /> chapter 4 section 2 paragraph 2.a, in-person notification covers deceased, DUSTWUN and missing Marines. MFPC telephones the PNOK and SNOK for wounded, ill and injured Marines. The Marine Corps goal is every required notification within 8 hours of learning of the casualty.
5. Route SGLI claim and death gratuity. Coordinate DD Form 397 (Claim Certification and Voucher for Death Gratuity Payment, pre-populated by MFPC) per MCO 3040.4<Citation source="MCO 3040.4" /> chapter 7 section 2 paragraph 2.c, and the SGLV 8283 (Claim for Death Benefits), which the beneficiary files through MFPC per MCO 3040.4<Citation source="MCO 3040.4" /> chapter 7 section 3 paragraph 2.c and DODFMR Vol 7A<Citation source="DODFMR Vol 7A" /> Chapter 47 paragraph 13.0. SGLI elections run in SOES per Chapter 47. Chapter 36 covers the death gratuity only.
6. Process casualty pay and benefits. For missing or MIA Marines, continue pay per DODFMR Vol 7A<Citation source="DODFMR Vol 7A" /> Chapter 34 paragraph 4.0 Entitlement While in Missing Status. Dependency continuation and survivor benefit processing per DODI 1300.18<Citation source="DODI 1300.18" />.
7. Coordinate funeral honors. Route burial detail, military honors, and chaplain support per MCO 3040.4<Citation source="MCO 3040.4" />. Funeral honors run through the CACO and MFPC.
8. Update MCTFS. Coordinate with the Personnel Administrative Center (PAC), which runs the deceased transaction in MCTFS per MCO 3040.4<Citation source="MCO 3040.4" />. Give the IPAC a copy of the PCR.
9. File the certified casualty packet. Stage in the casualty case file and route copies to the OMPF Field Folder.
10. Brief commander. The S-1 officer briefs case status, family support posture, and benefits delivered.

## Systems of record and forms

### Systems

- MCTFS. Holds casualty status code and final pay state.
- DCIPS. Defense Casualty Information Processing System for casualty roll-up.
- Personnel Administrative Center (PAC). Runs the deceased transaction in MCTFS.
- HQMC Casualty Section. Coordinates national-level reporting and family liaison.

### Forms

- Personnel Casualty Report (PCR). DCIPS-PCR spreadsheet emailed within one hour, per MARADMIN 186/25.
- DD Form 93 Record of Emergency Data. Identifies PNOK and beneficiaries.
- DD Form 397 Claim Certification and Voucher for Death Gratuity Payment. Death gratuity processing.
- SGLV 8283 SGLI death claim. Insurance benefit processing, not the death gratuity.
- DD Form 2064 Certificate of Death (Overseas). For overseas casualty processing.

## Common pitfalls

- Voice report or PCR late. Per MARADMIN 186/25, the voice report is due in 30 minutes and the template in one hour. The 8 hours in MCO 3040.4<Citation source="MCO 3040.4" /> chapter 4 is the notification goal from learning of the casualty. Submit immediately.
- PNOK notified by the wrong method. Per MCO 3040.4<Citation source="MCO 3040.4" /> chapter 4 section 2 paragraph 2.a, deceased, DUSTWUN and missing Marines get in-person notification. MFPC telephones the family for ill or injured Marines.
- DD Form 93 stale. The PNOK identification is wrong. Audit the DD Form 93 at check-in, TAD over 30 days, before deployment, before PCS departure and triennially.
- SGLI claim packet incomplete. Beneficiary processing delays. Stage every required form before family signature.
- Casualty status code not updated in MCTFS. Pay continues incorrectly. Coordinate with the PAC to run the deceased transaction and give the IPAC a copy of the PCR per MCO 3040.4<Citation source="MCO 3040.4" />.

## Decision points

- Hostile, non-hostile, or training-related casualty category. Per MCO 3040.4<Citation source="MCO 3040.4" />, category drives notification routing and benefit processing. The S-1 officer routes per the right category.
- CACO assignment. The CACO must be an SNCO, warrant officer or commissioned officer. Equal or higher grade than the casualty is a preference. The unit or MFPC assigns per MCO 3040.4<Citation source="MCO 3040.4" /> chapter 4 section 1 paragraph 2.c(1).
- Mass casualty response. Multiple casualties trigger the unit casualty assistance plan. The commander activates per MCO 3040.4<Citation source="MCO 3040.4" />.

## Authority

This category sits under MCO 3040.4<Citation source="MCO 3040.4" /> Marine Corps Casualty Assistance Program. DoD-level policy at DODI 1300.18<Citation source="DODI 1300.18" />. S-1 responsibilities sit at MCO 5000.14D<Citation source="MCO 5000.14D" /> MCAP enclosure 4. The processing workflow maps to NAVMC 3500.3E T-R events 0102-OPER-1002 (Supervise casualty management), 0111-OPER-2001 (Process Personnel Casualty Report), and 0102-GENA-2111 (Advise Commander on PNOK Notification). The IGMC Casualty Affairs FA checklist provides the audit baseline.
