## Role and responsibility

Per MARADMIN 093/25<Citation source="MARADMIN 093/25" /> paragraph 4, all DoN personal military decorations from Marine Corps commands are originated in iAPS (improved Awards Processing System). Award originators submit the recommendation in iAPS. Unit award administrators identify the outbound population before PCS season. The S-1 chief supervises iAPS users in the section. The awards clerk prepares nominations for the originator.

Each person signs only the block for their own role. Per MCO 1650.19J<Citation source="MCO 1650.19J" /> Enclosure 5, the originator completes the originator block, each endorser signs the forwarding endorsement block, and only an authorized approval authority signs Block 24 to approve the award. The S-1 officer signs only when holding one of those roles.

Per MARADMIN 665/17<Citation source="MARADMIN 665/17" /> paragraph 2, iAPS supports originators, endorsers, and awarding authorities. Outputs include the iAPS case, the signed approval, the uploaded citation and certificate, and the unit awards register entry.

## Trigger events

- New Marine joins the awards processing section and needs iAPS access.
- Award nomination ready for iAPS entry.
- Signature action queued for an endorser or the approval authority.
- Approved award ready for citation and certificate upload.
- PCS season begins and the outbound population needs review.
- Administrative action required on existing award entry.

## Processing workflow

1. Confirm iAPS access and role. Originators, endorsers, and awarding authorities each hold a different role in the system.
2. Originate the award in iAPS. Per MARADMIN 093/25<Citation source="MARADMIN 093/25" /> paragraph 4, every DoN PMD from a Marine Corps command is originated in iAPS.
3. Enter the Summary of Action. Per paragraph 4.a, the SOA limit is two pages. Use the Print 11533 (PDF) button in iAPS to verify length.
4. Route through chain for endorsements. The originator enters the Via addressees per MCO 1650.19J<Citation source="MCO 1650.19J" /> Enclosure 5 Block 23. Each endorser signs their own endorsement.
5. Hold the approval signature. Per Enclosure 5 Block 24, only an authorized approval authority signs and approves the award. The S-1 prepares and routes. The S-1 officer does not sign for the approval authority.
6. Upload the citation and certificate. Per MARADMIN 093/25 paragraph 4.b, attach only the citation and certificate to the certificate section of the iAPS case.
7. Track iAPS case status. Pull case status on a fixed cycle.
8. Document iAPS activity. Update the unit awards register with the case number, status, and outcome.

## Systems of record and forms

### Systems

- iAPS (improved Awards Processing System). Originating system for DoN PMDs from Marine Corps commands.
- CMC (MMPB-31). HQMC Awards Branch receives nominations in iAPS.
- MCTFS. Holds award entries on approval.
- OMPF. Final record of approved awards.
- Unit awards register. Local register tracking iAPS submissions.

### Forms

- Print 11533 (PDF). iAPS button to render the SOA for a page-length check.
- Citation. Narrative justifying the award (uploaded to the iAPS certificate section).
- Summary of Action. Detailed action narrative, two pages maximum (entered in iAPS).

## Common pitfalls

- Award originated outside iAPS. Per MARADMIN 093/25<Citation source="MARADMIN 093/25" /> paragraph 4, all DoN PMDs from Marine Corps commands originate in iAPS.
- SOA over two pages. The iAPS SOA field has no limit, so check with Print 11533 (PDF) before routing.
- S-1 officer signs the approval block without holding approval authority. Per MCO 1650.19J Enclosure 5 Block 24, only an authorized approval authority signs.
- Attachments beyond the citation and certificate. Attach only those two in the certificate section.
- Administrative action handled outside iAPS. The audit trail breaks.

## Decision points

- Originator versus processor role. The originator submits the recommendation. The awards clerk and S-1 chief prepare and route it.
- Delegated authority. The S-1 officer validates approval authority against delegation memoranda, including MARADMIN 077/25<Citation source="MARADMIN 077/25" />.
- Administrative action versus correction. The S-1 chief routes per the action type.
