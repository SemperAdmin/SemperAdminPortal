## Purpose

The Unit Punishment Book (UPB), NAVMC 10132<Citation source="NAVMC 10132" />, is the legal document of record for Non-Judicial Punishment proceedings under Article 15 UCMJ. S-1 admin clerks prepare the UPB for the commander before mast and document results after. The UPB drives unit diary entries, OMPF filing, and pay impacts.

## When You Prepare a UPB

- Commander has accepted an NJP referral.
- Member has elected to accept NJP (or refused trial by court-martial).
- Charges have been documented and witness statements collected.
- The mast is scheduled and on the calendar.

## Conditions and Standard

Per T&R event 0111-GENA-1003, you perform this work with access to web-based systems (Command Legal Action System CLA) or database systems, the Electronic Service Record (ESR), and source documents on hand. The standard is to ensure no format, typographical, or grammatical errors.

## Performance Steps

### Pre-Mast (Items 1 through 4, 17 through 20, and 22)

1. Item 1 - UCMJ offenses alleged and summaries. List each offense by article with a summary with date, time, and place. Leave out victim PII. Per MCO 5800.16<Citation source="MCO 5800.16" /> Volume 14 paragraph 011105.A, model Manual for Courts-Martial charge language is allowed but not required. Do not add offenses after the accused signs.
2. Item 2 - Article 31 rights advisement and NJP election. The accused signs to acknowledge the rights, the right to demand trial by court-martial, and the election to accept NJP. If the accused refuses, the commander signs.
3. Item 3 - Commander rights certification. The commander certifies the Article 31 advisement and the court-martial demand right, including the vessel exception when it applies.
4. Item 4 - Current unauthorized absences over 24 hours and marks of desertion.
5. Items 17 through 20 - Unit, accused name, rank or grade, and EDIPI. Pull from MCTFS and check against the ESR.
6. Item 22 - Victim status, sex, race, and ethnicity.

### Post-Mast (Items 5 through 16 and 21)

1. Item 5 - Findings. The commander enters G for guilty or NG for not guilty for each offense listed in Item 1.
2. Item 6 - Punishment imposed and date. Record each punishment imposed. Common entries include reduction in grade, forfeiture of pay, restriction, extra duties, and correctional custody.
3. Item 7 - Suspension of execution of punishment, or NONE. Note any suspended terms and the suspension period.
4. Items 8 and 9 - NJP authority. Item 8 names the NJP authority. The NJP authority signs Item 9.
5. Items 10 through 12 - Notice of final disposition and appeal rights. Item 11 records the advisement of the right to appeal. In Item 12 the accused elects to appeal or not.
6. Items 13 through 15 - Appeal. Record the date of appeal, the decision, and the notice of the decision.
7. Item 16 - Final administrative action completed.
8. Item 21 - Remarks. Enter the required chronological entries per the form instructions.

### Required Attachments

Per MCO 5800.16<Citation source="MCO 5800.16" /> Volume 14 paragraph 011106, the officer imposing NJP prepares a summary transcript of the proceedings, and the summary is appended to the UPB form. Paragraph 011103 governs the UPB packet. Use the Appendix A-1-f form or a locally prepared form with enough detail to show what happened at the proceedings.

## Pay Impact Coordination

Forfeitures and reductions affect pay. Coordinate with the supporting PAC.

| Punishment | Pay System Impact |
|---|---|
| Reduction in grade | UD/MIPS reduction transaction. The reduction takes effect on the date the commander imposes the punishment if unsuspended. Report it with the imposition date. |
| Forfeiture of pay (unsuspended) | Takes effect on the imposition date via UD/MIPS. Enter whole dollars per month for a stated number of months. |
| Forfeiture of pay (suspended) | No immediate deduction. Track suspension period. |
| Restriction | No direct pay impact. Document for command records. |
| Extra duties | No direct pay impact. Document for command records. |
| Correctional custody | No direct pay impact. Do not report a confinement transaction. |

## Common Errors

S-1 audits surface these errors most often on UPB prep.

1. **Incomplete offense summary.** The Item 1 summary lacks the date, time, or place. Fix before the accused signs.
2. **Missing rights advisement signature.** Member did not sign the Item 2 acknowledgment. Fix before mast.
3. **Wrong commanding officer signature.** Acting commander not properly designated in writing. Fix by attaching designation memo.
4. **Late forfeiture transaction.** UD/MIPS not submitted within the pay period. Fix by submitting with retroactive effective date.

## Routing

After completion, the UPB packet routes through these stops.

1. S-1 review (admin chief and S-1 officer)
2. Commander signature (final)
3. Member receives copy
4. UD/MIPS transactions submitted to PAC
5. UPB filed in command legal records
6. Copy of the UPB forwarded to the IPAC/Administrative section for unit diary reporting and scanning into the Marine's OMPF and ESR, per MCO 5800.16 Volume 14 paragraph 011109
7. Promotion eligibility flag updated in MOL if required

## Related Roles

This event reads differently depending on your role.

- **Marines** facing NJP review their rights, election options, and the appeal process. They sign the UPB acknowledging procedural rights.
- **Leaders** counsel Marines pre-NJP, witness statements, and post Page 11 entries documenting the underlying conduct.
- **Commanders** preside at mast, render findings, impose punishment, and sign the UPB. They own the legal sufficiency of the proceeding.
