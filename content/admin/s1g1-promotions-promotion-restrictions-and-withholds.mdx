## Role and responsibility

The S-1 chief owns the restriction screen per MCO P1400.32D<Citation source="MCO P1400.32D" /> Chapter 1 paragraph 1204 (Promotion Restrictions). The S-1 officer routes Page 11 entries. The commander makes the non-recommendation decision. Who signs the clearance memorandum is local practice, because MCO P1400.32D names no signer. The promotions clerk maintains the unit restriction register.

Source documents flow from NJP records, court-martial actions, failed PFT or CFT records, weight control program (BCP) entries, and chain-of-command non-recommendation decisions. Restriction actions land in MCTFS as a promotion suspension marker. Outputs include the Page 11 restriction counseling entry per MCO P1400.32D paragraph 1204.5, the unit restriction register entry, and the clearance memorandum when applicable.

Per MCO P1400.32D Chapter 1 paragraph 1204, restrictions cover discipline (NJP, court-martial), performance (failed PFT/CFT), and administrative (not recommended for reenlistment RE-4/4B, RE-3C/3P, pending separation, BCP/MAP) state. Per paragraph 1203.4a, the enlisted promotion system has no medical prerequisites. Hospitalization, temporary limited duty, and a pending PEB do not remove eligibility or selection outside the exceptions listed in paragraph 1203.4a. For officers, MCO 1400.31D<Citation source="MCO 1400.31D" /> sets the adverse information withhold (AIW) and promotion delay rules.

## Trigger events

- NJP imposed or charges preferred against the Marine.
- Failed PFT, CFT, body composition, or weight standards.
- Chain-of-command non-recommendation decision.
- Officer adverse information surfaces after selection.
- Restriction clearance documentation arrives from the chain.

## Processing workflow

1. Identify the restriction trigger. Pull the discipline, performance, or administrative event from the source system or memorandum.
2. Validate the restriction basis. Per MCO P1400.32D<Citation source="MCO P1400.32D" /> Chapter 1 paragraph 1204, confirm the event meets the restriction criteria.
3. Route the NOT RECOMMENDED entry and the Page 11. Per MARADMIN 559/23<Citation source="MARADMIN 559/23" /> paragraph 2, a NOT RECOMMENDED entry carries a Page 11 entry. Per MCO P1400.32D<Citation source="MCO P1400.32D" /> paragraph 1204.5, a Marine in the grade of corporal or below under a restriction needs a Page 11 counseling entry. Draft, route, and file the Page 11.
4. Update MCTFS. Submit the restriction transaction through UD-MIPS per MCTFSPRIUM<Citation source="MCTFSPRIUM" /> per the restriction type.
5. Track restriction duration. Record the start date, basis, clearance criteria, and projected clearance date in the unit restriction register.
6. Process officer AIW. Per MCO 1400.31D<Citation source="MCO 1400.31D" />, the officer adverse information withhold suspends the officer promotion pending review.
7. Document withhold, denial, or removal. Per Chapter 1 paragraph 1204, the action lands as a Page 11 entry, an officer record entry, or a removal-from-list action depending on the path.
8. Brief the Marine or officer. The S-1 chief briefs the Marine on the restriction basis and the clearance path.
9. Route clearance documentation. The chain of command clears the restriction via memorandum. The S-1 cuts the MCTFS clearance transaction. Restrictions end by elapsed period or event. A waiver needs its own Page 11 per MCO P1400.32D paragraph 1204.5.
10. Audit the unit roster. Reconcile the unit promotion roster against the restriction register at each cycle.

## Systems of record and forms

### Systems

- MCTFS. Holds restriction markers per type.
- UD-MIPS. Cuts restriction and clearance transactions.
- MOL. Surfaces restriction state and Page 11 entries to the Marine.
- OMPF and OQR. Hold the Page 11 and officer record entries.
- Unit restriction register. Local register of active restrictions.

### Forms

- NAVMC 118 (11) Administrative Remarks. Page 11 documenting non-recommendation, restriction, or clearance.
- NAVMC 10132 Unit Punishment Book. NJP record and discipline source document.
- BCP Entry. Administrative source document.
- Officer AIW Letter. Per MCO 1400.31D.

## Common pitfalls

- NOT RECOMMENDED entry not reported in MCTFS before the 1st of the month. The Marine promotes automatically despite chain-of-command intent. Report the entry before the 1st per MARADMIN 559/23<Citation source="MARADMIN 559/23" /> paragraph 2, with the Page 11 as the supporting record.
- Restriction marker not cut in MCTFS. The pay and personnel system shows the Marine as eligible. Cut the transaction per MCTFSPRIUM<Citation source="MCTFSPRIUM" />.
- Officer AIW handled without MCO 1400.31D reference. The officer process diverges from the order. Route per MCO 1400.31D<Citation source="MCO 1400.31D" />.
- Clearance memorandum not staged in OMPF. The OMPF lands without the clearance record. File the clearance memorandum and any waiver Page 11.
- Restriction screen skipped at cycle. The Marine sits in zone with an active hold. Per MCO P1400.32D<Citation source="MCO P1400.32D" /> Chapter 1 paragraph 1204, run the screen at every cycle.

## Decision points

- Discipline restriction duration. Per MCO P1400.32D<Citation source="MCO P1400.32D" /> Chapter 1 paragraph 1204, paragraph 1204.4j sets a fixed 3-month restriction from the date NJP is awarded. A punitive reduction to Pvt through Cpl adds the full time in grade requirement for the lower grade. The S-1 officer routes per the NJP record.
- Medical state is not a restriction. Per MCO P1400.32D<Citation source="MCO P1400.32D" /> paragraph 1203.4a, hospitalization and temporary limited duty do not remove eligibility outside the exceptions listed in paragraph 1203.4a. Do not cut a restriction marker for limited duty or a PEB referral. Use the enumerated paragraph 1204.4 restrictions only.
- Officer AIW versus enlisted restriction. Per MCO 1400.31D<Citation source="MCO 1400.31D" />, the officer process runs through CMC (MMPR-1), the promotion branch, under the post-board screening and withhold process in paragraph 5003. Enlisted SNCO restrictions report to CMC (MMPR-2) per MCO P1400.32D Chapter 1 paragraph 1204.7.
