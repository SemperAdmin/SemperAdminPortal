## Role and responsibility

MPMN function. The commander identifies, signs the referral, and decides administrative absence. The S-1 chief builds the referral, runs the MCTFS and MOL actions, and tracks every timeline. The IPAC stops bonus, special, and incentive pays. Every document routes through MPO per MARADMIN 189/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 189/26" /> paragraph 5.

Authorities. The Commander, Navy Installations Command is the Consolidated Disposition Authority (CDA) and the only authority to initiate. SECNAV is the separation authority. Paragraph 5.a.

Handle every document as protected health information. Access stays with personnel holding a specific need to know for official duties. Paragraph 12.b.

## Trigger events

- MPO confirms BUMED Deployability Action Cell medical verification. Paragraph 4.
- The commander signs a referral letter. Paragraph 3.b.
- The CDA notifies the Marine of separation proceedings. Paragraph 5.b.1.
- The Marine elects or waives a board. Paragraph 5.b.5.
- The Marine requests retirement in lieu of separation. Paragraph 8.
- The Marine is referred to the DES, or becomes subject to another administrative separation. Paragraph 9.

## Processing workflow

1. MPMN. Build the referral in naval letter format per paragraph 3.b. Name, command assigned, EDIPI, and whether the Marine receives special pay or a bonus with a recoupment recommendation and justification. Title the file with the Marine's initials and the last five digits of the EDIPI. Save as PDF.
2. MPMN. Route the signed referral to MPO. MPO verifies completeness and routes to the CDA. Paragraph 3.c.
3. MPMN. On identification, report the Marine in MCTFS as non-deployable with strength category code X and duty status code C per paragraph 10 and MCTFSPRIUM<Citation source="MCTFSPRIUM" />. The status stays until the process concludes.
4. MPMN. Assign duty limitation code E, ADMIN, NON-DEPLOY per paragraph 4.a.1.
5. MPMN. Confirm the Marine starts administrative absence in the MOL Leave and Liberty module, type "Other (Enter Reason in Comments Box)," with this comment per paragraph 4.a.3. "Administrative absence in accordance with ALNAV 011/26 for the duration of the retirement/separation process."
6. MPMN. Stop bonus, special, and incentive pays at the start of administrative absence per paragraph 4.a.2 and 7.d.1. Full pay and benefits continue.
7. MPMN. SMCR. Report each excused drill as EA, OTHER by unit diary per paragraph 4.b.2 with the history statement per paragraph 4.b.3. "Excused absence in accordance with ALNAV 011/26 for the duration of the retirement/separation process." Excused drills earn no drill pay or retirement points.
8. MPMN. Track the board timeline. Notification within 30 calendar days of referral, the Marine's 10-calendar-day election to the CDA and MPO, and at least 30 days from notification to board. Paragraph 5.b and 5.e.1.
9. MPMN. Close the pre-separation checklist before the board per paragraph 12.d. Final physical page 3 of DD 2808 or DD 2807, DD Form 2648, Commanding Officer's Interview for enlisted Marines, DD Form 2963, and the unit check-out sheet.
10. MPMN. Build the twice-monthly report to MPO through the major subordinate command per paragraph 12.c. MPO holds the template.

## Separation codes and terms

| Item | Officer | Enlisted | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basis | Continued service not consistent with the interests of national security | Best interest of the service, Secretarial Authority | Paragraph 5.c |
| SPD code | JDK2 | JFF8 | Paragraph 5.c |
| Board | Board of Inquiry | Administrative separation board | Paragraph 5.b.2 |
| Characterization, sole basis | Honorable | Honorable | Paragraph 5.f.2 |
| Reentry code | RE-3G | RE-3G | Paragraph 7.a |

- No personnel security action runs on the JDK2 basis solely for meeting these criteria. Paragraph 5.c.1.
- Remaining service obligation is waived, including Post-9/11 GI Bill transfer and inactive reserve obligations. Paragraph 7.b.
- Involuntary separation pay eligibility generally requires 6 years of continuous active duty. Paragraph 7.c.
- Recoupment of unearned bonuses and special and incentive pays runs under 37 U.S.C. 373, with a DC M&RA recommendation on the separation package. Paragraph 7.d.2 and 7.d.3.
- Transition benefits stand, except SkillBridge. Paragraph 7.e.

## Retirement requests

Per paragraph 8.b, the request reaches MMSR and MPO within 30 calendar days of identification. Requested date no later than 6 months from identification. Use NAVMC 10274<Citation source="NAVMC 10274" /> per Appendix J, routed via the first general officer and DC M&RA.

- TERA for more than 18 and less than 20 years of active service. SECNAV approves. Paragraph 8.a.2.
- TERA exception to policy for more than 15 and less than 18 years. USW(P&R) approves. Pending requests do not delay the board. Paragraph 8.a.3.
- Marines already approved for retirement transfer no later than 6 months from identification. Paragraph 8.c.

## Common pitfalls

- Extension submitted to finish processing. Barred by MARADMIN 539/26.
- Referral file misnamed. Paragraph 3.b.5 requires initials plus the last five digits of the EDIPI, as PDF.
- Special pay left running into administrative absence. Paragraph 4.a.2 stops it.
- MCTFS status left on the old code. Paragraph 10 requires strength category X and duty status C from identification.
- 24-hour notification missed. MPO hears within 24 hours of a medical verification request, a decision not to use administrative absence or excuse drill, an anticipated board delay, a DES referral, or a concurrent administrative separation.
- Board timeline missed. A missed 30-day board minimum goes to MPO within 24 hours of anticipating it.
- Health information shared outside need to know. Paragraph 12.b.

## Decision points

- Concurrent DES referral. Both run. The DES determination takes precedence, and SECNAV holds the final separation decision until it concludes. Paragraph 9.a.2.
- Concurrent administrative separation on another basis. The other processing takes precedence. This processing holds in abeyance and resumes if the Marine is not separated. Paragraph 9.b.1.
- EAS inside the process. The Marine separates on EAS per MARADMIN 539/26. The message does not state the SPD code or separation pay outcome in this case. Get the answer from MPO before EAS.
