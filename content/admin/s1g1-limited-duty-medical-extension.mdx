## Role and responsibility

The MTF medical officer recommends the medical extension per MCO 1900.16<Citation source="MCO 1900.16" /> Chapter 8 paragraph 8111 (Medical Extension) when the Marine's recovery requires additional time beyond the end of contract. The medical extension extends the contract, not the duty period. The commanding officer approves the first extension on the NAVMC 321M per paragraph 8111.1.a(1), and CMC (MMSR-4) approves later extensions. The S-1 chief tracks the extension period and the extended re-evaluation gate. The IPAC outbound counselor cuts the updated LIMDU transaction in MCTFS through UD-MIPS per MCTFSPRIUM<Citation source="MCTFSPRIUM" />.

Source documents flow from the TLD period, the medical re-evaluation, the medical officer recommendation for extension, the NAVMC 321M, the commanding officer or CMC approval, the Page 11 entry per MCO P1070.12K<Citation source="MCO P1070.12K" /> Chapter 6, and the extended re-evaluation appointment. Medical extension outputs include the approved extension, the documented total LIMDU period (12 months cumulative for enlisted at the MTF), the Page 11 entry, the extended re-evaluation gate, the DES referral package when warranted per Chapter 8 Section 2, or the fit-for-full-duty return record.

Per Chapter 8 paragraph 8111.1.a(3) (Medical Extension), the medical extension is a contract extension. For Marines in a TLD status, it covers the TLD period up to 6 months. For Marines pending DES referral or PEB determination, it runs up to 12 months. For short-term health assessment or medical treatment, it runs up to 2 months. The TLD period itself runs in up to two 6 month periods, 12 months cumulative for enlisted at the MTF, per paragraphs 8003.24 and 8102. A MEB goes to the PEB when the Marine is unlikely to return to full duty within the 12 month cumulative period.

## Trigger events

- TLD re-evaluation arrives with continued medical limitation.
- MTF medical officer recommends additional recovery time.
- Marine requires additional time under contract to support fit-for-full-duty return.
- 12 month cumulative TLD limit approaches.
- Re-evaluation during the extension period surfaces continued condition warranting DES.
- Fit-for-full-duty return during the extension period.

## Processing workflow

1. Receive the MTF medical extension recommendation. The MTF medical officer recommends the extension based on the re-evaluation.
2. Validate the extension request. Per MCO 1900.16<Citation source="MCO 1900.16" /> Chapter 8 paragraph 8111.1.a(3) (Medical Extension), the extension covers the TLD period up to 6 months, DES or PEB cases up to 12 months, and short-term care up to 2 months.
3. Confirm total LIMDU period. Per Chapter 8 paragraphs 8003.24 and 8102, TLD runs 12 months cumulative for enlisted at the MTF. The extension extends the contract, not the duty period.
4. Route the NAVMC 321M. Per Chapter 8 paragraph 8111.1.a, route it to the commanding officer for the first extension and to CMC (MMSR-4) for later ones.
5. Record the approval. The S-1 documents the commanding officer or CMC (MMSR-4) approval on Page 11.
6. Cut updated LIMDU duty status transaction. Per Appendix E and MCTFSPRIUM, the IPAC cuts the updated duty status transaction in UD-MIPS reflecting the extended period.
7. Document on Page 11. Per MCO P1070.12K<Citation source="MCO P1070.12K" /> Chapter 6, cut a Page 11 documenting the medical extension, the new period, the medical scope, and the extended re-evaluation gate.
8. Schedule the extended re-evaluation gate. Per Chapter 8 paragraph 8110, the unit and the IPAC track the extended appointment.
9. Refer to DES on continued condition. Per Chapter 8 Section 2 paragraphs 8201-8208 and DODI 1332.18, the MTF refers to the Disability Evaluation System when the medical condition continues past the extension and warrants PEB review.
10. Close extension. On fit-for-full-duty return, the unit closes the LIMDU status in MCTFS. On DES referral, the LIMDU status transitions per Section 2.

## Systems of record and forms

### Systems

- Marine Corps Total Force System (MCTFS). Holds LIMDU duty status with the extension reflected.
- UD-MIPS. Cuts the updated transaction per Appendix E.
- Disability Evaluation System (IDES) Workflow. Per Chapter 8 Section 2.
- MTF Records. Hold the medical extension recommendation.
- Unit LIMDU Roster. Reflects the extended period.

### Forms

- Medical Extension Recommendation. Per Chapter 8 paragraph 8111.
- NAVMC 321M Medical Extension. Per paragraph 8111.1.a.
- Commanding Officer or CMC (MMSR-4) Extension Approval. Per paragraph 8111.1.a.
- NAVMC 118 (11) Administrative Remarks. Page 11 documenting the extension per MCO P1070.12K paragraph 4006.
- Extended Re-Evaluation Appointment Memorandum. Per paragraph 8110.

## Common pitfalls

- Extension routed to the wrong approver. Per Chapter 8 paragraph 8111.1.a(1), the commanding officer approves first extensions and CMC (MMSR-4) approves later ones.
- Extension length set wrong. Per Chapter 8 paragraph 8111.1.a(3), the 2 month limit applies to short-term assessment or treatment only. TLD extensions run up to 6 months and DES cases up to 12.
- TLD exceeds 12 months cumulative without a MEB to the PEB. Per paragraphs 8003.24 and 8102, enlisted TLD runs up to 12 months at the MTF. Past 12 months, TLD needs CMC (MMSR-4) approval through the AMEB(R), or the Marine goes to the PEB under Section 2 when return to duty is unlikely.
- MCTFS transaction not updated. Per Appendix E and MCTFSPRIUM, the IPAC cuts the updated duty status transaction.
- Page 11 omitted on extension. Per MCO P1070.12K paragraph 4006, document the extension.
- Extended re-evaluation appointment missed. Per paragraph 8110, the unit and the IPAC track the extended appointment.
- DES referral skipped on continued PEB-warranting condition. Per Chapter 8 Section 2 paragraphs 8201-8208, PEB-warranting findings refer to DES.

## Decision points

- Extension versus DES referral. Per Chapter 8 paragraph 8111.1.a(3), extension length follows the Marine's medical status. Per Section 2, DES referral covers cases warranting PEB review.
- Total LIMDU threshold. Per paragraphs 8003.24 and 8102, enlisted TLD runs 12 months cumulative at the MTF. Per paragraph 8111, the extension extends the contract, not the duty period.
- Fit-for-full-duty return versus continued extension. The MTF medical officer recommends. The commanding officer approves the first extension and CMC (MMSR-4) approves later ones.
