## Reserve vs Active Promotion Policy

Reserve promotions to Corporal and Sergeant run on the same JEPES framework as the Active Component, on a quarterly cycle. MCO P1400.32D<Citation source="MCO P1400.32D" /> W/CH 2 governs enlisted promotions. MCO 1616.1<Citation source="MCO 1616.1" /> sets JEPES. MARADMIN 503/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 503/26" /> sets FY27 Reserve Component guidance.

- Promotion authority. Per MARADMIN 503/26 paragraphs 1 and 2, the Reserve Corporal and Sergeant promotion authority MARADMIN ends. MCO P1400.32D W/CH 2 is the promotion authority for all USMCR LCpls and Cpls, AR, SMCR, and IRR, effective with 1 October 2026 promotions. Warrants cite the order.
- Score. The JEPES promotion score, not the legacy composite score. JEPES replaced composite scores for promotion to Cpl and Sgt.
- Cycle. Quarterly, effective the first day of October, January, April, and July. The Active Component promotes monthly under MARADMIN 502/26.
- IRR. IRR Marines promote on the SMCR promotion parameters and cutting scores.
- SNCO and officer promotions run through reserve selection boards.

## FY27 Effective and Record Cutoff Dates

Per MARADMIN 503/26 paragraph 5.b, date of rank equals the effective date. The JEPES score for consideration is set as of the record cutoff date.

| Promotion effective date | Record cutoff date |
| --- | --- |
| 1 October 2026 | 31 August 2026 |
| 1 January 2027 | 30 November 2026 |
| 1 April 2027 | 28 February 2027 |
| 1 July 2027 | 31 May 2027 |

Per paragraph 3, cutting scores post in MCTFS after each month's select grades post, and on the Enlisted Promotions website. Commands keep each cycle's cutting scores for future reference.

## Eligibility

- Time in grade and time in service per MCO P1400.32D Chapter 1 paragraph 1202. Per MARADMIN 222/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 222/26" />, the Sgt time in service minimum is 30 months for the Total Force, effective 1 October 2026.
- PME complete for the next grade. The MCTFS PME flag reads "Y".
- A recommended status in the MOL PromRec module.
- A JEPES promotion score at or above the cutting score for the IMOS, as of the record cutoff date.
- No restriction under MCO P1400.32D Chapter 1 paragraph 1204.

The JEPES Score Points Incentive under MARADMIN 695/21<Citation source="MARADMIN 695/21" /> adds points for Corporals who accept an SMCR drilling obligation. See the Reserve Incentive Programs page.

## Performance Steps for Quarterly Promotion Processing

### Step 1 - PERA. Verify JEPES Data Before the Record Cutoff

1. Pull the unit eligibility roster for the next quarter.
2. Confirm rifle, MCMAP, PFT, CFT, PME, education, and command input marks are posted in MCTFS by the record cutoff date.
3. Submit UD/MIPS corrections for data errors before the cutoff. Data posted after the cutoff counts toward a later quarter or the junior remedial process.

### Step 2 - PERA. Certify Promotion Recommendations

1. Certify recommended and not recommended entries in the MOL PromRec module before the certification due date, per PAA 03-21<Citation source="PAA 03-21" />.
2. For each Marine not recommended for promotion to Cpl or Sgt, complete the Page 11 counseling required by MARADMIN 150/16<Citation source="MARADMIN 150/16" />, and repeat it each quarter the Marine stays not recommended.

### Step 3 - PERA. Reconcile Select Grades and Cutting Scores

1. Compare select grades on the Diary Feedback Report against the quarter's cutting scores in MCTFS JEPA.
2. File the cutting scores with the quarter's promotion records, per MARADMIN 503/26 paragraph 3.

### Step 4 - PERA. Prepare Promotion Warrants

1. Cite MCO P1400.32D W/CH 2 as the promotion authority.
2. Enter the quarterly effective date as the date of rank.
3. Route to the commander for signature.

### Step 5 - PERA. Process Unit Diary Entries

1. Submit the UD/MIPS promotion transaction.
2. Verify the EDFR returns clean.
3. Confirm the new grade posts for the next drill pay processing.

### Step 6 - PERA. File and Distribute

1. Present the original warrant to the Marine.
2. Route a copy to the OMPF through the supporting PAC.
3. Keep a copy in the unit personnel file.

## Common Promotion Errors

1. JEPES data posted after the record cutoff. The Marine misses the quarter. Post before the cutoff.
2. Not recommended entry with no Page 11. MARADMIN 150/16 requires the quarterly counseling.
3. Promotion processed for an ineligible Marine. Time in grade, time in service, or PME shortfall missed.
4. Wrong effective date. Reserve promotions take effect on the quarterly date, not the warrant signature date.
5. Warrant cites a promotion MARADMIN. For FY27 promotions, cite MCO P1400.32D W/CH 2.
6. Unit diary entry missing or failed. The grade never updates in MCTFS.

## SNCO Promotion Process

SNCO promotions to Staff Sergeant and above run through reserve selection boards for the SMCR, IMA, and IRR, with monthly SNCO promotion messages for the AR, SMCR, and IRR.

- Eligibility rests on time in grade, time in service, and MOS qualification.
- The board reviews the Master Brief Sheet and the OMPF.
- I&I admin supports board preparation with record audits.

## Officer Promotion Process

Reserve officer promotions follow MCO 1400.31D<Citation source="MCO 1400.31D" />, Marine Corps Promotion Manual Volume 1, with reserve-specific provisions.

- Reserve selection boards convene for promotion to Captain through Colonel, unrestricted reserve and Active Reserve, with separate reserve warrant officer boards.
- Promotion to higher grades requires confirmation per statute.
- I&I admin processes officer promotion records and supports board preparation. I&I does not run the boards.

## Related Roles

- Marines keep JEPES data current and confirm their PME flag.
- Leaders verify record accuracy and recommend or do not recommend promotion.
- Commanders certify recommendations and sign warrants.
