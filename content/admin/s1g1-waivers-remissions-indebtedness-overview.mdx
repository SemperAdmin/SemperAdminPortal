## Role and responsibility

S-1 admin specialists own waiver and remission of indebtedness processing at the unit level. The S-1 clerk receives the debt notice and validates against source documents. The S-1 chief counsels the Marine on options and builds the package. The S-1 officer signs by direction on the package and routes through the IPAC pay clerk to DFAS for waivers and to the Service for remissions.

Source documents flow from MCTFS debt notices, LES debt entries, audit findings, and command direction. Waiver packages route under 10 USC 2774<Citation source="10 USC 2774" />. Remission packages route under 10 USC 7837<Citation source="10 USC 7837" />. Outputs include the DFAS adjudication letter, the MCTFS debt closeout, the certified case packet, and the OMPF Field Folder entry.

MCAAT inspects the case packet for legal authority correctness, validation against source documents, and DFAS routing. A waiver routed under the wrong authority gets denied without prejudice. Determine the right authority at package build, not after DFAS rejects.

## Trigger events

- MCTFS debt notice posts to the Marine's record.
- LES shows debt collection start.
- Audit identifies overpayment caused by clerk fault.
- Marine separates with unresolved pay debt.
- Court-martial action affects pay creating debt.
- Special pay lapse generates overpayment.
- Travel claim rejection generates debt.

## Processing workflow

1. Receive the debt notice. Pull from MCTFS, the LES, or audit finding. Cross-check EDIPI, debt amount, and creation date. The S-1 clerk runs receipt.
2. Validate the debt. Trace the debt to the source transaction and supporting KSD. Confirm the debt is valid and the amount is correct per DoDFMR Vol 16<Citation source="DoDFMR Vol 16" /> Chapter 4.
3. Counsel the Marine. Brief on waiver under 10 USC 2774<Citation source="10 USC 2774" /> for collection of erroneous payment, or remission under 10 USC 7837<Citation source="10 USC 7837" /> for hardship grounds.
4. Determine the legal authority. Waiver applies when collection would be against equity and good conscience. Remission applies when hardship justifies forgiveness for an enlisted Marine still on active duty per 10 USC 7837<Citation source="10 USC 7837" />.
5. Build the package. Stage the source documents, the legal authority memorandum, the Marine's statement, and any hardship support. Index per DoDFMR Vol 16<Citation source="DoDFMR Vol 16" /> Chapter 4.
6. Route through the chain. A commander or supervisor endorsement is optional for Marine applications per DD Form 2789 item 20. Route the package whether or not an endorsement is attached.
7. Submit the package. Route the certified waiver package to DFAS through the IPAC pay clerk and track the DFAS case number. Forward remission requests on the Service-designated form to the Service. Per DoDFMR Vol 16<Citation source="DoDFMR Vol 16" /> Chapter 4 paragraph 9.2, DFAS does not process remission or cancellation requests for any Service.
8. Track the DFAS adjudication. Pull case status from DFAS on a fixed cycle. Notify the Marine and the chain on resolution.
9. Process the approved case. On waiver or remission approval, Disbursing and Finance reports the liquidation TTC/SEQ 559/000 per PAA 02-24<Citation source="PAA 02-24" /> paragraph 4. The S-1 does not cut it. Confirm collection stops.
10. Process the denied case. On denial, route the case to collection per DoDFMR Vol 16<Citation source="DoDFMR Vol 16" /> Chapter 4. Counsel the Marine on appeal options.
11. File the certified case packet. Place in the OMPF Field Folder under the pay tab.

## Systems of record and forms

### Systems

- MCTFS. Holds the debt state and processes debt closeout transactions.
- DFAS Indianapolis. Adjudicates waiver packages. DFAS does not process remission requests.
- Disbursing and Finance. Reports the liquidation TTC/SEQ 559/000 on approval per PAA 02-24.
- OMPF Field Folder. Holds the certified case packet.

### Forms

- DD Form 2789 Waiver or Remission of Indebtedness Application. Standard application form for waivers under 10 USC 2774.
- Marine Statement of Hardship. Supporting statement for remission packages.
- Command Endorsement Memorandum. Optional for Marine applications per DD Form 2789 item 20.

## Common pitfalls

- Wrong legal authority on the package. Waiver routed under 10 USC 7837 or remission under 10 USC 2774. The request is denied. Determine authority at build per DoDFMR Vol 16<Citation source="DoDFMR Vol 16" /> Chapter 4.
- Marine separated before the package adjudicates. The Marine loses remission eligibility under 10 USC 7837<Citation source="10 USC 7837" />. Submit before separation.
- Package held for a command endorsement. A commander endorsement is optional for Marine waiver and remission applications per DD Form 2789 item 20. Do not hold or return the package for a missing endorsement.
- Liquidation not reported on approval. The debt continues to collect. Confirm Disbursing and Finance reports TTC/SEQ 559/000 after DFAS approval.

## Decision points

- Waiver versus remission. Waiver applies on government error and equity grounds (10 USC 2774). Remission applies on hardship grounds for enlisted Marines on active duty (10 USC 7837). The S-1 chief routes per the right authority.
- Submission before or after separation. Remission requires active duty enlisted status. Submit before separation per 10 USC 7837<Citation source="10 USC 7837" />.
- Appeal of denial. The Marine appeals a DFAS denial. The S-1 officer routes the appeal through DFAS per DoDFMR Vol 16<Citation source="DoDFMR Vol 16" /> Chapter 4.
