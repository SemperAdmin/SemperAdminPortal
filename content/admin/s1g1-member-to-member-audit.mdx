## Why the Member Married-to-Member Audit Exists

Per MCTFSPRIUM 12-120103<Citation source="MCTFSPRIUM 12-120103" /> paragraph 2, the member married-to-member (M2M) audit is a review and comparison of the entitlements of two service members who are married to each other. It prevents unauthorized or duplicate payments of pay and allowances to both members.

The audit covers the member's and the service spouse's entitlements and allowances. Focus on the items where erroneous payments occur most often.

- Basic Allowance for Housing (BAH)
- Family Separation Allowance (FSA)
- Overseas Housing Allowance (OHA)
- Government quarters
- Cost of Living Allowance (COLA)
- Dual travel entitlements or allowances for the same class of dependents or the active duty service spouse

## Audit Occasions and Cadence

The reporting unit or servicing Personnel Administration Center owns the audit. Complete the audit on these occasions.

| Occasion | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Member joins a new unit | Complete the M2M data sheet (PRIUM Table 12-3). Audit the period from the last M2M audit through the join date. Finish NLT 60 days from the join date |
| Member gains a service spouse or spouse status changes | Complete the M2M data sheet. A Major Service Component Code change counts as a status change |
| Annual | Repeat the audit at least every 12 months from the previous M2M audit |
| Divorce of two married members | Conduct the audit |

An E-6 or above verifies the data sheet when the audit runs by telephone.

## Audit Tools

- M2M data sheet, PRIUM Table 12-3
- OMPF audit of both members
- TRS Remarks Summary screens, Leave and Earnings Statement (LES), and COGNOS Analytics showing entitlements and allowances for both members
- DFAS MilPay Repository when the spouse's pay records are not in MCTFS
- Spouse LES for the audit period when the spouse serves in another Service and the repository is not available

## Performance Steps

### Step 1 - Identify the Married-to-Member Couple

Pull the dependency record for each member.

- Confirm the spouse is a service member
- Confirm which reporting unit holds each member
- Note the Service and component of the spouse

### Step 2 - Complete the M2M Data Sheet

Complete PRIUM Table 12-3 for each member.

- Record the date of marriage and the date the joint household began
- Collect the marriage certificate and birth certificates for children, whether or not claimed for BAH or travel
- Scan and upload the data sheet and marriage certificate into each member's OMPF when the unit holds both members
- Keep an accurate data sheet in the OMPF even when the member has other dependents

### Step 3 - Review and Compare Entitlements

Compare the member's entitlements with the spouse's for the audit period.

- BAH, FSA, OHA, COLA, and Government quarters status
- Dual travel entitlements for the same dependents
- Each LES or MilPay Repository record for the period

### Step 4 - Handle a Spouse in Another Unit

Route discrepancies for the member outside your reporting unit.

- Submit a Miscellaneous Action Notice (MAN) in the Document Tracking Management System (DTMS) to the appropriate unit
- Keep a copy of the MAN with the audit packet

### Step 5 - Report Corrections

Process each finding.

- Submit UD/MIPS transactions for every required change
- Local practice, not an MCTFSPRIUM 120103 requirement: coordinate with the PAC on recoupment for any duplicate payment
- Local practice, not an MCTFSPRIUM 120103 requirement: verify the corrections on the next EDFR

### Step 6 - Report Completion

Report Training Event Code Z1 (ADMIN ONLY), member-to-member audit complete.

## Common Errors

1. **Audit missed at the 60-day mark.** The unit joins the couple and does not complete the audit NLT 60 days from the join date.
2. **Annual audit lapses.** More than 12 months pass since the last M2M audit.
3. **Data sheet not in the OMPF.** Both members need an accurate Table 12-3 on file.
4. **Spouse in another Service not compared.** The unit audits one LES and skips the spouse's pay record.
5. **Divorce without an audit.** The unit does not audit when the married members divorce.
6. **Z1 not reported.** The audit finishes and the completion event never posts.

## Documentation Standards

Keep all documentation used for M2M audits and annual reviews of pay and entitlements for the current year plus 2 years. Electronic format is acceptable.

## Related Roles

- **Marines** married to another service member provide spouse LES copies when the repository is not available and report status changes to the reporting unit.
- **Leaders** at S-1 track the 60-day and 12-month deadlines for each couple.
- **Commanders** hold the reporting unit accountable for the audit and, as local practice, for recoupment of duplicate payments.
