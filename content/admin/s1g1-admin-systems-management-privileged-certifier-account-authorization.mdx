## Role and responsibility

The S-1 officer (0102) signs every privileged certifier authorization. The S-1 chief (0111 SSgt or higher) supervises certifier training and audits the certifier list monthly. The unit security manager validates the certifier's privileged access agreement against the cybersecurity baseline. The IPAC certifier coordinator runs parallel authorization for installation-tied certifier roles.

Source documents flow from grade and date-of-rank verification, certifier-specific training certificates, signed privileged access agreements, and the unit certifier roster. The MCTFS certifier role request routes through DoD E-ICAM effective 21 September 2026 per PAA 02-26<Citation source="PAA 02-26" />. Upload the privileged user agreement, appointment letter, and authorization letter with the request, since E-ICAM blocks attachments at every later stage. UD-MIPS Certifier roles route through Naval Identity Services (NIS) Information Owner approver groups. Outputs include the activation confirmation, the certifier roster entry, the Page 11 documenting authorization, and the OMPF Field Folder systems tab entry.

MCAAT inspects the certifier roster against the privileged access agreement file, the monthly audit signature, and the diary certification log. A certifier signing diary lines without a current privileged access agreement triggers a finding under MCO 5239.2B<Citation source="MCO 5239.2B" />. Authorize before activate, audit monthly.

## Trigger events

- 0111 reaches SSgt with sufficient date of rank for certifier role.
- Billet change moves a Marine into a certifier-required position.
- Outgoing certifier detaches and the unit needs a replacement.
- Annual certifier reauthorization window opens.
- Misconduct event requires certifier suspension.
- New certifier-specific training release requires recertification.
- Audit identifies an unauthorized certifier on the roster.

## Processing workflow

<Callout variant="warning" title="Certifier roles move to E-ICAM, privileged mainframe accounts do not">
PAA 02-26<Citation source="PAA 02-26" /> paragraph 6 draws a hard line. The E-ICAM end-user workflow carries MCTFS certifier role requests from 21 September 2026. It does not replace the appointment or establishment process for privileged TASO, Limited Security Control Account, Zone Control Accessor ID, Division Control Accessor ID, or Department Control Accessor ID accounts. Those stay under Marine Corps Cyberspace Operations Group procedures until superseding guidance publishes. Commanders and directors keep responsibility for appointing TASOs and validating the organizational requirement for privileged mainframe security access.
</Callout>

<Callout variant="info" title="Two certifier paths">
The MCTFS certifier role routes through DoD E-ICAM, where the supervisor, security manager, Information Owner, and Information System Security Manager approve in sequence before provisioning. Roles requiring a DD Form 577 add a 577 approver step. UD-MIPS Certifier roles route through Naval Identity Services (NIS) Information Owner approver groups. For UD-MIPS the approvers are the Certifier Commander (COM) and the Certifier Account Provisioner (IPAC Director or IPAC Deputy). For DTMS the approvers are the CO, the Disbursing Officer, or the IPAC Director.
</Callout>

1. Validate grade and date of rank. Confirm the candidate holds SSgt or higher with sufficient DoR per the local certifier policy and MCO 5239.2B<Citation source="MCO 5239.2B" /> Chapter 4. The S-1 chief validates.
2. Confirm certifier-specific training. Verify completion of MCTFS certifier training, UD-MIPS certifier training, and current annual cyber awareness training per DOD 8500.01E<Citation source="DOD 8500.01E" />.
3. Sign the privileged access agreement. The candidate signs the privileged access agreement acknowledging certifier responsibilities. The unit security manager countersigns per MCO 5239.2B<Citation source="MCO 5239.2B" />. IT Level I privileged users also draw an NCPASS token for multifactor authentication, held with the agreement by the ISSM or ISSO per MCTFS FoS Access Policy and Procedures 8.0<Citation source="MCTFS FoS Access Policy and Procedures 8.0" />.
4. Submit the MCTFS certifier role request. Route the request through DoD E-ICAM with the privileged user agreement, appointment letter, and authorization letter attached as PDFs. Route UD-MIPS Certifier role requests through NIS to the Information Owner approver group.
5. Activate the certifier account. The E-ICAM approval chain validates and releases the MCTFS certifier role. NIS approves the UD-MIPS Certifier role. Confirm activation per system.
6. Test certifier function. Submit a sample diary line and certify it in a training environment when available. Validate the certified line posts correctly per MCTFSPRIUM<Citation source="MCTFSPRIUM" />.
7. Document authorization. Cut a Page 11 entry recording certifier authorization, training completion, and privileged access agreement date per MCO P1070.12K<Citation source="MCO P1070.12K" /> Chapter 6.
8. Audit the certifier list monthly. Pull the diary certification log and reconcile against the certifier roster. The S-1 chief signs the audit per MCO 5239.2B<Citation source="MCO 5239.2B" />.
9. Reauthorize on the E-ICAM clock. Privileged roles recertify every 90 days against the supervisor, and the privileged user agreement, appointment letter, and authorization letter run an annual clock. The supervisor holds 10 days to act on a campaign before it escalates. An unanswered campaign revokes the role. Run certifier-specific training renewal at the annual cycle.
10. Suspend on misconduct. The S-1 officer suspends certifier access on any pending investigation affecting record integrity per MCO P1070.12K<Citation source="MCO P1070.12K" />.

## NIS approver groups and proxy

NIS ties Information Owner approver group membership to specific platform permissions.

- UD-MIPS approvers. Certifier Commander (COM) and Certifier Account Provisioner (IPAC Director or IPAC Deputy).
- DTMS approvers. Commanding Officer, Disbursing Officer, or IPAC Director.
- Proxy and delegation. The Certifier Account Provisioner role in UD-MIPS holds the delegation and proxy function for the platform.
- MISSO Manager access. Requests to add MISSO Managers route through NIS to MISSA based on application and jurisdiction. After implementation these groups do not tie to application permissions, so a new MISSO COM requests MISSO Manager access separately.

## Systems of record and forms

### Systems

- MCTFS. Holds the certifier role and certified diary lines. Certifier access submits through DoD E-ICAM effective 21 September 2026.
- UD-MIPS. Cuts and certifies diary lines requiring certifier signature. Certifier roles route through NIS.
- DoD E-ICAM. Carries the MCTFS certifier role request through the supervisor, security manager, Information Owner, and Information System Security Manager chain.
- Diary certification log. Local log of certified lines per certifier.

### Forms

- Privileged User Agreement, Appointment Letter, and Authorization Letter. Uploaded as PDFs with the E-ICAM request. Each runs an annual recertification clock.
- Privileged Access Agreement. Required for every certifier.
- NCPASS Token. Required for IT Level I privileged users, held with the agreement by the ISSM or ISSO.
- MCTFS Certifier Training Certificate. Required for activation.
- UD-MIPS Certifier Training Certificate. Required for activation.
- Cyber Awareness Training Certificate. Required annually.
- Unit Certifier Roster. Local register of authorized certifiers.
- DD Form 577 Appointment and Termination Record. Current 577 policy stays in effect. NIS holds no document uploads, so units maintain certifier and disbursing 577s externally for DTMS. The self-contained 577 process for UD-MIPS stays unchanged.
- NAVMC 118 (11) Administrative Remarks. Page 11 documenting certifier authorization.

## Common pitfalls

- Certifier signing diary lines without a current privileged access agreement. MCAAT writes the finding. Sign the agreement before activation per MCO 5239.2B<Citation source="MCO 5239.2B" />.
- Certifier-specific training expired. The certifier signs without current credentials. Run renewal at the annual cycle.
- Certifier suspended on misconduct, but role not removed in MCTFS. The certifier retains technical access. Submit the removal to MISSA at suspension per MCO 5239.2B<Citation source="MCO 5239.2B" />.
- Monthly certifier audit skipped. Stale certifier authorizations sit on the roster. Pull the diary log and reconcile each month.
- Privileged access agreement filed without the security manager signature. The agreement does not stand on review. Capture both signatures at signing per MCO 5239.2B<Citation source="MCO 5239.2B" />.

## Decision points

- Grade and DoR threshold exception. The unit lacks a SSgt with sufficient DoR. The S-1 officer routes a written exception through the commander before MISSA validates per MCO 5239.2B<Citation source="MCO 5239.2B" />.
- Certifier suspension scope. The certifier is under preliminary inquiry, command-directed inquiry, or judicial action. The S-1 officer suspends at the level matching the inquiry severity per MCO P1070.12K<Citation source="MCO P1070.12K" />.
- Reactivation after suspension. The investigation closes without adverse finding. The S-1 officer reactivates with a fresh privileged access agreement and refreshed training.

## Authority

PAA 02-26<Citation source="PAA 02-26" /> moves the MCTFS certifier role request to DoD E-ICAM from 21 September 2026 and, at paragraph 6, leaves privileged TASO and mainframe security control accounts under Marine Corps Cyberspace Operations Group procedures. This sub-page sits under MCTFS FoS Access Policy and Procedures 8.0<Citation source="MCTFS FoS Access Policy and Procedures 8.0" />, which sets privileged access controls and the NCPASS token requirement. MCO 5239.2B<Citation source="MCO 5239.2B" /> Marine Corps Cybersecurity Program sets the privileged access agreement requirement for certifier roles. MCO 5000.14D<Citation source="MCO 5000.14D" /> MCAP enclosure 6 establishes support-office roles. DOD 8500.01E<Citation source="DOD 8500.01E" /> Cybersecurity sets DoD-level privileged access controls. NAVMC 3500.3E<Citation source="NAVMC 3500.3E" /> T-R event 0102-GENA-1015 covers administrator role assignment and removal. MCTFSPRIUM<Citation source="MCTFSPRIUM" /> defines certifier function within UD-MIPS diary submission.
