<Callout variant="danger" title="The pre-2023 SLA model is gone">
Per MARADMIN 545/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 545/26" /> paragraph 6.a, the first Marine general officer approves SLA, with no further delegation and no by direction signature. The cap is 90 days total per paragraph 6.c, not 120. The four MCO 1050.3J Chapter 2 paragraph 9.a categories no longer govern. Rebuild any desk guide or turnover binder written against them.
</Callout>

## Role and responsibility

PERA function. The S-1 chief reviews packages and validates the qualifying category. The S-1 clerk builds the package and tracks it through the chain. The unit commanding officer endorses the request and restores approved days in MOL. The first Marine general officer in the administrative chain approves or disapproves. No one at the S-1 signs for the CO or the GO on an SLA action.

Per MARADMIN 545/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 545/26" /> paragraph 4.a, the message rescinds the MARADMIN 341/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 341/26" /> suspension and replaces the processes and procedures in MARADMIN 188/25<Citation source="MARADMIN 188/25" />. ALNAV 056/26<Citation source="ALNAV 056/26" /> carries the SECNAV designations and the delegation to CMC, further delegated to Dir, MP, M&RA, per paragraph 2.b.

Inputs are deployment orders, web orders, deployment manifests, operational travel claims, and the Marine's leave history. Outputs are the approved package, the MOL restoration with its comment, and the retained record.

## Trigger events

- Release of MARADMIN 545/26 on 3 November 2026. Paragraph 7.a directs review of every pending, held, and returned package.
- A Marine lost leave at fiscal year end. Paragraph 6.c.2 restores FY26 days after approval.
- The MPO designated units list adds or changes a ship or unit. Paragraph 5.c.
- A Marine returns from a deployment or attachment in a qualifying category.
- The future FY designated units list publishes on the MPO website 60 days before FY closeout. Paragraph 8.b.

## Qualifying categories

Per MARADMIN 545/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 545/26" /> paragraph 4.b, FY26 SLA runs under three categories.

| Paragraph | Category | Threshold | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4.b.1 | Operation EPIC FURY, per DOWI 1327.06<Citation source="DOWI 1327.06" /> paragraph 3.8.d | Per paragraph 3.8.d. FY26 only per paragraph 4.d | Deployment orders |
| 4.b.2 | Designated deployable ship or mobile unit | More than 60 consecutive days away from homeport or PDS, unit on the MPO list | Web orders, deployment manifest, or operational travel claim |
| 4.b.3 | HFP or IDP area | At least 120 consecutive days | Orders and pay area dates |

Paragraph 3.b counts task-organized and provisional formations, including MEUs, ARG Marine elements, SPMAGTFs, Crisis Response Forces, JTF enablers, and provisional battalions, squadrons, task forces, or composite detachments formed under an approved execute or deployment order. Paragraph 3.c counts Marines joined permanently or attached on TAD under formal deployment orders during the designated window. Eligibility runs for the verified attachment only.

Other qualifying duty is closed. Per paragraph 4.c.3, return those requests without action before submission to HQMC.

## Processing workflow

1. PERA. Pull every pending, held, and returned SLA package. Per MARADMIN 545/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 545/26" /> paragraph 7.a, packages returned under MARADMIN 341/26 get the same review.
2. PERA. Classify each request against paragraph 4.b.1, 4.b.2, or 4.b.3. The S-1 chief validates the category.
3. PERA. For paragraph 4.b.2, check the ship or unit against the FY26 designated units list on the [MPO page](https://www.manpower.marines.mil/Divisions/Plans-and-Policies/Manpower-Plans-and-Policy/Manpower-Military-Policy-MPO/). Confirm more than 60 consecutive days away from homeport or PDS.
4. PERA. Return other qualifying duty requests without action per paragraph 4.c.3. Note the return in the package log.
5. PERA. Build the package per paragraph 7.c. NAVMC 10274<Citation source="NAVMC 10274" /> requesting SLA, verification of assignment or TAD attachment during the qualifying period, and the CO's written endorsement.
6. PERA. Route through the administrative chain to the first Marine GO per paragraph 7.b. No intermediate approval exists.
7. PERA. Receive the GO decision at the unit S-1 per paragraph 7.d.
8. PERA. Stage the approved days for the CO. The CO restores qualifying days in MOL and comments with the approving GO and the approval date per paragraph 7.d.1.
9. PERA. Retain the package at the restoring unit S-1 per paragraph 7.d.2 and MCO 5210.11F<Citation source="MCO 5210.11F" />.
10. PERA. Track the Marine's SLA expiration and balance. SLA expires at the end of the second FY for which it is approved per paragraph 6.c.1. No new SLA while the balance sits at 30 days or more per MARADMIN 188/25<Citation source="MARADMIN 188/25" /> paragraph 7.b.

## Worked example

Per MARADMIN 545/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 545/26" /> paragraph 6.c.2.

1. 1 October 2026. The Marine loses 27 days. Balance 60.
2. After release, the Marine submits. The GO approves.
3. 30 October 2026. The CO restores 27 days in MOL. Balance 87.
4. FY26 SLA restored in FY27 stays available until 1 October 2028.

## Systems of record and forms

### Systems

- MOL. Restoration entry and comment by the CO. The Marine's view under Leave/Liberty, Leave Overview, SLA History per paragraph 8.d.
- LES. SLA balance and forecast lost leave per paragraphs 8.d and 8.e.
- MCTFS. Leave balance of record behind MOL and the LES.

### Forms

- NAVMC 10274 Administrative Action Form. The SLA request per paragraph 7.c.1.
- CO written endorsement per paragraph 7.c.3.
- Web orders, deployment manifest, or operational travel claim per paragraph 7.c.2.

## Common pitfalls

- SLA signed by direction. Per MARADMIN 545/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 545/26" /> paragraph 6.a, the first Marine GO holds the authority and delegates none of it. A by direction approval is invalid.
- Restoration with no comment. Per paragraph 7.d.1, the MOL entry names the approving GO and the approval date. An unannotated restoration fails inspection.
- Package sent to the OMPF instead of retained. Paragraph 7.d.2 keeps it at the restoring unit S-1.
- Paragraph 4.b.2 request for a unit off the list. Check the MPO list first. Request the addition through paragraph 8.a, do not forward the package.
- Other qualifying duty forwarded to HQMC. Per paragraph 4.c.3, return it before submission.
- Returned packages left in the drawer. Paragraph 7.a requires review of every held and returned package.
- Leave lost to poor leave management submitted as SLA. Per paragraph 6.b, the GO approves only leave lost to national security operations, natural disaster, or catastrophe.
- Charging SLA before current FY leave. Per paragraph 8.c and MCO 1050.3J<Citation source="MCO 1050.3J" />, leave charges last in, first out.

## Decision points

- Category overlap. A Marine fits paragraph 4.b.2 and 4.b.3. The S-1 chief selects the category with the cleanest proof. One approval covers the restoration.
- Unit off the list. The command submits formal justification, endorsed by a GO, to Dir, MP through the MPO SharePoint site per paragraph 8.a. CAC required. Hold the package until the list updates.
- GO authority over 4.b.1 and 4.b.3. Paragraph 6.b frames GO approval around designated ships and mobile units. When the chain questions GO authority over an EPIC FURY or HFP or IDP request, send the policy question to MPO per paragraph 9.
