## Role and responsibility

The CBA Manager owns the unit Centrally Billed Account program per DoD GTCC Regulations Section 040205 (CBA Managers) and MCO 4600.40C<Citation source="MCO 4600.40C" /> Enclosure 2 Appendix C. The CBA Specialist (CBA-S) reconciles the monthly CBA invoice against authorized expenses per DoD DTS Regulations section 030402 (Centrally Billed Account Specialist). A CBA-S who certifies CBA invoices for payment holds Certifying Officer appointment per DoD FMR Vol 5 Chapter 5 and 31 U.S.C. 3325. The APC at the unit coordinates CBA application, account activity, and delinquency action. The Authorizing Official approves the underlying DTS authorization charging the CBA.

Source documents flow from the CBA application package per Section 040903, the Annex 11 CBA appointment letter, the monthly CBA invoice, the authorized DTS authorizations, the Annex 3 CBA delinquency timeline, the CBA mandatory reports per Section 041402.01, the Exception Status request per Section 041308, the Reinstatement request per Section 041309, and the Report X CBA Aging Analysis Report per Enclosure 2 Appendix C paragraph 21. CBA outputs include the active CBA account, the reconciled monthly invoice, the certified payment, the cleared delinquency, the Exception Status approval, the reinstatement record, and the audit trail for IGMC inspection.

Per MCO 4600.40C<Citation source="MCO 4600.40C" /> Enclosure 2 Chapter 3 paragraph 1, a CBA pays for official travel of travelers who do not have an IBA. The two CBA types are DMO transportation accounts and unit travel cards. Per Section 040205 (CBA Managers), the CBA Manager owns the unit CBA scope.

## Trigger events

- New CBA account required for centrally arranged transportation or lodging.
- Monthly CBA invoice arrives.
- CBA delinquency notification arrives per Annex 3.
- Exception Status request from the unit on a delinquent CBA.
- Cancelled CBA requires reinstatement per Section 041309.
- Billing cycle closes and the CBA mandatory reports come due per Section 041402.01.
- Every billing cycle, the three CBA reports including Report X CBA Aging Analysis Report are requested and reconciled per Enclosure 2 Appendix C paragraph 21.
- IGMC GTCCP FA inspection notice arrives.

## Processing workflow

1. Appoint the CBA Manager and CBA Specialist. Per Annex 11 (Required Template for CBA Appointment Letter), the unit commander signs the CBA appointment letter. The CBA Manager holds program-owner scope. The CBA-S holds reconciliation scope. A CBA-S certifying invoices for payment holds Certifying Officer appointment per DoD DTS Regulations section 030402.
2. Request the CBA. Per Section 040903 (CBA Applications), send the CBA request to the CPM. Once the CPM grants approval, the command completes the hard copy application form and the CPM submits it. The application carries the unit's centrally arranged travel scope.
3. Process monthly CBA invoices. Per MCO 4600.40C<Citation source="MCO 4600.40C" /> Enclosure 2 Appendix C and DoD DTS Regulations section 030402, the CBA-S reconciles the monthly CBA invoice against authorized expenses charged to CBAs.
4. Reconcile authorized expenses. The CBA-S maps each CBA charge to a DTS authorization. Unmatched charges route to investigation.
5. Pull CBA mandatory reports. Per Section 041402.01 and Section 0414 (Travel Card Management Reports), APCs and CPMs run the mandatory CBA reports after the completion of all accounts billing cycles. Per MCO 4600.40C Enclosure 2 Appendix C paragraph 21, unit-level APCs request and reconcile the three minimum reports after each billing cycle.
6. Support exception status requests. Per Section 041308 (Exception Status for CBAs), the CPM requests Exception Status for a delinquent CBA no earlier than 55 days past billing. The vendor permits three per rolling 12 months. The unit supplies the justification.
7. Process CBA reinstatement. Per Section 041309 (Reinstatement of Cancelled CBAs), the unit requests reinstatement of a cancelled CBA through the CPM.
8. Retain CBA reconciliation records. Per MCO 5210.11F<Citation source="MCO 5210.11F" />, retain the monthly reconciliation, the certified invoice, and the supporting authorizations.
9. Submit Report X. Per Enclosure 2 Appendix C paragraph 23.b(3), submit the CBA Aging Analysis Report on the published cycle.
10. Coordinate with the AO and Disbursing. The AO approves the DTS authorization charging the CBA. The Disbursing or Finance office processes the certified CBA invoice for payment.

## Systems of record and forms

### Systems

- GTCC vendor Electronic Access System (EAS). Holds the CBA account.
- DTS Authorization module. Holds the underlying authorizations charging the CBA.
- CBA Reconciliation Worksheet. Local format driven by the CBA-S.
- Defense Finance and Accounting Service (DFAS). Receives the certified invoice for payment.

- CBA Application Package. Submitted per Section 040903.
- CBA Appointment Letter. Required template per Annex 11.
- CBA Invoice. Issued by the GTCC vendor.
- CBA Exception Status Request. Per Section 041308.
- CBA Reinstatement Request. Per Section 041309.
- Report X CBA Aging Analysis Report. Per Enclosure 2 Appendix C paragraph 23.b(3).

## Common pitfalls

- CBA-S certifying invoices without Certifying Officer appointment. Per DoD DTS Regulations section 030402 and DoD FMR Vol 5 Chapter 5, the certifying CBA-S holds DD Form 577 Certifying Officer appointment. Without the appointment, the certification fails.
- Monthly invoice reconciled outside the cycle. Per Enclosure 2 Appendix C, the reconciliation runs monthly. A missed cycle breaks the audit trail.
- Unmatched CBA charges left unresolved. Each charge maps to a DTS authorization. Unmatched charges route to investigation.
- Exception Status requested too early or too often. Per Section 041308, the CPM requests it no earlier than 55 days past billing, and the vendor permits three per rolling 12 months.
- Reinstatement requested without scope justification. Per Section 041309, the request carries the operational justification.
- Annex 11 CBA appointment letter format missed. Per Annex 11, the template is required.
- Report X cadence missed. Per Enclosure 2 Appendix C paragraph 23.b(3), the report runs on the published cycle.

## Decision points

- CBA versus IBA payment. Per MCO 4600.40C Enclosure 2 Chapter 2 paragraph 3e, IBA holders charge airfare and other official travel costs to the IBA. CBAs serve travelers without an IBA and group travel. The unit travel pattern drives the call.
- Exception Status timing. Per Section 041308, the CPM requests Exception Status no earlier than day 55 past billing.
- Reinstatement scope. Per Section 041309, reinstatement covers a cancelled CBA. The request carries the operational scope.
- CBA-S appointment as Certifying Officer. Per DoD DTS Regulations section 030402, the CBA-S certifying invoices for payment holds Certifying Officer appointment. A non-certifying CBA-S routes invoices to a separately appointed Certifying Officer.
