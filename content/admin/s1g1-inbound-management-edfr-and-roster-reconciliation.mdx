## Role and responsibility

The IPAC inbound counselor views the DFR on the date the cycle becomes available after the inbound MCTFS join per MCTFSPRIUM 120303<Citation source="MCTFSPRIUM 12-120303" />. The S-1 chief audits the EDFR against the unit alpha roster and identifies discrepancies. The S-1 officer signs by direction on rejection-clearing actions and briefs aged rejections to the commander. The IPAC counselor cuts correcting KSDs per MCTFSPRIUM<Citation source="MCTFSPRIUM" /> codes to resolve EDFR rejections.

Source documents flow from the EDFR system, the unit alpha roster, the inbound MCTFS join transaction, the dependency documents, and the prior LES. EDFR and roster outputs include the certified DFR within 10 calendar days of the cycle, the updated alpha roster, the corrected MCTFS data, the retained EDFR copies per records schedule, the aged rejection brief, and the audit trail.

Per MCO 1300.8 and MCTFSPRIUM, EDFR is the primary reconciliation report between MCTFS data and the unit roster. Rejections surface on the cycle feedback report after a diary transaction and route through the IPAC for correction.

## Trigger events

- Inbound MCTFS join completes triggering EDFR pull.
- Routine weekly EDFR cycle.
- Diary correction cuts a new EDFR entry.
- New unit standup triggers initial alpha roster reconciliation.
- Dependency change triggers BAH reconciliation.
- IGMC inspection covers EDFR posture.

## Processing workflow

1. View the DFR. Per MCTFSPRIUM 120303<Citation source="MCTFSPRIUM 12-120303" /> paragraph 1, view the DFR on the date the cycle becomes available after the MCTFS join.
2. Review EDFR. Identify diary rejections, data errors, dependency mismatches, BAH errors, and COLA errors.
3. Compare to alpha roster. The unit alpha roster reflects assigned Marines. The EDFR reflects MCTFS data. Discrepancies surface where the two diverge.
4. Identify discrepancies. Common discrepancies include BAH rate mismatch, dependency status, COLA initiation, EAS errors, and SGLI election.
5. Submit correcting KSD. Per MCTFSPRIUM<Citation source="MCTFSPRIUM" />, the correcting KSD attaches to the corrected diary transaction.
6. Re-cut the diary transaction. The corrected transaction submits through UD-MIPS.
7. Certify and correct. Per MCTFSPRIUM 120303 paragraph 1, certify each DFR within 10 calendar days. Correct each reject as a new diary entry inside the 5 business day timeliness goal per MCTFSPRIUM 20103.
8. Update the alpha roster. The unit roster reflects the corrected MCTFS data.
9. Brief aged rejections. Rejections approaching or exceeding the 5 business day timeliness goal route to the S-1 officer and the commander.
10. Retain EDFR copies. Per the records retention schedule, retain EDFR copies for the documented period.

## Systems of record and forms

### Systems

- EDFR. Source of rejection and reconciliation data.
- Marine Corps Total Force System (MCTFS). Master record.
- UD-MIPS. Cuts correcting transactions.
- Unit Alpha Roster. Local record of assigned Marines.
- MOL. Surfaces Marine-level data.

### Forms

- EDFR Report. Viewed per MCTFSPRIUM 120303 cycle.
- Correcting KSD Packet. Per MCTFSPRIUM codes.
- Alpha Roster Update Memorandum. Local format.
- Aged Rejection Brief. Local format to the commander.

## Common pitfalls

- DFR not viewed on the cycle day after join. Per MCTFSPRIUM 120303, the DFR cycle catches rejections early.
- DFR not certified within 10 calendar days. Per MCTFSPRIUM 120303, certify every DFR within 10 calendar days.
- Correcting KSD missing required document type code. Per MCTFSPRIUM<Citation source="MCTFSPRIUM" />, the code matches the document.
- Alpha roster diverges from MCTFS. The two records reconcile through the EDFR cycle.
- Aged rejections not briefed to the commander. The commander needs visibility on pay-affecting open rejections.
- Dependency mismatch not resolved. The dependency documents support BAH and FSA. Mismatch drives rate errors.
- COLA initiation rejection delayed. OCONUS COLA pays from the duty assumption date.

## Decision points

- Rejection priority. Pay-affecting rejections (BAH, dependency, COLA, FSA) resolve first. Non-pay rejections (EAS, AFADBD) resolve next.
- Correcting KSD versus diary correction. Some rejections clear through a new KSD attachment. Others require a new diary transaction.
- Escalation to MARFOR. Persistent rejections route to MARFOR support for system-level resolution.
- Aged rejection commander brief. Rejections at 3-4 working days route to the S-1 officer. Rejections past the 5 business day timeliness goal route to the commander.
