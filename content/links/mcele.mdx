MARADMIN 467/26 paragraph 3a lists MCeLE as the home of self-paced and instructor-led data and AI courses.

- Basic AI Course, CDETBAIC01. Mandatory for every Active Duty and Reserve Marine, officer and enlisted, NLT 31 December 2026 per MARADMIN 214/26.
- Basic Data Literacy Course, DATA0BAS01. Hosted by II MEF. An introduction to working with and interpreting data.

Log in with your CAC or a username and password. Civilians in Marine Corps departments are encouraged to take the Basic AI Course.
