The Multi-Host Internet Access Portal (MIAP) is the DISA portal for access to the Marine Corps Total Force System (MCTFS). Admin specialists, leaders, and commanders with MCTFS access need it.

From 1 September 2026, personnel use MIAP to access MCTFS. Reflection Desktop, the locally installed application, is scheduled for removal from Marine Corps workstations on or after 31 August 2026. Commands confirm every MCTFS user holds validated MIAP access per PAA 01-26, paragraph 4.a.

The change covers the access method only. MCTFS permissions, roles, and authorized procedures stay as they were (PAA 01-26, paragraph 5). For help, call the DISA help line at 1-844-347-2457.
