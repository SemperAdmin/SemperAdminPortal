MARADMIN 467/26 paragraph 3j lists the Microsoft Organizational Skilling program, formerly the Enterprise Skills Initiative. It offers structured learning paths, free on-demand videos, hands-on labs, and expert-led courses for cloud and AI roles.

Before you start, link a personal Microsoft account to your .mil account. You need the link to use the resources.
