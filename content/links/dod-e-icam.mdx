Department of Defense Enterprise Identity, Credential, and Access Management
replaces AutoSAAR as the authoritative method to request, modify, and revoke
MCTFS end-user access effective 21 September 2026 per PAA 02-26. The link above
is the MCTFS instance named in the E-ICAM-MCTFS Account Validation Quick Start
Guide. Sign in with a Common Access Card or Personal Identity Verification
credential over NIPRNet on a government furnished computer, select the
certificate, then enter the PIN. Chrome, Edge, and Firefox work. Internet
Explorer does not. Sessions end after 10 minutes.

Two notes before you start. Fields on this site load slowly, so give each page
time to populate. The colored banner across the top names the environment you
reached, pink for production and orange for pre-production.

Existing users and supervisors validate identity and roles here by
25 September 2026. Accounts left unvalidated on 30 September 2026 are subject to
immediate disablement and a fresh request. The Account Provisioning and SAAR-N
Processing page carries the step-by-step validation walkthrough.

Route technical problems to the DISA Global Service Desk. Route questions about
MCTFS roles, supporting documentation, functional approval, and mainframe
account status to the supporting MISSO.
