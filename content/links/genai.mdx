GenAI.mil is the DoW generative AI platform.

MARADMIN 467/26 paragraph 3f lists live virtual training events hosted by Google. Register at genai.mil/events to learn AI fundamentals and the basics of building your own AI agents. Paragraph 3g adds Google Skills for self-paced courses through the platform.
