CROSS is the Marine Corps records management system of record. Commands record the CDRM appointment here, upload the signed NAVMC 10030 Commander's Records Management Acknowledgement Agreement, and post annual records management training rosters. The command's page holds these under the Documents tab, Miscellaneous folder. MCO 5210.11F<Citation source="MCO 5210.11F" /> drives the requirements. MCBul 5210 carried interim changes and shows a cancellation date of August 2026, so treat it as cancelled until ARD names a successor. The IGMC records management checklist inspects for an active CDRM appointment recorded in the system. The link opens the redesigned CROSS site on SharePoint, CAC required on the mil network.
