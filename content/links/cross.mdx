CROSS is the Marine Corps records management system of record. Commands record the CDRM appointment here, upload the signed NAVMC 10030 Commander's Records Management Acknowledgement Agreement within 90 days of assuming command, and post annual records management training rosters. The command's page holds these under the Documents tab, Miscellaneous folder. MCBul 5210 and MCO 5210.11F drive the requirements, and the IGMC records management checklist inspects for an active CDRM appointment recorded in the system. The link opens the redesigned CROSS site on SharePoint, CAC required on the mil network.
