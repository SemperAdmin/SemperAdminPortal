PAA 02-26 routes DoD E-ICAM technical assistance here. Open a ticket for a CAC,
PIV, or ECA sign-in failure, for credential registration with the identity
provider, for a stalled work item, or for a workgroup with no remaining members
to approve a request.

This desk does not answer MCTFS questions. Route role selection, supporting
documentation, functional approval, and mainframe account status to the
supporting MISSO. General E-ICAM questions go to the Community Cyber board at
https://services.disa.mil/csm or to the E-ICAM manager inside your organization.
