The HQMC Enlisted Promotions page for Sergeants and below. It posts each month's cutting scores by MOS for promotion to Corporal and Sergeant, with guidance on viewing cutoff scores in MCTFS, time in grade and time in service, PME, commander responsibilities, and the junior remedial process. MARADMIN 502/26 ended the monthly promotion MARADMIN, so this page and the MCTFS JEPA screen now carry the monthly scores. Path on manpower.marines.mil.
