The Marine Corps Cyberspace Operations Group mainframe security site is the home
for Terminal Area Security Officer business. PAA 02-26 moved MCTFS end-user
access requests to DoD E-ICAM, and it left this lane alone. Commands still
appoint and maintain a primary and alternate qualified TASO. The TASO still
creates the User ACID at check-in, deletes it at check-out, supports suspension
and reactivation, reports misuse and account discrepancies, and reviews access
when people transfer, separate, or retire.

Appointment and establishment of privileged TASO, Limited Security Control
Account, Zone Control Accessor ID, Division Control Accessor ID, and Department
Control Accessor ID accounts also stay under MCCOG procedures until superseding
guidance publishes. Do not route those through DoD E-ICAM.
