## Why Your Section Needs an SSRM

Your section's emails, orders, reports, and decision papers are federal records. The S-1 does not hold them. You do. The Command Designated Records Manager (CDRM) runs the program across the command. The Staff Section Records Manager (SSRM) runs it inside one section, directorate, or program.

IGMC checklist 5210 item 0104 checks for an SSRM in CROSS for every section holding records outside the S-1. The inspector opens CROSS, not your desk. No appointment in CROSS, no credit.

Authority runs through MCO 5210.11F<Citation source="MCO 5210.11F" /> paragraph 4c(3)(b), which has the administrative officer advise the commander on appointing records personnel in each staff section, and MCBul 5210<Citation source="MCBul 5210" /> paragraph 3.b.(2), which has the CDRM appoint support personnel for sections needing attention.

## The Procedure

1. Accept the appointment. Owner, section OIC or SNCOIC. Trigger, CDRM request or a section without an SSRM. Confirm your name shows on the command CROSS page.
2. Train. Owner, you. Trigger, appointment and every 12 months after. Take MarineNet M01RMT0700, the DON Waypoints course, or the CROSS course USMC RM-Everyone's Responsibility Training. Send the certificate to the CDRM for upload to Documents, Training Documents. IGMC items 0103 and 0104.
3. Inventory the section's records. Owner, you. Trigger, appointment and annual review. List what the section creates and keeps, by subject. Give the list to the CDRM to map against the master file plan. IGMC item 0105.
4. Move records into SharePoint Online. Owner, you, with the Site Collection Administrator. Trigger, any record sitting on a shared drive, a desktop, or a personal OneDrive. Records belong in the SharePoint Online Records Library under the schedule the CDRM assigns. MARADMIN 331/24 directs the shared drive migration.
5. Report paper. Owner, you. Trigger, any paper record kept in the section. Tell the CDRM what it is and where it sits. Paper on premises needs a NAVMC 10030/1 waiver. IGMC item 0201.
6. Feed the essential records inventory. Owner, you. Trigger, the annual inventory. Name the records your section needs to keep operating in a crisis. Confirm the copies are current. IGMC items 0301 through 0303.
7. Train the section. Owner, you. Trigger, a Marine joins, and the annual cycle. New personnel complete training within 60 days of arrival under NARA Bulletin 2017-01<Citation source="NARA Bulletin 2017-01" />. Send completion to the CDRM for the command roster.
8. Hand off. Owner, you. Trigger, PCS, EAS, or a billet change. Name your replacement to the CDRM before you leave.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Text messages are records too">
Business conducted by text message is a record. Forward the record copy to your official Marine Corps account within 20 days under 44 U.S.C. 2911, per MCBul 5210 paragraph 3.b.(1)(e). Use Microsoft Teams Chat on a government device for command business.
</Callout>

## Decision Points

<Callout variant="info" title="Delete or keep">
The schedule decides, not the clutter. SECNAV M-5210.1<Citation source="SECNAV M-5210.1" /> Part III sets how long each record lives. Ask the CDRM for the schedule before you delete anything from the Records Library. A litigation hold freezes destruction.
</Callout>

<Callout variant="info" title="Record or working copy">
A draft, a convenience copy, or a reference copy is not the record. The signed final, and the email carrying a decision, are. Keep the record in the Records Library. Clear working copies on your own schedule.
</Callout>

## What the Inspector Asks You

- Show me your appointment in CROSS.
- Show me your training certificate.
- Where does your section keep its records.
- Which of your records are essential records.
- Do you hold any paper.

Answer from CROSS and SharePoint Online. A verbal answer with no upload behind it fails the item.

## Coaching Your Marines

- Save to the Records Library, not to a desktop.
- Name files so the next Marine finds them.
- Forward business texts inside 20 days.
- Finish annual records training on time.
