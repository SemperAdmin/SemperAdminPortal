## Start Here If You Are New to POV Shipment and Mileage

Privately Owned Vehicle (POV) handling at PCS depends on whether the move is CONUS or OCONUS. For OCONUS PCS, the Government ships ONE POV at no cost (only two Service members married to each other ship two). For CONUS PCS, the Government pays MALT (Monetary Allowance In Lieu of Transportation) at a per-mile rate when the Marine drives the POV. POV storage applies only in the cases JTR 0532-A1 lists, such as an OCONUS PDS where POV shipment is prohibited. Shipping a second POV applies only to two Service members married to each other.

This page walks you through POV from your seat as a General Marine. By the end you will know.

- POV shipment for OCONUS PCS (one POV at Government expense)
- MALT mileage for CONUS PCS
- POV storage under JTR 0532
- Second POV rule
- The USTRANSCOM Global POV Contract site
- Insurance and import requirements at OCONUS destinations

Every fact on this page comes from JTR Chapter 5 paragraphs 053001 and 050203, DTR 4500.9-R Volume 4, and the USTRANSCOM Global POV Contract site.

## POV Shipment (OCONUS PCS)

Per JTR Chapter 5 paragraph 053001<Citation source="JTR" />, the Government ships ONE POV at no cost to the Marine for OCONUS PCS. POV transportation is discretionary and the Government restricts it for some duty stations. Confirm with your PPSO.

### Eligibility

The Marine on PCS to or from an OCONUS PDS qualifies for POV shipment. The POV must be.

- Owned by the Marine or the Marine's dependent.
- Properly registered and insured.
- Within the size and weight limits for the shipping carrier.

### One POV Standard

The authorization is ONE POV. JTR 053001 gives no approval path for a second POV, and no MMEA or MMOA request adds one.

### Second POV Rule

Per JTR 053001-B.2.b<Citation source="JTR" />, only two Service members married to each other ship two POVs, one each. Every other Marine ships one. A second vehicle moves at your own expense.

### POV Shipment Process

1. The Marine schedules POV shipment through the USTRANSCOM Global POV Contract site at https://www.pcsmypov.com/. Confirm the site with your PPSO.
2. The Marine drops off the POV at the designated origin VPC (Vehicle Processing Center).
3. The Government carrier ships the POV by ocean vessel. JTR Table 5-64 lists transportation by air as not authorized.
4. The Marine picks up the POV at the destination VPC.

Typical shipment time is 30 to 60 days depending on origin and destination.

### POV Insurance and Registration

The Marine maintains insurance during shipment. The carrier provides limited liability coverage but the Marine's insurance is the primary coverage.

The destination country's POV registration and inspection requirements apply upon arrival. Common OCONUS destinations require.

- Local registration with the host nation.
- Local insurance per the host nation requirements.
- Vehicle inspection (often more rigorous than U.S. standards).
- Specific emissions or safety modifications in some countries.

The PPSO and the Marine's gaining unit S-1 provide host nation specifics.

## MALT (Monetary Allowance In Lieu of Transportation)

Per JTR Chapter 5 paragraph 050203, MALT is the per-mile reimbursement for the Marine driving the POV during a CONUS PCS.

### MALT Rate

The MALT rate is published by GSA and updates periodically. The rate is $0.235 per mile for travel on or after 1 July 2026, and $0.205 per mile from 1 January to 30 June 2026, per DTMO UTD/CTD 46-26. One MALT payment covers everyone riding in the vehicle.

### Authorized Mileage

The Marine receives MALT for the official distance between the old PDS and the new PDS per the official military distance lookup. The route does not have to be the most direct route, but MALT pays only the official distance.

### MALT for Multiple Drivers

When you and an authorized dependent relocate on a PCS move and drive two POVs, MALT is paid for each vehicle, including car ferry fees for each POV, per JTR 050203 Table 5-1 row 1<Citation source="JTR" />. Passengers riding in the same vehicle draw no separate MALT.

### MALT Plus Per Diem

Marines driving a POV during PCS receive MALT for mileage AND per diem for travel days. The PCS Per Diem page covers per diem during PCS travel.

## POV Storage

Per JTR Chapter 5, POV storage at Government expense applies in specific scenarios.

### When Storage Applies

An unaccompanied tour does not earn POV storage. Per JTR 0532-A1<Citation source="JTR" />, storage for one POV applies only when you are in one of these cases.

- Ordered to an OCONUS PDS where POV transportation is not permitted by country, area, law, regulation, or required POV modification.
- Sent on TDY in support of a contingency operation for more than 30 days.
- Sent on TDY in support of humanitarian assistance or emergency operations declared by an Executive Order or the administering Secretary.
- Authorized a POV on a ship home port change with 31 or more days between departure and arrival.
- Authorized a POV on a unit PCS with the unit deployed 31 days or more en route.
- Required to remove a POV from an OCONUS PDS for reasons beyond your control.

POV storage when POV transportation is authorized is not allowed. The PPSO coordinates the storage location and arrangements.

### Storage Duration

Storage runs in place of POV shipment to and from the PDS where shipment is prohibited.

### Storage Restrictions

Storage at Government expense is for one POV. Storage of additional POVs is at the Marine's expense unless special authorization applies.

## USTRANSCOM Global POV Contract Site

The USTRANSCOM Global POV Contract site at https://www.pcsmypov.com/ is the central portal for POV shipment.

### Functions

- Schedule POV shipment.
- Locate the nearest VPC.
- Track the shipment status.
- File claims for damage or loss.

### Marine Interaction

The Marine creates an account, schedules pickup, drops off the POV at the VPC, tracks the shipment, and picks up at the destination VPC.

## POV Mileage Reimbursement Submission

The Marine submits the MALT mileage claim through the Defense Travel System (DTS) on DD Form 1351-2<Citation source="DD Form 1351-2" /> (Travel Voucher) at the new PDS.

### Required Documentation

- Mileage from official military distance lookup.
- Travel dates.
- Route taken (if non-standard, with justification).

The AO approves the voucher. DFAS processes the payment.

## Common Questions Marines Ask

Will the Government ship my car to Japan?

Yes per JTR Chapter 5 paragraph 053001. The Government ships ONE POV at no cost for OCONUS PCS, subject to restrictions at some duty stations. Schedule shipment through the USTRANSCOM Global POV Contract site at https://www.pcsmypov.com/.

I have two cars. Will the Government ship both?

The Government ships one POV. Only two Service members married to each other ship two. No MMEA or MMOA approval path exists for a second POV.

I am driving from Camp Lejeune to Camp Pendleton. Will I get mileage reimbursement?

Yes per JTR Chapter 5 paragraph 050203. MALT pays $0.235 per mile for travel on or after 1 July 2026 per DTMO UTD/CTD 46-26. The mileage is based on the official military distance.

I am going OCONUS unaccompanied. Where does my car go?

An unaccompanied tour alone does not earn POV storage. If you ship to the OCONUS PDS, the Government ships one POV. Storage applies only when shipment to the PDS is prohibited or another JTR 0532-A1 case applies. The PPSO coordinates the storage location.

How long does POV shipment take?

Typically 30 to 60 days for ocean shipment between CONUS and OCONUS. Track through the USTRANSCOM Global POV Contract site.

Do I need insurance during POV shipment?

Yes. Maintain your standard insurance during shipment. The carrier provides limited liability but your insurance is the primary coverage. The destination country may require local insurance upon arrival.

Will my MALT cover all my driving expenses?

MALT is the per-mile rate for moving expenses. It does NOT cover gas, lodging, or meals separately. The PCS Per Diem framework covers travel-day expenses (lodging, meals).
