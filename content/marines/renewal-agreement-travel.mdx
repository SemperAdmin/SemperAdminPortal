## Start Here If You Are New to COT and OTEIP

Consecutive Overseas Tour (COT) is the framework when a Marine moves directly from one OCONUS PDS to another OCONUS PDS. COT leave travel gives you and your eligible dependent Government-funded travel to your home of record between the two tours. Renewal Agreement Travel (RAT) is a civilian employee entitlement under JTR Part H section 0550<Citation source="JTR" />. Marines have no RAT entitlement. A Marine who extends an overseas tour uses the Overseas Tour Extension Incentive Program (OTEIP) under MARADMIN 450/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 450/26" />, which covers the three OTEIP incentives ($2,000, or SR&R absence with or without funded transport).

This page walks you through COT and OTEIP from your seat as a General Marine. By the end you will know.

- COT framework for direct OCONUS-to-OCONUS PCS
- COT leave eligibility and allowances under JTR 050812
- The relationship to OTEIP (Pay and Entitlements category)
- Why RAT does not apply to Marines

Every fact on this page comes from JTR Chapter 5 paragraphs 050809 and 050812<Citation source="JTR" />, Vol 7A Chapter 14, and MCO 1300.8<Citation source="MCO 1300.8" /> Chapter 6 Section 13.

## What COT Is

Per JTR Chapter 5 paragraph 050809<Citation source="JTR" />, Consecutive Overseas Tour is the framework when a Marine stationed OCONUS is selected to serve a consecutive overseas tour at another OCONUS PDS.

### COT Eligibility

Per JTR Chapter 5 paragraph 050809<Citation source="JTR" />, COT applies when.

- The Marine completes an OCONUS tour.
- The Marine is reassigned to a different OCONUS PDS.

### COT Benefits

A Marine on COT receives.

- All standard PCS allowances (DLA, HHG, etc.) for the PCS to the new OCONUS PDS.
- COT leave travel under JTR 050812-A1<Citation source="JTR" /> when you meet the Table 5-25 rules. You must be stationed OCONUS and ordered to an IPCOT or a COT. Either one tour is unaccompanied, or both tours are accompanied and the total time at the two PDSs equals or exceeds the sum of the unaccompanied tour lengths. A dependent must be command-sponsored for both tours and accompany you during both tours.
- Travel between authorized locations, with City Pair Program airfares, from the old PDS to your home of record and then to the new PDS, per JTR 050812-B. Travel to any other destination is limited to the cost to your home of record.

OTEIP does not attach to a COT move. OTEIP rewards extending at the current location, and per MARADMIN 450/26 the extension period ends on departure from the OCONUS location on PCS orders.

### COT and Travel Coordination

The Marine and dependents take COT leave through the home of record per JTR 050812-B. Per MCO 1300.8<Citation source="MCO 1300.8" /> Chapter 6 Section 12, leave taken while traversing CONUS in conjunction with the PCS orders counts as the COT travel. The Marine receives no deferred COT leave travel afterward.

## OCONUS Tour Extension Incentive Program (OTEIP)

Per MARADMIN 450/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 450/26" />, a Marine who extends an OCONUS assignment 12 months or more at an eligible location elects one of three incentives.

1. $2,000, paid in 12 installments of $166.66.
2. SR&R absence, 30 days non-chargeable leave.
3. SR&R absence, 15 days plus round-trip Government transportation to the nearest CONUS port of entry, 20 days for an extension of 13 months or more.

The Marine elects when using the incentive, not at the extension request. The Overseas Tour Extension Incentive Program page on the Pay and Entitlements category covers OTEIP in detail.

### OTEIP and COT Leave Relationship

OTEIP option 3 includes funded transportation, but it is an OTEIP incentive, not a JTR travel entitlement. Per MARADMIN 450/26 paragraph 3, OTEIP and IPCOT leave travel are separate and not interchangeable. OTEIP travel is funded by a line of accounting requested from CMC (MMIB-3). MARADMIN 450/26 paragraph 7.e encourages Marines to submit requests at least 30 days before the desired travel date.

## Extension Agreement Obligation

OTEIP requires a 12-month extension agreement per MARADMIN 450/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 450/26" />. The Pay Problems and Fixes page covers the recoupment framework for unfulfilled service agreements.

## Marine Corps Service Regulation

Per Vol 7A Chapter 14 paragraph 4.3, the Marine Corps regulation for OCONUS tour extensions and incentives is Marine Corps Order 1300.8 Chapter 6 Section 13. Under this authority, MARADMIN 450/26 publishes the current OTEIP rules. OTEIP carries no specialty list. From 1 November 2026, every OCONUS location qualifies except Hawaii and the Marine Corps Forces Europe area.

## Common Questions Marines Ask

I am at MCAS Iwakuni and want to extend my tour. What benefits do I get?

Extending 12 months or more at Iwakuni qualifies you for OTEIP under MARADMIN 450/26. You pick $2,000, 30 days SR&R, or 15 to 20 days SR&R with a funded round trip. Per MARADMIN 450/26, OTEIP and IPCOT leave travel are not interchangeable. Your career counselor confirms which applies to your request.

Do Marines get Renewal Agreement Travel (RAT)?

No. RAT is a civilian employee entitlement under JTR Part H section 0550<Citation source="JTR" />. A Marine who extends overseas uses OTEIP under MARADMIN 450/26, and COT leave travel under JTR 050812 applies when you move to a second OCONUS PDS.

I am moving from MCAS Iwakuni to Camp Hansen, Okinawa. Is this a COT?

Per JTR Chapter 5 paragraph 050809<Citation source="JTR" />, yes. OCONUS-to-OCONUS PCS to a consecutive overseas tour qualifies as a Consecutive Overseas Tour. Standard PCS allowances apply. OTEIP does not attach to a COT move.
