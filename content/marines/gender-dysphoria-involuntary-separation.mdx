<Callout variant="warning" title="Two deadlines run short">
  You have 10 calendar days from your notification of separation to elect a board. Miss it and you waive the board. If you later seek a retention waiver, you have 15 calendar days to submit it, and nothing is accepted after day 15. Talk to a defense counsel as soon as you are notified.
</Callout>

## Who This Page Is For

MARADMIN 189/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 189/26" /> covers Marines who have a current diagnosis or history of, or show symptoms consistent with, gender dysphoria, and who did not request voluntary discharge under ALNAV 038/25. It applies to the Total Force, active and reserve.

This page covers your rights, your deadlines, your pay, and your options. Every fact comes from MARADMIN 189/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 189/26" /> and MARADMIN 539/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 539/26" />.

## The Standards Rule

Per paragraph 1.a, you meet the standards for your biological sex, and DEERS and every other system of record show your sex accurately. Any exception to policy you held for grooming, uniform, heads, barracks, or locker rooms is revoked.

## How Marines Are Identified

Per paragraph 2, identification happens one of two ways.

1. Your Periodic Health Assessment (PHA). If you report a diagnosis, history, or symptoms on the PHA questionnaire, medical evaluates you against medical standards. Nobody directs you to take a PHA outside your normal schedule.
2. Your commander's awareness. It is limited to four sources.
   - A prior request from you for an exception to policy on sex-based standards.
   - A prior request from you to change the sex marker in DEERS.
   - A medical treatment plan, restricted duty status, or deployment waiver tied to a documented diagnosis.
   - Your own written statement to your chain of command.

If your commander has other facts, the commander takes them to the first general officer in your chain. The general officer decides case by case with a judge advocate and the Bureau of Medicine and Surgery (BUMED) Deployability Action Cell, based on a medical record review.

## What Happens After Identification

1. BUMED reviews your medical record. If you meet the medical criteria, BUMED lists you as non-deployable and not medically ready. Paragraph 3.a.
2. Your command sends a referral letter to Manpower Military Policy (MPO), which sends it to the Consolidated Disposition Authority (CDA). The CDA is the Commander, Navy Installations Command. Paragraph 3.
3. Your command places you on administrative absence. You start pre-separation requirements right away, including the Transition Assistance Program. You get maximum flexibility to finish them remotely or in civilian clothes. Paragraph 4.

## Your Pay During Administrative Absence

Per paragraph 4.a, you keep full pay and benefits. You do not receive bonus, special, or incentive pays while in administrative absence.

You start the absence yourself in Marine Online (MOL). Go to the Leave and Liberty module and select "Other (Enter Reason in Comments Box)." Enter this comment exactly.

"Administrative absence in accordance with ALNAV 011/26 for the duration of the retirement/separation process."

### Selected Marine Corps Reserve

Per paragraph 4.b, your commander normally excuses you from drill for the length of the process. Excused drills earn no drill pay and no retirement points.

## Your Rights

Per paragraph 5.b, you hold these rights.

1. The CDA notifies you through your command within 30 calendar days of your referral.
2. You are entitled to a board no matter how long you have served. Enlisted Marines get an administrative separation board. Officers get a Board of Inquiry.
3. You consult a military defense counsel, or a civilian defense counsel at your own expense.
4. You elect a board by responding to the CDA and MPO within 10 calendar days of notification. No response in 10 days waives the board.
5. You get at least 30 days from notification before a board convenes. Paragraph 5.e.1.

## What the Board Decides

Per paragraph 5.f, the board decides only two questions. Do you have a diagnosis or history of, or show symptoms consistent with, gender dysphoria? If so, should you be separated?

If gender dysphoria is the only basis, your characterization of service is Honorable. Nothing less favorable is authorized.

The Secretary of the Navy (SECNAV) is the separation authority. Paragraph 5.a.2.

## After the Board

Per paragraph 6.b, the Assistant Secretary of the Navy for Manpower and Reserve Affairs reviews your package and recommends to SECNAV.

- Board finds a basis, the Assistant Secretary agrees. You choose to accept it or submit a retention waiver.
- Board finds a basis, the Assistant Secretary disagrees. You are retained.
- Enlisted board finds no basis, the Assistant Secretary agrees. You are retained.
- Enlisted board finds no basis, the Assistant Secretary disagrees. You choose to accept it or submit a retention waiver.
- Officer board finds no basis. The case closes and you are retained.

If you waived your board, MPO tells you of your right to submit a retention waiver. Paragraph 6.c.

## Retention Waiver

Per paragraph 6.d, you need all three.

1. 36 consecutive months of stability in your sex without clinically significant distress or impairment in social, work, or other important areas.
2. You never attempted to transition to a sex other than your biological sex.
3. You are willing and able to meet all standards for your sex.

Per paragraph 6.e, you file on two tracks at the same time.

1. Send your waiver package with your evidence directly to MPO within 15 calendar days of notification of the Assistant Secretary's recommendation.
2. Give the same complete package to your chain of command. Your command routes it to the first general officer, whose endorsement assesses compelling government interest.

SECNAV alone approves a retention waiver. Paragraph 6.f.

## If You Separate

Per paragraph 7, these terms apply.

- Reentry code RE-3G. Coming back requires an accession waiver.
- Any remaining service obligation is waived, including a Post-9/11 GI Bill transfer obligation and any inactive reserve obligation.
- Involuntary separation pay is possible. In general you need at least 6 years of continuous active duty.
- Bonus, incentive, and special pays stop when administrative absence starts. The Department of the Navy has authority to recoup unearned amounts under 37 U.S.C. 373.
- You keep every other separation and transition benefit you are entitled to, except SkillBridge. SkillBridge is not authorized.

## Retirement Instead of Separation

Per paragraph 8, retirement is an option if you have the service for it.

- Eligible for transfer to the Fleet Marine Corps Reserve or the retired list. Request retirement within 30 calendar days of identification. The retirement date is no more than 6 months after identification.
- More than 18 but less than 20 years of active service. You are potentially eligible for Temporary Early Retirement Authority (TERA). Ask MPO through your command.
- More than 15 but less than 18 years. You request an exception to policy for TERA. Pending requests do not delay your board.
- Already approved for retirement. You transfer no later than 6 months after identification.

## Other Processes Running at the Same Time

- Disability Evaluation System (DES). Gender dysphoria alone is not a disability. If you have another qualifying condition, you are referred to the DES. The DES result takes precedence, and SECNAV holds the final separation decision until the DES finishes. Paragraph 9.a.
- Another administrative separation. Processing under this policy pauses until the other board finishes. If you are not separated there, this processing resumes. Paragraph 9.b.

## Your EAS Arrives First

Per MARADMIN 539/26<Citation source="MARADMIN 539/26" />, extensions to finish this process are no longer authorized. If your Expiration of Active Service (EAS) arrives before your proceedings finish, you separate on your scheduled EAS.

The message does not say how your separation code or involuntary separation pay apply in this case. If your EAS falls inside the process, ask your command to get the answer from MPO before EAS.

## Your Privacy

Per paragraph 12.b, your commander protects your health information like any other protected health information. Only personnel with a specific need to know for official duties see it.

## Where to Get Help

- Defense counsel. Your servicing defense office, as soon as you are notified.
- Military OneSource. 24/7 at 1-800-342-9647 or militaryonesource.mil, for you and your family. Paragraph 13.
- Your command. Per paragraph 12.f, your commander advises you on mental health, financial, and separation resources and checks in regularly.
- Process questions. Your S-1, which routes to MPO.
