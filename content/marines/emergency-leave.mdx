## Start Here If You Are New to Emergency Leave

Emergency leave is granted for death or imminent illness or injury of immediate family. Your leave granting authority approves it, and no 30-day limit applies. American Red Cross verification is not required, even for funded travel. Emergency leave IS chargeable against your accrued leave balance, and funded leave travel covers overseas to CONUS, CONUS to overseas, and between overseas areas. Immediate family covers your family and your spouse's family: parents, children, and siblings, including step and half relationships, and a person standing in loco parentis. Per MCO 1050.3J<Citation source="MCO 1050.3J" /> Chapter 2 paragraph 12.d, this status normally existed for 5 continuous years before you entered the Marine Corps, and your leave granting authority makes the final call. Grandparents are not on the list unless they stood in loco parentis. Commanders are required to liberally approve emergency leave during family emergencies.

This page walks you through emergency leave from your seat as a General Marine. By the end you will know.

- Who approves emergency leave and when CMC approval is not needed
- Immediate family coverage definition
- Why American Red Cross verification is optional
- Funded leave travel and which routes it covers
- Pay continuation during emergency leave
- Bereavement leave for the death of a spouse or child
- The 24/7 emergency leave processing requirement

Every fact on this page comes from MCO 1050.3J<Citation source="MCO 1050.3J" /> Chapter 2 paragraph 12 and Chapter 4 paragraph 5 (19 May 2009), the Joint Travel Regulations, and American Red Cross Emergency Communications procedures.

## What Emergency Leave Is

Per MCO 1050.3J<Citation source="MCO 1050.3J" /> Chapter 2 paragraph 12, emergency leave is granted in extraordinary circumstances involving death or imminent illness or injury of immediate family.

Emergency leave IS charged against the Marine's accrued leave balance (chargeable leave). The Marine Corps provides expedited approval and funded leave travel (for OCONUS Marines) to support the Marine during family emergencies.

## Eligibility (Qualifying Events)

Emergency leave is granted for.

- Death of immediate family.
- Imminent death (terminal illness with limited time remaining) of immediate family.
- Serious illness or injury of immediate family requiring the Marine's presence.

### Immediate Family Definition

Immediate family for emergency leave purposes includes.

- Spouse.
- Children (sons and daughters, including step and half relationships).
- Parents (biological, adoptive, step, in loco parentis).
- Spouse's parents (father, mother, or person standing in loco parentis).
- Siblings (brothers and sisters, including step and half relationships).
- Other family members at command discretion based on circumstances.

Per MCO 1050.3J<Citation source="MCO 1050.3J" /> Chapter 2 paragraph 12.c.(1), the immediate family list is father, mother, person standing in loco parentis, son, daughter, brother, and sister, including step and half relationships. Grandparents are not on the list. A grandparent qualifies when the grandparent stood in loco parentis for you. Otherwise your commander decides.

The local commander has discretion to interpret immediate family broadly when circumstances warrant.

## American Red Cross Emergency Communications

The American Red Cross provides emergency communications verification through Service members and family members. The Red Cross verifies the family emergency and provides the Marine and the Marine's command with documentation.

### Red Cross Verification Is Not Required

Per MCO 1050.3J<Citation source="MCO 1050.3J" /> Chapter 2 paragraph 12.f, American Red Cross verification is not required, even for funded emergency leave. The command considers your request on its merits.

The leave granting authority asks for help confirming the emergency only when the authority has reason to doubt it. The Red Cross is one option for the check. Phone calls, e-mail, or contact with a family member, minister, physician, or hospital staff also work.

### How to Use the Red Cross Process (Optional)

If your command asks for help confirming the emergency, the family member contacts the American Red Cross at 1-877-272-7337 (or local Red Cross chapter). The Red Cross.

1. Verifies the emergency through hospital, mortuary, or medical facility records.
2. Communicates the verification to the Marine's command.
3. Provides documentation supporting the leave request and any funded travel.

## Approval Process

Per MCO 1050.3J<Citation source="MCO 1050.3J" /> Chapter 2 paragraph 12 and Chapter 4 paragraph 5.

### 24/7 Processing

Emergency leave processing is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The unit Officer of the Day (OOD), Staff Duty Officer (SDO), or appropriate after-hours command duty position handles emergency leave requests outside normal business hours.

### Standard Approval Authority

The Marine's commanding officer or designated leave granting authority approves emergency leave. Per MCO 1050.3J<Citation source="MCO 1050.3J" /> Chapter 2 paragraph 12.b, the leave granting authority approves emergency leave for more than 60 days without CMC approval, as long as the leave uses only accrued leave and advance leave leaving an advance leave balance of 30 days or less.

### Extensions

Extensions go through the same leave granting authority. Per MCO 1050.3J<Citation source="MCO 1050.3J" /> Chapter 2 paragraph 12.e(7), any Marine Corps activity grants an extension for a Marine on emergency leave from an overseas unit only if the Marine cannot contact the parent command. Common grounds for extension include.

- Continuing medical crisis requiring the Marine's presence.
- Extended bereavement processing or estate matters.
- Military requirement for the Marine's presence (e.g., serving as fiduciary).

## Pay Continuation

Per MCO 1050.3J<Citation source="MCO 1050.3J" /> Chapter 2 paragraph 12, the Marine on emergency leave continues to receive.

- Basic pay.
- BAH.
- BAS.
- Special pays the Marine is entitled to.
- Other entitlements per the standard framework.

The Marine remains in a duty status (in a leave status) for pay purposes.

## Funded Leave Travel for OCONUS Marines

Per MCO 1050.3J<Citation source="MCO 1050.3J" /> Chapter 4 paragraph 5 and the Joint Travel Regulations, Marines on emergency leave are eligible for funded leave travel from overseas to CONUS, from CONUS to overseas, and between overseas areas, and return when required, provided an AMC channel exists for any segment of the journey.

### What Funded Leave Travel Covers

The funded leave travel provides government transportation (typically a commercial airline ticket) from the OCONUS PDS to the funded location nearest the family emergency. The Government covers the round-trip transportation cost.

### Documentation Requirements

The Marine submits.

- The emergency leave request.
- The proposed travel itinerary.
- The destination address (the family emergency location).

The S-1 routes the funded leave travel request through the appropriate channels (typically the Defense Travel System or the local TMO) for processing.

## Relationship to Bereavement Leave

Bereavement leave is a separate entitlement. Per DoDI 1327.06<Citation source="DoDI 1327.06" /> paragraph 3.11.j, a Marine whose spouse or child dies earns up to 14 consecutive days of non-chargeable bereavement leave, as MARADMIN 220/23 implemented. Your unit commander grants it.

You are eligible when you have fewer than 30 days of accrued leave on the date of death. With 30 or more days, you become eligible once your balance falls below 30 days. The period ends no later than 14 days after the funeral, burial, or memorial service. Bereavement leave combines with emergency leave, annual leave, and other leave types.

Emergency leave still covers the other qualifying events, such as imminent death, serious illness or injury, and the death of a parent or sibling.

## Effect on Promotion, Awards, and Career

Emergency leave does NOT adversely affect.

- Promotion timing.
- Awards eligibility.
- Career advancement.
- Security clearance.

Marines on emergency leave coordinate with the chain of command to ensure career and personal milestones are not impacted.

## Documentation

Common documentation includes.

- Death certificate, medical records, or hospital documentation.
- Funeral or memorial service information.
- Family member contact information for the chain of command.

The unit S-1 processes the emergency leave through MOL.

## Verifying Emergency Leave Authorization

A leave authorization confirms the emergency leave entitlement.

### Step 1. Notify the Chain of Command

The Marine notifies the chain of command immediately upon learning of the family emergency. The notification can be made directly to the OOD/SDO outside normal business hours.

### Step 2. Red Cross Contact (Optional)

Red Cross verification is not required. If your command doubts the emergency, the family member contacts the American Red Cross at 1-877-272-7337.

### Step 3. Submit the Emergency Leave Request

The Marine submits the leave request through the chain of command. The request includes the duration, destination, and emergency contact information.

### Step 4. Coordinate Funded Leave Travel (OCONUS)

For OCONUS Marines, the S-1 coordinates funded leave travel through the Defense Travel System or the local TMO.

### Step 5. Verify the Authorization

The leave authorization is documented in MOL. The Marine receives the authorization.

### Step 6. Resolve Discrepancies

Coordinate with your S-1. The reporting unit (I&I S-1 with direct UD/MIPS access, or a battalion S-1 routing through their PAC) routes through DFAS Marine Corps Pay.

## Common Questions Marines Ask

My grandmother is dying. Can I take emergency leave?

Only in some cases. Grandparents are not on the immediate family list in MCO 1050.3J<Citation source="MCO 1050.3J" /> Chapter 2 paragraph 12.c.(1). A grandparent qualifies when the grandparent stood in loco parentis for you. Otherwise your commander decides, so ask your chain of command.

How long can I take for emergency leave?

No 30-day limit applies. Your leave granting authority approves emergency leave, including more than 60 days without CMC approval, when the leave uses only accrued leave and advance leave leaving an advance balance of 30 days or less.

Does emergency leave count against my 60-day annual leave cap?

Yes. Emergency leave IS chargeable against your accrued leave balance. Unlike convalescent leave (which is non-chargeable), emergency leave reduces your accrued leave balance.

Will the Marine Corps pay for my flight home for the emergency?

Yes, when the trip is overseas to CONUS, CONUS to overseas, or between overseas areas and an AMC channel exists. Funded leave travel is provided per MCO 1050.3J<Citation source="MCO 1050.3J" /> Chapter 4 paragraph 5 and the Joint Travel Regulations.

What if my family emergency happens during a deployment?

The Red Cross emergency communications process is available to support deployed Marines, though verification is not required. Your commander decides on the leave. Funded travel follows JTR paragraph U7205, per MCO 1050.3J<Citation source="MCO 1050.3J" /> Chapter 2 paragraph 12.e(1), and is not automatic.

Do I need American Red Cross verification?

No. Per MCO 1050.3J Chapter 2 paragraph 12.f, Red Cross verification is not required, even for funded emergency leave. Your command asks for help confirming the emergency only when it has reason to doubt it.

What is the Red Cross phone number?

The American Red Cross emergency communications line is 1-877-272-7337. Available 24/7.

Can I extend my emergency leave?

Yes if extraordinary circumstances justify. The extension is routed through the chain of command for approval.

I am a single parent. My child is sick. Can I take emergency leave?

Yes. Imminent illness of an immediate family member (your child) qualifies for emergency leave. Coordinate with your chain of command and provide medical documentation.

Will my pay continue during emergency leave?

Yes. Basic pay, BAH, BAS, and special pays continue throughout emergency leave.

## Where to Go for Help

### Routing by Issue

- Emergency leave request. Through your chain of command immediately upon learning of the family emergency. Contact OOD/SDO outside normal business hours.
- American Red Cross emergency communications. The family member contacts 1-877-272-7337.
- Funded leave travel for OCONUS Marines. Coordinate with your S-1 and the local TMO.
- LES verification during emergency leave. Pull through MyPay each payday.
- Extensions. Submit through your leave granting authority with documentation of the continuing need.
- DFAS Marine Corps Pay direct contact. Phone 1-888-332-7411 (DSN 312-571-9450 from overseas).

- Leaders ensure 24/7 emergency leave processing through OOD/SDO. Coach Marines on Red Cross verification. It is optional and the command uses it only when it doubts the emergency.

- Commanders liberally approve emergency leave during family emergencies. Emergency leave beyond 60 days needs no CMC approval when advance leave stays at 30 days or less.
