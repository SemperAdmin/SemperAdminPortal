## Start Here If You Are New to MEA

You do not draw MEA as a Marine. Miscellaneous Expense Allowance is a civilian employee entitlement under JTR section 0541<Citation source="JTR" />. The JTR has no flat-rate or itemized MEA for Service members. A Marine on PCS draws Dislocation Allowance (DLA) for relocation costs and a separate pet allowance for pet costs.

This page walks you through where MEA applies and where your PCS money comes from. By the end you will know.

- MEA applies to civilian employees only
- DLA is your relocation allowance
- Pet costs run under JTR 050107
- Where to go for help

Every fact on this page comes from JTR Chapter 5 sections 0505 and 0541 and paragraph 050107.

## What MEA Is

Per JTR section 0541<Citation source="JTR" />, a civilian employee must submit a travel claim to receive MEA reimbursement. Per JTR paragraph 054101-A<Citation source="JTR" />, a PCS or TCS must be authorized or approved, and the civilian employee must sign an appropriate service agreement. The flat-rate and itemized-cap rules belong to the civilian framework. They do not apply to you.

## What Pays Marines Instead

### Dislocation Allowance

DLA under JTR Chapter 5 section 0505<Citation source="JTR" /> is your relocation allowance. The Dislocation Allowance page covers the rates and rules.

### Pet Costs

Pet costs run under JTR paragraph 050107-A2 and A3<Citation source="JTR" />. The allowance covers one household pet, a cat or a dog, per move. A CONUS move covers mandatory microchipping, boarding fees, hotel service charges, licensing fees at the new PDS, and pet shipping fees. The cap is $550. An OCONUS move adds quarantine fees and titer level testing for entry. The cap is $2,000. The Pet Transportation Allowance page covers the rules.

## What Does Not Pay

- Lodging during the move. TLE covers CONUS PCS lodging.
- Meals during the move. Per diem covers meals.
- HHG damage or loss. The carrier or PPSO claims process covers it.
- Housing costs. BAH covers housing.

## Common Questions Marines Ask

Do I get MEA on my PCS?

No. MEA is for civilian employees. Claim DLA under JTR section 0505 and pet costs under paragraph 050107.

Where do I claim pet quarantine fees?

Under the pet allowance in JTR paragraph 050107. Submit the claim through DTS with your receipts.
