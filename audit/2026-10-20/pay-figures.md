## Upcoming changes (2027)

Forward-looking only. Nothing here is effective yet.

- Basic pay, 1 Jan 2027. The statutory ECI formula gives 3.6 percent. The President's FY2027 budget proposes a tiered 7, 6, 5 percent raise favoring junior enlisted. The House Armed Services version backs the tiered raise. The Senate version backs 3.6 percent. No final decision as of 20 Oct 2026. Source: https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IN12706.html and https://www.fedmanager.com/news/senate-house-clash-over-military-pay-raise-as-fy2027-ndaa-moves-forward
- BAS, 1 Jan 2027. New rates follow the DoD food-cost index and are not announced yet.
- TSP and IRS limits for 2027. The IRS had not published them as of this audit. Projections run $25,000 to $25,500 for the elective deferral limit, with catch-up near $8,000. Announcement expected late October to mid November 2026 (2026 limits came out 13 Nov 2025). Source: https://401kspecialistmag.com/2027-401k-contribution-limit-projected-to-rise-to-25500/
- HFP and IDP rise to $450 and $275 on 1 Oct 2026 and are already live on the portal. The FMR text still shows $225, so FMR-based citations on the portal need the MARADMIN as the lead authority.
- FY2027 per diem, effective 1 Oct 2026: standard CONUS lodging $113, M&IE $68. Travel spanning 1 Oct may use both fiscal-year rates.
- MALT and TDY mileage reset again with the IRS and GSA CY2027 rates. The July 2026 midyear bump to $0.76 and $0.235 shows the rate moves within a year, so pages that cite a number need a date label.
- Enlistment bonus ceiling of $75,000 authorized through 10 Jan 2027. The pages state the end date, so a renewal check is due before January.
- FY27 SRB program runs 1 Oct 2026 to 30 Sep 2027 and HQMC states it may suspend or terminate the program earlier if funds run out (MARADMIN 208/26 and 429/26).
- DLA rates reset 1 Jan 2027 using the basic pay adjustment.

3. Pulled primary text from the local archive with PyMuPDF: FMR Vol 7A chapters, DTMO MAP/CAP memos for POV mileage (46-26, 73-25) and DLA (72-25), and MARADMINS 003/26, 023/26, 150/26, 208/26, 429/26, 433/26, 573/24.

WebFetch to dfas.mil, militarypay.defense.gov, and the media.defense.gov memo returned HTTP 403, so the archived copies of the DTMO memos served as primary evidence for MALT and DLA.
5. Severity rules: wrong current value is major (blocker if inside a calculator, none found). Stale year label presented as current is minor. Inconsistent values across pages are major.
